Privacy Policy

App: FluxVPN Effective: July 10, 2026 Last Updated: July 28, 2026

FluxVPN ("the App", "we", "us", "our"), operated by Tranquil Mind Technology JSC, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices and rights you have. By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

FluxVPN is a VPN app. Fast, secure VPN with one-tap connect and encrypted browsing. You do not need to create an account to use it.

No-activity-logging. FluxVPN does not monitor, record, or store the websites you visit, your DNS queries, the contents of your traffic, or a per-user history of your connection times or bandwidth. The VPN tunnel is established on your device by the Android VPN service and we do not inspect the traffic that passes through it. The data we do collect (through the third-party SDKs listed below) relates to advertising, analytics, crash diagnostics, and install attribution — not to your browsing activity.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information processed only on your device (not collected by us)

The App's core features run on your device. The following is processed locally and is not collected, transmitted, or stored on our own servers:

  • VPN connection. The App establishes an encrypted VPN tunnel on your device using the Android VPN service (VpnService / BIND_VPN_SERVICE). This permission is used solely to route your device's network traffic through the server you select; the App does not read, inspect, or record the contents of that traffic.
  • VPN server list and configuration. The App downloads VPN server details and OpenVPN configuration files to your device; the selected server and connection settings are stored locally.
  • Notifications. With your permission, the App shows connection-status and reminder notifications on your device.
  • Settings and preferences. Your in-app settings (such as language and selected server) are stored only on your device.

The App does not request GPS or precise-location permission and does not access your device's GPS location.

You can delete this data at any time by clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App.

1.2 Information collected automatically by third-party services

When you use the App, the third-party services listed in Section 3 may automatically collect:

  • Advertising & device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID / IDFA) and basic device information (model, OS version, app version).
  • Approximate location at city/region level, derived from your IP address by ad/attribution networks and by our IP-geolocation provider (ip-api.com), which the App queries to display connection details such as your IP, country, city, and ISP — not GPS-based tracking.
  • App activity & diagnostics such as feature usage (for example connect/disconnect and server-selection events), session data, and crash reports. These events do not include the websites you visit or the contents of your VPN traffic.

1.3 Information you provide

The App does not require account registration and does not ask for personal information such as your name, email, or phone number, unless you voluntarily contact support.

1.4 VPN traffic and activity logs

Because FluxVPN is a VPN, we want to be explicit: we do not keep browsing logs. Specifically, we do not monitor, log, or store on our servers your browsing or navigation history, the websites or apps you access, your DNS queries, the contents of your network traffic, or a per-user record of your original IP address, connection timestamps, session duration, or bandwidth. Your public IP address is transmitted to the third-party services in Section 3 (for example the IP-geolocation, advertising, and analytics providers) in the ordinary course of their operation, but we do not maintain our own log of your VPN activity.

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide the VPN service — download VPN server lists and configurations and establish the encrypted tunnel on your device; remote configuration keeps the App's server list and settings up to date.
  • Show connection details — display your IP, approximate location (city/region), ISP, and the selected server's location on a map, using an IP-geolocation lookup and Google Maps.
  • Advertising and measurement — to serve ads (which fund the free App) and measure ad performance and install attribution.
  • Analytics and improvement — to understand how the App is used and improve features and stability.
  • Fraud prevention, security and compliance — to detect invalid ad traffic and abuse.
  • Legal compliance — to comply with applicable laws and enforce our terms.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information for money. Information is shared only:

  • With the ad, analytics, and attribution partners listed in Section 3, to the extent described above. Under some U.S. state privacy laws (for example the CPRA), the transfer of identifiers to advertising partners for personalized ads may be considered a "sale" or "sharing" — Section 7 explains how to opt out.
  • For legal reasons, if required by law, regulation, legal process, or enforceable governmental request, or to protect our rights, users, or the public.
  • In a business transfer, such as a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, in which case this Policy will continue to apply to previously collected data.

5. Legal Bases (EEA/UK — GDPR)

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on:

  • Consent — for personalized advertising and, where required, analytics. You may withdraw consent at any time via the in-app consent dialog or system settings.
  • Legitimate interests — for core App operation, security, fraud prevention, non-personalized advertising, and improvement, balanced against your rights.
  • Legal obligation — where processing is required by law.

6. Data Retention

We operate backend infrastructure to deliver VPN server lists and configuration files, but we do not keep browsing or activity logs and we do not maintain a per-user database of your VPN sessions (see Section 1.4). Our third-party partners retain the data they collect for the periods stated in their own policies (for example, Google Analytics for Firebase retains user-level data for up to 14 months; crash logs are retained for around 90 days). Data processed on your device (Section 1.1) remains until you clear the App's data or uninstall the App.

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or port your data, to object to or restrict processing, to withdraw consent, and to lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority (GDPR/UK GDPR); or to know, delete, correct, and opt out of the "sale"/"sharing" of personal information and targeted advertising without discrimination (CCPA/CPRA and similar U.S. state laws); or equivalent rights under other laws (LGPD, PIPEDA, and others).

To exercise any right, email contact@tranquilmind.co. Because the App has no accounts, please include your Android Advertising ID (Settings → Google → Ads) and device model so we and our partners can locate data associated with your device. You can also act directly on your device: reset your Advertising ID, opt out of ads personalization, use the in-app consent dialog, or clear the App's data.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for users aged 18 or older and is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us at contact@tranquilmind.co and we will work with our partners to delete it.

9. International Data Transfers

Our ad and analytics partners may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States. Where required, transfers are protected by safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or equivalent mechanisms maintained by those partners.

10. Data Security
