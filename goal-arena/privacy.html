Privacy Policy

App: GoalArena: Football Live Score Effective: October 30, 2026 Last Updated: October 30, 2026

Effective date: 30 October 2026

GoalArena: Football Live Score ("GoalArena", "the App", "we", "us") is operated by Tranquil Mind Technology JSC ("Tranquil Mind"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles, why, and the choices you have. Contact: contact@tranquilmind.co.

1. Information we collect

GoalArena shows live football scores, fixtures, lineups and World Cup information. We do not ask you to create an account, and the App does not request your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Information handled by the App:

  • Settings stored on your device. Language, notification choices and the list of match reminders already scheduled are kept locally on your device (shared preferences / local storage). They are not sent to us.
  • Network requests for football data. To display scores and fixtures, the App contacts our football data proxy, which retrieves data from the third-party API-Football service. Like any internet request, these requests expose your IP address and basic technical metadata (such as app version and device type) to the servers involved.
  • Diagnostics and usage analytics. The App uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics (Google) to collect crash reports, app-interaction events, device model, OS version, app version and an app-instance identifier, in order to fix bugs and improve the App.
  • Remote configuration. The App uses Firebase Remote Config and Cloud Firestore (Google) to receive app settings such as minimum version and feature switches.
  • Attribution and advertising. The App includes the AppsFlyer attribution SDK and a Google AdMob-based advertising library. Where advertising or attribution is enabled in a given version, these services may process an advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID on Android, IDFA on iOS if you grant tracking permission), IP address and device information. On iOS the App asks for App Tracking Transparency permission, and where required it shows a consent form (IAB TCF) so you can choose; you can change this in your device settings.
  • Notifications. If you allow notifications, the App schedules local reminders (for example before kick-off) on your device and runs a periodic background task to check upcoming fixtures. Notification content is generated on your device.

Permissions the App declares (Android): ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, AD_ID, INTERNET, POST_NOTIFICATIONS, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED, WAKE_LOCK

  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — checks whether the device is online before loading scores and fixtures.
  • AD_ID — allows advertising and attribution services (AdMob, AppsFlyer) to read the Google Advertising ID where advertising or attribution is enabled; you can reset or delete this ID in Android settings.
  • INTERNET — downloads live scores, fixtures, lineups, team images and app configuration.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — shows match reminder and kick-off notifications, only after you grant permission (Android 13 and later).
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED — restores scheduled match reminders after the device restarts.
  • WAKE_LOCK — keeps the device awake briefly so background checks and scheduled reminders can complete.

2. How we use information

  • To provide scores, fixtures, lineups and match reminders you request.
  • To keep the App stable, measure usage and fix crashes.
  • To enable app configuration, updates and (where enabled) advertising and attribution.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms.

We do not sell your personal information.

We share data only with the service providers above, to comply with law, or with your consent. Providers may process data in countries other than your own, with safeguards required by applicable law.

5. Data retention

Local settings remain on your device until you clear app data or uninstall the App. Analytics and crash data are retained by the providers for their standard retention periods.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Deny or revoke notification permission in your device settings.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in Android/iOS settings, and decline tracking on iOS.
  • Clear app data or uninstall to remove locally stored settings.
  • Depending on your region (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, LGPD, PIPEDA) you may request access, correction, deletion or restriction of your data by emailing contact@tranquilmind.co. Because we do not hold an account for you, we may need to ask for device identifiers to locate analytics data.

7. Children's privacy

GoalArena is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided data, contact contact@tranquilmind.co and we will delete it.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical measures, including encrypted (HTTPS) connections to our services where available. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

9. Changes to this policy
