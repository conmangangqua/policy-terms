Privacy Policy

App: GoalArena Effective: July 27, 2026 Last Updated: July 27, 2026

GoalArena ("the App", "we", "us", "our"), operated by Tranquil Mind Technology JSC, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices and rights you have. By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

GoalArena is a free, ad-supported football companion that provides live scores, fixtures, results, league standings, match details and lineups, team and player profiles, a World Cup hub, favorite-team following, and optional match reminders. You do not need to create an account to use it. The football data shown in the App is retrieved from third-party sports-data providers.

1. Information We Collect

We do not ask you to register an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or precise GPS location. We do not knowingly collect any information you type beyond what is needed to operate the App (for example, a search term you enter to find a team or league is sent to our data provider to return results).

The App and the third-party services it uses automatically collect certain information from your device:

  • Device and advertising identifiers — including your device's advertising ID (Google Advertising ID / Apple IDFA), and app-instance/analytics identifiers, used for advertising, measurement, and analytics.
  • Approximate location — a coarse, city-/region-level location derived from your IP address by our advertising and analytics partners. We do not collect precise (GPS) location and the App does not request location permission.
  • App activity — how you interact with the App (screens viewed, features used, favorite teams followed, in-app searches, ads viewed or tapped), used for analytics and advertising.
  • Crash logs and diagnostics — crash reports, error logs, and performance/diagnostic data used to keep the App stable and improve it.
  • Device and technical information — device model, operating system version, language/region settings, network state, and similar technical data.

Your favorite teams and app preferences are stored locally on your device (using on-device storage) and are not transmitted to us as personal account data.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information above to:

  • Provide and operate the App (deliver live scores, fixtures, standings, lineups, and match reminders);
  • Remember your preferences and personalize your experience (e.g. showing your favorite teams first);
  • Serve and measure advertising, including personalized ads where you have consented;
  • Perform analytics to understand usage and improve features, stability, and performance;
  • Detect, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, invalid ad traffic, and security issues;
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We do not use your information to process payments, because the App contains no in-app purchases or subscriptions.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information for money. We share information only:

  • With the third-party service providers listed in Section 3, for the purposes described (advertising, analytics, attribution, crash reporting, and delivering football data);
  • To comply with the law, respond to lawful requests, or protect our rights, users, and the public;
  • In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to this Policy.
U.S. state privacy note. Because the App shows personalized advertising and shares device/advertising identifiers and app-activity data with advertising partners, this activity may be considered a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information (for targeted advertising) under certain U.S. state laws (e.g. California's CCPA/CPRA). You can opt out of ad personalization using the controls in Section 3, and exercise your rights as described in Section 7.

5. Legal Bases (EEA/UK — GDPR)

Where the GDPR applies, we rely on:

  • Consent — for personalized advertising and, where required, analytics (you may withdraw consent at any time via the App's ad-consent controls);
  • Legitimate interests — for core functionality, security and fraud prevention, non-personalized measurement, and improving the App;
  • Legal obligation — where we must retain or disclose information to comply with the law.

6. Data Retention

We retain automatically-collected data only as long as necessary for the purposes described, or as required by law. Advertising and analytics identifiers are retained according to each partner's retention periods (see their policies in Section 3). Crash and diagnostic data are kept for a limited period to investigate and fix issues. Preferences stored locally on your device remain until you clear the App's data or uninstall the App.

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • GDPR (EEA/UK): access, correct, delete, port, restrict, or object to processing of your personal data; withdraw consent; and lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.
  • CCPA/CPRA (California) and similar U.S. state laws: know what personal information is collected, request deletion or correction, opt out of the "sale"/"sharing" of personal information and targeted advertising, and not be discriminated against for exercising these rights.
  • Other regions (e.g. LGPD in Brazil, PIPEDA in Canada): equivalent rights under applicable local law.

Because the App has no user accounts, we may not hold information that identifies you by name. To help us locate any data associated with your device when you make a request, please include your advertising ID and device details (model and OS version). To exercise any right, contact us at contact@tranquilmind.co.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13 (nor to children under the applicable minimum age of digital consent in your region, such as 16 in parts of the EEA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under that age. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us at contact@tranquilmind.co and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

9. International Data Transfers

Your information may be processed in countries other than your own, including the United States, where our service providers operate. Where required, we and our partners rely on appropriate safeguards (such as Standard Contractual Clauses) for such transfers.

10. Data Security

We use encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Third-Party Content

The App displays football information (scores, fixtures, statistics, team and player data) supplied by third-party sports-data providers. This content may be delayed or contain errors and should not be relied upon for betting or any decision with financial consequences. We are not responsible for the accuracy of third-party content or for external links; please review the relevant third party's policies.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. Material changes will be posted here with a new "Last updated" date. Your continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated Policy.
