Privacy Policy

App: Klipnest Effective: 2026-08-21 Last Updated: 2026-08-21

Klipnest ("the App", "we", "us", "our"), operated by Tranquil Mind Technology JSC, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how that information is used and shared, and the choices and rights you have. By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

Klipnest is an Android media downloader with a built-in browser. It lets you open TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, X (Twitter), Dailymotion and other websites inside the App, save the videos, music and images you are allowed to download to your phone, and manage everything offline in a local library with tabs, browsing history and bookmarks. The App has no user account: there is no registration, no login, no profile, no cloud sync, no messaging and no user-to-user content — you can use every feature without telling us who you are.

1. Information we collect

We never ask you for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, calendar or precise location. There is no form in the App that requests personal details.

Two categories of information exist: what stays only on your device (Section 1.1), and what is collected automatically by the App and by the third-party services listed in Section 3 (Sections 1.2 to 1.6).

1.1 What never leaves your device

  • Your browsing inside the App — web addresses you open, page content, open tabs, terms typed in the address bar, cookies, cache and site data are handled by the Android WebView engine and kept in the App's private, sandboxed storage.
  • Browsing history, bookmarks and tabs — the App keeps a local history list, a bookmark list and a tab list in an on-device database (Hive). We operate no server that receives them, so there is nothing for us to upload or back up. Deleting an entry, clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App removes this data.
  • Downloaded files — videos, audio, images and documents you save are written to your device's storage. They are never uploaded to us. The READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, READ_MEDIA_IMAGES and (on Android 9 and older) READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE permissions exist solely so the App can write your downloads and show them back to you in the local library.
  • Media handling on device — thumbnail generation, video compression, playback, and the built-in document/PDF viewer all run locally.
  • App settings — language, theme and preferences are stored locally.

Downloads themselves are direct: when you tap download, your device requests the file from the website that hosts it. That website — not Klipnest — receives your IP address, user-agent and any cookies your browser session carries. We never see, log or proxy the address of the page you visit or the file you download.

1.2 Device and app information

Device model and manufacturer, operating system version, screen metrics, language and region settings, time zone, network connection type, App version and build number. Used to run the App correctly, deliver the right remote configuration, serve and measure advertising, and investigate problems.

1.3 Identifiers

  • Advertising ID (Google Advertising ID / GAID) — a resettable identifier supplied by Android and used by the advertising, analytics and attribution services in Section 3. The App declares com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID for this purpose.
  • App-instance identifiers — the Firebase installation / app-instance ID used by Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics and Remote Config, and an installation identifier used by Adjust for attribution.
  • Android Privacy Sandbox signals — the bundled advertising libraries may use Android's Ad Services APIs where your device supports them.

1.4 Approximate location derived from your IP address

The App requests no location permission and never reads GPS, Wi-Fi or cell positioning. Our advertising, analytics and attribution providers may infer an approximate location — usually country or city level — from the IP address your device presents when it contacts them.

1.5 App activity and diagnostics

  • In-app events — which screens are opened, which buttons are tapped, that a download started or completed, that a purchase was made, and similar interaction events, sent to Firebase Analytics and Adjust. These events record the interaction only; they do not carry the URL, page title, domain, search term or file name involved.
  • Crash logs and diagnostics — stack traces, native crash traces, device state and performance data collected by Firebase Crashlytics (including its NDK component) so we can fix stability problems.

1.6 Purchase status

If you buy Klipnest Premium, Google Play Billing tells the App which entitlement you hold (weekly, monthly or lifetime) so it can remove advertising. Google processes the payment; we never receive your card number or billing address. The purchase event is also reported to Firebase Analytics and Adjust as revenue attribution.

2. How we use information

  • Provide and operate the App, its browser, its downloader and its local library.
  • Remember your settings, tabs, history and bookmarks on your device.
  • Show advertising, and measure ad performance, install attribution and campaign effectiveness.
  • Analytics and product improvement.
  • Fraud prevention, security and abuse detection.
  • Process and validate purchases and subscriptions.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information for money. We share the automatically collected data described in Sections 1.2 to 1.6 with the third-party partners listed in Section 3 so they can serve and measure ads, provide analytics and attribution, and process purchases. We may also disclose information for legal reasons (to comply with law or a valid request, or to protect our rights and users' safety) or as part of a merger, acquisition or other business transfer.

Because the App serves personalized advertising, some U.S. state privacy laws may treat that as a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information, or as "targeted advertising". You may opt out as described in Sections 3 and 7.

5. Legal bases (EEA/UK — GDPR)

  • Consent — personalized advertising and analytics where consent is required (collected via the UMP consent form).
  • Legitimate interests — core functionality, security, fraud prevention and product improvement.
  • Contract / legal obligation — processing purchases and meeting legal duties.

6. Data retention

We keep data only as long as necessary for the purposes above or as required by law. Advertising, attribution and analytics identifiers are retained according to each partner's own retention periods; crash and diagnostic data is kept for a limited period. Everything described in Section 1.1 stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App.

7. Your rights

  • GDPR (EEA/UK): access, correction, deletion, portability, objection, restriction, withdrawal of consent, and the right to complain to a data protection authority.
  • CCPA/CPRA (U.S.): the right to know, delete and correct, to opt out of the sale/sharing of personal information and of targeted advertising, and to non-discrimination.
  • Other regions (LGPD, PIPEDA and equivalents): the corresponding rights where applicable.

Because the App has no accounts, we hold no profile that identifies you by name. To exercise a right, email us at the address in Section 13 and include your advertising ID and device details so we can locate any related data with our partners. You can also delete everything the App stores locally at any time by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling it.

8. Children's privacy

Klipnest is intended for users aged 13 or over, or the higher age of digital consent in your country. It is a general-audience utility and is not directed to children. The App opens the public internet, and the pages reachable through it are neither produced nor filtered by us; parents and guardians should supervise younger users and consider Android Family Link parental controls.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA/UK where a higher age applies). If you believe a child has provided personal information, write to us at the address in Section 13 and we will delete it along with any associated identifiers.

9. International data transfers

We are based in Vietnam and our service providers operate globally, including in the United States and the European Union. Where required, we and our partners rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

10. Data security

We use encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) for our own and our partners' connections, and reasonable technical and organisational safeguards. Pages you open in the in-app browser use whatever protocol the destination site offers; the App permits plain HTTP so that older download sources remain reachable, so please be careful with sensitive information on non-HTTPS pages. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

11. Third-party content

The App is a browser and downloader: the sites, media and files you reach through it are created and hosted by third parties. We do not host, upload, index, cache on our servers, or control any of that content, and we are not responsible for it or for the privacy practices of the sites you visit. Please review their policies and terms before using them.
