Privacy Policy

App: Klipnest Effective: 2026-09-13 Last Updated: 2026-09-13

Klipnest ("the App", "we", "us", "our"), operated by Tranquil Mind Technology JSC, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how that information is used and shared, and the choices and rights you have. By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

Klipnest is an Android media downloader with a built-in browser. You can paste a link or open a web page inside the App, save the videos, music, images and documents you are allowed to download to your phone, and manage them offline in a local library with tabs, browsing history and bookmarks. The App has no user account: there is no registration, no login, no profile, no cloud sync, no messaging and no user-to-user content. You can use every feature without telling us who you are.

1. Information we collect

We never ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, calendar or location. No form in the App requests personal details.

Information falls into three groups:

  • data that stays only on your device (Section 1.1);
  • the links you choose to download, which the App may need to send to a download-processing service (Section 1.2);
  • data collected automatically by the App and the third-party services listed in Section 3 (Sections 1.3 to 1.7).

1.1 What stays on your device

  • Your browsing inside the App. The Android WebView engine handles the web addresses you open, page content, open tabs, terms typed in the address bar, cookies, cache and site data. All of it is kept in the App's private, sandboxed storage.
  • Browsing history, bookmarks and tabs. The App keeps a local history list, bookmark list and tab list in an on-device database (Hive). They are not uploaded to us or backed up by us. Deleting an entry, clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App removes this data.
  • Downloaded files. The videos, audio, images and documents you save are written to your device's storage and are never uploaded to us. The App uses the READ_MEDIA_VIDEO and READ_MEDIA_IMAGES permissions (and READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE on Android 9 and older) only to save your downloads and show them in your local library.
  • Media handling. Thumbnails, video compression, playback and the built-in PDF, text and presentation viewers all run on your device.
  • Notifications. Download-progress notifications and occasional reminders are created and scheduled on your device. We run no push-messaging service. You can turn notifications off in Android settings.
  • App settings. Your language, theme and other preferences are stored locally.

1.2 Links you choose to download

A download starts when you paste a link or tap Download on a page. The App first has to work out where the actual media file is. How it does this depends on the source:

  • Directly from the website. For direct file links, public cloud-drive share links and some social sites, your device contacts the hosting website or its public embed service itself. In some cases this happens in a hidden in-app browser view. That website receives your IP address, user-agent and any cookies your in-app browsing session holds, under its own privacy policy.
  • Through a download-processing service. For some video sites, the App sends the link of the post or video you chose to a third-party download-processing service. This includes API providers reached through the RapidAPI marketplace. The service replies with the address of the downloadable file. It receives the link, your device's IP address and standard technical request headers. It does not receive your name, your advertising ID, or your history, bookmarks or files. These providers may keep request logs under their own terms and privacy policies. We do not combine these links with your advertising ID or analytics data.

After the file address is found, the file itself is downloaded directly from the website that hosts it.

These links are sent only when you ask to download something. If you don't use the download feature for a given page, nothing about that page is sent.

1.3 Device and app information

We collect your device model and manufacturer, operating system version, screen metrics, language and region settings, time zone, network connection type, and the App version and build number. We use this to run the App correctly, deliver the right remote configuration, serve and measure advertising, and investigate problems.

1.4 Identifiers

  • Advertising ID (Google Advertising ID / GAID): a resettable identifier supplied by Android and used by the advertising, analytics and attribution services in Section 3. The App declares com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID for this purpose.
  • App-instance identifiers: the Firebase installation / app-instance ID used by Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics and Remote Config; an installation identifier used by Adjust for attribution; and the identifiers the Facebook SDK uses for app events.
  • Android Privacy Sandbox signals: the bundled advertising libraries may use Android's Ad Services APIs on devices that support them.

1.5 Approximate location derived from your IP address

The App requests no location permission and never reads GPS, Wi-Fi or cell positioning. Our advertising, analytics and attribution providers may estimate an approximate location, usually at country or city level, from the IP address your device uses to contact them.

1.6 App activity and diagnostics

  • In-app events: screens opened, buttons tapped, downloads started or completed, ads shown or clicked, and similar interaction events. They are sent to Firebase Analytics, Adjust and the Facebook SDK. These events record only the interaction. They do not include the URL, page title, domain, search term or file name involved.
  • Crash logs and diagnostics: stack traces, native crash traces, device state and performance data, collected by Firebase Crashlytics (including its NDK component) so we can fix stability problems.

1.7 Purchase status

If the App offers an optional Klipnest Premium purchase in your region and you buy it, Google Play Billing tells the App which entitlement you hold so it can remove advertising. Google processes the payment, and we never receive your card number or billing address. Purchase events may be reported to Firebase Analytics and Adjust for revenue attribution. When you open the App, it may also ask Google Play whether you have an earlier purchase to restore.

2. How we use information

  • Provide and operate the App, including its browser, downloader, local library and players.
  • Locate the downloadable file for a link you chose (Section 1.2).
  • Keep your settings, tabs, history and bookmarks on your device.
  • Show advertising and measure ad performance, install attribution and campaign effectiveness.
  • Analytics, stability monitoring and product improvement.
  • Prevent fraud and invalid ad traffic, and keep the App secure.
  • Process and validate purchases, where offered.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information for money. We share the automatically collected data described in Sections 1.3 to 1.7 with the partners listed in Section 3, so they can serve and measure ads, provide analytics and attribution, and process purchases. Links you choose to download are passed to download-processing services only as described in Section 1.2. We may also disclose information for legal reasons (to comply with the law or a valid request, or to protect our rights and the safety of users) or as part of a merger, acquisition or other business transfer.

The App serves personalized advertising, so some U.S. state privacy laws may treat this as a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information, or as "targeted advertising". You can opt out as described in Sections 3 and 7.

5. Legal bases (EEA/UK — GDPR)

  • Consent: personalized advertising and analytics where consent is required (collected through the UMP consent form).
  • Performance of our service at your request: sending a link you chose to a download-processing service so the download can happen.
  • Legitimate interests: core functionality, security, fraud prevention and product improvement.
  • Contract / legal obligation: processing purchases and meeting legal duties.

6. Data retention

We keep data only as long as necessary for the purposes above or as required by law:

  • Advertising, attribution and analytics identifiers are kept according to each partner's retention periods.
  • Crash and diagnostic data is kept for a limited period.
  • Download-processing providers may keep request logs for their own operational periods.
  • Everything described in Section 1.1 stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App.

7. Your rights

  • GDPR (EEA/UK): access, correction, deletion, portability, objection, restriction, withdrawal of consent, and the right to complain to a data protection authority.
  • CCPA/CPRA (U.S.): the right to know, delete and correct, to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information and of targeted advertising, and to non-discrimination.
  • Other regions (LGPD, PIPEDA and equivalents): the corresponding rights where applicable.

The App has no accounts, so we hold no profile that identifies you by name. To exercise a right, email us at the address in Section 13. Include your advertising ID and device details so we can find related data held by our partners. You can also delete everything the App stores locally at any time by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling it. To limit ad personalization right away, reset your advertising ID or change your consent choice as described in Section 3.

8. Children's privacy

Klipnest is intended for users aged 13 or over, or the higher age of digital consent in your country. It is a general-audience utility and is not directed to children. The App opens the public internet, and we neither produce nor filter the pages you can reach through it. Parents and guardians should supervise younger users and may want to use parental controls such as Android Family Link.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA/UK, where a higher age applies). If you believe a child has provided personal information, write to us at the address in Section 13. We will delete it together with any associated identifiers.

9. International data transfers

We are based in Vietnam, and our service providers operate globally, including in the United States and the European Union. Where required, we and our partners rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

10. Data security

Our analytics, advertising and attribution partners receive data over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), and we use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards. Pages you open in the in-app browser use whatever protocol the destination site offers. The App allows plain HTTP so that older download sources still work, so please take care with sensitive information on non-HTTPS pages. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

11. Third-party content

The App is a browser and downloader. The sites, media and files you reach through it are created and hosted by third parties. We do not host, upload, index, store on our servers, or control any of that content. We are not responsible for it or for the privacy practices of the sites you visit, so please review their policies and terms before using them.
