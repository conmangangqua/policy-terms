Privacy Policy

App: Vid Nova AI Effective: July 24, 2026 Last Updated: July 28, 2026

Vid Nova AI ("the App", "we", "us", "our"), operated by Tranquil Mind Technology JSC, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices and rights you have. By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

Vid Nova AI is a video app. AI video generator that turns text and photos into short clips fast. You do not need to create an account to use it.

1. Information We Collect

We do not require you to register an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, or phone number to use the App. We collect the following categories of information:

a) Content you provide for video generation

  • Photos you select from your device for image-to-video generation.
  • Text prompts and effect selections you enter to generate a video.

These inputs are transmitted to our third-party AI processing provider (see Section 3) solely to render your requested video and return the result to you. We do not use your photos or prompts to identify you.

b) Information collected automatically

  • Device and advertising identifiers (e.g., Android Advertising ID, device model, OS version, language, app version).
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address (city/region level; we do not collect GPS/precise location).
  • App activity such as features used, videos generated, screens viewed, and interactions, used for analytics and advertising.
  • Purchase information such as the products you buy, purchase status, and transaction identifiers (payment card details are handled by Google Play, not by us).
  • Crash logs and diagnostics such as error reports and performance data.

We do not collect your contacts, calendar, SMS, precise/GPS location, microphone audio, or health data. Video generation is performed on our provider's servers; local storage on your device (e.g., saved videos, preferences) stays on your device unless you choose to share it.

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the App and generate the videos you request.
  • Process in-app purchases and manage your credit balance and subscriptions.
  • Serve and measure advertising, including personalized ads where permitted.
  • Perform analytics to understand usage and improve the App.
  • Detect, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, security, and technical issues.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information for money. We share information with:

  • The third-party services listed in Section 3, for the purposes described.
  • Authorities or others where required by law, legal process, or to protect rights, safety, and security.
  • A successor entity in connection with a merger, acquisition, or asset transfer.
U.S. state privacy notice. Because we use advertising and analytics partners that may combine identifiers for personalized/targeted advertising, some U.S. state laws (e.g., California's CPRA) may treat this as a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information. You can opt out of personalized ads using the device controls in Section 3 and the ad-consent options where offered.

5. Legal Bases (EEA/UK — GDPR)

  • Consent — personalized advertising and analytics where consent is required.
  • Legitimate interests — core functionality, security, fraud prevention, and product improvement.
  • Contract / legal obligation — processing purchases and meeting legal duties.

You may withdraw consent at any time; this does not affect processing already carried out.

6. Data Retention

We keep information only as long as necessary for the purposes above or as required by law. Photos and prompts submitted for generation are processed to deliver your video and retained by our AI provider only as needed to provide and secure the service. Advertising, analytics, crash, and diagnostic data are retained according to each partner's retention periods.

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, port, restrict, or object to processing of your personal information, and to withdraw consent or opt out of targeted advertising.

  • EEA/UK (GDPR): access, rectification, erasure, portability, restriction, objection, withdraw consent, and complain to a data protection authority.
  • California (CCPA/CPRA): know, delete, correct, and opt out of "sale"/"sharing" and targeted advertising, without discrimination.
  • Other regions (e.g., LGPD, PIPEDA): equivalent rights under local law.

To exercise your rights, contact us at contact@tranquilmind.co. Because the App has no user accounts, please include your Advertising ID and device details so we can locate and act on the relevant data.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for users aged 13 and older (or the higher minimum age of digital consent in your country, such as 16 in parts of the EEA). The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your information may be processed in countries other than your own, including the United States and other jurisdictions where our providers operate. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards (e.g., Standard Contractual Clauses).

Do not submit content you do not have the right to use, and do not create content that is illegal, harmful, sexual, hateful, or that impersonates real people.
