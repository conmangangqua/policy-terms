Privacy Policy — Vid Nova AI

App: Vid Nova: Video Editor & Maker Effective: November 02, 2026 Last Updated: November 02, 2026

Effective date: October 30, 2026 Operator: Tranquil Mind Technology JSC ("we", "us", "our") Contact: contact@tranquilmind.co

This Privacy Policy explains how Vid Nova AI (the "App") handles information when you use it on Android or iOS. By using the App you agree to this Policy.

1. Information we collect

Content you provide

  • Text prompts you type to create an image or video. They are sent to our image-generation service (a Cloudflare Workers endpoint operated by us) to produce the picture.
  • Photos you choose with the system photo picker or camera, when you use the image-to-video feature. The selected photo is sent to the same service to be transformed. We do not read your other photos.
  • Content reports you submit about AI output (selected reasons, optional note, content identifier, the prompt, platform). They are stored in Google Firebase Cloud Firestore so we can review them.

Processed on your device

  • Videos are assembled on your device from generated images. A depth model and an on-device content-safety (NSFW) detector run locally; the photos are not sent to a third party for these steps.
  • Settings, credits balance and preferences are stored locally on your device.

Collected automatically

  • Usage and diagnostics: app interactions, crash reports and device/OS information through Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics; remote configuration through Firebase Remote Config.
  • Attribution: install and in-app event data through AppsFlyer.
  • Advertising: the Android Advertising ID, IP address and device information used by Google AdMob and its mediation partners (Meta Audience Network, Pangle, Mintegral, AppLovin) to serve and measure ads, including rewarded ads. On iOS we ask for App Tracking Transparency permission, and where required we show a consent form (Google UMP / IAB TCF).
  • Purchases: subscriptions and credit packs are processed by Google Play Billing or Apple; we receive purchase status but not your payment card details.
  • App identifier: the app package name is sent to our generation service with each request, to apply usage limits.

We do not ask you to create an account and do not knowingly collect your name, email or precise location.

Permissions the App declares (Android): ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, AD_ID, BILLING, INTERNET, POST_NOTIFICATIONS, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED

  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — check whether a connection is available before generating content or loading ads.
  • AD_ID — lets advertising and attribution SDKs read the Android Advertising ID for ad delivery and measurement; you can reset or delete it in Android settings.
  • BILLING — lets you buy credits or subscriptions through Google Play.
  • INTERNET — send prompts/photos to the generation service, load ads, and use Firebase and AppsFlyer.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — show local notifications, such as reminders you allow. We ask on Android 13+ and you can refuse or turn it off at any time in system settings.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED — restore scheduled reminder notifications after the device restarts.

2. How we use information

  • To generate images and videos you request and deliver results.
  • To operate credits, purchases and rewarded ads.
  • To measure and improve performance, fix crashes and prevent abuse.
  • To show advertising and measure its effectiveness.
  • To review reports of harmful AI content and enforce our Terms.
  • To send the notifications you allow.

4. Retention

Prompts and photos are used to produce your result and are not kept by us as a library of your content. Generated videos are stored on your device until you delete them. Content reports and analytics data are kept as long as needed for the purposes above, then deleted or anonymized.

5. AI-generated content

Results are produced by AI and may be inaccurate or inappropriate. Do not submit photos of other people without permission. Use the in-app report option to flag content.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Decline or revoke notification, camera or photo access in system settings.
  • Reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in device settings; on iOS, change tracking permission in Settings.
  • Depending on where you live (for example EEA/UK GDPR, California CCPA/CPRA, Brazil LGPD, Canada PIPEDA) you may request access, correction, deletion or restriction of your data by emailing contact@tranquilmind.co. We respond within the period required by law.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the higher age required locally). We do not knowingly collect children's data; contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security and transfers

We use HTTPS and reputable providers, but no method is fully secure. Data may be processed in countries other than yours, with appropriate safeguards where required.

9. Changes

We may update this Policy and will change the effective date above. Continued use means acceptance.
