Privacy Policy

App: Torchwise Effective: 2026-10-11 Last Updated: 2026-10-11

Torchwise ("the App", "we", "us", "our"), operated by Tranquil Mind Technology JSC, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices and rights you have. By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

Torchwise is a flashlight utility: a one-tap LED torch with brightness control, a live compass, a camera magnifier, LED flash alerts for incoming calls and app notifications, a colour screen light, SOS, strobe and disco modes, a Quick Settings tile and an optional home-screen (launcher) mode. The App is free and supported by advertising. You do not need to create an account to use it.

1. Information we collect

We collect only the limited data described below. Your flash timing, alert filters, colours, language and launcher preferences are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to us.

Information collected automatically and shared with our service providers

  • Device and app identifiers — your device's advertising ID (resettable, declared via the com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID permission) and app-instance identifiers (for example a Firebase installation ID), device model, operating system version, language and app version.
  • Approximate location — a coarse, IP-address-derived location (country/city level) inferred by our advertising, attribution and analytics partners. The App does not request or access precise (GPS) location and declares no location permission.
  • App activity and usage — in-app interactions such as screens viewed and features used (for example turning on the torch, opening the magnifier, enabling flash alerts), plus app-open, install and session events.
  • Advertising interaction data — ad requests, impressions, clicks and, where offered, rewarded-ad completions, processed by the advertising partners listed in Section 3.
  • Crash logs and diagnostics — crash reports, stack traces, device state at the time of a crash and performance data used to keep the App stable.
  • Install attribution data — the advertising ID and campaign/referrer information used to attribute an install to an advertising campaign.
  • Network state (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) — used to check connectivity before loading ads and remote configuration. We do not collect your network names or Wi-Fi scan results.

Data processed only on your device (never uploaded)

  • Phone call state (READ_PHONE_STATE) — used only to know that the phone is ringing so the LED can flash. We do not read phone numbers, contacts or call history.
  • Notifications (Notification access, only if you enable it) — used only to detect that a notification arrived and which app posted it, so the LED can flash for the apps you selected. Notification content is not stored, collected or transmitted.
  • Installed apps list (<queries> for apps with a launcher entry) — used only to show your apps in the flash-alert app picker and in the optional home-screen mode, and to open the app you tap. The App does not request QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES and does not send your app list anywhere.
  • Camera (CAMERA, requested only when you open the Magnifier) — the live preview is shown on screen so you can zoom in on small text or objects, and the camera LED can be switched on. A "freeze" frame is kept in memory only while the Magnifier is open. The App does not save, upload or share camera images or video.
  • Motion and magnetic sensors — used only to show the compass heading on screen. Sensor readings are not stored or transmitted, and the compass does not use or collect your location.
  • Local reminders (POST_NOTIFICATIONS, SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM) — the App can show its own reminder that the flashlight is one tap away. These notices are scheduled on your device, contain no personal data, and can be turned off in your system notification settings.

Information you provide

The App has no accounts, forms, profiles or user-to-user messaging. If you contact us by email, we receive only the information you choose to send and use it to reply and improve the App.

The App does not collect your contacts, photo library, message content, microphone audio, precise location, health data or financial data.

2. How we use information

  • Provide and operate the App's flashlight, compass, magnifier, flash-alert, screen-light, Quick Settings tile and launcher features;
  • Show and measure advertising, including rewarded ads where the App offers them;
  • Understand how the App is used and improve it (analytics);
  • Deliver remote configuration and feature flags;
  • Detect, diagnose and fix crashes and technical issues;
  • Attribute app installs to advertising campaigns and measure campaign performance;
  • Prevent fraud and abuse, and comply with legal obligations.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information for money. We share the data described above only:

  • with the third-party service providers listed in Section 3, to provide advertising, analytics, attribution and crash reporting;
  • when required by law, legal process, or to protect rights, safety and security;
  • as part of a merger, acquisition or asset sale, in which case this Policy will continue to apply.

U.S. state privacy laws. Some U.S. states (for example California) define the disclosure of identifiers to advertising partners for personalized ads as a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information. You can opt out by disabling ads personalization or resetting your advertising ID in your device settings, or by declining personalized-ads consent where the consent form is shown.

5. Legal bases (EEA/UK — GDPR)

  • Consent — personalized advertising (via the consent form) and, where required by local law, analytics;
  • Legitimate interests — operating, securing and improving the App, measuring campaigns, and showing non-personalized ads;
  • Legal obligation — where processing is required by law.

6. Data retention

We keep data only as long as necessary for the purposes above or as required by law. Advertising, attribution and analytics partners retain identifiers for the limited periods described in their policies (for example Google Analytics for Firebase retains certain identifier-linked data for up to 14 months). Crash and diagnostic data is retained for a limited period needed for troubleshooting. Your settings remain on your device until you change them, clear the App's data or uninstall it.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your personal data; to object to or restrict processing; to withdraw consent; and to lodge a complaint with your data-protection authority (GDPR/UK GDPR). California and other U.S.-state residents may have rights to know, delete, correct and opt out of "sale"/"sharing" and targeted advertising, without discrimination. Residents of other regions (for example Brazil — LGPD, Canada — PIPEDA) have similar rights under local law.

To exercise any right, email contact@tranquilmind.co. Because the App has no accounts, please include your advertising ID and device model in your request so we and our partners can locate data associated with your device. Uninstalling the App deletes all settings stored on your device and stops all further collection.

8. Children's privacy

The App is intended for users aged 13 and older (or the higher minimum age of digital consent in your country, for example 16 in parts of the EEA) and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The App has no chat, messaging, comments or user-to-user content sharing. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us at contact@tranquilmind.co and we will delete it.

9. International data transfers

The data described above may be processed on servers outside your country, including in the United States. Where required, transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses implemented by our service providers.

10. Data security
