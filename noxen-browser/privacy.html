Privacy Policy

App: Noxen Browser Effective: 2026-08-10 Last Updated: 2026-08-10

Noxen Browser ("the App", "we", "us", "our"), operated by Tranquil Mind Technology JSC, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices and rights you have. By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

Noxen Browser is a private, lightweight web browser with a built-in ad blocker and incognito-by-default browsing. You do not need to create an account to use it. The web pages you open, your browsing history, and site data are handled on your device; by default the App does not retain your history or site data.

1. Information we collect

We do not require an account and do not ask you for personal details. The App and its third-party SDKs automatically collect a limited set of technical data that leaves the device:

  • Device & advertising identifiers — including the Google Advertising ID (AD_ID), used for advertising and attribution.
  • Approximate location — derived from your IP address by our advertising/analytics partners (not GPS; the App requests no location permission).
  • App activity — in-app interactions and events (e.g. screens viewed, ads served/blocked), used for analytics and ad measurement.
  • Crash logs & diagnostics — stability and performance data to help us fix issues.

We do not collect the URLs you visit, your browsing history, or the content of web pages you view — those stay on your device.

2. How we use information

  • Provide and operate the App and its ad-blocking features.
  • Show and measure advertising (including personalized ads) and attribute installs.
  • Analytics and product improvement.
  • Fraud prevention, security, and abuse detection.
  • Legal compliance.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information for money. We share the limited technical data described above with the third-party services listed in Section 3 for advertising, attribution, analytics, and stability. Because the App serves personalized ads, some U.S. state laws (e.g. California CCPA/CPRA) may treat this ad-related data use as a "sale" or "sharing" for targeted advertising; you may opt out as described in Sections 3 and 7. We may also disclose information for legal reasons or in a business transfer.

5. Legal bases (EEA/UK — GDPR)

  • Consent — personalized ads and analytics where consent is required.
  • Legitimate interests — core functionality, security, and improvement.
  • Legal obligation — compliance with applicable law.

6. Data retention

We keep data only as long as necessary for the purposes above or as required by law. Advertising/analytics identifiers are retained per each partner's retention period; crash and diagnostic data are kept for a limited period.

7. Your rights

  • GDPR (EEA/UK): access, correct, delete, port, object, restrict, withdraw consent, and lodge a complaint with a data protection authority.
  • CCPA/CPRA (California): know, delete, correct, and opt out of the "sale"/"sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising; non-discrimination for exercising rights.
  • Other regions (LGPD, PIPEDA, etc.): equivalent rights may apply.

Because the App has no accounts, please include your advertising ID and device details when contacting us so we can locate any associated data. Contact: contact@tranquilmind.co.

8. Children's privacy

The App is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. As a general-purpose web browser, it can access the open internet, so it is not designed for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. International data transfers

Your data may be processed outside your country, including in the United States. Where required, we and our partners use appropriate safeguards (e.g. Standard Contractual Clauses).

10. Data security

We use encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and other reasonable measures to protect data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.
