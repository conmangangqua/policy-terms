Privacy Policy

App: Noxen Browser Effective: 2026-08-07 Last Updated: 2026-08-07

Noxen Browser ("the App", "we", "us", "our"), operated by Tranquil Mind Technology JSC, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices and rights you have. By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

Noxen Browser is a private mobile web browser: it lets you open and manage multiple tabs, reach popular sites through one-tap shortcuts, block web advertising and trackers with a built-in blocker, choose from 25 interface languages, and receive optional reminders to come back to the App. You do not need to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal details to use Noxen Browser — there is no login, no profile, no messaging, and no user-to-user content.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask you for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, or precise location. The App has no registration form and no user account.

The following information is collected automatically by the App and the third-party services listed in Section 3:

1.1 Device and app information

Device model and manufacturer, operating system version, screen and language/region settings, app version and build, network connection type, and time zone. Used to operate the App, deliver the correct configuration, and diagnose problems.

1.2 Identifiers

  • Advertising ID (Google Advertising ID / GAID) — a resettable identifier provided by Android. The App declares the com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID permission and the Android Privacy Sandbox ad-services permissions because the advertising and attribution SDKs listed in Section 3 are bundled with it.
  • App-instance identifiers — Firebase installation ID / app instance ID used by Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics and Remote Config; an installation identifier used by Adjust for install attribution.
  • Install referrer — the Google Play install referrer string, read through the BIND_GET_INSTALL_REFERRER_SERVICE permission, so we can tell which campaign or listing brought you to the App.

1.3 Approximate location (derived from IP address)

We do not request GPS or any location permission and the App does not access your device location. Our service providers may infer an approximate location (typically country or city level) from your IP address for analytics, fraud prevention and regional configuration.

1.4 App activity and diagnostics

Anonymous in-app events (for example: splash shown, language screen viewed, onboarding step completed, search bar tapped, settings row tapped, share/rate/feedback tapped) and screen views. These events record that an action happened, together with the interest topic you optionally pick during onboarding. They do not contain the web addresses you visit, the pages you open, or the words you type into the search bar.

We also collect crash reports and diagnostic data (stack traces, exception messages, device state at the time of the crash, app version) through Firebase Crashlytics.

1.5 Purchase status

The App bundles Google Play Billing so it can restore an existing ad-free entitlement. When the App starts, it asks Google Play whether your Google account already owns one of our products; Google Play returns the purchase status. We never see or store your payment card, billing address or Google account credentials. See Section 3.3.

1.6 What we do not collect: your browsing

This is the most important point for a browser:

  • Browsing history, open tabs, bookmarks, form input, cookies and site data stay on your device. They are handled by the Android WebView engine and stored in the App's private storage. We do not upload them, we do not back them up to our servers, and we have no server that could receive them.
  • The App has no analytics event, log or crash field that records a URL, page title or search query.
  • The built-in ad and tracker blocker runs entirely on your device. Its blocklist (~250 advertising and tracking domains derived from public EasyList/EasyPrivacy sources) is compiled into the App. No page you open is sent to us or to any blocklist server for checking.
  • Clearing the App's data from Android Settings, or uninstalling the App, removes this locally stored browsing data.

1.7 Data the websites you visit receive

When you open a website in Noxen Browser, that website — like with any browser — receives your IP address, user-agent string and any cookies it has previously set, and may set new cookies (third-party cookies are enabled by default in the WebView). If you use the address bar to search, your query is sent to Google Search, the App's default search provider. This information is collected by those third parties under their privacy policies, not ours. See Section 11.

2. How we use information

  • To provide and operate the App — render pages, keep your tabs, apply your chosen language, apply the ad-block setting.
  • To deliver configuration remotely — Firebase Remote Config lets us change App settings (such as feature toggles and advertising configuration) without shipping an update.
  • To measure installs and campaign performance — attribution SDKs tell us which marketing source an install came from, in aggregate.
  • To understand usage and improve the App — anonymous, aggregated event and screen analytics.
  • To keep the App stable — crash and performance diagnostics.
  • To restore purchases — confirm with Google Play whether you already own an ad-free entitlement.
  • To send optional reminders — local re-engagement notifications, only if you granted notification permission.
  • For advertising — see Section 3.2. Advertising is not active in the current version of the App.
  • To comply with law — respond to lawful requests and enforce our Terms.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information for money, and we do not share your browsing history with anyone.

We share the limited data described above only:

  • with the service providers in Section 3, for the purposes stated there;
  • when required by law, legal process, or a valid government request, or to protect our rights, safety, or property, or to investigate fraud or abuse;
  • in a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, in which case we will post a notice of the change.

U.S. state privacy laws. Advertising is not active in the current version of the App, so no data is currently shared for cross-context behavioural advertising. If we enable personalised advertising in a future update, that activity may be considered "sharing" (and in some states a "sale") of personal information under laws such as the CCPA/CPRA. In that case you will be able to opt out through the in-app consent controls, by resetting or deleting your advertising ID (Section 3.2), or by contacting us at the address in Section 13.

5. Legal bases for processing (EEA / UK — GDPR)

  • Consent — personalised advertising and, where required by local law, analytics and marketing measurement; you may withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legitimate interests — operating and securing the App, crash diagnostics, aggregated non-personalised analytics, preventing fraud and abuse.
  • Performance of a contract — providing the App and restoring or fulfilling any purchase you make.
  • Legal obligation — retaining records and responding to lawful requests.

6. Data retention

  • Locally stored browsing data (tabs, cookies, cache, site data, your language and ad-block preference) stays on your device until you clear the App's data or uninstall it. We hold no copy.
  • Analytics and crash data are retained by Google for the periods set out in the Firebase documentation and our project settings (typically up to 14 months for event-level analytics data; crash records are retained for a limited diagnostic period).
  • Attribution data is retained by Adjust under our agreement with them, generally for the period needed for marketing measurement.
  • We keep support correspondence only as long as needed to handle your request and to meet legal obligations.

7. Your rights and choices

7.1 In-app and on-device controls

  • Clear your browsing data — clear the App's storage from Android Settings → Apps → Noxen Browser → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the App.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID — see Section 3.2.
  • Turn off notifications — Android Settings → Apps → Noxen Browser → Notifications, or decline the notification permission.
  • Ad and tracker blocking — the built-in blocker can be switched on or off in the App.

7.2 GDPR (EEA / UK)

You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, and port your personal data, to object to processing based on legitimate interests, to withdraw consent at any time, and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

7.3 CCPA / CPRA (California) and other U.S. states

You have the right to know what personal information is collected and how it is used and disclosed, to delete it, to correct it, to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information and of targeted advertising, to limit the use of sensitive personal information, and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights. We do not knowingly sell or share the personal information of consumers under 16 years of age.

7.4 Other regions

Residents of Brazil (LGPD), Canada (PIPEDA), and other jurisdictions with comparable laws have equivalent rights of access, correction, deletion, and objection.

7.5 How to exercise your rights

Email us at contact@tranquilmind.co. Because the App has no user account, we cannot identify you from a name or email alone. Please include your Android advertising ID, your device model and the approximate date of installation so that we can locate any associated records with our analytics and attribution providers. We will respond within the period required by applicable law. Verification requests are used only to process your request.

8. Children's privacy

Noxen Browser is intended for users aged 13 and over (or the higher age of digital consent in your country). It is a general-audience utility and is not directed to children.

The App gives access to the open internet, and the pages you can reach are not created, filtered, or controlled by us. Parents and guardians should supervise younger users and consider using Android's Family Link / parental controls and Google's SafeSearch.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA/UK, where a higher age applies). If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact contact@tranquilmind.co and we will delete it and any associated identifiers.

9. International data transfers

We are based in Vietnam and our service providers operate globally, including in the United States, the European Union, and Singapore. Your information may therefore be processed outside your country of residence, where data protection laws may differ. Where required, transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or an adequacy decision.

10. Data security

All communication between the App and our service providers uses encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Data stored by the App stays in Android's private, sandboxed app storage, protected by the operating system.

Note that the App allows plain-text (http://) connections so that legacy websites remain reachable, as browsers generally do. Traffic to such sites is not encrypted by the site itself; look for the padlock/https:// before entering sensitive information on any page.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Third-party content and websites

Noxen Browser is a gateway to the open web. We do not create, own, endorse, moderate, or control the websites, services, advertisements, or content you reach through it — including the sites behind the built-in shortcuts. Your use of any third-party website is governed by that site's own terms and privacy policy, and any data you submit there goes to that site, not to us. We are not responsible for third-party content, availability, or data practices. Please review the privacy policy of every site you use.

The built-in blocker reduces, but cannot eliminate, advertising and tracking on the pages you visit.
