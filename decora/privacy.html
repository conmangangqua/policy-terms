Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 30, 2026

Decora: DIY Wallpaper Maker ("Decora", "the App") is operated by Tranquil Mind Technology JSC ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the App handles and the choices you have. Contact: contact@tranquilmind.co.

1. Information we collect

Content you create or choose. Photos and videos you pick from your gallery (via the system picker) are used to build wallpapers, collages and cutouts. Background removal (cutout) runs on your device with an on-device model. Your designs and settings are stored locally on your device and are not uploaded to our servers.

Device and usage data. We use Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to collect app interactions, crash reports and diagnostic data (such as device model, OS version, app version). We use Firebase Remote Config to fetch app configuration.

Advertising and attribution data. The App shows ads through Google AdMob (google_mobile_ads) and uses an attribution service (Adjust, with Meta/Facebook attribution) that may collect your Android Advertising ID (GAID/AD_ID), IP address and install/ad-interaction events to measure installs and show relevant ads.

Network information. The App checks whether your device is online so ads and configuration load correctly.

Permissions the App declares (Android): ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, AD_ID, INTERNET, POST_NOTIFICATIONS, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED, SET_WALLPAPER

  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — detect whether the device is online before loading ads and remote configuration.
  • AD_ID — read the Android Advertising ID for advertising and attribution (you can reset or delete it in Android settings).
  • INTERNET — load ads, remote configuration, analytics and crash reporting.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — show reminder/re-engagement notifications; you can deny or turn them off in system settings.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED — re-schedule locally scheduled notifications after the device restarts.
  • SET_WALLPAPER — apply your chosen image or video as your home or lock screen wallpaper at your request.

2. How we use information

To provide wallpaper, collage and cutout features; apply wallpapers you choose; send notifications you allow; show ads and measure their performance; analyse usage and fix crashes; and keep the App secure and improve it.

5. Data retention and deletion

Designs stay on your device until you delete them or uninstall the App. Analytics and attribution data is retained by providers according to their policies. To request deletion of data associated with you, email contact@tranquilmind.co.

6. Your choices

Reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings; deny or revoke notification permission in system settings; deny gallery access; uninstall the App at any time. Depending on your region (GDPR, CCPA and similar laws) you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to processing — contact us to exercise them.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child provided data, contact us and we will delete it.
