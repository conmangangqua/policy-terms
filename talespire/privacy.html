Privacy Policy

App: Talespire Effective: 2026-11-18 Last Updated: 2026-11-18

Talespire ("the App", "we", "us", "our"), operated by Tranquil Mind Technology JSC, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices and rights you have. By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

Talespire is a reading app for Talespire Originals, a catalog of original serial fiction (romance, werewolf, billionaire, mafia, revenge and fantasy stories) that you can read as text, read as an illustrated comic for selected stories, or listen to using your device's text-to-speech voice. Stories built into the App can be read offline; newly added stories, translations and comic panels are downloaded once and then kept on your device. You do not need to create an account to use the App, and the App contains no advertising and no in-app purchases.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location. The App collects only the following, automatically, through the Google Firebase services listed in Section 3:

  • App activity and usage events — for example which screens are opened, which stories and chapters are opened, which reading mode or feature is used, and app-lifecycle events (Firebase Analytics).
  • Device and app identifiers — the Firebase app-instance ID and Firebase installation ID, the Android advertising ID (collected by the Firebase Analytics SDK by default; we use it only for analytics, never for advertising), device model, operating-system version, app version and language setting.
  • Approximate location — the country or region that Google derives from your IP address when analytics events are received. We never request GPS, Wi-Fi or precise location.
  • Crash logs and diagnostics — stack traces, device state and performance data when the App crashes or misbehaves (Firebase Crashlytics).

Stored only on your device and never sent to us: your library (stories in progress, finished and saved), reading progress and chapter position, reading settings (text size, line spacing, tone, brightness, typeface, reading mode, Focus mode), Listen-mode settings (speed, sleep timer), recent searches, your chosen language, and whether you have seen the onboarding and "How to read" screens. Clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App removes this data.

Content downloads: the App periodically downloads the story catalog, and the text, cover art and comic panels of newly added stories or translations, from our content server hosted on Cloudflare (novel-content.buibathanh97.workers.dev). As with any web request, the server receives your IP address and standard request information (such as user agent and time of request). We do not send any account, device identifier, reading-history or analytics data with these requests, and we do not use them to identify you.

Update check: when the App starts it may check whether a newer version is available on Google Play and read a remote configuration flag that tells it whether an update is required. These requests carry the App's package name, version and the Firebase installation ID.

Permissions the App requests:

  • Internet access (android.permission.INTERNET) — to download stories, covers and comic panels, load remote configuration, check for updates, and send analytics and crash reports.
  • Notifications (android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS) — only if you allow notifications; used for reading reminders about a story you left unfinished and for the Listen-mode playback controls. Reminders are scheduled locally on your device; we do not use push-notification servers.
  • Exact alarms (android.permission.SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM) — to deliver a reading reminder at the time it was scheduled on your device. No data leaves the device for this.
  • Media playback in the background (android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK, together with FOREGROUND_SERVICE and WAKE_LOCK) — only while Listen mode is playing; keeps the story being read aloud by your device's built-in text-to-speech engine while the screen is off, with play, pause and stop controls in the notification. The story text is processed by the text-to-speech engine installed on your device; the App does not record audio and does not send the text to any server for speech synthesis.

2. How we use information

  • To provide and operate the App, including downloading and displaying stories, covers and comic panels.
  • To understand how the App is used in aggregate and to improve its features, content catalog, translations and performance.
  • To detect, diagnose and fix crashes and technical problems.
  • To decide whether an app update is required and to adjust reading-reminder settings and wording through remote configuration.
  • To comply with legal obligations and to protect our rights and the safety of users.

We do not use your information for advertising, we do not build advertising profiles, and the App contains no advertising SDKs.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for targeted advertising or cross-context behavioral advertising. Information is transferred only to the service providers listed in Section 3, which process it on our behalf under their own privacy commitments; to comply with law, regulation, legal process or an enforceable government request; to protect the rights, property or safety of Tranquil Mind Technology JSC, our users or the public; or as part of a merger, acquisition, financing or sale of assets, with notice where required.

5. Legal bases (EEA/UK — GDPR)

  • Legitimate interests — operating the App, keeping it secure and stable, deciding when an update is required, and improving it through aggregated analytics and crash reports.
  • Consent — where local law requires consent for analytics identifiers or similar technologies; you can withdraw consent as described in Section 7.
  • Legal obligation — where we must retain or disclose information by law.

6. Data retention

Analytics data is retained by Google Firebase according to the retention period configured for the project, which is no longer than 14 months for user-level data. Crash reports are retained by Firebase Crashlytics for up to 90 days. Content-server access logs are retained by Cloudflare for a short period for security and operations. Data stored only on your device stays there until you clear the App's storage or uninstall the App.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your personal information, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent (GDPR/UK GDPR); to know, delete and correct personal information, to opt out of sale or sharing (we do neither), and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights (CCPA/CPRA); and equivalent rights under LGPD (Brazil), PIPEDA (Canada), the Australian Privacy Act, Vietnam's Decree 13/2023 on personal data protection, and other laws. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

Because the App has no accounts, we cannot identify you by name. To make a request, email contact@tranquilmind.co from any address and include your device model, operating-system version and the approximate dates you used the App so we can locate related analytics and crash records. You can also delete the data we hold about your installation yourself: clearing the App's storage or reinstalling the App generates a new app-instance ID, and resetting your Android advertising ID (Section 3) breaks the link to earlier analytics data. We respond to verified requests within the time required by applicable law (30 days under GDPR, 45 days under CCPA).

8. Children's privacy

The App is intended for readers aged 16 and over; its stories may contain mature themes such as violence, alcohol and romantic situations. The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the higher minimum age required in your country, such as 16 in parts of the EEA), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us at contact@tranquilmind.co and we will delete it.

9. International data transfers

Our service providers may process information in countries other than yours, including the United States, where Google and Cloudflare operate data centers. Where required, transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, as provided by those service providers.

10. Data security

Data sent from the App travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), and we rely on the security measures of our service providers. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Third-party content and links

Stories, translations, cover art and comic panels in the App are original works published by Tranquil Mind Technology JSC; some are written, translated or illustrated with the help of AI tools and reviewed before publication. Links to external websites (such as Google Play or our policy pages) are governed by those sites' own policies; we are not responsible for their content or practices.
