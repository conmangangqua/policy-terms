Privacy Policy

App: Talespire Effective: 2026-11-13 Last Updated: 2026-11-13

Talespire ("the App", "we", "us", "our"), operated by Tranquil Mind Technology JSC, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices and rights you have. By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

Talespire is a reading app for Talespire Originals, a catalog of original serial stories (romance, werewolf, billionaire, mafia, revenge and fantasy) available in several languages. Stories built into the App can be read offline; newly added stories and translations are downloaded once and then kept on your device. You do not need to create an account to use it.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location. The App collects only the following, automatically, through the Google Firebase services listed in Section 3:

  • App activity and usage events — for example which screens are opened, books opened, and features used (Firebase Analytics).
  • Device and app identifiers — the Firebase app-instance ID, device model, operating-system version, app version, and language setting.
  • Approximate location — country/region derived by Google from your IP address; we never request GPS or precise location.
  • Crash logs and diagnostics — stack traces and performance data when the App crashes or misbehaves (Firebase Crashlytics).

Stored only on your device (never sent to us): your library (books in progress, finished, and saved), reading progress and chapter position, reading settings (font size, line spacing, theme, brightness, reading mode), recent searches, and your chosen language. Deleting the App removes this data.

Story updates: the App periodically downloads the story catalog, and the text and cover of newly added stories or translations, from our content server hosted on Cloudflare (novel-content.buibathanh97.workers.dev). As with any web request, the server receives your IP address and standard request information; we do not send any account, device identifier or reading-history data with these requests, and we do not use them to identify you.

Permissions the App requests:

  • Internet access (android.permission.INTERNET) — to download book text and covers, load configuration, and send analytics and crash reports.
  • Notifications (android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS) — only if you turn on reading reminders; used to remind you about a book you started. Reminders are scheduled locally on your device.
  • Exact alarms (android.permission.SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM) — to deliver your reading reminder at the time it was scheduled. No data leaves the device for this.

2. How we use information

  • To provide and operate the App, including downloading and displaying books.
  • To understand how the App is used and improve features, content, and performance.
  • To detect, diagnose, and fix crashes and technical problems.
  • To decide whether an app update is required (via remote configuration).
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not use your information for advertising, and the App contains no advertising SDKs.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for targeted advertising. Information is shared only with the service providers in Section 3 so they can operate on our behalf, when required by law or to protect rights and safety, or as part of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets (with notice where required).

5. Legal bases (EEA/UK — GDPR)

  • Legitimate interests — operating the App, keeping it secure and stable, and improving it through aggregated analytics and crash reports.
  • Consent — where local law requires consent for analytics; you can withdraw it as described in Section 7.
  • Legal obligation — where we must retain or disclose information by law.

6. Data retention

Analytics data is retained by Google Firebase according to the retention period configured for the project (no longer than 14 months). Crash reports are retained by Firebase Crashlytics for up to 90 days. Data stored only on your device stays there until you clear the App's data or uninstall it.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or port your personal information, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent (GDPR/UK GDPR); to know, delete, and correct personal information and not be discriminated against for exercising these rights (CCPA/CPRA — we do not sell or share personal information); and equivalent rights under LGPD (Brazil), PIPEDA (Canada), and other laws. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

Because the App has no accounts, we cannot identify you by name. To make a request, email contact@tranquilmind.co and include your device model and the approximate dates you used the App so we can locate related analytics records. You can also reset the analytics identifier by clearing the App's data or reinstalling it.

8. Children's privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13 (or the higher minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. International data transfers

Our service providers may process information in countries other than yours, including the United States. Where required, transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

10. Data security

Data sent from the App travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), and we rely on the security measures of our service providers. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Third-party content

Stories in the App are original works published by Tranquil Mind Technology JSC. Links to external websites (such as Google Play) are governed by those sites' own policies; we are not responsible for their content or practices.

12. Changes to this Policy
