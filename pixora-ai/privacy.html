Privacy Policy — Pixora AI

App: Pixora Ai Effective: October 30, 2026 Last Updated: October 30, 2026

Effective date: October 30, 2026

Pixora AI ("the App", "we", "us") is operated by Tranquil Mind Technology JSC. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles, why, and the choices you have. Contact: contact@tranquilmind.co.

1. Information we collect

Photos you choose or take. You select photos from your device or take them with the camera so the App can edit them. Background removal and NSFW checks run on your device. For AI generation and AI tools (for example AI art and object removal), the photo (and any mask or text prompt you enter) is sent over the internet to our image-generation service, a Cloudflare Workers AI gateway, which returns the result. The request also carries an app identifier and whether your account is premium, used for usage limits. We do not use your photos to identify you.

Content reports. If you report AI-generated content, we store the report reasons, optional note, the prompt, an image fingerprint (a hash, not the image itself), the platform and a timestamp, in Google Firebase Firestore. No account identifier is attached.

Device and usage data. Through Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config) and Google AdMob we process technical data such as device model, OS version, app version, crash logs, advertising ID, IP address-derived approximate location, and interaction events. Network state is read to know whether you are online.

Purchases. Subscriptions are handled by Google Play Billing. We receive purchase status, not your payment card details.

Permissions the App declares (Android): CAMERA, INTERNET, READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE

  • CAMERA — lets you take a photo inside the App to edit or transform it. Photos are not taken in the background.
  • INTERNET — needed to send photos and prompts to the AI service, load remote configuration, show ads, process purchases and send analytics and crash reports.
  • READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE — lets the App read the photos you choose from your gallery for editing.
  • WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE — lets the App save edited and generated images to your gallery when you choose to save.

2. How we use information

  • To provide editing and AI features and return results to you.
  • To show ads and, where required, collect consent (EU/UK users see a consent form).
  • To process subscriptions and restore purchases.
  • To measure performance, fix crashes and improve the App.
  • To review and act on reported AI content, and to detect NSFW content.

4. Sharing and retention

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with the providers above and when required by law. Photos sent for AI processing are used to produce your result. Analytics and crash data are kept as long as needed for the purposes above. Content reports are kept to review and handle abuse. Edited images saved to your gallery stay on your device until you delete them.

5. Your choices and rights

You can deny or revoke camera and storage permissions in Android Settings; features that need them will then be unavailable. You can reset your advertising ID in Android settings and manage ad consent in the App where offered. Depending on where you live (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, LGPD, PIPEDA) you may request access, correction or deletion of your data by emailing contact@tranquilmind.co.

6. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect their data; contact us to have it removed.

7. AI-generated content

AI results may be inaccurate or inappropriate. Use the in-app report option to flag content; we review reports.

8. Security and international transfers

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Providers may process data in countries other than yours, with safeguards required by law.

9. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the effective date above. Continued use means acceptance.
