Privacy Policy

App: Fast Fetch Effective: July 27, 2026 Last Updated: July 27, 2026

Fast Fetch ("the App", "we", "us", "our"), operated by Tranquil Mind Technology JSC, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices and rights you have. By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

Fast Fetch is a media downloader and file manager that lets you save videos, images, music, and documents from links and websites, play and organize them offline, and browse a catalog of short dramas and films. You do not need to create an account to use it.

1. Information We Collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email, phone number, or contacts. The App works primarily on your device. The following information is collected automatically by the third-party services listed in Section 3 when you use the App:

  • Device and advertising identifiers — such as your device's advertising ID and similar identifiers, used for advertising, analytics, and fraud/security.
  • Approximate location — a coarse location derived from your IP address by our advertising and analytics partners (we do not collect precise/GPS location, and the App requests no location permission).
  • App activity / usage data — in-app events, interactions, and screens viewed, used for analytics and advertising measurement.
  • Crash logs and diagnostics — crash reports, error data, and performance/diagnostic information used to keep the App stable.

Stored only on your device (not collected by us): the files you download (videos, images, music, documents), your download history, bookmarks, watch progress, language and app settings, and your media library. These stay in local storage on your device and are not transmitted to us.

We do not collect payment information: the App has no in-app purchases or subscriptions.

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide and operate the App — core features run on-device; remote configuration keeps the App's settings up to date.
  • Advertising and measurement — to serve ads (which fund the free App) and measure ad performance and install attribution.
  • Analytics and improvement — to understand how the App is used and improve features and stability.
  • Fraud prevention, security and compliance — to detect invalid ad traffic and abuse.
  • Legal compliance — to comply with applicable laws and enforce our terms.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information for money. We share the automatically-collected data described above only with the third-party service providers listed in Section 3, so they can perform advertising, analytics, attribution, and crash-reporting on our behalf or their own account as ad networks.

Because the App shows personalized advertising, this sharing of device/advertising identifiers and app-activity data with advertising partners may be considered a "sale" or "sharing" (for cross-context behavioral advertising) under certain U.S. state privacy laws. You can opt out via the ad-consent (UMP/ATT) choices above and your device's ad-personalization settings.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to enforce our Terms, protect rights and safety, or in connection with a merger, acquisition, or asset transfer.

5. Legal Bases (EEA/UK — GDPR)

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on:

  • Consent — for personalized advertising and, where required, analytics. You may withdraw consent at any time via the in-app consent dialog or system settings.
  • Legitimate interests — for core App operation, security, fraud prevention, non-personalized advertising, and improvement, balanced against your rights.
  • Legal obligation — where processing is required by law.

6. Data Retention

We do not operate our own servers and keep no user database. Our third-party partners retain the data they collect for the periods stated in their own policies (for example, Google Analytics for Firebase retains user-level data for up to 14 months; crash logs are retained for around 90 days). Data processed on your device (Section 1.1) remains until you clear the App's data or uninstall the App.

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or port your data, to object to or restrict processing, to withdraw consent, and to lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority (GDPR/UK GDPR); or to know, delete, correct, and opt out of the "sale"/"sharing" of personal information and targeted advertising without discrimination (CCPA/CPRA and similar U.S. state laws); or equivalent rights under other laws (LGPD, PIPEDA, and others).

To exercise any right, email contact@tranquilmind.co. Because the App has no accounts, please include your Android Advertising ID (Settings → Google → Ads) and device model so we and our partners can locate data associated with your device. You can also act directly on your device: reset your Advertising ID, opt out of ads personalization, use the in-app consent dialog, or clear the App's data.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and, because it can access and stream mature entertainment content, is designed for users aged 16 and older. It is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under that age. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

9. International Data Transfers

Our ad and analytics partners may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States. Where required, transfers are protected by safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or equivalent mechanisms maintained by those partners.

10. Data Security

Data transmitted by the SDKs described above is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. We and our partners apply reasonable technical and organizational measures; however, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

11. Third-Party Content

The App lets you access content from third parties, including websites you download from and a catalog of short dramas and films streamed from third-party servers. We do not control and are not responsible for third-party content, websites, or their privacy practices; please review their policies. You are responsible for ensuring you have the right to download or save any content you access through the App.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. Material changes will be posted in the App or on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Your continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated Policy.
