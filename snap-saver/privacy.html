Privacy Policy

App: Snap Saver Effective: October 30, 2026 Last Updated: October 30, 2026

Effective date: October 30, 2026 Operator: Tranquil Mind Technology JSC ("we", "us", "our") Contact: contact@tranquilmind.co

Snap Saver ("the App") lets you download and save videos from sites such as TikTok, YouTube and Instagram, and manage the saved files on your device. This policy explains what information the App handles and why.

1. Information we collect

Information stored on your device. Videos and other files you download, your library and app settings are stored locally on your device. We do not upload your downloaded files to our servers.

Information collected automatically. When you use the App, the SDKs listed in Section 3 may collect:

  • Device and app information (device model, operating system version, app version, language, time zone, crash reports and diagnostics).
  • Usage and analytics events (screens viewed, feature usage).
  • Advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID) and ad interaction data, used to show and measure ads.
  • Approximate network and IP-derived location, as received by our service providers.
  • Purchase status, when you buy a subscription or in-app product through Google Play Billing (payment details are handled by Google, not by us).

Information you provide. Links (URLs) you paste into the App are processed to fetch the requested media. If you contact us, we receive your email address and message.

Permissions the App declares (Android): AD_ID, INTERNET, READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM, WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE

  • AD_ID — lets advertising and attribution SDKs (Google AdMob and mediation partners, Adjust) read the Advertising ID to serve and measure ads. You can reset or delete the Advertising ID in your device settings.
  • INTERNET — required to load the pages and media links you enter, download files, show ads, and send analytics, crash reports and configuration requests.
  • READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE — used on Android 9 (API 28) and lower to list and open the videos and files you downloaded and saved in the device's shared storage (e.g. the Download folder), and to let you pick files from your device. It is not used on newer Android versions (declared with maxSdkVersion="28").
  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM — lets the App schedule local notifications/reminders at a precise time you can expect. No data is sent to us when this is used.
  • WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE — used on Android 9 (API 28) and lower to save the videos and files you download into the device's shared storage (e.g. the Download folder), and to rename or delete them when you ask. It is not used on newer Android versions (declared with maxSdkVersion="28").

2. How we use information

  • To provide the App's features: downloading, saving, playing and organizing your files.
  • To show ads and measure their performance, which helps keep the App free.
  • To process purchases and restore subscriptions.
  • To analyze usage, fix crashes and improve the App.
  • To send local notifications, if you allow them.
  • To comply with legal obligations and respond to your requests.

4. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information for money. We share information only with the service providers above, when required by law, or to protect rights and safety. Sharing with advertising partners may be considered a "sale" or "sharing" under some laws such as the CCPA; see Section 7.

5. Data retention

Files stay on your device until you delete them or uninstall the App. Analytics and diagnostic data are kept by the providers above for the periods set in their policies. You can request deletion as described in Section 7.

6. Security

We use reasonable safeguards, and traffic with our service providers is transmitted over the network using standard protections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, LGPD and PIPEDA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your data, to object to or restrict processing, to withdraw consent, and to opt out of the sale/sharing of personal information or targeted advertising. To exercise these rights, email contact@tranquilmind.co. You can also reset your Advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your device settings.

8. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where applicable law requires), and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact contact@tranquilmind.co and we will delete it.

9. International transfers

Our service providers may process data in countries other than your own. Where required, appropriate safeguards are used.

10. Content you download

The App is a tool. You are responsible for downloading only content you have the right to save, and for respecting the terms of the source sites and applicable copyright law. We do not host the content you download.

11. Changes to this policy
