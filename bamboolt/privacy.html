Privacy Policy

App: Bamboolt Effective: October 29, 2026 Last Updated: October 29, 2026

Effective date: October 29, 2026 Publisher: Tranquil Mind Technology JSC ("we", "us") Contact: contact@tranquilmind.co

Bamboolt ("the App") lets you decorate your Android status bar and battery indicator with cute panda skins, stickers and a small on-screen pet. This policy explains what the App accesses, what leaves your device, and who receives it.

1. Information the App handles

Stored only on your device. Your chosen skins, settings and preferences are stored locally (local database and shared preferences). We do not run an account system and we do not ask you to sign up.

Information collected automatically by SDKs. The third-party SDKs listed in section 3 may collect: advertising ID (Google Advertising ID), IP address, device model and OS version, language and country, app version, installation and usage events, crash and performance diagnostics, and ad interaction data (impressions, clicks).

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, location, microphone or camera data.

2. Permissions

Permissions the App declares (Android): INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, ACCESS_WIFI_STATE, POST_NOTIFICATIONS, SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM, SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW, FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED, VIBRATE, com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID.

PermissionPurpose
INTERNETLoad ads, remote configuration, analytics and crash reports.
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATERead connectivity state to redraw the Wi-Fi/mobile signal icon on the status bar overlay, and to check whether ads can load. Only signal level is read, not network content.
ACCESS_WIFI_STATERead Wi-Fi signal level to redraw the Wi-Fi icon on the status bar overlay.
POST_NOTIFICATIONSShow reminder notifications you enable.
SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARMSchedule reminder notifications at the time you choose.
SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOWDisplay the decorative pet walking on the screen and the notch overlay outside the App.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE / FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USEKeep the decorative pet displayed while the App is in the background, at your request.
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETEDRestore your overlay and scheduled reminders after the device restarts.
VIBRATEHaptic feedback.
com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_IDLet the advertising and attribution SDKs (Google AdMob, Adjust and mediation partners) read the Advertising ID. You can reset or delete it in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads.

Accessibility Service. The App declares an Android Accessibility Service (StatusBarAccessibilityService) that is used only to draw emoji/skins on the status bar overlay and to detect which app is in the foreground for the "Not-Allowed App" feature. The service is configured with canRetrieveWindowContent = false: it does not read the content of your screen, does not read what you type, and does not collect or transmit any data from other apps. It is enabled only if you turn it on in Android Settings, and you can turn it off at any time.

App visibility. The App queries launcher-capable apps (via <queries>, not QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES) to show the app list for the "Not-Allowed App" feature. This list stays on your device.

4. Advertising and consent

The App shows ads. Where required (EEA, UK and similar regions) we ask for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before personalized ads. You can change your choice via the consent option in the App if shown, and reset your Advertising ID in Android Settings.

5. Retention and deletion

Data stored on your device is removed when you clear the App data or uninstall the App. Data held by third-party SDK providers is retained under their policies. To request deletion of data associated with you, contact contact@tranquilmind.co.

6. Your rights

Depending on your location (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, LGPD, PIPEDA and similar laws) you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict use of your data, and to opt out of personalized advertising. Contact contact@tranquilmind.co and we will respond within the period required by law.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will act on it.

8. Security and international transfers

Data sent by SDKs is transmitted over encrypted connections where supported. Providers may process data in countries other than yours, under their safeguards.

9. Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above will change when we do.
