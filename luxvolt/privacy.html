Privacy Policy

App: Luxvolt Effective: 2026-10-06 Last Updated: 2026-10-06

Luxvolt ("the App", "we", "us", "our"), operated by Tranquil Mind Technology JSC, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices and rights you have. By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

Luxvolt is a flashlight utility: a one-tap LED torch, LED flash alerts for incoming calls, SMS and app notifications, a colour screen light, SOS and strobe modes, a Quick Settings tile and an optional home-screen (launcher) mode. You do not need to create an account to use it. The App contains no advertising and no in-app purchases.

1. Information we collect

Collected automatically and sent to our service providers (Google Firebase):

  • App interactions: for example which screens are opened and which features are used, together with the app version.
  • Device and app identifiers: the Firebase app instance / installation identifier and, where your device makes it available, the Android advertising ID. These identifiers are used only for analytics (counting unique installs and measuring usage), not for advertising.
  • Approximate location: a coarse location (such as country or city) that Firebase Analytics derives from your IP address. The App does not request location permission and does not collect precise (GPS) location.
  • Crash logs and diagnostics: crash reports, stack traces, device model, operating system version and similar technical information, sent through Firebase Crashlytics.

Accessed by the App to provide its features:

  • Installed apps: the App reads the list of apps on your device that can be launched (name, icon and package name). It uses this list to show your apps in the flash-alert app picker and in the optional home-screen mode, and to open the app you tap. We do not upload this list to our servers, and we do not use it for analytics or advertising.
  • Phone call state (permission READ_PHONE_STATE): used on your device only, to know that the phone is ringing so the LED can flash. We do not read phone numbers, contacts or call history.
  • Notifications (Notification access, only if you turn it on): used on your device only, to detect that a notification arrived and which app posted it. The LED then flashes for the apps you selected. Notification content is not stored or transmitted.
  • Settings: your flash timing, alert filters, selected apps, colours and language are stored locally on your device.

The App switches the camera LED on and off, but it never takes photos or records video. It does not collect your contacts, photos, message content or microphone audio, and it does not collect precise location.

Local notifications: the App may show reminder notifications that are generated on your device. You can turn them off at any time in your device's notification settings for Luxvolt.

2. How we use information

  • To provide and operate the App's flashlight, alert, screen-light and home-screen features.
  • To understand how the App is used and to improve it (analytics).
  • To detect, diagnose and fix crashes and errors.
  • To keep the App secure and to comply with legal obligations.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not build advertising profiles.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising or targeted advertising. We share information only:

  • with the service providers listed above, which process it on our behalf;
  • when required by law, or to protect the rights, property or safety of us, our users or others;
  • as part of a business transfer, such as a merger, acquisition or sale of assets.

5. Legal bases (EEA/UK — GDPR)

  • Legitimate interests: operating the App, keeping it secure, fixing crashes and improving it.
  • Consent: where local law requires consent for analytics.
  • Legal obligation: where we must keep or disclose information by law.

6. Data retention

Firebase Analytics data is kept according to the Firebase retention settings (up to 14 months). Crash reports are kept for up to 90 days. The installed-apps list and your settings stay on your device until you clear the App's data or uninstall it.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the following rights:

  • EEA/UK (GDPR): to access, correct, delete or port your personal data; to object to or restrict processing; to withdraw consent; and to lodge a complaint with a data-protection authority.
  • California and other U.S. states (CCPA/CPRA and similar laws): to know, delete and correct personal information; to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information (we do neither); and the right to non-discrimination.
  • Other regions: similar rights apply under laws such as the LGPD (Brazil) and PIPEDA (Canada).

Requesting deletion: email contact@tranquilmind.co with the subject "Data deletion – Luxvolt". The App has no accounts, so please include your device model and the approximate dates you used the App, and your advertising ID if available. This helps us locate related analytics records. We will respond within the time required by law. You can also stop all further collection at any time by uninstalling the App.

8. Children's privacy

The App is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under the higher age of digital consent in your country, such as 16 in some EEA countries). If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. International data transfers

Our service providers may process information in countries other than yours, including the United States. Where required, these transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

10. Data security

Data sent to our service providers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS), and we take reasonable measures to protect it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.
