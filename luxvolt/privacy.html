Privacy Policy

App: Luxvolt Effective: 2026-10-13 Last Updated: 2026-10-13

Luxvolt ("the App", "we", "us", "our"), operated by Tranquil Mind Technology JSC, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices and rights you have. By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

Luxvolt is a flashlight utility: a one-tap LED torch, LED flash alerts for incoming calls, SMS and app notifications, a colour screen light, SOS and strobe modes, a Quick Settings tile and an optional home-screen (launcher) mode. The App is free and supported by advertising. You do not need to create an account to use it.

1. Information we collect

Collected automatically and sent to our service providers:

  • App usage and diagnostics — app interactions (for example which screens are opened), app version, device model, operating system version, language, country/region, coarse location derived from your IP address, and crash logs, collected through Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics.
  • Advertising identifier — your Google Advertising ID (permission com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID) and, for some ad networks, a non-resettable device/app instance identifier. Used by Google AdMob, its mediation partners and our attribution provider to serve and measure ads and to prevent ad fraud.
  • Device and connection information — device model and manufacturer, operating system version, screen characteristics, network type and connection state (permission ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE), IP address, time zone and locale. Shared with ad and attribution providers for ad delivery, fraud prevention and measurement.
  • Ad interaction data — ad requests, impressions, clicks, view duration and rewarded-ad completions.
  • Install and attribution data — install referrer, first-open and in-app events (for example ad impressions), sent to Adjust and Meta so we can measure which campaign brought you to the App.
  • Firebase installation / app instance identifier — used to count unique installations, group crash reports and deliver remote configuration.

Processed only on your device (never uploaded):

  • Phone call state (permission READ_PHONE_STATE) — used only to know that the phone is ringing so the LED can flash. We do not read phone numbers, contacts or call history.
  • Notifications (Notification access, only if you enable it) — used only to detect that a notification arrived and which app posted it, so the LED can flash for the apps you selected. Notification content is not stored or transmitted.
  • Installed apps list — used only to show your apps in the flash-alert app picker and in the optional home-screen mode, and to open the app you tap.
  • Settings — your flash timing, alert filters, colours, unlocked colour themes and language are stored locally on your device.

The App does not use the camera to take pictures (it only switches the camera LED), and it does not collect GPS location, contacts, photos, messages content or microphone audio.

2. How we use information

  • To provide and operate the App's flashlight, alert, screen-light and launcher features.
  • To display advertising, including rewarded ads that unlock optional colour themes, and to measure ad performance and revenue.
  • To measure the effectiveness of our marketing campaigns (install attribution).
  • To understand how the App is used and improve it (analytics), and to deliver remote feature configuration.
  • To detect, diagnose and fix crashes and errors (diagnostics).
  • To prevent fraud and abuse, keep the App secure and comply with legal obligations.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information for money. Information is shared with the advertising, attribution and analytics providers listed above, which may use it for their own ad-serving, measurement and fraud-prevention purposes. Under some U.S. state privacy laws (for example California's CCPA/CPRA), serving personalised advertising and sharing advertising identifiers with ad networks can be treated as a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising. See section 8 for how to opt out. We also disclose information when required by law or to protect rights and safety, and as part of a business transfer (merger, acquisition or sale of assets).

5. Advertising, personalisation and your choices

  • Ad consent (EEA/UK/Switzerland) — on first launch we show a Google-certified consent message (Google UMP, IAB TCF) asking whether you allow personalised ads. Your choice is stored and passed to ad partners. You can change it later in the App's settings where the privacy options entry is available.
  • Non-personalised ads — if you refuse consent, ads are served as non-personalised (contextual); you will still see ads, because advertising funds the App.
  • Advertising ID — you can reset or delete your Advertising ID, and opt out of ad personalisation, in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. Deleting the Advertising ID stops ad personalisation and limits ad measurement.
  • Rewarded ads — some optional colour themes can be unlocked by watching a rewarded ad. Watching is voluntary; core flashlight features never require an ad.
  • No in-app purchases — the App has no subscriptions and no in-app purchase products.

6. Legal bases (EEA/UK — GDPR)

  • Consent — personalised advertising, ad measurement and, where required by local law, analytics (collected via the UMP consent message).
  • Legitimate interests — operating the App, serving non-personalised ads, preventing fraud, keeping the App secure and fixing crashes.
  • Legal obligation — where we must keep or disclose information by law.

You can withdraw consent at any time through the privacy options in the App or by resetting your Advertising ID in Android settings.

7. Data retention

Analytics data is kept according to Google Firebase retention settings (up to 14 months) and crash reports for up to 90 days. Advertising and attribution data is retained by each provider under its own policy (typically up to 24 months for Adjust and ad partners). Settings and unlocked themes stored on your device remain until you clear the App's data or uninstall it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your personal data, to object to or restrict processing, to withdraw consent, and to lodge a complaint with a data-protection authority (GDPR/UK GDPR). California residents have the right to know, delete and correct personal information, to opt out of the sale/sharing of personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising, and to non-discrimination (CCPA/CPRA); similar rights apply under LGPD (Brazil), PIPEDA (Canada) and other laws.

Opt out of sale/sharing and of personalised ads: reject personalised ads in the consent message, turn on "Delete advertising ID" / opt out of ad personalisation in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads, or email us at the address in section 12.

Because the App has no accounts, please include your Advertising ID, device model and the approximate date you used the App when you contact us, so we can locate related records. You can also stop all collection at any time by uninstalling the App.

9. Children's privacy

The App is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed to children. It is not enrolled in Google Play's Designed for Families programme. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the higher age of digital consent in your country), and we do not knowingly serve personalised ads to them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. International data transfers

Our service providers may process information in countries other than yours, including the United States, Singapore and the European Union. Where required, transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

11. Data security

Data sent to our service providers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS), and we take reasonable measures to protect it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.
