Privacy Policy

App: Chargybara Effective: 2026-09-28 Last Updated: 2026-09-28

Chargybara ("the App", "we", "us", "our"), operated by Tranquil Mind Technology JSC, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices and rights you have. By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

Chargybara is a personalization app that replaces your Android status bar battery icon with hand-drawn capybara artwork and lets you customize the status bar, notch, stickers and an on-screen pet. You do not need to create an account to use it.

1. Information we collect

We collect only the limited data described below. Your icon choices, status bar and notch settings, favourites, recently used items and excluded-app selections are processed and stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to us.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and app identifiers — your device's advertising ID (resettable, declared via the com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID permission) and app-instance identifiers (e.g. a Firebase installation ID), device model, operating system version, language, and app version.
  • Approximate location — a coarse, IP-address-derived location (country/city level) inferred by our advertising and analytics partners. The App does not request or access precise (GPS) location and declares no location permission.
  • App activity — in-app interactions such as screens viewed, features used (e.g. applying a battery icon, enabling a charging effect) and ad interactions.
  • Crash logs and diagnostics — crash reports, performance data, and diagnostic information used to keep the App stable.
  • Advertising interaction data — ad requests, impressions, clicks and rewarded-ad completions, processed by the advertising partners in Section 3.

Data processed only on your device

To draw your customizations on screen, the App uses an Accessibility Service (BIND_ACCESSIBILITY_SERVICE) and a display-over-other-apps permission (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW), together with a foreground service for the overlay and the screen pet. The Accessibility Service is used solely to align the custom status bar with the system UI and to render your battery icon, stickers and status bar elements. It does not read your messages, passwords, notification contents or the text of other apps, and none of the information it can technically observe is collected by us, stored off-device, or shared with anyone. The list of apps you choose to exclude from the custom bar stays on your device.

The App also declares POST_NOTIFICATIONS, SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED and VIBRATE to show local reminders (such as low-battery alerts) and to restore the overlay after a restart. These notifications are generated on your device; we do not use them to collect data. ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE and ACCESS_WIFI_STATE are used to show the connection indicator in the status bar and to check connectivity before loading ads — we do not collect your network names or Wi-Fi scan results.

Information you provide

The App has no accounts, forms, or profiles. If you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to send.

2. How we use information

  • Provide and operate the App;
  • Show and measure advertising, including rewarded ads that unlock content;
  • Understand how the App is used and improve it (analytics);
  • Deliver remote configuration and feature flags;
  • Detect, diagnose, and fix crashes and technical issues;
  • Attribute app installs to advertising campaigns;
  • Prevent fraud and abuse, and comply with legal obligations.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information for money. We share the data described above only:

  • with the third-party service providers listed in Section 3, to provide advertising, analytics, and attribution;
  • when required by law, legal process, or to protect rights, safety, and security;
  • as part of a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, in which case this Policy will continue to apply.

U.S. state privacy laws. Some U.S. states (e.g. California) define the disclosure of identifiers to advertising partners for personalized ads as a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information. You can opt out of this by disabling ads personalization or resetting your advertising ID in your device settings, or by declining personalized-ads consent where the consent form is shown.

5. Legal bases (EEA/UK — GDPR)

  • Consent — personalized advertising (via the consent form) and, where required, analytics;
  • Legitimate interests — operating, securing, and improving the App, measuring campaigns, and showing non-personalized ads;
  • Legal obligation — where processing is required by law.

6. Data retention

We keep data only as long as necessary for the purposes above or as required by law. Advertising and analytics partners retain identifiers for the limited periods described in their policies (e.g. Google Analytics for Firebase retains certain identifier-linked data for up to 14 months). Crash and diagnostic data is retained for a limited period needed for troubleshooting. Your settings and favourites remain on your device until you change them or uninstall the App.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or port your data; to object to or restrict processing; to withdraw consent; and to lodge a complaint with your data-protection authority (GDPR/UK GDPR). California and other U.S.-state residents may have rights to know, delete, correct, and opt out of "sale"/"sharing" and targeted advertising, without discrimination. Residents of other regions (e.g. Brazil — LGPD, Canada — PIPEDA) have similar rights under local law.

To exercise any right, email contact@tranquilmind.co. Because the App has no accounts, please include your advertising ID and device model in your request so we and our partners can locate data associated with your device. Uninstalling the App deletes all settings stored on your device.

8. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction, e.g. 16 in parts of the EEA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The App has no chat, messaging, comments, or user-to-user content sharing. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us at contact@tranquilmind.co and we will delete it.
