Privacy Policy

App: Handini Effective: 2026-10-01 Last Updated: 2026-10-01

Handini ("the App", "we", "us", "our"), operated by Tranquil Mind Technology JSC, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices and rights you have. By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

Handini lets you cast glowing AR magic effects with your hand: it uses your device camera to recognize your hand gestures and draws particle effects that follow them. You do not need to create an account to use the App.

1. Information we collect

Camera (processed on your device only). With your permission, the App opens your front or rear camera to recognize your hand and draw effects in real time. Hand recognition runs entirely on your device using an on-device machine-learning model (Google MediaPipe Hand Landmarker). Camera frames and the hand positions computed from them are processed in memory and are not uploaded, stored on our servers, or shared with anyone. We do not use the camera to identify you, and we do not collect facial or biometric identifiers.

Information collected automatically. When you use the App, the following data is sent to our service providers:

  • App activity and usage data — screens viewed, features used, session information, and app interactions (via Google Firebase Analytics).
  • Device and app identifiers — the Firebase app-instance ID, device model, operating-system version, app version, language and similar technical information.
  • Approximate location — a coarse location (country / region) derived from your IP address by Firebase Analytics. We do not request or collect precise (GPS) location.
  • Crash logs and diagnostics — crash reports, stack traces, and device state at the time of a crash (via Google Firebase Crashlytics).

Information stored only on your device. Your settings and preferences (such as the selected language, onboarding status, and notification preferences) are saved locally on your device and are not sent to us.

Notifications. If you allow notifications, the App schedules local reminder notifications on your device. These are generated on the device; we do not collect a push-notification token for this.

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos from your gallery, microphone audio, or precise location. The App does not contain advertising and does not offer in-app purchases at this time.

2. How we use information

  • To provide and operate the App, including real-time on-device hand tracking and AR effects.
  • To remember your settings and preferences on your device.
  • To send local reminder notifications you have allowed.
  • To understand how the App is used and to improve its features and performance (analytics).
  • To detect, diagnose, and fix crashes and technical problems.
  • To protect the security and integrity of the App and to comply with legal obligations.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We share information only:

  • with the service providers listed in Section 3, who process it on our behalf to provide analytics and crash reporting;
  • when required by law, regulation, legal process, or a governmental request, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of our users, us, or others;
  • in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to this Policy.

When you use the share feature, the App opens your device's share sheet; what you share and with whom is handled by the app you choose and its own privacy policy.

5. Legal bases (EEA/UK — GDPR)

  • Legitimate interests — operating the App, keeping it secure, fixing crashes, and improving it through aggregated analytics.
  • Consent — camera access and notifications (granted through your device's permission prompts, which you can withdraw at any time in system settings), and analytics where consent is required by local law.
  • Legal obligation — where we must process data to comply with the law.

6. Data retention

  • Camera frames and hand-tracking results are not retained; they exist only in device memory while the camera is active.
  • Settings stored on your device remain until you clear the App's data or uninstall it.
  • Firebase Analytics data is retained according to our Firebase retention settings (up to 14 months for user-level data), after which it is deleted or aggregated.
  • Crashlytics crash reports are retained for a limited period (currently up to 90 days).

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • GDPR / UK GDPR: access, correct, delete, or port your personal data; object to or restrict processing; withdraw consent at any time; and lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.
  • CCPA / CPRA (California and other U.S. states): know what personal information we collect, use, and disclose; request deletion and correction; opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information (we do not sell or share it); and not be discriminated against for exercising your rights.
  • Other regions (e.g. Brazil's LGPD, Canada's PIPEDA): similar rights of access, correction, deletion, and information.

Because the App has no accounts, we cannot link data to your name. To make a request, email us at contact@tranquilmind.co and include your device model and the approximate dates you used the App so we can locate the related analytics records. You can also stop all data collection at any time by uninstalling the App, and you can revoke camera or notification permission in your device settings.

8. Children's privacy

The App is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13 (or the higher minimum age of digital consent in your country, such as 16 in some EEA countries). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us at contact@tranquilmind.co and we will delete it.

9. International data transfers

Our service providers (such as Google) may process data in countries other than your own, including the United States. Where required, such transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

10. Data security

Data sent to our service providers is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. Camera processing stays on your device. We take reasonable measures to protect information, but no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.
