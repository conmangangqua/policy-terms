Privacy Policy

App: Ringdazzle Effective: 2026-11-24 Last Updated: 2026-11-24

Ringdazzle ("the App", "we", "us", "our"), operated by Tranquil Mind Technology JSC, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices and rights you have. By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

Ringdazzle is a fake video call simulator: you pick a star, choose how the incoming call screen looks, schedule when it rings, play a short voice line you recorded yourself when you answer, and chat with scripted characters. Every call and chat in the App is a simulation made for entertainment. You do not need to create an account to use it.

1. Information we collect

Information that stays on your device (not collected by us). The photos and videos you choose for custom callers, the callers you create, your call history, your favorites, your settings (sound, vibration, flash, ringtone, call-screen style, language, parent-zone limits) and the voice clips you record are stored only in the App's private storage on your device. They are not uploaded to our servers or to any third party.

Voice recordings. If you tap the record button when creating a caller, the App uses your microphone to record a clip of up to 30 seconds. The clip is saved only on your device, is played back when you answer a pretend call, and is deleted when you delete the voice or the caller, or uninstall the App. The App never records in the background, and the clip is never sent to us or to any third party.

Camera. During a pretend call the App can show your front camera as a small preview so that you appear in the video call. The preview is not recorded or transmitted by the App. If you use the in-call screen-recording button, the recording is saved on your device and leaves it only if you choose to share it through your phone's share sheet.

Age information. On first launch the App asks for your date of birth to set an age tier (under 13, 13 to 17, 18 and over). Only the tier is stored, on your device; the date itself is not stored or transmitted.

Information collected automatically (leaves your device). Through the third-party services listed in Section 3, we and they receive:

  • Device and advertising identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID), device model, operating system version, language and app version.
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address (country or city level). The App does not request any location permission.
  • App activity, such as the screens you open, the features you use and the ads you see. When you report a character from the call screen, the report reason and the character identifier are sent as an analytics event.
  • Crash logs and performance diagnostics.

Catalog content. Caller thumbnails and call videos are downloaded from our content server when you browse or call. These requests carry only what is needed to fetch the content; no account or personal identifier is attached.

Permissions the App may request (Android): CAMERA (in-call preview), RECORD_AUDIO (record a caller voice, only when you tap record), POST_NOTIFICATIONS and USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT (ring while the screen is locked), SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM (ring at the time you chose), RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED (re-arm a scheduled call after a restart), WAKE_LOCK (turn the screen on for the call), VIBRATE, INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE (content, ads, analytics, updates) and com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID (advertising measurement). Photos and videos are picked through the Android photo picker, so no storage permission is requested.

2. How we use information

To provide and operate the App; to ring the pretend call you scheduled; to remember your settings on your device; to show and measure advertising, including rewarded ads; to understand which features are used and to fix crashes; to detect fraud and keep the App and our advertising secure; to review content reports; and to comply with the law.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information for money. We share the information described in Section 3 with those third-party services so that they can provide advertising, attribution, analytics and crash reporting; when required by law or to protect rights and safety; and in a business transfer such as a merger or acquisition. Because advertising partners may use your advertising ID and approximate location for personalized ads, this can count as "sharing" or a "sale" under some U.S. state privacy laws. You can opt out as described in Sections 3 and 7.

5. Legal bases (EEA/UK — GDPR)

Consent (personalized advertising and analytics where consent is required); legitimate interests (running and securing the App, fixing crashes, measuring and improving features, preventing fraud); and legal obligation.

6. Data retention

Data on your device is kept until you delete it in the App or uninstall the App. Data held by our service providers is kept only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law, according to their own retention periods: advertising and attribution identifiers are retained by those partners for a limited period, and crash and diagnostic data is retained by Firebase for a limited period.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have the right to access, correct, delete, port, object to or restrict the use of your data, to withdraw consent, and to lodge a complaint with a data-protection authority (GDPR/UK GDPR); to know, delete and correct, to opt out of sale, sharing and targeted advertising, and not to be discriminated against (CCPA/CPRA and other U.S. state laws); and similar rights under LGPD (Brazil), PIPEDA (Canada) and other laws.

To exercise a right, email contact@tranquilmind.co. Because the App has no accounts, include your advertising ID and device details so we can locate your data with our service providers. You can also delete everything stored on your device by uninstalling the App.

8. Children's privacy

The App is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. The App asks for your date of birth on first launch to set an age tier. If the date shows that you are under 13, the App asks for a parent or guardian to confirm, limits how often it shows reminder calls, and provides a Parent Zone with screen-time and quiet-hours controls protected by a parent check. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 beyond the automatic data described in Section 1. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact contact@tranquilmind.co and we will delete it.

Child Safety Standards (CSAE)

Ringdazzle includes a pretend chat with scripted replies and lets you add a photo, video and name for a custom caller. For that reason Tranquil Mind Technology JSC publishes the following Child Safety Standards for the Ringdazzle app.

  • Zero tolerance. Child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM), is strictly prohibited in the App. This covers images, video, audio, text and AI-generated content; any sexualization of minors; grooming, sextortion, solicitation and trafficking; and any attempt to create, request, share or promote such material, whether in content a user adds or in content the App produces.
  • What the App does. Chat replies come only from scripted content written by our team and bundled with the App; the App does not let users send messages or media to other people and has no user-to-user features. Photos, videos and names added for custom callers stay on the user's device and are never shared with other users. Users can report a character from the call screen; the report is logged as an analytics event and reviewed by our team. The App has no live human moderation of user content because no user content leaves the device.
  • If we learn of a violation. We remove the content or feature involved, suspend the access concerned, and report apparent CSAM to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and/or the competent local authorities.
  • Legal compliance. We comply with applicable child-safety laws and align with the Google Play Child Safety Standards policy.
  • Child-safety contact: Tranquil Mind Technology JSC, contact@tranquilmind.co.
