Privacy Policy

App: Spotlite Effective: 2026-08-11 Last Updated: 2026-08-11

Spotlite ("the App", "we", "us", "our"), operated by Tranquil Mind Technology JSC, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices and rights you have. By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

Spotlite is a vertical short-drama streaming app: you browse a catalogue of series and watch episodes inside the App. You do not need to create an account to use it — there is no sign-up, no login, and we do not ask you for your name, email address or phone number inside the App.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask you to fill in any form or profile. The information below is collected automatically by the App and by the third-party services listed in section 3.

Device and advertising identifiers. Your Android advertising ID (AAID), an app-instance identifier used by analytics, and device identifiers used by our advertising and attribution partners.

Device and technical information. Device model, manufacturer, operating-system version, screen and language settings, app version, network type, and your IP address.

Approximate location derived from IP address. We do not request GPS or any location permission. Our advertising, analytics and attribution partners may infer an approximate location (typically country or region) from your IP address.

App activity. Which screens you open, which series and episodes you view and how far you watch, what you search for inside the App, which items you save, ad impressions and clicks, and coins earned or spent.

Purchase and subscription status. Whether you hold an active Spotlite VIP subscription, the plan type, and the transaction identifiers needed to validate and restore it. Payment card details are handled entirely by Google Play — we never see or store them.

Crash and diagnostic data. Crash stack traces, error logs and basic performance data when the App fails.

Preferences stored on your device. Your chosen interface language, subtitle setting, saved series, watch history, coin balance and playback position are stored locally on your device. This data does not leave your device unless it is included in the diagnostic data described above.

2. How we use information

  • To provide and operate the App: load the catalogue, stream episodes, remember where you stopped, and keep your saved list, history and coin balance working.
  • To show advertising, including the rewarded ads that let you earn coins, and to measure whether those ads were shown and viewed.
  • To validate purchases and to unlock and restore your Spotlite VIP subscription.
  • To measure marketing performance: our attribution partner tells us which campaign or channel an install came from, so we know where our users come from.
  • To analyse usage and improve the App, and to diagnose crashes and errors.
  • To detect and prevent fraud, abuse and security incidents.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information for money. We share data only with the service providers listed in section 3, who process it for the purposes described there.

We may also disclose information where we believe in good faith that it is required by law, legal process or a governmental request, or where it is necessary to protect our rights, our users, or the security of the App. If our business is transferred, merged or acquired, information may be transferred as part of that transaction.

Note for U.S. state residents. Because personalised advertising is available in the App, the sharing of identifiers with advertising partners may be treated as a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information under California and other U.S. state privacy laws. You can opt out by declining personalised ads in the consent form (where offered), by resetting or deleting your advertising ID as described in section 3, or by contacting us at the address in section 12.

5. Legal bases (EEA and UK — GDPR)

  • Consent — personalised advertising and, where required, analytics. You may withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legitimate interests — operating and securing the App, non-personalised advertising, crash diagnostics, and product improvement.
  • Performance of a contract — providing the Spotlite VIP subscription you purchased.
  • Legal obligation — retaining records where the law requires it.

6. Data retention

We keep information only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this Policy. Advertising, attribution and analytics identifiers are retained by the partners in section 3 according to their own retention schedules. Crash and diagnostic records are kept for a limited period and then deleted or aggregated. Data stored locally on your device — language, subtitles, saved series, history, coins and playback position — remains there until you clear the App's data or uninstall the App.

7. Your rights

EEA and UK (GDPR). You have the right to access your data, to have it corrected or deleted, to receive it in a portable form, to object to or restrict processing, to withdraw consent, and to lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority.

California (CCPA/CPRA). You have the right to know what personal information is collected and how it is used and disclosed, to request deletion or correction, to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information and of targeted advertising, and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

Other regions. Equivalent rights are available under Brazil's LGPD, Canada's PIPEDA and comparable laws.

Because Spotlite has no accounts, we cannot identify you from a name or email address. To exercise a right, email us at the address in section 12 and include your Android advertising ID together with your device model and approximate date of use, so we can locate the relevant records with our partners. We will respond within the period required by applicable law.

8. Children's privacy

Spotlite is intended for users aged 18 and over and is not directed to children. The catalogue contains dramatic content aimed at an adult audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information to us, contact us at the address in section 12 and we will delete it. In the EEA and the UK, where the applicable age of digital consent is higher than the age at which consent may be given, we rely on the higher local age.

9. International data transfers

The service providers listed in section 3 operate globally, so your information may be processed in countries outside your own, including the United States. Where required, transfers out of the EEA or the UK rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

10. Data security

Traffic between the App and our servers and partners is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Third-party content

The series available in Spotlite are supplied by third-party content providers and remain the property of their respective rights holders. Ads shown in the App are supplied by the advertising partners listed above and may link to third-party websites and apps. We do not control that content and are not responsible for it; review the relevant third party's terms and privacy policy before interacting with it.

12. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. Material changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Continuing to use the App after a change means you accept the updated Policy.
