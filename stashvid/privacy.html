Privacy Policy

App: Stashvid Effective: 2026-08-22 Last Updated: 2026-08-22

Stashvid ("the App", "we", "us", "our"), operated by Tranquil Mind Technology JSC, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices and rights you have. By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

Stashvid lets you paste a link and save videos, images, music and documents to your device for offline access, and browse the web to find media to save. You do not need to create an account to use it.

1. Information we collect

Information collected automatically

  • Device and advertising identifiers — including the Google Advertising ID (AAID), device model, operating-system version, language and country settings. Used by our advertising, attribution and analytics partners.
  • Approximate location derived from IP address — coarse, country/region level. We do not collect GPS or precise location, and the App requests no location permission.
  • App activity and diagnostics — screens opened, features used, download events, and crash reports with stack traces and device state.
  • Network information — IP address and connection type, as part of ordinary internet communication with our partners' servers.

Information you provide

  • Links you paste and pages you visit in the in-app browser. These are processed to fetch the media you asked for. We do not operate an account system and do not build a user profile from this activity on our own servers.

Information that stays on your device

  • Downloaded files, bookmarks, browsing history and folders. These are stored locally on your device using the App's storage permissions and are not transmitted to us. Uninstalling the App or clearing its data removes them.

2. How we use information

  • Provide and operate the App, including fetching and saving the media you request.
  • Display advertising, including measuring ad performance and limiting repeated ads.
  • Measure marketing attribution — understanding which campaign led to an install.
  • Analytics and product improvement — understanding which features are used.
  • Diagnose crashes and improve stability and security.
  • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse and technical misuse.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information for money. We share information only:

  • with the third-party services listed in Section 3, for the purposes described there;
  • when required by law, regulation, legal process or an enforceable governmental request;
  • to protect the rights, property or safety of our users, the public or us;
  • in connection with a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, where this Policy will continue to apply to the transferred information.

Note for United States residents. Sharing device and advertising identifiers with advertising partners for personalized advertising may be treated as a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information under certain U.S. state privacy laws, including the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended. You may opt out by declining personalized advertising in the consent form, or by limiting ad tracking in your Android settings as described above.

5. Legal bases (EEA/UK — GDPR)

  • Consent — personalized advertising, and analytics where consent is required.
  • Legitimate interests — operating the App, securing it against abuse, measuring aggregate performance and improving features.
  • Legal obligation — retaining records or responding to lawful requests where the law requires it.

Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it at any time; withdrawal does not affect processing carried out before withdrawal.

6. Data retention

We retain information only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, or as required by law. Our advertising, attribution and analytics partners retain identifiers according to their own published retention periods. Crash and diagnostic records are kept for a limited period sufficient to investigate and fix defects. Files you download, and your bookmarks and history, remain on your device until you delete them or uninstall the App.

7. Your rights

EEA/UK (GDPR). You have the right to access, correct, delete, port, object to and restrict processing of your personal data, to withdraw consent, and to lodge a complaint with your national data-protection authority.

California (CCPA/CPRA). You have the right to know what personal information is collected and how it is used, to delete it, to correct it, to opt out of its sale or sharing and of targeted advertising, and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

Other regions. Residents of Brazil (LGPD), Canada (PIPEDA) and other jurisdictions have comparable rights under their local law.

To exercise any right, contact us at the address in Section 13. Because the App has no accounts, we cannot identify you by name — please include your advertising ID and device model in your request so we can locate the relevant records with our partners.

8. Children's privacy

The App is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, or under the higher age of digital consent that applies in your country (up to 16 in parts of the European Economic Area). If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. International data transfers

Your information may be processed in countries other than your own, including the United States, where our service providers operate. Where required, such transfers rely on appropriate safeguards, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

10. Data security

We do not own, control, endorse or take responsibility for that content or those websites, and their own terms and privacy policies apply to your use of them. You are responsible for ensuring that you have the right to download and use any content you save.
