Privacy Policy

App: IPTV Velix Effective: July 28, 2026 Last Updated: July 28, 2026

IPTV Velix ("the App", "we", "us", "our"), operated by Tranquil Mind Technology JSC, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices and rights you have. By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

IPTV Velix is a media player for IPTV playlists that you supply yourself (M3U/M3U8 or Xtream Codes). We do not host, provide, or distribute any TV channels, streams, or media. You do not need to create an account to use the App, and we do not operate a login or user-profile system.

1. Information We Collect

We do not ask you to register, and we do not collect your name, email address, or phone number.

Data you provide that stays on your device

Playlist URLs, uploaded playlist files, and Xtream Codes login details (server, username, password) that you enter are stored locally on your device to operate the App. We do not transmit these credentials or playlists to our servers.

Data collected automatically

Through the third-party services in Section 3:

  • Device and advertising identifiers — e.g. an app-instance ID and the Android Advertising ID, used for analytics.
  • Approximate location — a coarse, city-level location derived from your IP address by our analytics provider. We do not collect precise/GPS location, and the App requests no location permission.
  • App activity — in-app interactions and events (e.g. screens opened, features used), used to understand and improve the App.
  • Crash logs and diagnostics — technical information about crashes and performance (device model, OS version, stack traces, timing) so we can fix problems.

We do not access your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, calendar, or SMS.

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the App.
  • Diagnose crashes, monitor stability, and improve performance and features.
  • Understand aggregate usage through analytics.
  • Protect against fraud, abuse, and security threats, and comply with legal obligations.

We do not use your information to serve advertising, and the App contains no ads.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information for money, and we do not share it with third parties for cross-context behavioral advertising. We share data only:

  • with the service providers listed in Section 3, to the limited extent needed to run the App;
  • to comply with law, legal process, or enforceable governmental requests; and
  • in connection with a merger, acquisition, or asset transfer, subject to this Policy.

5. Legal Bases (EEA/UK — GDPR)

Where GDPR applies, we rely on: our legitimate interests in operating, securing, and improving the App (analytics, crash diagnostics, fraud prevention); consent where required by local law for certain analytics; and legal obligation where applicable. You may object to or withdraw consent as described in Section 7.

6. Data Retention

We retain automatically-collected data only as long as necessary for the purposes above or as required by law. Analytics and identifier data follow our analytics provider's standard retention periods; crash and diagnostic data are kept for a limited period to investigate and fix issues. Data you store locally (playlists, Xtream login) remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App.

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, port, restrict, or object to the processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent or complain to a data protection authority (GDPR); to know, delete, correct, and opt out of the "sale"/"sharing" of personal information and targeted advertising, with no discrimination for exercising these rights (CCPA/CPRA); and similar rights under LGPD, PIPEDA, and other laws.

Because the App has no accounts, we cannot look you up by name or email. To exercise a right, email us at the address in Section 13 and include your Android Advertising ID and device details so we can locate the relevant data. You can also stop all automatic collection by uninstalling the App and can reset your advertising ID in device settings.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for users aged 18 and over and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it. We handle age of digital consent in the EEA and COPPA obligations in the U.S. accordingly.

9. International Data Transfers

Your information may be processed in countries other than your own, including the United States, where our service providers operate. Where required, transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

10. Data Security

We use HTTPS/TLS encryption in transit and reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Third-Party Content

The App plays streams and content from IPTV sources that you supply. We do not control, host, endorse, or take responsibility for that content or for the third-party servers you connect to. You are responsible for the sources you add and for ensuring you have the right to access them. Review the terms and privacy practices of your own IPTV provider.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. Material changes will be posted here with a new "Last updated" date. Your continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance.
