Privacy Policy

App: Lingo Owl: AI English Tutor Effective: October 30, 2026 Last Updated: October 30, 2026

Effective date: 30 October 2026

Lingo Owl: AI English Tutor ("Lingo Owl", "the App", "we") is operated by Tranquil Mind Technology JSC ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the App handles, why, and the choices you have. Contact: contact@tranquilmind.co.

1. Information we collect

Information you provide or generate while using the App

  • Chat text. Messages you type to the AI tutor are sent to a third-party AI language-model provider (configured remotely; the current default is DeepSeek) to generate replies.
  • Voice input. When you use speaking practice, the App records your voice through the microphone. Recognition uses the speech engine on your device where available; otherwise the recording is uploaded to a speech-to-text provider (default: Groq, Whisper model) to be turned into text. We do not use recordings for advertising.
  • Account information. If you sign in with Google or Apple (via Firebase Authentication), we receive your account identifier, display name and email address.
  • Learning data. Progress, lessons, quiz results and settings are stored on your device (Hive / SharedPreferences) and, when you are signed in, synchronised to Cloud Firestore.
  • Purchase status. Subscription / purchase state is handled by RevenueCat and Google Play / App Store; we do not receive your payment card details.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and app information (device model, OS version, app version, language, time zone), crash reports and usage analytics (Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics).
  • Advertising identifier and ad-interaction data, used by Google AdMob, its mediation partners (Meta Audience Network, Unity Ads, Bigo Ads) and Adjust for ad delivery and attribution.
  • A push-notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging) so we can send reminders and announcements.
  • Network connectivity state, used to decide whether online features are available.

Permissions the App declares (Android): INTERNET, POST_NOTIFICATIONS, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED, RECORD_AUDIO, com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID

  • INTERNET — connect to the AI tutor, speech recognition, sign-in, sync, analytics and ad services.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — show the daily study reminders and push announcements you opt into on Android 13+. You can decline or turn this off in system settings.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED — re-schedule your study reminders after the device restarts or the App updates; no data is collected through it.
  • RECORD_AUDIO — capture your voice during speaking and conversation practice so it can be converted to text and answered by the tutor. Recording starts only when you tap the microphone; you can deny or revoke the permission at any time and still use text chat.
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID — let the ad and attribution SDKs (AdMob, Adjust) read the Google advertising ID. You can reset or delete it in Android settings.

2. How we use information

To provide the AI tutor, speaking practice and lessons; to sign you in and sync progress; to send reminders you request; to process subscriptions; to show ads; to measure performance, fix crashes and improve the App; and to comply with law.

4. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information for money. Data is shared with the service providers above, when required by law, or in a business transfer. Ad partners may receive the advertising ID and device information for advertising purposes; where law requires, you can opt out via your device's ad settings.

5. Retention and deletion

Local data stays on your device until you clear it or uninstall the App. Synced data is kept while your account exists. You can delete your account and synced data in the App, or email contact@tranquilmind.co and we will delete it within a reasonable time. Voice recordings are used only to obtain the transcript.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, LGPD and PIPEDA) you may request access, correction, deletion, portability, or object to / restrict processing, and withdraw consent. Contact contact@tranquilmind.co.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the higher age required locally). We do not knowingly collect their data; contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security
