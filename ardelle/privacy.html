Privacy Policy

App: Ardelle Effective: 2026-11-13 Last Updated: 2026-11-13

Ardelle ("the App", "we", "us", "our"), operated by Tranquil Mind Technology JSC, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices and rights you have. By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

Ardelle is a short drama streaming app: you can browse, search, watch and download short series, earn and spend in-app coins, and optionally subscribe to Ardelle VIP. You do not need to create an account to use it. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.

1. Information we collect

We collect only the information described below. Most of it is collected automatically by the App and by the third-party services listed in Section 3.

a) Device and advertising identifiers. Your device's advertising ID (Google Advertising ID), app-instance and installation identifiers, and similar identifiers assigned by our analytics, advertising, attribution and security partners.

b) Approximate location. Our advertising partners may infer your approximate (city- or region-level) location from your IP address in order to serve and measure ads. The App does not request access to your device's GPS or precise location.

c) App activity. How you use the App, for example screens viewed, series and episodes watched, ad interactions, check-ins and tasks completed, and purchase events.

d) In-app search queries. When you search for a series, the search text is sent to our content service to return results. Your recent-search list is stored only on your device, and you can clear it in the App.

e) Purchase information. When you buy a subscription or a coin pack, Google Play processes the payment. We receive (through RevenueCat, our purchase-management provider) the product purchased, transaction and subscription status, and an anonymous app user ID, so that we can unlock what you paid for and restore purchases. We never receive your full payment card details.

f) Crash logs, diagnostics and security signals. Crash reports, performance data, device model, operating system version, app version, and security signals (for example, whether the App has been tampered with or is running on a rooted or hooked device), used to keep the App stable and secure.

g) Information you send us. If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message.

Stored only on your device (not collected by us): your watch history, saved series, continue-watching progress, downloaded episodes, coin balance, check-in streak, hidden genres and filter settings, invite code, reminder settings, and any live-channel playlist you add. This data stays on your device and is deleted when you clear the App's data or uninstall it. Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device; we do not use push-notification servers.

Live channels. If you add your own M3U playlist, the App connects directly to the links in that playlist. Those stream providers are chosen by you, not us, and they will receive your IP address and device information under their own policies.

2. How we use information

We use the information above to:

  • provide and operate the App, including streaming, search, downloads, coins and unlocks;
  • process and restore purchases and subscriptions;
  • show ads, including personalized ads where you have consented or where allowed by law, and measure ad performance and app installs;
  • understand how the App is used and improve it (analytics);
  • detect, investigate and prevent crashes, fraud, abuse, tampering and security incidents;
  • respond to your support requests; and
  • comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service.

Ad consent. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland (and in other regions where required), the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for your consent before personalized ads are shown and before data is used for related purposes. If you do not consent, you may still see non-personalized ads, which may still rely on limited data such as approximate location and device information for frequency capping, fraud prevention and measurement.

Your advertising ID. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time in your device settings (on most Android devices: Settings → Security & privacy → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads). After you opt out, the advertising ID is no longer available to the App and our partners.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information for money. We share information only:

  • with the service providers and partners listed in Section 3, for the purposes described there;
  • when required by law, regulation, legal process or a government request, or to protect the rights, safety and property of our users, the public or us; and
  • in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing or sale of all or part of our business, subject to this Policy.

Because the App shows personalized ads, sharing identifiers and app activity with advertising partners may count as "selling" or "sharing" personal information, or as "targeted advertising", under some U.S. state privacy laws (for example, California, Colorado, Virginia and Connecticut). You can opt out as described in Sections 3 and 7.

5. Legal bases (EEA/UK — GDPR)

If you are in the EEA or the UK, we process your information on these legal bases:

  • Consent: personalized advertising and related ad measurement, and analytics where consent is required. You may withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legitimate interests: operating and improving the App, crash reporting, security, fraud prevention and non-personalized advertising, balanced against your rights.
  • Contract: providing purchases and subscriptions you request.
  • Legal obligation: complying with tax, accounting and other legal requirements.

6. Data retention

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes in this Policy or as required by law. Analytics data is kept according to our Firebase retention settings (up to 14 months). Crash and diagnostic data is kept for a limited period (typically up to 90 days). Purchase records are kept as long as needed to provide your purchases and to meet tax and accounting obligations. Our advertising, attribution and security partners keep data according to their own policies linked in Section 3. Data stored only on your device remains there until you clear the App's data or uninstall the App.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • (GDPR / UK GDPR) access, correct, delete or port your personal data; object to or restrict processing; withdraw consent at any time; and lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority;
  • (California and other U.S. states) know what personal information we collect, use and share; request deletion or correction; opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information and of targeted advertising; and not be discriminated against for exercising these rights;
  • (Brazil — LGPD, Canada — PIPEDA, Vietnam and other regions) similar rights to access, correct and delete your data and to withdraw consent, as provided by applicable law.

How to exercise your rights. Email contact@tranquilmind.co. Because the App has no accounts, we can only find data linked to your device's identifiers, so please include your advertising ID (and, if possible, your device model and the approximate dates you used the App). We may need to verify your request and will respond within the time required by law. You can also:

  • opt out of personalized ads or reset your advertising ID in your device settings (Section 3);
  • clear your on-device data by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App; and
  • manage or cancel subscriptions in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.

Data deletion. To ask us to delete data associated with your device, email contact@tranquilmind.co with the subject "Data deletion request" and your advertising ID. We will delete or anonymize the data we hold and ask our service providers to do the same where they act on our behalf. Uninstalling the App deletes all data stored on your device.

8. Children's privacy

The App is intended for users aged 16 and older and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under the age of digital consent in your country, such as 16 in parts of the EEA). If you believe a child has used the App and provided us with personal information, contact us at contact@tranquilmind.co and we will delete it.

9. International data transfers

We are based in Vietnam, and our service providers may process information in other countries, including the United States, Singapore and member states of the EU. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for these transfers, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

10. Data security

Data sent between the App and our service providers is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. We use reasonable technical and organizational measures, including app-integrity and anti-tampering protection, to protect information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Third-party content

The App shows series provided by content partners and lets you play streams from playlists you add yourself. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party content providers, stream sources or websites you open from the App. Please review their policies.

12. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will post the updated Policy at this address with a new "Last updated" date and, where appropriate, notify you in the App. Your continued use of the App after the update means you accept the revised Policy.
