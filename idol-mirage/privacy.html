Privacy Policy

App: Idol Mirage: Celeb Prank Call Effective: July 28, 2026 Last Updated: July 28, 2026

Idol Mirage ("the App", "we", "us", "our"), operated by Tranquil Mind Technology JSC, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices and rights you have. By using the App, you agree to this Policy. On Google Play the App is listed as "Idol Mirage: Celeb Prank Call".

Idol Mirage is an entertainment app that simulates playful, fictional "prank" video and voice calls and text chats with AI characters; every call, character, and conversation is simulated and is not a real person. You do not need to create an account to use it.

1. Information We Collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email, phone number, or contacts. The App automatically collects the following data, which is transmitted off your device by the third-party services listed in Section 3:

  • Device and other identifiers — including the Android advertising ID and app-instance identifiers.
  • Approximate location — coarse, city-level location derived from your IP address by our advertising and analytics partners (we do not request GPS/precise location permission).
  • App activity — in-app interactions and events (for example, screens viewed and features used).
  • Purchase history — the status of in-app purchases and subscriptions you make.
  • Crash logs and diagnostics — technical information used to diagnose crashes and improve stability.
Processed on your device only (not collected). The front camera "self-view" shown during a simulated call and any photo you choose to create a custom character are processed locally on your device and are not uploaded to us or any third party. Screen recordings you create are saved on your device and only leave it if you choose to share them via your device's share sheet.

8. Children's Privacy & Child Safety Standards

The App is intended for users aged 18 and over and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the App, contact us and we will take appropriate action.

Child Safety Standards (CSAE)

Tranquil Mind Technology JSC and Idol Mirage ("Idol Mirage: Celeb Prank Call" on Google Play) have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). This section constitutes our published Child Safety Standards.

Prohibited content and conduct. We strictly prohibit, and do not allow anywhere in the App, any content or activity that:

  • depicts, describes, promotes, solicits, or facilitates the sexual abuse or exploitation of minors, in any format — images, video, audio, text, or AI-generated content;
  • sexualizes, or presents in a sexual manner, any person who is or appears to be a minor;
  • involves grooming, sextortion, solicitation, or trafficking of minors, or any attempt to create, request, share, or promote such material.

This prohibition applies both to any input a user provides and to any output generated by the App's AI characters.

Prevention and response. Idol Mirage's characters and dialogue are pre-authored and curated by our team (the App does not host open, user-to-user messaging), which lets us keep content within our standards. In addition:

  • An in-app "Report" control is available on the call and character screens so users can flag inappropriate content (reasons include "inappropriate content", "scam", and "other"); reports are sent to our team for review.
  • The App includes an age gate and parental controls (screen-time limits, quiet hours).
  • We review reports and, where a violation is found, we remove or disable the offending content and may suspend access.
  • If we become aware of apparent CSAM, we will report it to the U.S. National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and/or the appropriate local authorities, and cooperate with law enforcement, as required by applicable law.

We comply with applicable child-safety laws and align these standards with the Google Play Child Safety Standards policy.

Child-safety point of contact. Report child-safety concerns to our child-safety contact at contact@tranquilmind.co.
