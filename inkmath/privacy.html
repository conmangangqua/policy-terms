Privacy Policy

App: InkMath: Math Solver & Graph Effective: October 30, 2026 Last Updated: October 30, 2026

Effective date: October 30, 2026 Operator: Tranquil Mind Technology JSC ("we", "us") Contact: contact@tranquilmind.co

This Privacy Policy explains how InkMath: Math Solver & Graph (the "App") handles information. By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

Content you create on your device. Your handwritten strokes, typed expressions, solved results, graphs and history are processed and stored locally on your device (local storage). Handwriting recognition uses Google ML Kit Digital Ink Recognition, which runs on-device; the recognition model may be downloaded to your device by Google Play services the first time it is needed. We do not run an account system and do not upload your math notes to our servers.

Information collected automatically by third-party SDKs. The App integrates:

  • Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics (Google): app usage events, crash reports, device model, OS version, app version, and app instance identifiers.
  • Firebase Remote Config (Google): fetches app configuration values.
  • Google AdMob (google_mobile_ads) with mediation adapters (Meta Audience Network, Pangle, Mintegral, AppLovin, Unity Ads, Bigo Ads): advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID), IP address, device and ad-interaction information used to serve and measure ads.
  • Adjust and the Meta (Facebook) SDK: install attribution, using the Android Advertising ID and device information.

You can reset or opt out of personalized ads by resetting your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).

Local notifications. If you allow notifications, the App schedules reminder notifications locally on your device. These are not sent from a server and do not require any personal data to leave your device.

Permissions the App declares (Android): ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, AD_ID, INTERNET, POST_NOTIFICATIONS, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED

  • INTERNET: connect to the network for Firebase services, ads, attribution and app update checks.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: detect whether the device is online before loading ads and remote configuration.
  • AD_ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID): read the Android Advertising ID for ad serving, measurement and install attribution.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: show reminder notifications if you grant permission; you can revoke it at any time in system settings.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: re-schedule your local reminder notifications after the device restarts.

2. How we use information

  • To provide the App's features (recognize handwriting, solve expressions, draw graphs).
  • To measure usage, diagnose crashes and improve stability and performance.
  • To show and measure advertising and attribute installs.
  • To show reminder notifications you have allowed.
  • To respond to your support requests.

4. Data retention and deletion

Content stored on your device stays there until you delete it or uninstall the App. Data held by third-party providers (analytics, ads, attribution) is retained according to their policies. To request deletion of data associated with you, contact contact@tranquilmind.co.

5. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, LGPD, PIPEDA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, to object to processing, and to withdraw consent. Contact us at contact@tranquilmind.co to exercise these rights. EEA/UK users can also lodge a complaint with their local data protection authority.

6. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Security

8. International transfers

Our service providers may process data in countries other than your own. Where required, appropriate safeguards are applied by those providers.
