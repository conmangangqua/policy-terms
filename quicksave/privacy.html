Privacy Policy

App: QuickSave: Video Downloader Effective: October 30, 2026 Last Updated: October 30, 2026

Effective date: October 30, 2026

QuickSave: Video Downloader (the "App") is operated by Tranquil Mind Technology JSC ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles, how it is used, and the choices you have. Contact: contact@tranquilmind.co.

1. Information we collect

Information you provide or the App processes on your device

  • Links and web pages you open or paste. To download a video, audio file or image, you paste a link or browse a page in the App's built-in browser. The App sends requests to the site hosting that content (and, where needed, to download-resolution services) to fetch the file.
  • Downloaded files and folders. Files you download, the folders you create, and your download history are stored locally on your device (App storage and, where you choose, device storage). We do not upload these files to our servers.
  • Local settings. Language, onboarding state and similar preferences are stored on your device (shared preferences / local database).

Information collected automatically by third-party SDKs

  • Analytics and diagnostics: Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, Firebase Remote Config and Cloud Firestore (Google) collect app-instance identifiers, device model, OS version, app version, crash traces and usage events.
  • Attribution and measurement: AppsFlyer and Adjust collect advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID), install and event data to measure marketing performance and ad revenue.
  • Advertising: the App shows ads through Google AdMob with mediation adapters for Meta Audience Network, AppLovin, Unity Ads, Pangle, Mintegral and BIGO Ads. These providers may collect advertising ID, IP address, device information and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads, and may show personalized ads where permitted.
  • Purchases: where in-app purchases are offered, they are processed by Google Play Billing; we do not receive your payment card details.

Permissions the App declares (Android): INTERNET, READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM, WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE.

  • INTERNET — load the built-in browser, fetch the media you ask to download, load ads, and send analytics/crash data.
  • READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE — on Android 9 (API 28) and lower only (declared with maxSdkVersion="28"), read the media files you have downloaded so the App can list, play, share and organize them.
  • WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE — on Android 9 (API 28) and lower only (declared with maxSdkVersion="28"), save the videos, audio and images you download to device storage.
  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM — declared for the local notification component used for notifications and reminders from the App; it is not used to collect any data.

The App does not request access to your contacts, location, microphone, SMS or call logs. The App may also ask for the camera, photo-library or notification permission at runtime through the system permission dialog; you can decline or revoke these in system settings.

2. How we use information

  • To provide the download, playback, file-management and sharing features.
  • To keep the App working, diagnose crashes and improve performance.
  • To show ads and measure advertising and install attribution.
  • To comply with legal obligations and protect against abuse.

4. Consent and advertising choices

Where required (for example EEA/UK), the App shows a consent form (Google User Messaging Platform / IAB TCF) before personalized ads. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in Android Settings → Google → Ads.

5. Sharing and retention

We do not sell your personal information. Data is shared only with the service providers above. Analytics and diagnostics data is retained for as long as needed for those purposes under the providers' retention settings. Downloaded files stay on your device until you delete them or uninstall the App.

6. Your rights

Depending on your location (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, LGPD, PIPEDA and similar laws) you may request access, correction, deletion or restriction of your data, or object to processing. Email contact@tranquilmind.co. You can delete downloaded files inside the App or by uninstalling it.

7. Content you download

The App is a tool to save content that you are legally allowed to download. You are responsible for respecting the copyright and terms of the sites you use. We do not host the content.

8. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security and international transfers
