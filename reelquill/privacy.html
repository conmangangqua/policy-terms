Privacy Policy

App: Reelquill Effective: 2026-10-28 Last Updated: 2026-10-28

Reelquill ("the App", "we", "us", "our"), operated by Tranquil Mind Technology JSC, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices and rights you have. By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

Reelquill is a vertical short-drama streaming app: you swipe through series whose episodes are short. You do not need to create an account — there is no sign-up, login, or profile, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or contacts.

1. Information we collect

The App collects the following automatically, through the third-party services listed in Section 3 and through our own streaming service:

  • Device and advertising identifiers — the Android Advertising ID (the App declares the com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID permission), the Firebase installation/app-instance identifier, and an anonymous purchase identifier generated by our subscription provider. Used for advertising, analytics, install attribution, purchase validation, and fraud prevention.
  • Approximate location — derived by our advertising, analytics, and attribution providers from your IP address (country/region level, not GPS). The App does not request device location permission.
  • App activity — in-app events such as screens viewed, series and episodes opened, playback events, ads viewed, rewards earned, and paywall or purchase events. Used for analytics, install/activation attribution, and advertising measurement.
  • Crash logs and diagnostics — crash reports, stack traces, device model, operating-system version, and app version.
  • Purchase information — the status and type of any premium (VIP) subscription and of any coin-pack purchase, processed through Google Play Billing and our subscription provider (RevenueCat). We do not receive or store your payment card details.
  • App and device integrity signals — Firebase App Check (Google Play Integrity) attests that requests to our streaming service come from a genuine, unmodified copy of the App installed from Google Play; the attestation token is sent with each request. Our anti-tamper component (Talsec freeRASP) also checks whether the App has been modified or runs under a debugger, hooking framework, or rooted device, and reports these signals to its provider and to our analytics.
  • Install referrer — on first launch the App reads the Google Play Install Referrer. If it carries an invite code, we use it to grant a welcome coin bonus to the invited user on that device. The code is handled on the device; there is no account or server-side invite record.
  • Network connection state — the App checks whether a network connection is available (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) so it can show an offline notice. This check stays on your device.
  • Streaming service request data — when you browse the catalogue or play an episode, your device sends requests to our streaming service at epsdrm.com and to the content delivery networks that host the video files. These include your IP address, the series/episode identifier, the language code, the App Check token, and standard technical request data (user agent, timestamps), processed to deliver content and kept in server logs for security and troubleshooting.

Stored only on your device (not collected by us): continue-watching progress, saved/liked series, coin balance, daily check-in and task progress, language and playback preferences, your ad-consent choices (standard IAB TCF format), and any ad-free window earned from a rewarded ad. These are removed when you clear the App's data or uninstall it.

Daily reminder notification (optional). The App declares the Android POST_NOTIFICATIONS permission. It is requested only when you switch on the daily check-in reminder in your profile. The reminder is scheduled locally on your device; no data is sent to us to deliver it. You can turn it off in the App or in system settings. The App also includes a local-notification component (WorkManager based) used to bring users back to the App; it likewise runs on the device.

Permissions the App declares (Android): INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID, POST_NOTIFICATIONS. The App does not request camera, microphone, contacts, calendar, location, or file/photo access.

2. How we use information

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the App and deliver the series and episodes you request.
  • Remember your progress, saved series, coins, and preferences on your device.
  • Serve and measure advertising, including personalized advertising where you have consented, and grant rewards earned from rewarded ads.
  • Measure marketing campaigns (install and activation attribution).
  • Analyze usage to improve performance, content, and features.
  • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, piracy, tampering, and security issues.
  • Process and validate premium subscriptions and coin purchases, and restore them for you.
  • Send the daily reminder, if you opt in.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information for money. We share information with the providers listed in Section 3 to operate the App, deliver content, and serve advertising; for legal reasons; and in connection with a business transfer.

U.S. state privacy notice. Because the App serves personalized advertising, sharing advertising and device identifiers with ad partners may be considered a "sale" or "sharing" (for targeted advertising) under certain U.S. state laws. You can opt out using the ad-consent controls and your device's advertising-ID settings.

5. Legal bases (EEA/UK — GDPR)

  • Consent — personalized advertising and analytics where required; the optional reminder notification.
  • Legitimate interests — providing and securing the service, preventing fraud and piracy, improving the App.
  • Contract / legal obligation — processing subscriptions and purchases, and meeting legal requirements.

6. Data retention

We keep information only as long as necessary for the purposes described or as required by law. Advertising, attribution, and analytics identifiers are retained according to each partner's retention periods; crash data for a limited period; streaming server logs for a short period. Data stored on your device remains until you clear the App's data or uninstall it.

7. Your rights

  • GDPR (EEA/UK): access, correction, deletion, portability, objection, restriction, withdrawal of consent, and the right to complain to a data protection authority.
  • CCPA/CPRA (California) and other U.S. states: the right to know, delete, and correct, to opt out of sale/sharing and targeted advertising, and to non-discrimination.
  • Other regions (e.g. LGPD, PIPEDA): equivalent rights under applicable law.

To exercise your rights, contact contact@tranquilmind.co. Because the App has no accounts, include your Android Advertising ID and device details (and, for purchase requests, your Google Play order ID) so we can identify the associated data. You can delete on-device data at any time by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling it.

8. Children's privacy

The App is intended for users aged 16 and older and is not directed to children. Some series contain mature dramatic themes. The App has no chat, messaging, comments, profiles, or any way for users to communicate with each other or upload content. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16 (or the applicable minimum age of digital consent). If you believe a child has provided us with information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. International data transfers

Your information may be processed in countries other than your own, including the United States. Where required, we and our providers rely on appropriate safeguards (such as Standard Contractual Clauses).

10. Data security

We use encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS), app-attestation and anti-tampering protections, and other reasonable measures. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

11. Third-party content

The series in Reelquill are supplied through our streaming service and its delivery networks. Advertising is served by the partners listed in Section 3 and may link to third-party websites or stores. We do not control their privacy practices — please review their policies.
