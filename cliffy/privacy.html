Privacy Policy

App: Cliffy Effective: 2026-08-11 Last Updated: 2026-08-11

Cliffy ("the App", "we", "us", "our"), operated by Tranquil Mind Technology JSC, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices and rights you have. By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

Cliffy is a short-form vertical drama streaming app: you browse a catalogue of short series and watch episodes inside the App. You do not need to create an account, register, or sign in to use it — the App has no user profiles, no chat, no comments, and no user-to-user content sharing.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask you for your name, email address, phone number, postal address, photos, contacts, or precise GPS location, and the App requests no device permission other than internet access.

The App and the third-party services listed in Section 3 do collect the following information automatically while you use it:

Device and advertising identifiers

  • Android advertising ID (AAID/GAID) and, where applicable, app-set ID or vendor-generated identifiers used by our advertising and attribution partners.
  • Firebase installation ID (used to deliver Remote Config and to group analytics events).
  • RevenueCat anonymous app user ID (a random identifier generated on your device to keep track of your purchase entitlements; it is not linked to your name or email).

Device and technical information

  • Device model, manufacturer, operating system version, screen and locale settings, app version and build (dev/production), and network connection type.
  • IP address, from which our partners may derive an approximate, coarse location (typically country or city level). We do not collect precise or GPS location.

App activity and usage data

  • Screens viewed, series and episodes opened or played, playback and navigation events, language selection, and other in-app events.
  • Advertising interactions: ad impressions, clicks, rewarded-ad completions used to unlock episodes, and ad-consent choices.
  • Purchase and subscription events (started, completed, restored, failed).
  • The search box filters the catalogue already loaded on your device; your typed search terms are not transmitted to our servers.

Crash logs and diagnostics

  • Crash stack traces, exception messages, device state at the time of a crash, and performance/diagnostic data used to fix defects.

Purchase information

  • Subscription and purchase status, product identifiers, Google Play purchase tokens, and entitlement state. Payments are processed by Google Play — we never receive or store your payment card, bank details, or billing address.

Install and attribution data

  • Install referrer, campaign/attribution data, and ad-network identifiers used to measure which marketing campaign brought you to the App.

App integrity and security signals

  • Results of anti-tampering checks (for example whether the App has been modified, repackaged, run on a rooted device, or attached to a debugging/hooking framework), together with the App's package name and signing certificate fingerprint. These signals are used solely to protect the App and our licensed content from piracy and abuse.

Content requests to our servers

  • When you browse or play content, the App requests the catalogue and episode details from our content API. Those requests contain the series/episode identifiers you asked for, paging and language parameters, and standard network information such as your IP address and user-agent.

2. How we use information

We use the information above only for the following purposes:

  • Provide and operate the App — load the catalogue, stream episodes, remember your language and viewing progress on the device.
  • Advertising — show banner, interstitial, native, app-open, and rewarded ads, including rewarded ads that unlock episodes; measure ad performance and prevent ad fraud.
  • Attribution and measurement — understand which marketing campaign or channel led to an install, and measure the effectiveness of that marketing.
  • Analytics and improvement — understand which series and features are used so we can improve the catalogue and the App.
  • Stability — diagnose crashes and errors and fix them.
  • Purchases — activate, restore, and validate subscriptions and one-time purchases.
  • Security and anti-piracy — detect tampering, repackaging, emulation, and unauthorised access to licensed content.
  • Legal compliance — comply with applicable laws and respond to lawful requests.

We do not use your information to build a profile of you as an identified individual, and we do not carry out automated decision-making that produces legal effects concerning you.

Ad consent (UMP). Where required — in particular in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland — the App presents Google's User Messaging Platform consent form before ads are loaded, so you can choose whether personalized ads and the associated data processing are allowed. Your choice is stored on the device in the IAB TCF format and passed to our ad partners. You can change it later from the consent options offered in the App or by clearing the App's data.

Resetting or opting out of the advertising ID. On Android you can reset your advertising ID, or delete it entirely, in Settings → Google → Ads. Deleting the advertising ID stops apps from receiving it and signals to advertisers that you opt out of personalized advertising. You can also opt out of Google's personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information for money.

We share information only:

  • with the third-party services listed in Section 3, for the purposes described there;
  • with Google Play, to process and validate purchases;
  • when required by law, court order, or a valid request from a public authority;
  • to protect our rights, safety, licensed content, or to investigate fraud, piracy, or abuse;
  • in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, in which case we will notify users of any change in control of their information.

Notice for residents of certain U.S. states. Because the App shows personalized (interest-based) advertising and uses attribution partners, the sharing of device and advertising identifiers with advertising partners may be treated as a "sale" or as "sharing for cross-context behavioural advertising" under laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA, and similar laws in Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, Texas and other states. You may opt out at any time by:

  1. declining personalized ads in the consent form where it is shown; and/or
  2. deleting or resetting your Android advertising ID as described in Section 3; and/or
  3. contacting us at the address in Section 13 with your advertising ID so that we can forward an opt-out request to our partners.

We do not knowingly sell or share the personal information of consumers under 16 years of age.

5. Legal bases (EEA/UK — GDPR)

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on:

  • Consent — for personalized advertising, non-essential analytics, and attribution, collected through the ad-consent (UMP) form. You may withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legitimate interests — for core functionality, non-personalized advertising to fund a free app, crash diagnostics, security, anti-piracy and fraud prevention, and product improvement.
  • Performance of a contract — to deliver the content and the subscriptions or purchases you buy.
  • Legal obligation — to keep records and respond to lawful requests.

6. Data retention

We keep information only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, or as required by law:

  • Analytics and advertising identifiers are retained by our partners according to their own retention schedules (typically 2–14 months for event-level analytics data).
  • Crash and diagnostic reports are retained for a limited period, normally up to 90 days, and then deleted or aggregated.
  • Purchase and entitlement records are retained for as long as the subscription is active and afterwards for the period required by tax, accounting, and consumer-protection law.
  • Content-request server logs are retained for a short operational period for security and abuse prevention.

7. Your rights

If you are in the EEA, the UK, or Switzerland (GDPR): you have the right to access, correct, delete, port, restrict, and object to the processing of your personal data, to withdraw consent at any time, and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

If you are a California resident (CCPA/CPRA): you have the right to know what personal information is collected, used, and disclosed; to delete it; to correct it; to opt out of its sale or sharing and of targeted advertising; to limit the use of sensitive personal information; and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

Other regions: residents of Brazil (LGPD), Canada (PIPEDA), and other jurisdictions with comparable laws have equivalent rights of access, correction, deletion, and objection.

How to exercise your rights. Because the App has no accounts, we cannot identify you from your name or email address. To make a request, email us at the address in Section 13 and include your Android advertising ID (Settings → Google → Ads) together with your device model and the approximate date you installed the App, so that we can locate and delete the associated records with our partners. We will respond within the period required by applicable law (normally 30 days). You may also delete all data stored locally by the App at any time by uninstalling it or clearing its data in Android Settings.

8. Children's privacy

The App is intended for users aged 13 and over (or the higher minimum age of digital consent in your country — for example 16 in parts of the EEA). It is not directed to children, and its content is not designed for or targeted at children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under the applicable local age). Advertising in the App is not directed to children and is not treated as child-directed for the purposes of COPPA or the Google Play Families policy. If you believe a child has provided information to us, contact us at the address in Section 13 and we will delete the associated records.

9. International data transfers

We are based in Vietnam and our third-party partners operate globally, including in the United States and the European Union. Your information may therefore be transferred to, stored in, and processed in countries whose data protection laws differ from those of your country. Where required, such transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or an adequacy decision.

10. Data security

We use encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) for network communication, restrict access to the limited data we hold, and apply anti-tampering measures to the App. However, no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Third-party content

Cliffy streams short drama series that are supplied by content partners and delivered from third-party servers and content delivery networks. Requesting a video from those servers exposes your IP address and standard network information to them. Advertisements shown in the App and any link that opens outside the App (for example the Google Play listing when you rate or share the App) are likewise controlled by third parties. We are not responsible for the content, practices, or privacy policies of those third parties; please review their policies before interacting with them.
