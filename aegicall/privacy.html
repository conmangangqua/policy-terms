Privacy Policy

App: Aegicall Effective: 2026-08-12 Last Updated: 2026-08-12

Aegicall ("the App", "we", "us", "our"), operated by Tranquil Mind Technology JSC, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices and rights you have. By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

Aegicall is a caller ID and spam-blocking app for Android that identifies unknown callers, silences spam and robocalls, and helps you keep track of your calls and messages. You do not need to create an account to use it.

1. Information we collect

We collect only what the App needs to function and to show relevant advertising. We do not upload your contacts, call log, or message content to our servers.

Processed on your device (not sent to us):

  • Contacts (READ_CONTACTS) — matched locally against incoming numbers so we can show a saved name instead of a bare number. Your contact list is never collected, stored on our servers, or shared.
  • Call log & phone state (READ_CALL_LOG, READ_PHONE_STATE, CALL_PHONE) — read on-device to show your recent calls, identify callers, and let you place or screen calls.
  • Notifications (BIND_NOTIFICATION_LISTENER_SERVICE) — the message-tracker feature reads notifications on your own device so you can review messages; their content stays on the device.
  • Call screening (BIND_SCREENING_SERVICE) — used to filter spam/robocalls locally.
  • Your personal spam list and block list, stored locally on the device.

Collected automatically and sent to third parties (analytics/ads):

  • Device & advertising identifiers (including the Android Advertising ID, AD_ID).
  • Approximate, IP-derived location (coarse, from ad/analytics networks).
  • App activity and diagnostics (feature usage, crash logs).
  • Purchase status (whether you hold a subscription/one-time purchase).

2. How we use information

  • Provide and operate the App (caller ID, spam blocking, call and message tracking).
  • Advertising and measurement (to show and measure ads, including personalized ads).
  • Analytics and improvement (understand usage, fix crashes, improve features).
  • Fraud prevention and security.
  • Process purchases and restore entitlements.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information for money. We share data with the third-party services listed in Section 3 for the purposes described, for legal reasons (to comply with law or protect rights), and in a business transfer (merger/acquisition). Because ads may be personalized, some U.S. state laws treat sharing ad identifiers with advertising partners as a "sale"/"sharing"; you can opt out as described in Sections 3 and 7.

5. Legal bases (EEA/UK — GDPR)

  • Consent — personalized ads and analytics where consent is required.
  • Legitimate interests — core functionality, security, and improvement.
  • Contract / legal obligation — processing purchases and meeting legal duties.

6. Data retention

We keep data only as long as necessary for the purposes above or as required by law. Advertising and analytics identifiers are retained according to each partner's retention periods; crash and diagnostic data are kept for a limited period. Data processed on your device (contacts, call log, notifications, spam/block lists) remains under your control and is removed when you clear it or uninstall the App.

7. Your rights

  • GDPR (EEA/UK): access, correct, delete, port, object, restrict, withdraw consent, and lodge a complaint with a data protection authority.
  • CCPA/CPRA (California) and similar U.S. states: know, delete, correct, opt out of sale/sharing and targeted advertising, and non-discrimination for exercising your rights.
  • Other regions: LGPD (Brazil), PIPEDA (Canada), and comparable laws.

To exercise any right, contact us at contact@tranquilmind.co. Because the App has no accounts, please include your Android Advertising ID and device details so we can locate any relevant data held by our partners.

8. Children's privacy

The App is intended for users aged 18 and older and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it. We handle age-of-consent requirements for the EEA and COPPA accordingly.

9. International data transfers

Your data may be processed outside your country, including in the United States. Where required, we and our partners rely on appropriate safeguards (e.g. Standard Contractual Clauses).

10. Data security

We use encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.
