Privacy Policy — PitchSide: Live Football Score

App: PitchSide Effective: October 30, 2026 Last Updated: October 30, 2026

Effective date: October 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Tranquil Mind Technology JSC ("we", "us") handles information when you use the PitchSide: Live Football Score mobile app (the "App"). Contact: contact@tranquilmind.co

1. Information we collect

PitchSide does not require an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location.

Information collected automatically by the App and its SDKs

  • Device and app information: device model, operating system version, app version, language, time zone, and an app instance identifier, used for diagnostics, analytics, and service delivery (Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, Firebase Remote Config, Adjust).
  • Crash and performance data: stack traces and device state at the time of a crash (Firebase Crashlytics).
  • Usage data: screens viewed and features used (Firebase Analytics, Adjust).
  • Advertising data: the App integrates Google AdMob with mediation partners (Meta Audience Network, AppLovin, Unity Ads, Pangle, Mintegral, Bigo). Where ads are shown, these providers may collect the Android advertising ID, IP address, and ad interaction data, subject to your consent choices (including the consent form shown in regions such as the EEA and UK).
  • Purchases: if you buy a subscription or in-app product, the purchase is processed by Google Play. We receive purchase status, not your payment card details.

Information stored on your device

  • Your favourite teams, leagues, notification preferences, and app settings are stored locally (shared preferences and local cache).
  • Match data (scores, fixtures, lineups) is fetched from a third-party football data provider through our server proxy; it does not contain personal data about you.

Permissions the App declares (Android): ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, AD_ID, INTERNET, POST_NOTIFICATIONS, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED, WAKE_LOCK

  • INTERNET: load live scores, fixtures, and lineups, and communicate with analytics, crash reporting, and ad services.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: detect whether the device is online so the App can show an offline state and retry loading data.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: show match reminders and alerts (for example 30 minutes before kickoff and when a match starts). You can allow or deny this at the system prompt and change it at any time in system Settings.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: re-schedule your pending match reminders after the device restarts or the App is updated.
  • WAKE_LOCK: let background tasks (periodic match-data sync and reminder delivery) finish while the device is idle.
  • AD_ID: allow ad and attribution SDKs to read the Android advertising ID for advertising, measurement, and fraud prevention. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads.

2. How we use information

  • To provide live scores, fixtures, lineups, and match reminders.
  • To maintain, secure, and improve the App, and fix crashes.
  • To measure ad performance and install attribution, and to show ads where enabled.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information for money.

4. Your choices

  • Notifications: disable in system Settings at any time.
  • Advertising: reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads; use the consent options offered in the App where available.
  • Analytics/crash data: uninstalling the App stops further collection.

5. Data retention and security

Analytics and crash data are retained by the providers for their standard periods. Local data is deleted when you clear app data or uninstall. We use industry-standard safeguards (HTTPS), but no method is completely secure.

6. Your rights

Depending on your location (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, LGPD, PIPEDA), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or port your data, and to object to or restrict processing. Contact contact@tranquilmind.co and we will respond within the time required by law.

7. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA) and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. International transfers

Our service providers may process data in countries other than yours, with appropriate safeguards where required.

9. Changes

We may update this policy and will post the new version with an updated effective date.
