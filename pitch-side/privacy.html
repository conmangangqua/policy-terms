Privacy Policy

App: PitchSide Effective: July 27, 2026 Last Updated: July 27, 2026

PitchSide ("the App", "we", "us", "our"), operated by Tranquil Mind Technology JSC, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices and rights you have. By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

PitchSide provides live football scores, fixtures, lineups, standings, statistics and a FIFA World Cup 2026 hub, using data from third-party sports-data providers. You do not need to create an account or sign in to use it.

1. Information We Collect

We do not ask you to register, and we do not collect your name, email address, or contacts. The App collects only the limited data listed below, and only data that leaves your device is described here. Choices you make on your device (such as your favorite teams and leagues, selected language, and notification preferences) are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to us.

Data collected automatically by the App and its third-party SDKs:

  • Device and other identifiers — including the advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID / iOS IDFA where permitted), device model, operating-system version, language, and app-instance identifiers, used for analytics, advertising, and fraud/security.
  • Approximate location — a coarse, city/region-level location derived from your IP address by advertising and analytics providers. We do not collect precise GPS location, and the App requests no location permission.
  • App activity — in-app events and interactions (screens viewed, features used, matches/teams/leagues opened, in-app searches) used for analytics and to improve the App.
  • Crash logs and diagnostics — crash traces, error data, and performance/diagnostic information used to keep the App stable.
  • Purchase status — whether you hold an active Premium entitlement (weekly, monthly, or lifetime), used to unlock the ad-free experience. Payments are processed by Google Play; we do not receive or store your card or bank details.

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide and operate the App and its live-score, fixture, standings, and World Cup features.
  • Remember your on-device preferences (favorites, language, notifications).
  • Show and measure advertising (including, where you consent, personalized ads).
  • Perform analytics to understand usage and improve the App.
  • Detect, prevent, and investigate fraud, abuse, and security issues.
  • Process and validate in-app purchases and restore entitlements.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information for money. We share the limited data described above with the third-party services listed in Section 3 so they can perform advertising, analytics, attribution, and payment processing; when required by law, regulation, or valid legal process; and in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets (with notice where required).

U.S. state privacy caveat. Because the App can serve personalized ads, our use of the advertising identifier and related data for targeted advertising may be considered a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information under certain U.S. state laws (such as the California CPRA). You can opt out of this by declining the consent prompt, using the ad-personalization controls in your device settings, and/or contacting us as described in Section 7.

5. Legal Bases (EEA/UK — GDPR)

  • Consent — personalized advertising and, where required, analytics.
  • Legitimate interests — core functionality, security and fraud prevention, non-personalized measurement, and improving the App.
  • Contract / legal obligation — processing and validating in-app purchases and meeting legal requirements.

You may withdraw consent at any time; withdrawal does not affect processing carried out before withdrawal.

6. Data Retention

We and our partners keep data only as long as necessary for the purposes above or as required by law. Advertising and analytics identifiers are retained according to each partner's retention schedule (see their policies in Section 3); crash and diagnostic data are retained for a limited period to resolve stability issues.

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or port your data; to object to or restrict certain processing; to withdraw consent; to opt out of the sale/sharing of personal information and of targeted advertising; and to lodge a complaint with your data-protection authority.

  • EEA/UK (GDPR/UK GDPR): access, rectification, erasure, portability, restriction, objection, withdraw consent, complain to a supervisory authority.
  • California (CCPA/CPRA): know, delete, correct, opt out of sale/sharing and targeted ads, and non-discrimination for exercising your rights.
  • Other regions (e.g. Brazil LGPD, Canada PIPEDA): comparable rights under local law.

To exercise a right, email us at contact@tranquilmind.co. Because the App has no accounts, please include your advertising identifier (and device model/OS) in your request so we can identify the relevant data. We may need to verify your request before acting on it.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for a general audience with a minimum age of 13 and is not directed to children under 13 (or the higher minimum age of digital consent in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under that age. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us at contact@tranquilmind.co and we will delete it. We use non-personalized ad settings and consent controls consistent with Google Play's Families requirements where applicable.

9. International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed in countries other than your own, including the United States, where our service providers operate. Where required, we and our partners rely on appropriate safeguards (such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses) for these transfers.

10. Data Security

We use encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

This content belongs to its respective owners, and PitchSide is not responsible for its accuracy or availability. Please review the relevant third parties' policies.
