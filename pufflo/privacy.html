Privacy Policy

App: Pufflo Effective: July 28, 2026 Last Updated: July 28, 2026

Pufflo ("the Game", "the App", "we", "us", "our"), operated by Tranquil Mind Technology JSC, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices and rights you have. By playing the Game, you agree to this Policy.

Pufflo is a relaxing cartoon bubble-shooter: you pop ocean bubbles with a cheerful pufferfish and rescue cute sea creatures across levels. It is a single-player, offline-friendly game. You do not create an account, and we do not ask for your name, email, phone number, or contact details to play.

The short version. Pufflo shows no advertising, has no in-app purchases or real-money payments, does not use an advertising ID, and does not collect precise location. Your progress, coins, and settings stay on your device. The only data that leaves your device is anonymous crash and diagnostic information used to keep the Game stable.

1. Information We Collect

We do not ask you to register, and we do not collect names, email addresses, or contact details.

1.1 Information collected automatically (crash diagnostics)

To keep the Game stable, our crash-reporting provider (Google Firebase Crashlytics — see Section 3) collects limited technical information only when the Game encounters an error or crashes:

  • Crash logs and stack traces — the technical details of a crash or error.
  • Device and app state — device model, operating-system version, app version, language, available memory/storage, and whether the device is rooted, captured at the time of the crash.
  • A Crashlytics installation identifier — an anonymous, app-specific identifier used to group crash reports. It is not your advertising ID and is not used for advertising.

Crash reporting is only active in the published (release) version of the Game. We do not collect your name, email, precise location, contacts, photos, or microphone or camera content.

1.2 Information that stays on your device

The following are stored on your device only and are not uploaded to us or any third party:

  • Game progress and scores — levels completed, best scores, and rescued-creature collection progress.
  • In-game currency and items — your earned coins, unlocked skins, and boosters (bombs, rainbow bubbles). These are virtual items with no monetary value.
  • Preferences — your chosen language, sound and music settings, haptics, and onboarding status.

This data is kept locally using on-device storage and is removed when you clear the Game's data or uninstall it.

2. How We Use Information

We use the limited information described above to:

  • provide, operate, and maintain the Game and its features;
  • save your progress, coins, and settings on your device;
  • detect, diagnose, and fix crashes, bugs, and stability issues;
  • keep the Game secure and prevent misuse;
  • comply with legal obligations.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not build advertising profiles.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising. We share the limited crash-diagnostic data described above only with:

  • Google Firebase Crashlytics, our crash-reporting provider, to diagnose and fix problems;
  • authorities or others where required by law, or to protect our rights and users' safety;
  • a successor entity in a merger, acquisition, or asset transfer.

5. Legal Bases (EEA/UK — GDPR)

  • Legitimate interests — to keep the Game stable and secure through crash reporting and diagnostics, and to operate and improve the Game.
  • Legal obligation — to comply with applicable law.

Because the Game has no account and does not collect directly identifying information, most crash data is not linked to your identity.

6. Data Retention

Crash and diagnostic reports are retained by our provider for a limited period (generally up to 90 days for detailed reports) and then deleted or aggregated. Data stored locally on your device remains until you clear the Game's data or uninstall the Game.

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • GDPR (EEA/UK): access, rectify, erase, restrict, or object to processing, and to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.
  • CCPA/CPRA (California): know, delete, and correct personal information, and non-discrimination for exercising your rights. Note that we do not sell or "share" personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • Other regions (e.g. LGPD in Brazil, PIPEDA in Canada): comparable rights under local law.

Because the Game has no account and does not collect directly identifying information, we may be unable to associate crash data with a specific individual. You can remove all locally stored data at any time by clearing the Game's data or uninstalling it. To make a request, contact us at the email in Section 12.

8. Children & Families

Pufflo is a family-friendly cartoon game that may appeal to children. We take children's privacy seriously and have designed the Game to minimize data collection:

  • the Game shows no advertising and serves no third-party ads;
  • the Game does not use the Android Advertising ID (AD_ID) and does not perform ad targeting or personalized advertising to anyone, including children;
  • the Game has no in-app purchases and no real-money payments — all coins and items are earned in-game and have no monetary value;
  • the Game does not collect names, email addresses, precise location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera content;
  • game progress and settings are stored on the device.

The only data transmitted off the device is anonymous crash and diagnostic information used solely to keep the Game stable, which is not used to profile or advertise to any user. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond this. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information that we should not hold, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it. We handle applicable children's-privacy requirements (e.g. COPPA in the United States and the GDPR age of consent) accordingly, and we follow the Google Play Families Policy where it applies to the Game's distribution.

9. International Data Transfers

Crash-diagnostic data may be processed in countries other than your own, including the United States, where our service provider operates. Where required, transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards (e.g. Standard Contractual Clauses).

10. Data Security

We use encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and other reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. Material changes will be posted here with a new "Last updated" date. Your continued use of the Game after changes take effect constitutes acceptance.
