Privacy Policy

App: Whispr: Caller ID & Spam Block Effective: October 30, 2026 Last Updated: October 30, 2026

Effective date: October 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Tranquil Mind Technology JSC ("we", "us") handles information when you use the Whispr: Caller ID & Spam Block Android app (the "App"). Questions: contact@tranquilmind.co.

1. Information we collect

Data that stays on your device. The App's core features run locally:

  • Contacts. With your permission, the App reads your address book to show the Contact tab and to recognise who is calling. Contact data is not uploaded to our servers.
  • Spam and block lists. Phone numbers you add to the spam list or block list are stored in the App's private storage on your device and are used by the call-screening service to silence or reject matching incoming calls.
  • Notification content. If you enable notification access, the App reads notifications posted by messaging apps (Facebook Messenger, Instagram, WhatsApp, Telegram) so it can show you messages you have not opened yet. Those messages are stored only on your device and you can delete them in the App.
  • Local settings. Language, theme and reminder preferences are stored on your device.

Data processed by third-party services. The App contains Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config), Google AdMob with ad-mediation partners (Meta Audience Network, Pangle, Mintegral, AppLovin, Liftoff/Vungle, InMobi, DT Exchange/Fyber, Unity Ads, Bigo Ads), the Meta (Facebook) SDK and the Adjust SDK. These services may collect your advertising ID, device and app-version information, approximate region, IP address, app-usage events and crash diagnostics, to measure usage, fix crashes, serve and measure ads, and attribute installs. We do not send your contacts, your spam/block numbers or your notification content to these services. Where required, a consent prompt (Google UMP / IAB TCF) lets you choose your ad-privacy options.

Permissions the App declares (Android): android.permission.INTERNET, com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID, android.permission.READ_CONTACTS, android.permission.CALL_PHONE, android.permission.REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS, android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS, android.permission.RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED, android.permission.BIND_NOTIFICATION_LISTENER_SERVICE

  • INTERNET — connect to Firebase, ad and attribution services.
  • AD_ID — let Google and our ad/attribution partners read the advertising ID for ads measurement and install attribution. You can reset or delete it in Android settings.
  • READ_CONTACTS — read your address book so the App can list your contacts and recognise callers. Contacts are used on-device only and are not uploaded.
  • CALL_PHONE — place a call directly when you tap a number or contact inside the App. The App does not place calls without your action.
  • REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS — declared so the App can ask you to exempt it from battery optimisation, helping the notification listener and call-screening features keep working in the background. It is a system prompt you can decline.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — show local notifications and daily reminders from the App.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED — reschedule the App's local notifications after the device restarts.
  • BIND_NOTIFICATION_LISTENER_SERVICE — lets the Android system bind the App's notification listener, used only after you grant notification access, to show unseen messages from supported messaging apps.

The App also registers a call-screening service (Android 10+), which works only if you set Whispr as your caller-ID and spam app. It compares the incoming number with your on-device spam/block lists. The App does not request call-log, SMS or phone-state permissions.

2. How we use information

To provide caller recognition, spam silencing and blocking, unseen-message tracking and direct calling; to show ads; to measure usage and fix crashes; and to comply with law.

3. Sharing

We do not sell your contacts, call numbers or notification content. Third-party SDK providers listed above receive only the technical, advertising and diagnostic data described in Section 1, and act under their own privacy policies.

4. Retention and deletion

Data held on your device remains until you delete it in the App, clear the App's storage or uninstall the App. You can revoke Contacts, Phone, Notification and notification-access permissions at any time in Android settings. For data held by third-party SDKs, contact us at contact@tranquilmind.co and we will help with deletion requests where applicable.

5. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including the EEA/UK under GDPR, California under CCPA/CPRA, Brazil under LGPD and Canada under PIPEDA), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, to opt out of personalised advertising, and to withdraw consent. Contact contact@tranquilmind.co to exercise them.

6. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will act on it.

7. Security

Local data is kept in the App's private storage; data sent to our service providers travels over HTTPS. No method is completely secure.

8. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the effective date above. Continued use after an update means you accept it.
