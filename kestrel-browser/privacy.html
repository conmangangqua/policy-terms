Privacy Policy

App: Kestrel Browser Effective: 2026-10-30 Last Updated: 2026-10-30

Kestrel Browser ("the App", "we", "us", "our"), operated by Tranquil Mind Technology JSC, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices and rights you have. By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

Kestrel Browser is a private, lightweight mobile web browser with built-in ad and pop-up blocking. You do not need to create an account to use it.

1. Information we collect

We collect only the limited data described below. Your browsing data — the pages you visit, your browsing history, tabs, bookmarks, and in-browser searches — is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to us.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and app identifiers — your device's advertising ID (resettable) and app-instance identifiers (e.g. a Firebase installation ID), device model, operating system version, language, and app version.
  • Approximate location — a coarse, IP-address-derived location (country/city level) inferred by our advertising and analytics partners. We do not request or access precise (GPS) location.
  • App activity — in-app interactions such as screens viewed, features used (e.g. turning Ad Block on/off), and ad interactions. This does not include the web pages you visit.
  • Crash logs and diagnostics — crash reports, performance data, and diagnostic information used to keep the App stable.
  • Purchase status — whether you hold an active subscription, so we can remove ads and unlock premium features. Payments are processed entirely by Google Play; we never receive your card or bank details.

Information you provide

The App has no accounts, forms, or profiles. If you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to send.

Permissions the App declares (Android): ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, AD_ID, INTERNET, POST_NOTIFICATIONS, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED

  • INTERNET — load the web pages you visit and let the App reach its advertising, analytics, and subscription services.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — check whether the device is online so the App can handle connectivity changes gracefully.
  • AD_ID — let our advertising and attribution partners read your resettable advertising ID (see Section 3); you can reset it or opt out in device settings.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — show notifications (e.g. occasional re-engagement reminders) on Android 13+; the App asks for your permission first, and you can decline or turn notifications off at any time in system settings.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED — re-schedule the App's pending local notifications after the device restarts.

2. How we use information

  • Provide and operate the App (including remembering your settings and premium entitlement);
  • Show and measure advertising in the free version;
  • Understand how the App is used and improve it (analytics);
  • Detect, diagnose, and fix crashes and technical issues;
  • Attribute app installs to advertising campaigns;
  • Prevent fraud and abuse, and comply with legal obligations.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information for money. We share the data described above only:

  • with the third-party service providers listed in Section 3, to provide advertising, analytics, attribution, and payments;
  • when required by law, legal process, or to protect rights, safety, and security;
  • as part of a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, in which case this Policy will continue to apply.

U.S. state privacy laws. Some U.S. states (e.g. California) define the disclosure of identifiers to advertising partners for personalized ads as a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information. You can opt out of this by disabling ads personalization or resetting your advertising ID in your device settings, by declining personalized-ads consent where the consent form is shown, or by upgrading to the ad-free subscription.

5. Legal bases (EEA/UK — GDPR)

  • Consent — personalized advertising (via the consent form) and, where required, analytics;
  • Legitimate interests — operating, securing, and improving the App, measuring campaigns, and showing non-personalized ads;
  • Contract — providing your subscription;
  • Legal obligation — where processing is required by law.

6. Data retention

We keep data only as long as necessary for the purposes above or as required by law. Advertising and analytics partners retain identifiers for the limited periods described in their policies (e.g. Google Analytics for Firebase retains certain identifier-linked data for up to 14 months). Crash and diagnostic data is retained for a limited period needed for troubleshooting.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or port your data; to object to or restrict processing; to withdraw consent; and to lodge a complaint with your data-protection authority (GDPR/UK GDPR). California and other U.S.-state residents may have rights to know, delete, correct, and opt out of "sale"/"sharing" and targeted advertising, without discrimination. Residents of other regions (e.g. Brazil — LGPD, Canada — PIPEDA) have similar rights under local law.

To exercise any right, email contact@tranquilmind.co. Because the App has no accounts, please include your advertising ID and device model in your request so we and our partners can locate data associated with your device.

8. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction, e.g. 16 in parts of the EEA), and it provides open access to the web. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us at contact@tranquilmind.co and we will delete it.

9. International data transfers

The data described above may be processed on servers outside your country, including in the United States. Where required, transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses implemented by our service providers.

10. Data security

Data transmitted by the App is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect it. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

11. Third-party websites and content

The App is a web browser: the websites you visit are operated by third parties that may collect data under their own policies. This Policy does not cover those websites — please review their privacy policies. The App's ad- and pop-up-blocking features may limit, but cannot fully prevent, third-party tracking on the sites you visit.

12. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. Material changes will be posted here with a new "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes take effect means you accept the updated Policy.
