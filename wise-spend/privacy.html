Privacy Policy

App: WiseSpend – Money Manager Effective: July 28, 2026 Last Updated: July 28, 2026

WiseSpend – Money Manager ("the App", "we", "us", "our"), operated by Tranquil Mind Technology JSC, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices and rights you have. By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

WiseSpend is a personal money manager that lets you track expenses and income, set budgets, and monitor your net worth and savings. You do not need to create an account to use it, and the App does not connect to your bank or any financial institution.

Your financial data stays on your device. Your transactions, account balances, budgets, categories, notes, and net-worth records are stored locally on your device only. We do not upload them to our servers, we do not back them up to the cloud, and we have no access to them.

1. Information We Collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, or contact details to use the App.

1.1 Information collected automatically

When you use the App, our third-party providers (see Section 3) may collect:

  • Device and advertising identifiers — including the Android Advertising ID and other device identifiers, used for advertising, attribution, analytics, and fraud prevention.
  • Approximate location — derived from your IP address by advertising and analytics services for coarse ad targeting and measurement. We do not collect precise/GPS location, and the App requests no location permission.
  • App activity and usage data — screens viewed, features used, sessions, and interactions with content and ads.
  • Crash logs and diagnostics — technical information about crashes and performance to keep the App stable.

1.2 Information that stays on your device

The following are processed on your device only and are not uploaded to us or any third party:

  • Financial records. Your transactions (amounts, categories, dates, notes), income, accounts and balances, budgets, and net-worth entries are stored locally on your device (using on-device storage) and are not transmitted to our servers.
  • App preferences. Your settings, chosen currency, language, and display options are stored locally on your device.
No bank connection. WiseSpend does not link to bank accounts, credit cards, or payment providers, and it does not use aggregation services (such as Plaid). All amounts are entered manually by you and remain on your device.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information described above to:

  • provide, operate, and maintain the App and its features;
  • remember your settings and personalize your experience;
  • display and measure advertising (including personalized ads where you consent);
  • perform analytics and attribution to understand usage, measure marketing, and improve the App;
  • detect, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, and security issues;
  • comply with legal obligations.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information for money, and we never share your financial records. We share the limited information described above with:

  • the third-party services listed in Section 3, for the purposes described;
  • advertising and attribution partners who serve, measure, and attribute ads; for personalized ads, device/advertising identifiers, approximate location, and app-activity data are shared with these partners for their advertising purposes;
  • authorities or others where required by law, or to protect our rights and users' safety;
  • a successor entity in a merger, acquisition, or asset transfer.
U.S. state privacy notice. Because the App serves personalized advertising, some U.S. state laws (e.g. California/CCPA-CPRA) may treat the sharing of identifiers and app-activity data for cross-context behavioral advertising as a "sale" or "sharing." You can opt out by declining ad-personalization consent, resetting your advertising ID, or contacting us (Section 12).

5. Legal Bases (EEA/UK — GDPR)

  • Consent — for personalized advertising and, where applicable, analytics and attribution.
  • Legitimate interests — for core functionality, non-personalized measurement, security, and improving the App.
  • Legal obligation — to comply with applicable law.

You may withdraw consent at any time (see Sections 3 and 7).

6. Data Retention

We and our providers keep data only as long as necessary for the purposes above or as required by law. Advertising, attribution, and analytics partners retain identifiers according to their own retention schedules; crash and diagnostic data is kept for a limited period. Data stored locally on your device (including all of your financial records) remains until you delete it in the App or uninstall the App.

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • GDPR (EEA/UK): access, rectify, erase, port, restrict, or object to processing, and withdraw consent; and to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.
  • CCPA/CPRA (California): know, delete, and correct personal information; opt out of "sale"/"sharing" and targeted advertising; and non-discrimination for exercising your rights.
  • Other regions (e.g. LGPD in Brazil, PIPEDA in Canada): comparable rights under local law.

Because the App has no account and your financial data never leaves your device, we may not hold data that identifies you directly. You can delete your on-device data at any time within the App or by uninstalling it. To exercise a request tied to advertising data, contact us at the email in Section 12 and include your Android Advertising ID and device details so partners can locate the relevant data. You can also exercise ad choices directly via the UMP consent screen and your device's advertising-ID controls.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for users aged 18 and older and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the App, contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it. We handle age-of-consent requirements (e.g. under GDPR/COPPA) accordingly.

9. International Data Transfers

Your information may be processed in countries other than your own, including the United States, where our service providers operate. Where required, transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards (e.g. Standard Contractual Clauses).

10. Data Security

We use encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and other reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. Your financial records are kept in the App's private storage on your device. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. Material changes will be posted here with a new "Last updated" date. Your continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance.
