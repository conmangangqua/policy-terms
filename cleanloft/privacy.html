Privacy Policy

App: Cleanloft Effective: 2026-11-24 Last Updated: 2026-11-24

Cleanloft ("the App", "we", "us", "our"), operated by Tranquil Mind Technology JSC, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices and rights you have. By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

Cleanloft is a storage cleaner for Android: it finds junk files, duplicate and similar photos, screenshots and large files on your device, lets you preview them, and moves photos and videos you choose to an in-app recycle bin before anything is removed for good. It also shows storage usage, rarely used and recently active apps, checks whether installed apps are still listed on Google Play, reviews app permissions and install sources, and tests your Wi-Fi connection and download speed. You do not need to create an account to use it.

1. Information we collect

Files and media stay on your device. Cleanloft scans your photos, videos and files locally to find what can be cleaned, compresses photos and videos on the device, and keeps the recycle bin on the device. We do not upload your photos, videos, files or file names to our servers or to anyone else.

We collect only the following, automatically and in aggregate, through Google Firebase:

  • Device and app identifiers — a Firebase app-instance ID, device model, operating system version, app version and language. The App does not use the Android advertising ID.
  • App activity — which screens and features are used (for example, that a scan was started or finished), so we can improve the App.
  • Crash logs and diagnostics — stack traces and the state of the App when it crashed.
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address — country or region level, produced by the analytics service, not by reading your device location.

Installed apps. The App reads the list of apps installed on your device to show app sizes, rarely used apps, recently active apps and the security review. This list is processed on your device and is not sent to our servers. One feature is different: when you open Store Check, the App asks Google Play, for each installed app, whether that app is still listed. To do this it sends the app's package name to play.google.com, in the same way a web browser would. We do not store these requests, and Google receives only the package names and your IP address.

The App does not read your GPS location, contacts, microphone or camera, and it does not show third-party advertising.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We share the data in section 1 only with the service providers above, when the law requires it, or in a business transfer such as a merger or sale of assets.

5. Legal bases (EEA/UK — GDPR)

Legitimate interests (running, securing and improving the App, and fixing crashes); consent where the law requires it for analytics; legal obligation.

6. Data retention

Analytics and crash data are kept by Google Firebase for the periods set in its settings and are then deleted. Store Check, speed test and connectivity requests are not stored by us. Photos and videos moved to the in-app recycle bin stay on your device for 30 days and are then deleted; you can restore or delete them earlier from the Recently deleted screen. Other items you delete (junk, duplicate and large files, screenshots you remove permanently) are deleted immediately.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have the right to access, correct, delete, port, restrict or object to the use of your data, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a data protection authority (GDPR/UK GDPR); to know, delete, correct and opt out of sale or sharing, without discrimination (CCPA/CPRA); and similar rights under LGPD and PIPEDA.

How to request deletion. Email contact@tranquilmind.co with the subject "Cleanloft data deletion". Because the App has no account, include your device model, Android version and the approximate date you installed the App so we can locate the analytics and crash records for your app instance; we will delete them through Firebase's deletion tools and confirm by email. Uninstalling the App or clearing its data also resets the app-instance ID, so no new data is linked to the old one.

8. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the higher age of digital consent in your country) and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. International data transfers

Data processed by Google Firebase, Google Play and Cloudflare may be stored and processed in countries outside your own, including the United States. Where required we rely on safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

10. Data security

Data sent from the App uses encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS). We take reasonable measures to protect it, but no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.
