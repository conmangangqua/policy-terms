Privacy Policy — FluxVPN: Secure VPN Proxy

App: FluxVPN Effective: October 30, 2026 Last Updated: October 30, 2026

Effective date: October 30, 2026 Operator: Tranquil Mind Technology JSC ("we", "us", "our") Contact: contact@tranquilmind.co

This Privacy Policy explains how FluxVPN: Secure VPN Proxy (the "App") handles information when you use it on Android.

1. Information we collect

Information collected automatically

  • Device and app information: device model, operating system version, app version, language and a random app/installation identifier, collected through Firebase and Google AdMob for app functionality, diagnostics, analytics and advertising.
  • Usage and analytics data: screens viewed and feature interactions (Firebase Analytics).
  • Crash and performance data: crash logs and diagnostics (Firebase Crashlytics).
  • Advertising data: advertising ID and ad interaction data used by Google AdMob and the ad mediation partner Meta (gma_mediation_meta) to serve and measure ads. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in system settings.
  • Consent signals: your advertising consent choices (IAB TCF consent string) are stored on your device.
  • Network and VPN connection data: to show connection status and location, the App requests the public IP address and approximate geolocation of the VPN server you connect to from a third-party IP lookup service (ip-api.com), and retrieves lists of public VPN servers (VPN Gate, vpngate.net) and server lists from our own or partner servers. The App does not log the websites you visit or the content of your traffic through the VPN.
  • Push notifications: a Firebase Cloud Messaging token so we can send you notifications.
  • Remote configuration: the App reads settings from Firebase Remote Config and Cloud Firestore.

Information stored on your device

  • App preferences, server history and favorites are stored locally (Hive, SharedPreferences). You can remove them by clearing app data or uninstalling the App.

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, or files through the App. Your device's own GPS location is not used by the App's features (see the location permission below). The map shown in the App displays the location of the VPN server (via Google Maps).

Permissions the App declares (Android): ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE, INTERNET, POST_NOTIFICATIONS, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED, SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM, WAKE_LOCK

  • ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION — declared for location-related features of the map and server-location screens. In the current version, the App's location screens display the location of the VPN server rather than your device, and the code that requests device location is disabled; if the permission is requested, it is optional, used only while the App is open, and your precise device location is not sent to our servers.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — to detect whether your device is online and when connectivity changes, so the App can connect, reconnect or show status.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE — to keep the VPN connection running with an ongoing notification while the App is in the background.
  • INTERNET — to establish the VPN tunnel, load server lists, remote configuration, ads, analytics and crash reports.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — to show the VPN connection notification and optional reminders (for example, a reminder to reconnect to the VPN).
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED — to restore scheduled reminder notifications after the device restarts or the App is updated.
  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM — to schedule reminder notifications at the time you expect; the App checks that this permission is granted before scheduling a reminder.
  • WAKE_LOCK — to keep the device awake long enough to maintain the VPN connection and deliver notifications reliably.

2. How we use information

  • To provide and operate the VPN connection and show server and connection details.
  • To maintain and improve the App, fix crashes and analyze usage.
  • To show and measure advertising, and to respect your consent choices.
  • To send notifications and reminders.
  • To comply with legal obligations and prevent abuse.

We do not sell your personal information.

5. Data retention

6. Your rights and choices

Depending on your region (including GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, LGPD and PIPEDA), you may request access, correction, deletion or restriction of your data, and opt out of personalized advertising. You can revoke notification and location permissions in system settings, reset your advertising ID, or contact us at contact@tranquilmind.co. We will respond within the time required by law.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where applicable), and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.
