Privacy Policy

App: Dramzine Effective: 2026-10-29 Last Updated: 2026-10-29

Dramzine ("the App", "we", "us", "our"), operated by Tranquil Mind Technology JSC, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices and rights you have. By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

Dramzine is a vertical short-drama streaming app. You swipe through series whose episodes run one to two minutes each. You do not need to create an account to use it: there is no sign-up, login, or profile, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or contacts.

1. Information we collect

The App collects the following information automatically, through the third-party services listed in Section 3 and through our streaming service:

  • Device and advertising identifiers. These include the Android Advertising ID (the App declares the com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID permission), the Firebase installation and app-instance identifiers, and an anonymous purchase identifier generated by our billing provider. We use them for advertising, analytics, install attribution, purchase validation, and fraud prevention.
  • Approximate location. Our advertising, analytics, and attribution providers derive your country or region from your IP address. This is not GPS. The App does not request device location permission and does not collect precise location.
  • App activity. This means in-app events and interactions, such as screens viewed, series and episodes opened, playback events, ads viewed, rewards earned, daily check-ins and tasks completed, and paywall or purchase events. We use it for analytics, install and activation attribution, and advertising measurement.
  • In-app search queries. When you search, the words you type are sent to our streaming service so it can return matching series. They are processed to deliver results and may appear in server logs, as described below. They are not linked to your identity or used for advertising.
  • Crash logs and diagnostics. This includes crash reports, stack traces, device model, operating-system version, and app version. We use it to detect and fix stability issues.
  • Purchase information. We receive the fact, type, and status of your purchases: premium (VIP) subscriptions and coin packs. These purchases are processed through Google Play Billing and our subscription provider. We do not receive or store your payment card details.
  • App and device integrity signals. Our anti-tamper component checks whether the App has been modified or repackaged, or is running under a debugger, a hooking framework, an emulator, or a rooted device. It reports these signals, together with device model, OS version, app version, and app signature, to its provider and to our analytics. Firebase App Check (using Google Play Integrity) also verifies that requests come from a genuine copy of the App. These checks protect the licensed video content from unauthorized copying.
  • Streaming service request data. When you browse the catalogue, search, or play an episode, your device requests content from our streaming service at epsdrm.com and from the content delivery networks that host the video files. Those requests include your IP address, the series or episode requested, your search terms (when you search), the language code you selected, and standard technical data (user agent, timestamps). We process this data to deliver content and keep it in server logs for security and troubleshooting.

Stored only on your device (not collected by us). The following stay in local app storage and are removed when you clear the App's data or uninstall it:

  • your continue-watching progress and watch history;
  • the series you save or like;
  • your coin balance, check-in streak, and daily task progress;
  • any ad-free period earned from a rewarded ad;
  • your invite code;
  • your language, subtitle, and playback preferences;
  • your notification-reminder setting;
  • your ad-consent choices, stored in the standard IAB TCF format your device already keeps.

Invite links. If you install the App from a friend's invite link, the App reads the Google Play install referrer on your device to find the invite code and grant welcome coins. The invite code is generated on your device. We do not receive your friend's identity or contact details.

Permissions the App declares (Android). INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID, and POST_NOTIFICATIONS.

  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE checks whether a connection is available, so the App can pause playback and show an offline notice. The check stays on your device.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS is requested only if you turn on the daily check-in reminder. Reminders are scheduled locally on your device.

The App does not request camera, microphone, contacts, calendar, location, or file and photo access.

2. How we use information

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the App, and deliver the series, episodes, and search results you request.
  • Remember your progress, saved series, coins, and preferences on your device.
  • Serve and measure advertising, including personalized advertising where you have consented, and grant the rewards (coins, episode unlocks, or a temporary ad-free period) earned from rewarded ads.
  • Measure the effectiveness of our marketing campaigns through install and activation attribution.
  • Analyze usage to understand and improve performance, content, and features.
  • Detect, prevent, and investigate fraud, abuse, piracy, tampering, and security issues.
  • Process, validate, and restore your purchases (VIP subscriptions and coin packs).
  • Check whether a newer version of the App is available, and prompt you to update when required.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information for money. We share information:

  • with the third-party providers listed in Section 3, to operate the App, deliver content, and serve advertising;
  • for legal reasons, to comply with the law or to protect rights, property, and safety;
  • in connection with a business transfer, such as a merger or acquisition.

U.S. state privacy notice. The App serves personalized advertising. Under certain U.S. state laws, sharing advertising and device identifiers with ad partners may count as a "sale" or as "sharing" for targeted advertising. You can opt out using the ad-consent controls and the advertising-ID settings described above.

5. Legal bases (EEA/UK — GDPR)

  • Consent: personalized advertising, and analytics where consent is required.
  • Legitimate interests: providing and securing the service, preventing fraud and content piracy, and improving the App.
  • Contract and legal obligation: processing and validating purchases, and meeting legal requirements.

6. Data retention

We keep information only as long as necessary for the purposes described, or as required by law:

  • Advertising, attribution, and analytics identifiers are retained according to each partner's retention periods.
  • Crash and diagnostic data are retained for a limited period to diagnose issues.
  • Streaming server logs, including search requests, are kept for a short period for security and troubleshooting.
  • Data stored locally on your device remains until you clear the App's data or uninstall it.

7. Your rights

  • GDPR (EEA/UK): access, correction, deletion, portability, objection, restriction, withdrawal of consent, and the right to complain to a data protection authority.
  • CCPA/CPRA (California) and other U.S. states: the right to know, delete, and correct; to opt out of sale, sharing, and targeted advertising; and to non-discrimination.
  • Other regions (for example, LGPD and PIPEDA): equivalent rights under applicable law.

How to request deletion or exercise your rights. Email us at contact@tranquilmind.co. The App has no accounts, so please include the following so we can identify the associated data:

  • your Android Advertising ID;
  • relevant device details;
  • for purchase-related requests, your Google Play order ID.

You can also delete the data held on your device at any time by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling it.

8. Children's privacy

The App is intended for users aged 16 and older and is not directed to children. Some series contain mature dramatic themes. The App has no chat, messaging, comments, profiles, or any other way for users to communicate with each other or upload content. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16, or under the minimum age of digital consent in your region if that is higher. If you believe a child has provided us with information, contact us and we will delete it.
