Privacy Policy

App: Fast Fetch Effective: 2026-09-25 Last Updated: 2026-09-25

Fast Fetch ("the App", "we", "us", "our"), operated by Tranquil Mind Technology JSC, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices and rights you have. By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

Fast Fetch is a media downloader and file manager that lets you save videos, images, music, and documents from links and websites, play and organize them offline, and browse a catalog of short dramas and films. You do not need to create an account to use it.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email, phone number, or contacts. The App works primarily on your device. The following information is collected automatically when you use the App:

  • Device and advertising identifiers — your device's advertising ID (Android AD_ID permission) and similar identifiers, collected by the advertising, attribution, and analytics services listed in Section 3.
  • App-generated device code (x-device-id) — the App generates a random code the first time it runs, stores it on your device, and sends it in the x-device-id header with every request to our own download service so that service can count and limit requests per device and stop abuse. This code is not your IMEI, Android ID, or advertising ID and is not linked to your identity; it is a random value created by the App. Clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App erases it and a new one is generated.
  • App authentication token — each request to our download service also carries an app-level token (x-api-token) identifying the app itself, not you.
  • Approximate location — a coarse location derived from your IP address by our advertising and analytics partners. We do not collect precise/GPS location and the App requests no location permission.
  • App activity / usage data — in-app events, interactions, and screens viewed, used for analytics and advertising measurement.
  • Crash logs and diagnostics — crash reports, error data, and performance/diagnostic information used to keep the App stable.
  • Device integrity and security signals — the App uses Firebase App Check (Play Integrity) and the freeRASP security library, which check whether the App is genuine and whether the device is rooted/jailbroken, debugged, emulated, or tampered with, and transmit those signals to Google and to Talsec (the freeRASP provider). These checks protect our services from abuse; they do not read your files or personal content.
  • Links you choose to download — when you paste, open, or select a link to save media from, the App sends that link to our download service and, depending on the source, to third-party media-resolution services so they can locate the file and return a downloadable URL. These services receive the link you submit, your IP address, and the device code above. They do not receive any username or password for the source site — the App has no such login.

Stored only on your device (not collected by us): the files you download (videos, images, music, documents), your download history, bookmarks, watch progress, language and app settings, and your media library. These stay in local storage on your device and are not transmitted to us.

We do not collect payment information: the App has no in-app purchases or subscriptions.

2. How we use information

  • Provide and operate the App — core features run on-device; remote configuration (Firebase Remote Config) keeps the App's settings, download endpoints, and access tokens up to date without a new release.
  • Protect our download service — the device code and app token let us apply per-device and per-app rate limits, detect automated abuse, and keep the free service available to real users.
  • Advertising and measurement — to serve ads (which fund the free App) and measure ad performance and install attribution.
  • Analytics and improvement — to understand how the App is used and improve features and stability.
  • Fraud prevention, security and compliance — to detect invalid ad traffic, tampered app copies, and abuse.
  • Legal compliance — to comply with applicable laws and enforce our terms.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information for money. We share the automatically-collected data described above only with the third-party service providers listed in Section 3, so they can perform advertising, analytics, attribution, crash-reporting, and security checks on our behalf or on their own account as ad networks.

Because the App shows personalized advertising, this sharing of device/advertising identifiers and app-activity data with advertising partners may be considered a "sale" or "sharing" (for cross-context behavioral advertising) under certain U.S. state privacy laws. You can opt out via the ad-consent (UMP/ATT) choices above and your device's ad-personalization settings.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to enforce our Terms, protect rights and safety, or in connection with a merger, acquisition, or asset transfer.

5. Legal bases (EEA/UK — GDPR)

  • Consent — for personalized advertising and, where required, analytics. You may withdraw consent at any time via the in-app consent dialog or system settings.
  • Legitimate interests — for core App operation, security, abuse and fraud prevention (including the per-device rate limiting described above), non-personalized advertising, and improvement, balanced against your rights.
  • Legal obligation — where processing is required by law.

6. Data retention

We keep no user database and no user profiles. Our download service stores the app-generated device code only transiently, for the short counting windows needed to apply rate limits, and does not retain a download history tied to you. Our third-party partners retain the data they collect for the periods stated in their own policies (for example, Google Analytics for Firebase retains user-level data for up to 14 months; crash logs are retained for around 90 days). Data processed on your device remains until you clear the App's data or uninstall the App.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or port your data, to object to or restrict processing, to withdraw consent, and to lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority (GDPR/UK GDPR); or to know, delete, correct, and opt out of the "sale"/"sharing" of personal information and targeted advertising without discrimination (CCPA/CPRA and similar U.S. state laws); or equivalent rights under other laws (LGPD, PIPEDA, and others).

To exercise any right, email contact@tranquilmind.co. Because the App has no accounts, please include your Android Advertising ID (Settings → Google → Ads) and device model so we and our partners can locate data associated with your device. You can also act directly on your device: reset your Advertising ID, opt out of ads personalization, use the in-app consent dialog, or clear the App's data — clearing the App's data also erases the app-generated device code described in Section 1.

8. Children's privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and, because it can access and stream mature entertainment content, is designed for users aged 16 and older. It is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under that age. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

9. International data transfers

Our service providers and ad and analytics partners may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States. Where required, transfers are protected by safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or equivalent mechanisms maintained by those partners.

10. Data security

Data transmitted by the App and the SDKs described above is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. Requests to our download service are authenticated with an app token and protected by rate limiting and app-integrity checks. We and our partners apply reasonable technical and organizational measures; however, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

We do not control and are not responsible for third-party content, websites, or their privacy practices; please review their policies. You are responsible for ensuring you have the right to download or save any content you access through the App.
