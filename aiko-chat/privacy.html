Privacy Policy

App: Aiko Chat Effective: July 29, 2026 Last Updated: July 29, 2026

Aiko Chat ("the App", "we", "us", "our"), operated by Tranquil Mind Technology JSC, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices and rights you have. By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

Aiko Chat is an AI companion app where you chat by text or voice with fictional AI characters. You do not need to create an account to use it; your profile, chosen avatar, and preferences are stored on your device.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only data that leaves your device. We do not require registration and do not ask for your real name, email, or contacts.

Data you provide

  • Chat content. The messages you type or speak are sent to our AI service provider to generate responses. (Voice is converted to text on your device by your operating system's speech recognition; we do not upload raw audio recordings.)
  • On-device only (NOT collected by us): your display name, age, gender, interests, and chosen avatar image are stored locally on your device (via image picker and local storage) and are not transmitted to our servers.

Data collected automatically

  • Device & advertising identifiers (e.g. Android Advertising ID, device model, OS version).
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address (city/region level) by advertising partners for ad targeting — not GPS/precise location.
  • App activity / interactions (screens viewed, features used, session data).
  • Crash logs & diagnostics (stability and performance data).
  • Purchase status (whether you hold an active subscription; payment is processed by Google Play — we never receive your card details).

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide and operate the App and generate AI chat responses.
  • Personalize your experience and remember your progress and preferences.
  • Show and measure advertising.
  • Analytics, troubleshooting, and product improvement.
  • Fraud prevention and security.
  • Process and validate purchases.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information for money. We share data with the third-party services listed in Section 3 to operate the App and serve advertising. Because we serve personalized ads, certain U.S. state laws may classify this ad-related data flow as a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information; you may opt out via the ad controls in Section 3 and the rights in Section 7. We may also disclose data for legal reasons or in a business transfer.

5. Legal Bases (EEA/UK — GDPR)

  • Consent — personalized ads and analytics where required.
  • Legitimate interests — core functionality, security, and improvement.
  • Contract / legal obligation — processing purchases and legal compliance.

6. Data Retention

We keep data only as long as necessary for the purposes above or as required by law. Advertising and analytics partners retain identifiers per their own retention periods; crash and diagnostic data is kept for a limited period.

7. Your Rights

  • GDPR (EEA/UK): access, correct, delete, port, object, restrict, withdraw consent, and complain to a Data Protection Authority.
  • CCPA/CPRA (California): know, delete, correct, opt out of sale/sharing and targeted advertising, and non-discrimination.
  • Other regions (LGPD, PIPEDA, etc.): equivalent rights where applicable.

To exercise your rights, email contact@tranquilmind.co. Because the App has no account, please include your advertising ID and device details so we can locate any associated data.

8. Children's Privacy & Child Safety Standards

The App is intended for users 18 years and older and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has used the App, contact us and we will take appropriate action.

Child Safety Standards (CSAE)

Aiko Chat and its developer, Tranquil Mind Technology JSC, have a zero-tolerance policy toward child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM).

Prohibited. We strictly prohibit, across all text, images, audio, video, and AI-generated content:

  • Any CSAM or content that sexualizes, endangers, or exploits minors;
  • Grooming, sextortion, solicitation, or trafficking of minors;
  • Any attempt to create, request, share, promote, or access such material.

These rules apply equally to content you submit and to AI-generated output. Every AI character is an adult fictional persona; requests to sexualize any character presented as a minor are forbidden and blocked.

Prevention & response.

  • Our AI system is configured with content standards that refuse and filter sexual content involving minors.
  • Users can report CSAE concerns or violations at any time by emailing our child-safety contact: contact@tranquilmind.co.
  • On confirming a violation, we remove the offending content, suspend or terminate the user's access, and preserve relevant data.
  • We report apparent CSAM to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and/or the appropriate local authorities, as required by law.

Compliance. We align with the Google Play Child Safety Standards policy and applicable child-safety laws. Child-safety point of contact: contact@tranquilmind.co.
