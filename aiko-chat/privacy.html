Privacy Policy

App: Aiko Chat Effective: 2026-09-04 Last Updated: 2026-09-04

Aiko Chat ("the App", "we", "us", "our"), operated by Tranquil Mind Technology JSC, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices and rights you have. By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

Aiko Chat is an AI companion app where you chat by text or voice with fictional adult AI characters. You do not need to create an account to use it.

1. Information we collect

We collect only the data needed to run the App, show ads, and improve the service. Data that leaves your device includes:

  • Device & advertising identifiers — advertising ID and device/app identifiers used by our advertising and analytics partners.
  • Approximate (IP-derived) location — a coarse, country/region-level location our ad and analytics partners derive from your IP address. We do not collect GPS/precise location.
  • App activity & diagnostics — in-app events, feature usage, and crash logs / performance diagnostics used to keep the App stable and improve it.
  • Chat content you send — the text of messages you type (and text transcribed from your voice) is sent to our chat backend to generate the character's replies. This text is first routed through our own chat service, hosted on Cloudflare Workers and operated by us, which then relays it to a third-party AI model provider (Google Gemini, with DeepSeek as a fallback) that produces the reply. Alongside your message we send the fictional character's name/description, the selected language, and the recent conversation context needed to generate a coherent response.

Data that stays on your device (and is not transmitted to us):

  • Voice input — speech is converted to text on your device using the operating system's speech recognition; we do not upload raw audio recordings.
  • Profile photo / avatar — if you pick an image from your gallery as your avatar, it is stored locally on your device only and is not uploaded to our servers.

2. How we use information

  • Provide and operate the App and its chat features.
  • Generate AI character responses to the messages you send.
  • Show advertising and measure its performance.
  • Analytics, crash reporting, and product improvement.
  • Fraud prevention, safety, and security.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information for money. We share data with the third-party services listed in Section 3 so they can provide advertising, analytics, attribution, and AI chat functionality; for legal reasons; or in a business transfer. Because our ads may be personalized, some U.S. state privacy laws may treat this sharing for targeted advertising as a "sale" or "sharing"; you can opt out via the ad-personalization controls described above.

5. Legal bases (EEA/UK — GDPR)

  • Consent — personalized advertising and, where required, analytics.
  • Legitimate interests — core functionality, security, fraud prevention, and product improvement.
  • Legal obligation — compliance with applicable law.

6. Data retention

We keep data only as long as necessary for the purposes above or as required by law. Advertising, attribution, and analytics identifiers are retained according to each partner's retention periods; crash and diagnostic data are kept for a limited period. Chat message text is processed to produce replies and is not used to build an advertising profile.

7. Your rights

  • GDPR (EEA/UK): access, correct, delete, port, object, restrict, withdraw consent, and lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.
  • CCPA/CPRA (California): know, delete, correct, opt out of the sale/sharing of personal information and targeted advertising, and non-discrimination.
  • Other regions (e.g. LGPD, PIPEDA): equivalent rights under your local law.

Because the App has no user accounts, when you contact us to exercise a right, please include your advertising ID and device details so we can locate any associated data.

8. Children's privacy & Child Safety Standards

The App is intended for users 18 years and older and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has used the App, contact us and we will take appropriate action.

Child Safety Standards (CSAE)

Aiko Chat and its developer, Tranquil Mind Technology JSC, have a zero-tolerance policy toward child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM).

We strictly prohibit, across all text, images, audio, video, and AI-generated content:

  • Any CSAM or content that sexualizes, endangers, or exploits minors;
  • Grooming, sextortion, solicitation, or trafficking of minors;
  • Any attempt to create, request, share, promote, or access such material.

This applies to both user input and AI-generated output. Every AI character is an adult fictional persona; requests to sexualize any character presented as a minor are forbidden and blocked. Our AI system is configured with content standards that refuse and filter sexual content involving minors.

Reporting and response. Users can report CSAE concerns or violations at any time by emailing our child-safety contact: contact@tranquilmind.co, or through the in-app report option available on chat messages. We review reports, remove violating content, and suspend access for violators. We report apparent CSAM to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and/or local authorities as required.

We align with the Google Play Child Safety Standards policy and applicable child-safety laws.
