Privacy Policy

App: Rizzora Effective: 2026-08-28 Last Updated: 2026-08-28

Rizzora ("the App", "we", "us", "our"), operated by Tranquil Mind Technology JSC, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices and rights you have. By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

Rizzora is a pickup-line and conversation-opener app: it offers a curated library of lines, an on-device tester, and an AI "Wingman" that suggests openers and replies. You do not need to create an account to use it, and we do not ask for your name, email, or phone number.

1. Information we collect

1.1 Information you provide. Rizzora has no accounts or sign-up. If you use the AI Wingman, the text you type (topics, tone, notes) and — when you pick a chat screenshot — the message text extracted from that image on your device are sent to our AI provider to generate suggestions. Choose what you share; do not submit sensitive personal information you do not want processed.

1.2 Images you select. When you use the screenshot reader, the image you capture or pick stays on your device. Text is extracted on your device using Google ML Kit Text Recognition; the picture itself is not uploaded. Only the extracted text is sent to the AI provider to craft a reply suggestion.

1.3 Automatically collected information. For stability and configuration we collect crash logs and diagnostics (device model, OS version, app version, crash stack traces) and standard technical identifiers used to deliver remote configuration. We do not use advertising identifiers and we do not serve ads.

1.4 Data stored only on your device. Your favourites, saved lines, settings, language choice, and Love-Tester results are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to us.

2. How we use information

  • Provide and operate the App and its features (library, Love Tester, AI Wingman).
  • Generate AI opener/reply suggestions from the text and inputs you submit.
  • Deliver remote configuration (e.g. feature settings and AI model parameters).
  • Diagnose crashes and improve stability and quality.
  • Send the local daily-line notification you opt into.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell it.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information for money, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. We share the limited data described above only with the processors in Section 3, to run the App's features, and where required by law or in a business transfer.

5. Legal bases (EEA/UK — GDPR)

  • Consent — sending your inputs to the AI provider when you choose to use the Wingman; the local daily notification.
  • Legitimate interests — core functionality, security, crash diagnostics, and improvement.
  • Legal obligation — where the law requires.

6. Data retention

Text you submit to the AI provider is processed to return a suggestion and retained by that provider only per its own policy; we do not build a personal profile from it. Crash and diagnostic data is retained for a limited period for debugging. On-device data remains until you delete it or uninstall the App.

7. Your rights

  • GDPR (EEA/UK): access, rectification, erasure, portability, restriction, objection, withdrawal of consent, and the right to complain to a supervisory authority.
  • CCPA/CPRA (California): know, delete, correct, and opt out of "sale"/"sharing" (we do neither), with non-discrimination.
  • Other regions (LGPD, PIPEDA, etc.): equivalent rights apply.

Because the App has no accounts, contact us at the address below to exercise your rights; include enough detail (e.g. device and approximate time of use) so we can locate any relevant records.

8. Children's privacy & Child Safety Standards

Rizzora is intended for users aged 17 and older and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect data from children under the applicable minimum age; if you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

Child Safety Standards (CSAE)

Rizzora enforces a zero-tolerance policy against child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). This prohibition covers images, video, audio, and text, and applies to both user input and AI-generated output. Strictly forbidden: any content that sexualizes minors; grooming, sextortion, solicitation, or trafficking; and any attempt to create, request, share, or promote such material.

Prevention & response. The AI Wingman is instructed to refuse and not produce sexual content involving minors. If you encounter content or behaviour that violates this standard, report it to us at contact@tranquilmind.co (subject: "Child Safety Report"); reports are reviewed and, where warranted, we remove content and restrict access. Apparent CSAM is reported to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and/or local authorities as required by law.

Note on current controls: Rizzora has no public user-to-user feed or shared profiles; interactions are between you and an AI assistant, and generated content is not published to other users. We align with the Google Play Child Safety Standards policy and applicable child-safety laws. Child-safety point of contact: contact@tranquilmind.co.

9. International data transfers

Your data (e.g. text sent to the AI provider and crash diagnostics) may be processed in countries other than yours, including the United States. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

10. Data security

We use encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and reasonable technical measures. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.
