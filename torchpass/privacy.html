Privacy Policy

App: Torchpass Effective: 2026-10-05 Last Updated: 2026-10-05

Torchpass ("the App", "we", "us", "our"), operated by Tranquil Mind Technology JSC, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices and rights you have. By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

Torchpass is a flashlight utility: a one-tap LED torch with brightness control, a live compass, a camera magnifier, LED flash alerts for incoming calls, SMS and app notifications, a colour screen light, SOS, strobe and disco modes, a Quick Settings tile and an optional home-screen (launcher) mode. You do not need to create an account to use it, and the App does not show ads or offer in-app purchases.

1. Information we collect

Collected automatically and sent to our service providers (Google Firebase):

  • App usage (app interactions) — for example which screens and features are opened, and how often, collected through Firebase Analytics.
  • Crash logs and diagnostics — crash reports, error traces and technical performance data, collected through Firebase Crashlytics.
  • Device and app identifiers — the Firebase app instance / installation identifier and, on Android, the device's advertising ID, which Firebase Analytics uses to count unique installations and measure usage. We use these identifiers for analytics only, never for advertising.
  • Device information — device model, operating system version, language setting and App version.
  • Approximate location — Firebase Analytics derives a coarse location (country or city level) from your IP address for aggregate usage statistics. The App does not request location permission and never accesses your precise (GPS) location.

Installed apps:

  • The App reads the list of apps installed on your device to show them in the flash-alert app picker and in the optional home-screen mode, and to open the app you tap. Because the App is able to read this list, we disclose it as collected data. We use it only to provide those features; we do not upload the list to our servers or sell it.

Processed only on your device (never uploaded):

  • Phone call state (permission READ_PHONE_STATE) — used only to know that the phone is ringing so the LED can flash. We do not read phone numbers, contacts or call history.
  • Notifications (Notification access, only if you enable it) — used only to detect that a notification arrived and which app posted it, so the LED can flash for the apps you selected. Notification content is not read, stored or transmitted.
  • Camera (permission CAMERA, only when you open the Magnifier) — the live camera preview is shown on screen so you can zoom in on small text or objects, and the camera LED can be switched on. A "freeze" frame is kept in memory only while the Magnifier is open. The App does not save, upload or share camera images or video.
  • Motion and magnetic sensors — used only to show the compass heading on screen. Sensor readings are not stored or transmitted.
  • Settings — your flash timing, alert filters, colours, home-screen layout and language are stored locally on your device.
  • Reminder notifications (permission POST_NOTIFICATIONS) — the App may show occasional local reminder notifications. They are scheduled on your device; you can turn them off in your device's notification settings.
  • Uninstall survey answers — if you answer the optional "why are you leaving" questions, your choice is used only on screen and is not sent to us.

Information you choose to send us:

  • Feedback — if you choose "Send feedback", your own email app opens with our address. We receive only what you decide to send (your email address and message) and use it only to reply and improve the App.

The App does not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, videos, message content, call logs or microphone audio.

2. How we use information

  • To provide and operate the App's flashlight, compass, magnifier, flash-alert, screen-light and home-screen features.
  • To understand how the App is used and improve it (analytics).
  • To detect, diagnose and fix crashes and errors (diagnostics).
  • To answer feedback or support requests you send us.
  • To keep the App secure and comply with legal obligations.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not build advertising profiles.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural or targeted advertising. Information is shared only:

  • with the service providers listed above, which process it on our behalf;
  • when required by law, or to protect the rights, property or safety of our users, the public or us;
  • as part of a business transfer (merger, acquisition or sale of assets), subject to this Policy.

5. Legal bases (EEA/UK — GDPR)

  • Legitimate interests — operating the App, keeping it secure, fixing crashes and understanding usage in aggregate.
  • Consent — where local law requires consent for analytics; you can withdraw it at any time by contacting us or uninstalling the App.
  • Legal obligation — where we must keep or disclose information by law.

6. Data retention

Analytics data is kept according to Google Firebase retention settings (up to 14 months) and crash reports for up to 90 days, after which they are deleted or aggregated. Data stored on your device remains until you clear the App's data or uninstall it. Feedback emails are kept only as long as needed to handle your request.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your personal data, to object to or restrict processing, to withdraw consent, and to lodge a complaint with a data-protection authority (GDPR/UK GDPR). California residents have the right to know, delete and correct personal information, to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information (we do neither), and to non-discrimination for exercising these rights (CCPA/CPRA). Similar rights apply under LGPD (Brazil), PIPEDA (Canada) and other laws.

How to request deletion or access: email contact@tranquilmind.co with the subject "Privacy request – Torchpass". Because the App has no accounts, please include your device model, the approximate dates you used the App and, if you can, your device's advertising ID, so we can locate related analytics and crash records. We will respond within the time required by applicable law (normally 30 days). You can also stop all future collection at any time by uninstalling the App.

8. Children's privacy

The App is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the higher age of digital consent in your country, such as 16 in parts of the EEA). If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. International data transfers

Our service providers may process information in countries other than yours, including the United States. Where required, transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

10. Data security

Data sent to our service providers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS), and we take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

11. Third-party apps

In the optional home-screen mode the App can open other apps installed on your device. Those apps are not operated by us and are governed by their own privacy policies.
