Privacy Policy

App: GlucoMate: Blood Sugar & BP Effective: October 30, 2026 Last Updated: October 30, 2026

Effective date: October 30, 2026 Operator: Tranquil Mind Technology JSC ("we", "us") Contact: contact@tranquilmind.co

GlucoMate: Blood Sugar & BP (the "App") lets you log blood sugar, blood pressure and heart rate, view charts, measure heart rate with your phone camera, and set medication reminders. This policy explains what the App handles and why.

1. Information we collect

Health entries you type in. Blood sugar, blood pressure, heart rate readings and medication reminders you create are stored locally on your device (local database). We do not upload them to our servers and we have no account system.

Heart-rate measurement results. When you measure your heart rate, the App reads camera frames on your device to compute a pulse value. Camera images are processed in memory and are not saved or sent anywhere. Only the resulting number is stored locally.

Technical and usage data (automatically). The App uses Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Remote Config (Google) to collect app usage events, crash reports, device model, OS version, app version and similar diagnostics, and to deliver configuration. Crash collection is off in debug builds.

Advertising data. The free App shows ads through Google AdMob with mediation partners, and uses Adjust for attribution. These services may collect your advertising ID, IP address, device information and ad interaction data. Where required (e.g. EEA/UK), we ask for your consent through a consent form before requesting ads, and you can change your choice later where the privacy options are offered.

Permissions the App declares (Android): android.permission.INTERNET, android.permission.CAMERA, android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS, com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID, android.permission.SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM, android.permission.RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED

  • INTERNET — load ads, send analytics/crash reports and fetch remote configuration.
  • CAMERA — measure heart rate: the rear camera and flash (torch) detect the pulse in your fingertip. Requested only when you start a measurement; frames are not stored or transmitted.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — show the medication and measurement reminders you set (Android 13+).
  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM — fire your reminders at the exact time you chose. If you deny it, reminders fall back to approximate timing.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED — restore your scheduled reminders after the device restarts.
  • AD_ID — let ad and attribution partners access the Android advertising ID. You can reset or delete it in Android settings.

2. How we use information

To run the features above, show reminders, keep the App stable (crash analysis), measure performance, attribute installs, and serve and measure ads. We do not sell your health entries; they stay on your device.

4. Retention and deletion

Health entries and reminders remain on your device until you delete them in the App or uninstall it. Uninstalling removes the local data. Analytics and ad data held by third parties follow their own retention periods. To request deletion of data we may hold, contact contact@tranquilmind.co.

5. Your choices and rights

You can deny camera, notification or exact-alarm permissions in Android settings (the related feature will not work), reset your advertising ID, and change ad consent where offered. Depending on your location (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, LGPD, PIPEDA) you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or object to processing; contact us to exercise them.

6. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will act.

7. Security

Data is stored in the app's private storage on your device. Network transfers to the services above use industry-standard encryption. No method is perfectly secure.

8. Medical disclaimer

The App is for personal tracking and information only. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or prevent disease. Camera-based heart-rate readings are estimates. Consult a doctor for medical decisions.

9. Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above will change and material changes will be reflected in the App or on this page.
