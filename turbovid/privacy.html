Privacy Policy — TurboVid - Video Downloader

App: TurboVid Effective: October 30, 2026 Last Updated: October 30, 2026

Effective date: 2026-10-30

TurboVid - Video Downloader ("TurboVid", "the App", "we") is operated by Tranquil Mind Technology JSC ("Tranquil Mind", "we", "us"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles, why, and the choices you have. Contact: contact@tranquilmind.co

1. Information we collect

TurboVid has no user account or sign-in. We do not ask for your name, email address or phone number. The App handles the following information:

  • Downloaded files and local data. Videos, audio and other files you download, plus app settings, download history and favorites, are stored on your device (app storage and the device's Download folder). We do not upload your downloaded files to our servers.
  • Web pages you browse. The App has a built-in web browser (WebView). The pages you open are loaded directly from the websites you visit; those websites may apply their own privacy practices.
  • Device and usage information. Through the third-party SDKs listed in Section 3 we and our partners may receive device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), device model, OS version, app version, language, approximate location derived from IP address, crash reports, and in-app usage events.
  • Advertising and attribution data. Ad networks and attribution services process the advertising ID and ad interaction/revenue events to serve ads and measure campaigns.
  • Purchase information. If you buy a subscription or in-app item, the transaction is processed by Google Play. We receive purchase status, not your payment card details.
  • Notifications. Local reminder notifications are scheduled on your device; no personal data is needed to schedule them.

Permissions the App declares (Android): INTERNET, READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM, WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE

  • INTERNET — load web pages in the built-in browser, download the files you request, load ads, and send analytics, crash and attribution data.
  • READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE — read the videos and files you previously downloaded (and files you pick) from the device's Download folder so the App can list, play, open and share them. Declared for Android 9 (API 28) and lower only.
  • WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE — save the files you download to the device's Download folder. Declared for Android 9 (API 28) and lower only; newer Android versions do not need it.
  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM — deliver daily reminder notifications at the scheduled hour (the App uses exact, allow-while-idle scheduling for these local notifications).

The App does not request access to your photo, video or audio media libraries (READ_MEDIA_* permissions are removed from the build).

2. How we use information

  • To provide the features: browsing, downloading, playing and managing files.
  • To show ads and support the free App.
  • To process purchases and restore entitlements.
  • To measure performance, fix crashes and understand which features are used.
  • To measure advertising campaigns (attribution).
  • To send local reminder notifications.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

4. Advertising ID and consent

Where required (for example in the EEA/UK), we ask for your consent before personalised advertising or tracking. You can reset or opt out of your advertising ID in your device settings (Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads) and change consent choices where the App offers them.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information for money. We share data only with the service providers in Section 3 who process it on our behalf or under their own terms, and when required by law.

6. Data retention and deletion
