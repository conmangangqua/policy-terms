Privacy Policy

App: Fetchly Downloader Effective: July 28, 2026 Last Updated: July 28, 2026

Fetchly Downloader ("the App", "we", "us", "our"), operated by Tranquil Mind Technology JSC, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices and rights you have. By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

Fetchly Downloader is a video and file downloader with a built-in offline player and library: you paste a link and the App saves the video, audio, or file to your device to view offline. You do not need to create an account to use it.

1. Information We Collect

We do not ask you to register or provide personal details such as your name or email to use the App. The App works primarily on your device. We (and our third-party partners) automatically collect certain information needed to serve ads, measure performance, and keep the App working:

  • Device and advertising identifiers — including the Android Advertising ID (AAID), and on iOS the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) where you allow it, plus device model, operating system version, language, and similar technical data.
  • Approximate location — a coarse, city/region-level location derived from your IP address by our advertising partners for ad targeting and measurement. We do not collect precise/GPS location.
  • App activity — how you interact with the App (screens viewed, features used, downloads initiated) and, where applicable, in-app search or link input, used for analytics and advertising.
  • Crash logs and diagnostics — crash reports, error logs, and performance data used to diagnose problems and improve stability.
  • Feedback you choose to provide — if you submit optional feedback (for example, when uninstalling), we receive the text you enter.

Handled on your device only (not collected by us): the videos, audio, images, documents, and files you download or open; files you pick with the file/photo picker; your bookmarks, history, and settings. These are stored locally on your device and are not uploaded to our servers.

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the App and its download and playback features.
  • Serve and measure advertising, including personalized ads where you consent.
  • Analytics and product improvement (understand usage, fix bugs, improve stability).
  • Fraud prevention, security, and abuse/invalid-traffic detection.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms.

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, port, restrict, or object to processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent or opt out of targeted advertising:

  • EEA/UK (GDPR): access, rectification, erasure, portability, restriction, objection, withdrawal of consent, and the right to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.
  • California (CCPA/CPRA): to know, delete, correct, and opt out of the sale/sharing of personal information and targeted advertising, and non-discrimination for exercising these rights.
  • Other regions (e.g., LGPD, PIPEDA): equivalent rights under local law.

Because the App has no user accounts, we may need identifiers to locate any data associated with your device. When contacting us, please include your advertising ID (AAID/IDFA) and device details so we can act on your request. To request deletion, email us at the address in Section 13; you can also limit collection at any time using the advertising-ID and consent controls described above.
