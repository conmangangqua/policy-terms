Privacy Policy

App: Gamarine Effective: 2026-09-29 Last Updated: 2026-09-29

Gamarine ("the App", "we", "us", "our"), operated by Tranquil Mind Technology JSC, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices and rights you have. By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

Gamarine is an Android game hub that lists your installed games, shows live device readings such as memory, processor load and battery temperature, and lets you save display preferences to apply before you play. You do not need to create an account to use it, and we do not ask you to register, log in, or submit any personal details.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask you for your name, address, phone number, or email address, and the App has no sign-up or login. The following information is collected automatically by the App and the third-party services listed in Section 3:

  • Device and advertising identifiers — your Android advertising ID (the App declares the com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID permission), plus app-instance identifiers used by analytics and attribution.
  • Device and technical information — device model, Android version, language, region, screen characteristics, and mobile network or connection type.
  • Approximate location derived from IP address — our advertising and analytics partners infer an approximate area (typically country or city level) from your IP address. The App does not request GPS or any Android location permission and cannot read your precise location.
  • App activity and diagnostics — screens you open, features you use, settings you change inside the App, and whether you have an active subscription. Crash reports and performance diagnostics (stack traces, device state at the time of a crash) are collected by Firebase Crashlytics.
  • Purchase status — whether you hold an active subscription, received from Google Play Billing. We never receive your card number or bank details; all payment data is handled by Google.
  • Marketing attribution data — Adjust and AppsFlyer receive identifiers and install/event signals to determine which campaign or referral led you to install the App.

Information that stays on your device. The list of applications installed on your phone, the games you select, the display and density presets you save, and your language choice are read and stored locally on your device only. To show your game library the App queries Android for launchable apps (declared via the <queries> element) and, with your permission, reads usage statistics (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS) to detect which game is in the foreground so it can apply the preferences you saved for it. Device readings such as memory usage, processor load, battery temperature and network latency are measured on the device and displayed to you. None of this information is transmitted to us or to any third party.

2. How we use information

  • Provide and operate the App, including showing your game library and device readings.
  • Remember the preferences and presets you save on your device.
  • Display advertising and measure its performance.
  • Understand feature usage through aggregated analytics so we can improve the App.
  • Diagnose crashes, errors and performance problems.
  • Deliver configuration and feature updates through Firebase Remote Config.
  • Verify and restore your subscription status, and process purchases through Google Play.
  • Detect fraud, abuse and invalid advertising traffic, and maintain security.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information for money. We share the categories of information described in Section 1 with the third-party services listed in Section 3 so they can perform their functions. We may also disclose information when required by law, legal process or a valid government request, to protect our rights, safety or property, or in connection with a merger, acquisition or transfer of assets, in which case this Policy will continue to apply or you will be notified of any change.

Notice for U.S. state residents. When personalized advertising is enabled, the sharing of identifiers and app-activity information with advertising partners may be treated as a "sale" or "sharing" for targeted advertising under California (CCPA/CPRA) and similar U.S. state laws. You can opt out by declining or withdrawing advertising consent in the App and by turning off ad personalization in your device settings as described in Section 3, or by contacting us at the address in Section 13.

5. Legal bases (EEA and UK — GDPR)

  • Consent — personalized advertising and, where required, analytics and attribution. You may withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legitimate interests — operating and securing the App, non-personalized advertising, aggregated product improvement, and fraud prevention.
  • Performance of a contract — providing the subscription you purchase.
  • Legal obligation — retaining records where law requires.

6. Data retention

We retain information only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this Policy or as required by law. Crash and diagnostic reports are retained for a limited period sufficient to investigate and fix the underlying problem. Advertising, attribution and analytics identifiers are retained by the partners in Section 3 according to their own published retention periods. Preferences you save inside the App remain on your device until you change them, clear the App's data, or uninstall the App.

7. Your rights

EEA/UK (GDPR). You have the right to access, correct, delete, port, object to or restrict processing of your personal data, to withdraw consent at any time, and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

California (CCPA/CPRA). You have the right to know what personal information is collected, to delete it, to correct it, to opt out of its sale or sharing and of targeted advertising, and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

Other regions. Residents of Brazil (LGPD), Canada (PIPEDA) and other jurisdictions have comparable rights under their local law.

To exercise any right, email us at contact@tranquilmind.co. Because the App has no accounts, we cannot identify you from your email address alone — please include your advertising ID and your device model so we can locate and act on the relevant records. We respond within the period required by applicable law.

8. Children's privacy

The App is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under the applicable minimum age in your country, such as 16 in parts of the EEA). If you believe a child has provided information through the App, contact us at contact@tranquilmind.co and we will delete it.

9. International data transfers

The information described in this Policy may be processed in countries other than your own, including the United States, where our service providers operate. Where required, such transfers rely on appropriate safeguards, including the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

10. Data security
