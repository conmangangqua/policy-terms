Privacy Policy

App: Calorie & Fasting Tracker Effective: July 28, 2026 Last Updated: July 28, 2026

Calorie & Fasting Tracker ("the App", "we", "us", "our"), operated by Tranquil Mind Technology JSC, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices and rights you have. By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

Calorie & Fasting Tracker is a nutrition and intermittent-fasting app offering calorie and macro logging, barcode scanning, nutrition-label photo scanning, a water tracker, a fasting timer, and recipe browsing. You do not need to create an account to use it, and the App has no advertising and no in-app purchases.

Your health data stays on your device. Your profile (such as weight, height, age, and goals), food and calorie logs, water intake, and fasting history are stored only on your device. We do not operate accounts or a user database, and this information is never uploaded to us.

1. Information We Collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, or contact details to use the App.

1.1 Information that stays on your device

The following is processed on your device only and is not uploaded to us or any third party:

  • Health and fitness data — your profile (weight, height, age, gender, activity level, and weight/calorie goals), food and calorie logs, macro breakdowns, water-intake records, and fasting sessions and history. This data is saved in local storage on your device.
  • Food and nutrition-label photos. With your permission, the App uses your camera or photo gallery so you can capture or select a photo of a nutrition label or meal. Photos are analyzed entirely on your device using Google ML Kit on-device text recognition to read nutrition text; the photos are not uploaded to us or any server.
  • App preferences — your settings, chosen language, and similar preferences.

You can delete this data at any time by clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App.

1.2 Information collected automatically

When you use the App, our third-party providers (see Section 3) may collect:

  • Device and app-instance identifiers — including identifiers used by Google Analytics for Firebase, used for analytics and to keep the App stable.
  • Approximate location — derived from your IP address (city/region level) by analytics services. We do not collect precise/GPS location, and the App requests no location permission.
  • App activity and usage data — screens viewed, features used, and sessions.
  • Crash logs and diagnostics — technical information about crashes and performance to keep the App stable.

1.3 Information sent when you look up foods

When you search for a food by name or scan a product barcode, the App sends the search term or the barcode number to third-party food and nutrition databases (see Section 3) to return nutrition facts. These requests include only the food name or barcode you look up, together with standard technical request data such as your IP address. They do not include your health logs, your photos, or any identity information. Recipe content is bundled inside the App; only recipe/food images are loaded from third-party image hosts when displayed.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information described above to:

  • provide, operate, and maintain the App and its features;
  • look up nutrition facts for the foods and barcodes you choose to search;
  • remember your settings and personalize your experience;
  • perform analytics to understand usage and improve the App;
  • diagnose crashes and maintain security and stability;
  • comply with legal obligations.
No advertising, no tracking for ads. The App does not display third-party ads, does not use an advertising SDK, and does not collect your Advertising ID for ad targeting or install attribution.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising. Information is shared only:

  • with the third-party services listed in Section 3, strictly for the purposes described (analytics, crash reporting, and the food/barcode lookups you initiate);
  • with authorities or others where required by law, or to protect our rights and users' safety;
  • with a successor entity in a merger, acquisition, or asset transfer, in which case this Policy will continue to apply to previously collected data.

5. Legal Bases (EEA/UK — GDPR)

  • Consent — where required, for analytics.
  • Legitimate interests — for core functionality, food/barcode lookups you request, crash diagnostics, security, and improving the App.
  • Legal obligation — to comply with applicable law.

You may withdraw consent at any time (see Sections 3 and 7).

6. Data Retention

We do not operate our own servers and keep no user database. Our third-party providers retain the data they collect for the periods stated in their own policies (for example, Google Analytics for Firebase retains user-level data for up to 14 months; crash logs are typically kept for around 90 days). Data stored on your device — including your health logs, fasting history, and photos — remains until you clear the App's data or uninstall the App.

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • GDPR (EEA/UK): access, rectify, erase, port, restrict, or object to processing, and withdraw consent; and to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.
  • CCPA/CPRA (California): know, delete, and correct personal information; and non-discrimination for exercising your rights. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • Other regions (e.g. LGPD in Brazil, PIPEDA in Canada): comparable rights under local law.

Because the App has no account and your health data stays on your device, the fastest way to erase your data is to clear the App's data or uninstall the App. To exercise a request tied to analytics or crash data, contact us at the email in Section 12.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for users aged 18 and older and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the App, contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it. We handle age-of-consent requirements (e.g. under GDPR/COPPA) accordingly.

9. International Data Transfers

Your information may be processed in countries other than your own, including the United States, where our service providers operate. Where required, transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards (e.g. Standard Contractual Clauses).

10. Data Security

We use encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and other reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. Material changes will be posted here with a new "Last updated" date. Your continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance.
