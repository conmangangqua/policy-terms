Privacy Policy

App: Turbolt Effective: 2026-09-29 Last Updated: 2026-09-29

Turbolt ("the App", "we", "us", "our"), operated by Tranquil Mind Technology JSC, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices and rights you have. By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

Turbolt is a game hub for Android: it keeps the games you choose in one list and, for each game, quickly applies standard Android settings you could also change yourself (Do Not Disturb, screen brightness, screen timeout, volume). You do not need to create an account to use it, and the App has no account or sign-in system.

1. Information we collect

1.1 Information that stays on your device

The core features of Turbolt work on your device. The following information is read or stored only on your device and is not sent to us or to any third party:

  • List of installed apps. To let you add games, the App reads the name, package name and icon of apps that have a launcher entry on your device (standard Android launcher-intent query). This list is shown to you inside the App and is not uploaded.
  • Your game list, per-game profiles and settings. The games you add, the settings you choose for each game (brightness, screen timeout, volume, Do Not Disturb options), your selected mode, language and preferences are saved in the App's local storage.
  • Session summaries. After a game session the App shows how long you played, how much battery was used and the peak battery temperature. These figures are calculated and stored on your device.
  • Device status readings. The App reads memory (RAM) usage, battery level, charging status and battery temperature, free/used internal storage, and your screen's refresh rates, to display them in the App, in the floating game toolkit and in the home-screen widget.
  • System settings values. The App reads and, when you ask it to, changes screen brightness, auto-brightness, screen timeout, auto-rotate, touch sounds, haptic feedback, ringer/media volume and Do Not Disturb.

The App does not access your contacts, messages, call logs, photos, files, camera, microphone or location. The "starred contacts" option only sets Android's own Do Not Disturb rule; the App never reads who your starred contacts are.

1.2 Information collected automatically (leaves your device)

Through the Google Firebase services listed in Section 3, the following is collected automatically:

  • App activity: screens viewed and basic in-app events (for example, app opens and interactions with reminder notifications).
  • Device and app information: device model, operating-system version, app version, language, and app-instance / Firebase installation identifiers.
  • Approximate location: a coarse, country/region-level location that Google Analytics derives from your IP address. The App does not request location permission.
  • Crash logs and diagnostics: stack traces, device state at the time of a crash and performance data (Crashlytics). Crash collection is enabled only in release builds.

1.3 Network check (ping)

When the floating game toolkit is on, the App measures network latency by opening a brief connection to Google Public DNS (8.8.8.8, port 53) and timing how long it takes. No personal data or content is sent in this check; as with any internet connection, the receiving server necessarily sees your IP address.

1.4 Information you provide

The App has no forms or accounts. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include in your message.

2. How we use information

  • To provide and operate the App's features (your game list, per-game settings, modes, the game toolkit, the widget and the storage overview).
  • To send optional reminder notifications about the App (you can turn notifications off at any time in Android settings).
  • To understand how the App is used and to improve it (analytics).
  • To detect, diagnose and fix crashes and performance problems.
  • To respond to your requests and to comply with legal obligations.

We do not use your information for advertising. The App contains no advertising SDK and shows no ads.

4. How we share information

  • We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • We share information only with the service providers in Section 3, which process it on our behalf to provide their services.
  • We may disclose information if required by law, regulation, legal process or a valid governmental request, or to protect the rights, property or safety of our users, us or others.
  • If we are involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to this Policy.

5. Legal bases (EEA/UK — GDPR)

  • Legitimate interests: operating the App, keeping it secure and stable (crash reporting), and improving it (analytics).
  • Consent: where required by local law (for example, for analytics or notifications); you can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legal obligation: where we must process information to comply with the law.

6. Data retention

  • Data stored on your device remains until you delete it in the App, clear the App's data, or uninstall the App.
  • Google Analytics data is retained according to our Firebase retention setting (up to 14 months) and then deleted.
  • Crashlytics crash reports are retained for up to 90 days.
  • Emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to handle your request.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • GDPR / UK GDPR (EEA and UK): access, correct, delete or port your personal data; object to or restrict processing; withdraw consent; and lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.
  • CCPA / CPRA (California) and other U.S. states: know what personal information we collect, request deletion or correction, opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information (we do not sell or share it), and not be discriminated against for exercising your rights.
  • Other regions (for example Brazil's LGPD and Canada's PIPEDA): similar rights of access, correction, deletion and information about sharing.

You can delete all on-device data at any time by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling it. To exercise any right regarding data collected through Firebase, email us at contact@tranquilmind.co. Because the App has no accounts, please include your device model and the approximate date you used the App so we can help locate the relevant data.

8. Children's privacy

The App is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13 (or under the higher minimum age of digital consent in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. International data transfers

Our service providers may process information in countries other than your own, including the United States. Where required, such transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses used by Google.

10. Data security

Data sent to our service providers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). We take reasonable measures to protect information, but no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

11. Device permissions and how they are used

Turbolt asks only for the permissions below, and uses each one only for the feature described. None of them is used to collect or transmit personal data.

PermissionWhat it is used for
Modify system settings (WRITE_SETTINGS)Change screen brightness, auto-brightness, screen timeout, auto-rotate, touch sounds and haptic feedback when you apply a mode or a game profile. You grant it in Android settings.
Do Not Disturb access (ACCESS_NOTIFICATION_POLICY)Turn Do Not Disturb on or off for your game sessions, optionally still allowing calls from starred contacts and repeat callers. The App does not read your contacts.
Display over other apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW)Show the floating game toolkit bubble and the touch-lock layer on top of your game. The touch lock is a transparent layer the App draws on screen; it does not observe or record what you touch or what is on screen.
Foreground service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE)Keep the game toolkit running while you play, only after you switch it on.
Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS)Show the toolkit status notification and optional reminders about the App.
Internet and network state (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE)Firebase services described above and the ping check.
Installed-apps visibility (launcher query)Show the apps on your device so you can pick which ones to add as games.

The App does not use an Accessibility Service, does not request "All files access", and does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts or photo permissions. The Storage cleaner only clears the App's own cache and then opens Android's built-in storage manager; the App never deletes your personal files.
