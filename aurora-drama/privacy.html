Privacy Policy

App: Aurora Drama Effective: July 28, 2026 Last Updated: July 28, 2026

Aurora Drama ("the App", "we", "us", "our"), operated by Tranquil Mind Technology JSC, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices and rights you have. By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

Aurora Drama is an entertainment app that streams short, vertical drama series (romance, CEO/billionaire, mafia, werewolf, and more) in bite-sized episodes. You can browse as a guest without creating an account; you may optionally sign in with Google to sync your coins, purchases, and watch progress across devices.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the data needed to run, secure, and improve the App. Most of it is collected automatically by the third-party services listed in Section 3.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and advertising identifiers — including the Android Advertising ID and app-instance identifiers, used for analytics, attribution, and advertising.
  • Approximate location — derived from your IP address by our advertising and analytics partners. We do not collect precise/GPS location.
  • App activity — how you interact with the App (screens viewed, dramas and episodes watched, in-app search terms, features used).
  • Crash logs and diagnostics — technical data about crashes, performance, and errors so we can fix problems.
  • Purchase status — records of your in-app purchases and subscription status (validated through Google Play). We do not collect or store your payment card details; payments are handled by Google Play.
  • Push notification token — a device token used to deliver notifications (you can disable notifications in your device settings).
  • Device integrity signals — data used to detect tampering, fraud, and abuse and to protect your account and our service.

Information you provide

  • Account information — if you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and a Google account identifier to create and manage your account. If you use the App as a guest, we assign an anonymous user identifier instead.

We do not collect your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or files.

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide and operate the App and stream drama content.
  • Remember your progress, coins, unlocked episodes, and preferences, and personalize recommendations.
  • Show and measure advertising, including rewarded ads that grant in-app coins.
  • Understand usage through analytics and improve the App.
  • Detect, prevent, and respond to fraud, abuse, security threats, and tampering.
  • Process and validate in-app purchases and subscriptions.
  • Send you service and (where permitted) promotional notifications.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information for money.

We share information with:

  • the third-party service providers listed in Section 3, for the purposes described;
  • advertising partners that use device/advertising identifiers and approximate (IP-based) location to serve and measure ads;
  • authorities or others where required by law, to protect rights and safety, or to prevent fraud and abuse;
  • a successor entity in the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets.
U.S. state privacy caveat. Because we allow personalized advertising, the use of advertising identifiers may be considered "sharing" or "sale" for targeted advertising under certain U.S. state laws (e.g., California CPRA). You may opt out as described in Sections 3 and 7.

5. Legal Bases (EEA/UK — GDPR)

  • Consent — personalized advertising and analytics where consent is required; you may withdraw it at any time.
  • Legitimate interests — core functionality, security, fraud prevention, and improving the App.
  • Contract / legal obligation — processing purchases and meeting legal requirements.

6. Data Retention

We keep personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes above or as required by law. Advertising and analytics identifiers are retained according to our partners' retention periods; crash and diagnostic data are kept for a limited period. Account data is kept while your account is active and deleted on request (see Section 7).

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the following rights:

  • GDPR (EEA/UK): access, correct, delete, port, object to or restrict processing, and withdraw consent; you may also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.
  • CCPA/CPRA (California) and similar U.S. state laws: know, delete, correct, opt out of the "sale"/"sharing" of personal information and targeted advertising, and non-discrimination for exercising your rights.
  • Other regions (e.g. LGPD in Brazil, PIPEDA in Canada): equivalent access, correction, and deletion rights.

How to exercise your rights. Contact us at contact@tranquilmind.co.

  • If you have an account, we will verify your identity through your signed-in account. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at this email.
  • If you use the App as a guest (no account), please include your Android Advertising ID (or device identifier) in your request so we can locate the associated data.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for users aged 16 and over and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under this age (or the higher age of digital consent that may apply in your region). If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us at contact@tranquilmind.co and we will delete it.

9. International Data Transfers

Your information may be processed in countries other than your own, including the United States. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards (such as Standard Contractual Clauses) for these transfers.

10. Data Security

We use encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and other reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Third-Party Content

12. Changes to This Policy
