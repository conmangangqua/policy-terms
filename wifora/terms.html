Wifora is a Wi-Fi analysis toolkit. It lists the wireless networks around you with their signal strength, channel and security type; measures your connection speed, ping and jitter; charts signal strength over time; scans for devices on the local network you are connected to; generates and reads Wi-Fi QR codes; lets you save hotspot locations on a map and download map regions for offline use; helps you manage your phone's hotspot, including an optional timer; and, on rooted devices only, can display the Wi-Fi credentials the operating system has stored.
