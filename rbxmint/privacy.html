Privacy Policy

App: RBXmint Effective: 2026-10-14 Last Updated: 2026-10-14

RBXmint ("the App", "we", "us", "our"), operated by Tranquil Mind Technology JSC, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices and rights you have. By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

RBXmint is an RBX counter, calculator, and reward mini-games app for gaming fans. You do not need to create an account to use it, and the App does not ask for your name, email, or any sign-in.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask you for personal information. The App has no account, no login, and no forms that collect your identity.

Your counters, calculator inputs, mini-game progress, notification preferences, and settings are stored locally on your device (local database and app preferences) and are not uploaded to us.

The App does collect the following automatically, through the third-party advertising, attribution, and analytics services listed in Section 3:

  • Device and other identifiers — the Android advertising ID (the App declares the com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID permission), a Firebase app-instance ID, and vendor-specific identifiers used by the advertising networks, for serving and measuring ads, attribution, and analytics.
  • Approximate location derived from IP address — coarse region/country used to serve region-appropriate ads and for aggregate analytics. The App does not request any location permission and does not collect precise (GPS) location.
  • App activity and ad interactions — screens opened, features used, session counts, and ad events (impressions, clicks, which ad placement was shown).
  • Device and connection information — device model, operating-system version, language, screen metrics, app version, and network connectivity state (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE).
  • Crash logs and performance data — stack traces and device state captured when the App crashes or misbehaves, so we can fix bugs.

The App shows advertising (banner, interstitial, native, and app-open formats). It has no rewarded ads, no in-app purchases or subscriptions, and no user accounts.

The App uses local notifications to remind you about your counters and mini-games. On Android 13+ this requires the POST_NOTIFICATIONS permission, and scheduled reminders use SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM. Notifications are generated on your device — we do not collect a push token and we do not send server push messages for this purpose.

The App does not access your camera, microphone, photos, contacts, call logs, SMS, or files.

2. How we use information

  • Provide and operate the App and remember your local settings and progress.
  • Serve advertising and measure its performance (impressions, clicks, fill rate), including through ad mediation partners.
  • Attribution and marketing measurement — understand which campaign or source brought a user to the App.
  • Understand how the App is used so we can improve features and stability (analytics), and remotely configure features and ad placements (Firebase Remote Config).
  • Detect, diagnose, and fix crashes and performance problems.
  • Protect against invalid traffic, ad fraud, abuse, and security issues, and comply with legal obligations.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information for money. However, when you consent to personalized advertising (or where such consent is not legally required), the identifiers and ad-interaction data described above are shared with the advertising and measurement partners in Section 3 for advertising and analytics purposes. Under some U.S. state privacy laws (e.g. California's CPRA), this kind of sharing for cross-context behavioural advertising may be treated as a "sale" or "sharing" — see Section 7 for how to opt out.

We also disclose data where required for legal reasons (law, legal process, lawful requests) or in connection with a merger, acquisition, or business transfer.

5. Legal bases (EEA/UK — GDPR)

  • Consent — personalized advertising, advertising identifiers, attribution, and analytics identifiers where consent is required. Collected via the UMP/TCF consent message and withdrawable at any time.
  • Legitimate interests — operating the App, serving non-personalized ads, keeping the App stable and secure, preventing ad fraud, and improving the App through aggregate analytics.
  • Legal obligation — where we must retain or disclose data to comply with the law.

6. Data retention

We keep analytics, attribution, and diagnostic data only as long as necessary for the purposes above. The advertising, attribution, and analytics partners in Section 3 apply their own retention periods to the identifiers and event data they process. Data stored locally on your device remains until you clear the App's data or uninstall the App.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • GDPR/UK GDPR (EEA/UK): access, correct, delete, port, object to or restrict processing, and withdraw consent (including advertising consent via the in-app privacy options); you may also complain to your local data-protection authority.
  • CCPA/CPRA (California): know, delete, and correct your personal information, and opt out of the sale/sharing of personal information and of targeted advertising. To opt out, turn off ad personalization in your Android settings (Section 3) and/or contact us at the email in Section 12. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.
  • Other regions (LGPD, PIPEDA, etc.): equivalent rights under your local law.

Because the App has no account, please contact us at the email in Section 12 and include your device details and Android advertising ID so we can locate and delete the associated advertising/analytics data. You can also remove all locally stored data at any time by clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App.

8. Children's privacy

The App is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13 (or the higher minimum age of digital consent in your region). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Advertising in the App is not directed to children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. International data transfers

Your data may be processed in countries other than your own, including the United States, by the services listed in Section 3. Where required, appropriate safeguards (such as Standard Contractual Clauses) apply.

10. Data security

We use encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and apply reasonable technical and organizational measures. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.
