Privacy Policy

App: FameRing Effective: 2026-08-11 Last Updated: 2026-08-11

FameRing ("the App", "we", "us", "our"), operated by Tranquil Mind Technology JSC, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices and rights you have. By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

FameRing is an entertainment app that simulates "video calls" and chats with fictional AI characters using pre-recorded clips and scripted replies — every call, character, and message is a simulation for fun, and none are real people or real calls. You do not need to create an account to use FameRing.

1. Information we collect

We only collect information that leaves your device. Because FameRing has no login and no user profiles, we do not ask you for your name, email, or similar personal details.

a) Information collected automatically. When you use the App, our third-party partners (see §3) may collect:

  • Device and advertising identifiers — including the Google Advertising ID (AAID) and similar device or installation identifiers.
  • Approximate location — a coarse, city-level location derived from your IP address by our advertising partners for ad targeting and measurement. We do not collect precise/GPS location, and the App requests no location permission.
  • App activity and interactions — such as screens viewed, features used, in-app searches for characters, taps, and session information, used for analytics and advertising.
  • Crash logs and diagnostics — technical data about crashes, errors, and performance, used to keep the App stable.

b) Information you provide or create, kept on your device. Some features let you add content — for example choosing a photo from your gallery, taking a live camera preview so you appear in a fake call, creating your own character, or writing chat messages to an AI character. This content is stored and processed only on your device; we do not upload, receive, or store it on our servers. The camera preview is shown live and is not recorded or transmitted; the App does not use the microphone (the audio-recording permission is removed).

We do not collect your contacts, your photo library, your files, or your microphone audio.

2. How we use information

We (and our partners) use the information above to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the App and its features;
  • Show and measure advertising, including personalized ads where you have consented;
  • Perform analytics to understand usage and improve the App;
  • Detect, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, and security issues;
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We do not use your information to build a profile tied to your real-world identity, because we do not collect identifying account data.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information for money. We share data only with the third-party services listed above, when required by law, or in connection with a business transfer. Because we serve personalized advertising, some U.S. state privacy laws may treat the related data flows as a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising; you can opt out via the ad-consent controls and your device ad settings described above.

5. Legal bases (EEA/UK — GDPR)

We rely on: your consent (personalized ads and analytics where required); our legitimate interests (providing core functionality, security, and improving the App); and legal obligation where applicable.

6. Data retention

We keep data only as long as necessary for the purposes above or as required by law. Advertising and attribution partners retain identifiers according to their own retention periods; crash and diagnostic data is kept for a limited period. Content stored on your device remains until you delete it or uninstall the App.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, port, object to, or restrict processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent or complain to a data protection authority (GDPR); to know, delete, correct, and opt out of the sale/sharing or targeted advertising of your personal information, with a right against discrimination (CCPA/CPRA); and similar rights under other laws (LGPD, PIPEDA, and others). Because FameRing has no user accounts, please contact us at the email below and include your device advertising ID so we can act on your request.

8. Children's privacy & Child Safety Standards

FameRing is intended for users aged 13 and older. The App is not directed to children under 13, and it presents an age gate that blocks users who indicate they are under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the higher minimum age required in your region). If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will address it consistent with COPPA, the GDPR age-of-consent rules, and other applicable laws.

Child Safety Standards (CSAE) — Tranquil Mind Technology JSC / FameRing. We have a zero-tolerance policy toward child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). This section is our published child-safety standard for the App.

  • Prohibited absolutely. It is strictly forbidden to use FameRing to create, upload, request, generate, share, promote, or facilitate any material or conduct that sexually exploits, endangers, or abuses a minor. This covers images, video, audio, and text; AI-generated content; the sexualization of anyone who is or appears to be a minor; and grooming, sextortion, solicitation, or trafficking of minors. The prohibition applies both to anything a user enters and to any content generated or displayed by the App or its AI characters — no FameRing character is a minor or may be presented in a sexualized way.
  • Prevention. All in-app characters and their scripted call/chat content are created and curated by us (FameRing does not host user-to-user messaging or a free-form generative chatbot), which lets us keep the catalog free of CSAE content. Every character is clearly labeled "AI · not a real person."
  • Reporting & response. FameRing includes an in-app "Report content" tool (with reasons such as Inappropriate content and Scam or deception) so anyone can flag content. Reports are reviewed, and content or access that violates this standard is removed or suspended. Users may also email us directly (below).
  • Reporting CSAM to authorities. Where we become aware of apparent CSAM, we will remove it and report it to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and/or the appropriate local authorities, and cooperate with law enforcement as required by law.
  • Compliance. We comply with applicable child-safety laws and align these standards with the Google Play Child Safety Standards policy.
  • Child-safety point of contact. Report child-safety concerns to contact@tranquilmind.co. We aim to respond promptly.
