Privacy Policy

App: Timbersage Effective: 2026-10-17 Last Updated: 2026-10-17

Timbersage ("the App", "we", "us", "our"), operated by Tranquil Mind Technology JSC, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices and rights you have. By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

Timbersage is a woodworking companion that identifies wood from photographs, provides build plans with cut lists, and offers cutting-layout and lumber-cost calculators. You do not need to create an account to use it; there is no sign-in, and the App contains no advertising.

1. Information we collect

Information you provide or submit

  • Photographs you choose to submit for identification or to a chat message. When you take a photo with the in-app camera or pick one from your gallery and submit it for wood identification, craft identification, valuation or repair guidance, that image is transmitted to our wood-analysis service for processing and the result is returned to you.
  • Text you type into the in-app assistant. Your questions and the message history of a conversation are transmitted to our service so a reply can be generated.
  • Optional feedback you send from the in-app feedback prompts (a rating signal and any note you write).

Information collected automatically

  • Crash logs and diagnostics — stack traces, device model, operating-system version, App version and the state of the App at the time of a crash, collected by Firebase Crashlytics.
  • App activity and analytics events — screens opened, features used, session start/duration, App version, device model, operating-system version, language, coarse country derived from the IP address, and a Firebase-generated app-instance identifier, collected by Firebase Analytics.
  • Technical request data — IP address and standard request headers are necessarily processed by our service and hosting providers when the App makes a network request.

Information that stays on your device and is never transmitted to us

  • Your setup-quiz answers, unit preference (metric/imperial), language choice, saved and favourite plans, build progress, scan history, lumber-price and blade-kerf inputs, photographs of your own builds, and PDF exports. These are stored locally in the App's own storage and in your device's documents and downloads folders.

The App does not contain any advertising SDK, does not collect or use an advertising identifier, does not request location permission and does not read your contacts, calendar, microphone, SMS or call logs. The App does not offer accounts or logins and does not process payments.

2. How we use information

  • To operate the App's core functions: return a wood or craft identification for a photo you submit, answer your questions in the assistant, and deliver build plans and plan images.
  • To keep the App working: detect, diagnose and fix crashes and errors.
  • To understand which features are used so we can improve the App.
  • To act on feedback you choose to send.
  • To protect against abuse, fraud and misuse of our service, and to comply with legal obligations.

We do not use your information to build advertising profiles, and we do not use it to make automated decisions with legal effects.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information for money, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising or targeted advertising. We disclose information only:

  • to the service providers listed in Section 3, acting on our instructions for the purposes stated;
  • where required by law, legal process or a valid government request, or to protect our rights, safety or property;
  • in connection with a merger, acquisition or asset transfer, subject to this Policy.

5. Legal bases (EEA/UK — GDPR)

  • Contract / provision of the service — processing the photographs and messages you submit so the App can return a result.
  • Legitimate interests — crash reporting, security, abuse prevention and product improvement.
  • Consent — analytics where consent is required by local law, and the device permissions you grant (camera, photos, notifications), which you can withdraw at any time in your device settings.
  • Legal obligation — where we must retain or disclose information by law.

6. Data retention

  • Photographs and chat text submitted for processing are retained only as long as needed to produce and deliver the result and to guard against abuse, and then deleted or de-identified.
  • Crash and diagnostic data are retained for a limited period by Firebase Crashlytics under Google's retention settings.
  • Analytics data are retained under the Firebase Analytics retention period configured for the App.
  • Data stored on your device remain until you delete them in the App ("delete all data" in Settings) or uninstall the App.

7. Your rights

EEA / UK (GDPR): you have the right to access, correct, delete, port, object to and restrict processing of your personal data, to withdraw consent at any time, and to lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority.

California (CCPA/CPRA): you have the right to know what we collect, to delete and correct it, to opt out of sale or sharing (we do neither), and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

Other regions (LGPD, PIPEDA and similar laws): you have equivalent rights of access, correction, deletion and objection.

To exercise any right, email contact@tranquilmind.co. Because the App has no accounts, please include the approximate date and time of the request or submission you are asking about, your device model and App version, so that we can locate the relevant records; without such details we may be unable to identify any data relating to you. You can also delete everything held locally at any time from the App's Settings screen.

8. Children's privacy & Child Safety Standards

The App is intended for users aged 13 and over (or the higher age of digital consent in your country) and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, email contact@tranquilmind.co and we will delete it.

Child Safety Standards (CSAE) — applies to Timbersage, published by Tranquil Mind Technology JSC.

We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). It is strictly prohibited to upload, submit, request, generate, store, share or promote, through any part of the App including the camera, the photo picker and the in-app assistant, any material that:

  • depicts or describes the sexual abuse or sexual exploitation of a minor, in image, video, audio, text or AI-generated form;
  • sexualizes a minor in any way;
  • constitutes or facilitates grooming, sextortion, solicitation, or the trafficking of a minor.

This prohibition applies both to content you submit and to any attempt to make the assistant produce such content.

Prevention and response (what the App actually does). Timbersage is a woodworking utility: it has no user accounts, no user profiles, no user-to-user messaging and no public posting or community feed, so users cannot send content to one another. The only content you can submit is a photograph or a text question sent privately to our wood-analysis service, and the assistant is instructed and filtered to answer woodworking questions only; off-topic and abusive requests are refused. Submissions to our service are subject to automated abuse filtering, and we review material reported to us. If you encounter content or behaviour in the App that you believe violates these standards, report it to contact@tranquilmind.co (you can also use the in-app feedback form, which reaches the same team); we respond to reports as a priority. Where we find a violation we remove the material and block access to our service for the device or user involved. Where we become aware of apparent CSAM, we preserve the material as required, report it to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and/or the competent local authorities, and cooperate with their investigation.

We comply with applicable child-safety laws and align these standards with Google Play's Child Safety Standards policy. Child-safety point of contact: contact@tranquilmind.co.

9. International data transfers

We and our providers process data on servers that may be located outside your country, including in the United States. Where required, transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.
