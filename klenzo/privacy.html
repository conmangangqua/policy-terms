Privacy Policy

App: Klenzo Effective: 2026-08-27 Last Updated: 2026-08-27

Klenzo ("the App", "we", "us", "our"), operated by Tranquil Mind Technology JSC, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices and rights you have. By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

Klenzo is a phone cleaner that clears junk files, removes duplicates, compresses photos and videos, and shows how your storage is used. You do not need to create an account to use it.

1. Information we collect

Klenzo does most of its work on your device. Cleaning, compression, duplicate detection and storage analysis run locally, and the files and media they process are not uploaded to us.

We collect a limited set of data automatically through the services below:

  • Device & diagnostic data — device model, operating-system version, app version, language, and coarse, non-precise identifiers used by analytics.
  • App activity — in-app events and screens visited (e.g. which tools are opened), used to understand usage and improve the App.
  • Crash logs & diagnostics — stack traces and device state captured when the App crashes or errors.

Klenzo does not collect your photos, videos, contacts, precise location, or account credentials, and does not include advertising.

2. How we use information

We use the information above to: provide and operate the App; remember your preferences (such as language); understand feature usage and fix problems; keep the App secure and stable; and comply with legal obligations. We do not use it for advertising or to build advertising profiles.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information for money. We share the limited data above only with the service providers listed in Section 3, when required by law, or in connection with a business transfer. Because the App shows no personalized ads, we do not "sell" or "share" data for cross-context behavioral advertising.

5. Legal bases (EEA/UK — GDPR)

We rely on our legitimate interests (operating, securing and improving the App), your consent where required for analytics, and legal obligations where applicable.

6. Data retention

We keep analytics and diagnostic data only as long as necessary for the purposes above or as required by law. Crash and diagnostic records are kept for a limited period. Data processed locally on your device stays under your control and can be removed by clearing app data or uninstalling the App.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, port, object to, or restrict processing of your data, and to withdraw consent or complain to a data-protection authority (GDPR); to know, delete, correct, and opt out of the sale/sharing of your data (CCPA/CPRA); and similar rights under LGPD, PIPEDA and other laws. Because Klenzo has no accounts, please contact us at the email below with your device details so we can locate any associated diagnostic data.

8. Children's privacy

Klenzo is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your region). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. International data transfers

Data may be processed in countries other than yours (including the United States, where Google operates). Where required, appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses apply.

10. Data security

We use encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and reasonable technical measures to protect data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

11. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy. Material changes will be posted here with a new date; continued use of the App means you accept the updated Policy.
