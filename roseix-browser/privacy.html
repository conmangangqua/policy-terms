Privacy Policy

App: Roseix Browser Effective: 2026-08-13 Last Updated: 2026-08-13

Roseix Browser ("the App", "we", "us", "our"), operated by Tranquil Mind Technology JSC, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices and rights you have. By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

Roseix Browser is a fast, lightweight web browser with a built-in ad blocker that helps block ads and pop-ups on the websites you visit. You do not need to create an account to use it.

1. Information We Collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name or email through the App. We collect only the limited data described below, most of it automatically through third-party SDKs.

Collected automatically

  • Device and advertising identifiers — including the Android Advertising ID (AAID) and similar identifiers, used for analytics and advertising.
  • Approximate location — coarse location derived from your IP address by our advertising and analytics providers for ad targeting and measurement. We do not collect precise/GPS location.
  • App activity — in-app interactions and events (screens viewed, features used, ad interactions) for analytics and advertising.
  • Install & attribution data — install/referrer signals and your advertising identifier, used by our attribution provider to measure which marketing source led you to the App.
  • Crash logs and diagnostics — crash reports and performance/diagnostic data to keep the App stable.
  • Purchase status — whether you hold an active subscription (Premium), processed through Google Play Billing.

Data that stays on your device (not collected by us)

  • Your browsing history, tabs, bookmarks, and search queries are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to or collected by us. Premium adds a no-history browsing option. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes this local data.
  • Reminders and local notifications are scheduled and delivered entirely on your device. If you grant the notification permission, no notification content leaves your device.

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide and operate the App and its browsing and ad-blocking features.
  • Show and measure advertising (including personalized ads where permitted).
  • Analytics and product improvement.
  • Fraud prevention, security, and abuse detection.
  • Process and validate purchases and subscriptions.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

Ad mediation. AdMob serves ads and, through mediation, may pass an ad request to the partner ad networks listed above (Meta Audience Network, AppLovin, Pangle, Mintegral). These networks may receive your advertising identifier, coarse (IP-based) location, and ad-interaction data to select and measure ads, each under its own privacy policy.

Attribution. We use Adjust (which works together with the Meta SDK) to measure which marketing campaign or source led you to install the App, using your advertising identifier and install/attribution signals. It does not identify you by name.

Ad consent (UMP). Where required (e.g. the EEA/UK), the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request your consent for personalized advertising before such ads are served. Your consent choice is forwarded to the mediation partners above. You can change your choice later from the App's settings where available.

Resetting or opting out of the advertising ID. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization at any time in your device settings: Android → Settings → Privacy → Ads.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information for money. We share data only with the third-party providers listed in Section 3 — including our advertising, ad-mediation, and attribution partners — to operate the App, and for legal reasons or in connection with a business transfer.

U.S. state notice. Because the App serves personalized ads, sharing data with advertising partners may be considered a "sale" or "sharing" for targeted advertising under certain U.S. state laws (e.g. California/CPRA). You can opt out by disabling ad personalization / your advertising ID as described in Section 3, and by declining consent where prompted.

5. Legal Bases (EEA/UK — GDPR)

  • Consent — personalized advertising and, where required, analytics.
  • Legitimate interests — core functionality, security, fraud prevention, and product improvement.
  • Contract / legal obligation — processing purchases and meeting legal requirements.

6. Data Retention

We keep data only as long as necessary for the purposes above or as required by law. Advertising and analytics identifiers are retained according to our partners' retention periods; crash and diagnostic data are kept for a limited period. Locally stored browsing data remains on your device until you clear it or uninstall the App.

7. Your Rights

  • GDPR (EEA/UK): access, correct, delete, port, object, restrict, and withdraw consent; and to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.
  • CCPA/CPRA (California) and other U.S. states: know, delete, correct, opt out of "sale"/"sharing" and targeted advertising, and non-discrimination.
  • Other regions: equivalent rights under laws such as LGPD (Brazil) and PIPEDA (Canada).

To exercise your rights, contact us at contact@tranquilmind.co. Because the App has no accounts, please include your advertising ID and device details so we can identify the relevant data.

8. Children's Privacy & Child Safety Standards

The App is intended for users 18 years of age and older and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. As a general-purpose web browser, the App can access content across the internet and is not designed or intended for use by minors. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Child Safety Standards (CSAE) — Roseix Browser

Tranquil Mind Technology JSC has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). This section constitutes the published child safety standards for Roseix Browser, as published by Tranquil Mind Technology JSC.

What we prohibit. We strictly prohibit any use of the App that involves, facilitates, creates, requests, shares, or promotes CSAE or CSAM in any form — including images, video, audio, and text — as well as the sexualization of minors, grooming, sextortion, solicitation, and trafficking of children. This prohibition applies to all use of the App.

Nature of the App. Roseix Browser is a web browser. It does not host, and does not provide, user accounts, user-to-user messaging, comments, or a platform for users to upload, publish, or exchange user-generated content with other users. The App itself is not a means of distributing content between users. Web pages you visit are operated by independent third parties over whom we have no control.

Prevention and response.

  • We design and operate the App so it is not intended for use by, or to appeal to, children (18+ minimum age).
  • We comply with applicable child-safety laws and align with Google Play's Child Safety Standards policy.
  • Where we become aware of apparent CSAM connected to our services, we act to remove it, restrict or terminate access to the App where within our control, and report apparent CSAM to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and/or to the appropriate local authorities.

To report a child-safety concern related to the App, or to report suspected CSAE/CSAM, contact our child-safety point of contact below. Suspected exploitation of a child can also be reported directly to NCMEC at report.cybertip.org and to your local law enforcement.

Child-safety point of contact: contact@tranquilmind.co
