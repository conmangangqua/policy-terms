Privacy Policy

App: Junklift Effective: 2026-10-05 Last Updated: 2026-10-05

Junklift ("the App", "we", "us", "our"), operated by Tranquil Mind Technology JSC, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices and rights you have. By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

Junklift is an on-device storage cleaner that finds junk and cache files, duplicate and large files, compresses photos and videos, and shows what is using your storage. You do not need to create an account to use it, and we do not operate a user-login system or a server that stores your files.

1. Information we collect

Data the App reads and processes on your device. Junklift's core work happens on your phone. The following is read locally, shown to you in the App, and is not uploaded to our servers:

  • Files and media on your storage — file names, sizes, types, dates, and photo/video content that the App scans to detect junk, cache, large files, duplicates, and compressible media. Compression and deletion also run locally.
  • Installed-app information — the list of apps installed on the device, their install source, granted permissions and storage/usage figures, used for the App Manager, "rarely used apps", Permission Check and uninstall screens. The App needs Android's "query all packages" access to read this list. Because the App can access it, we disclose Installed apps as data collected for app functionality in our Google Play Data safety section. We do not upload the list to our servers or share it with third parties.
  • App usage statistics — the Android usage-stats data you grant in system settings, used to show which apps you rarely open.
  • Device state — available/total storage, battery level, and network status shown on the dashboard.
  • Recycle Bin — photos and videos you delete in the App are first moved to a private folder on your device so you can restore them. They are permanently deleted after 30 days, or earlier if you choose "Delete forever".

We do not upload, copy to our servers, or sell your files, photos, videos, or app list.

Data that does leave the device. Through the third-party services listed in section 3, we receive only aggregated, pseudonymous technical data:

  • Device and app identifiers — a Firebase installation/instance ID, the Android advertising ID where available (read by Firebase Analytics for analytics only, never for ads), device model, operating-system version, language, country/region, and app version.
  • Approximate, IP-derived location — country/region level only, inferred by our analytics provider from your IP address. The App does not request GPS or any location permission.
  • App activity — anonymous events such as which screen was opened or which clean action was completed, plus session counts, used to understand how the App is used.
  • Crash logs and diagnostics — stack traces, device state at the time of a crash, and performance data, used to fix bugs.

Network tools. The network-speed and connectivity checks send test requests to public endpoints (Cloudflare's speed-test service and Google's connectivity-check endpoint). Those providers see your IP address as they would for any web request; we do not receive or store the result.

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, microphone or camera input, or payment information. The App contains no advertising SDK, performs no ad targeting, and has no in-app purchases or subscriptions.

2. How we use information

  • To provide and operate the App's scanning, cleaning, compression and storage-analysis features (locally, on your device).
  • To diagnose crashes and errors and improve stability and performance.
  • To understand aggregate feature usage so we can improve the App.
  • To deliver remote configuration (for example enabling or tuning a feature without an app update).
  • To send local reminders about cleaning your device, if you allow notifications.
  • To comply with legal obligations and to protect against fraud, abuse, or security incidents.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or automated decision-making that affects you legally.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. Information is shared only:

  • with the service providers listed in section 3, acting on our behalf for analytics, crash reporting and configuration;
  • when required by law, legal process, or a valid governmental request;
  • to protect the rights, safety or property of users, the public, or us;
  • in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, in which case we will notify users of any change in how data is handled.

5. Legal bases (EEA/UK — GDPR)

  • Legitimate interests — operating and securing the App, fixing crashes, and understanding aggregate usage to improve the product.
  • Consent — where local law requires consent for analytics or for device notifications; you can withdraw it at any time in system settings or by contacting us.
  • Legal obligation — where we must retain or disclose data by law.

6. Data retention

Locally scanned data (files, app list, usage stats) is held only in memory or in the App's local storage on your device and is not retained by us. Items in the Recycle Bin stay on your device for up to 30 days. Uninstalling the App removes its local data, including the Recycle Bin. Analytics data is retained by Google Firebase for its standard retention period (up to 14 months for event data). Crash reports are retained for a limited period (up to 90 days by default) for debugging. We keep nothing longer than necessary or than required by law.

7. Your rights

EEA/UK (GDPR): you may request access to, correction, deletion, portability, or restriction of your data, object to processing based on legitimate interests, withdraw consent, and lodge a complaint with your data-protection authority.

California (CCPA/CPRA): you have the right to know what personal information is collected, to request deletion or correction, to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information (we do neither), and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

Other regions (LGPD, PIPEDA, and similar laws): equivalent rights to access, correct, delete, and object apply.

Because the App has no accounts, we cannot identify you from a request alone. How to request deletion of your data: email us at the address in section 12 and include your device model, operating-system version, App version, the approximate date of use and, if you can, your Android advertising ID, so we can locate any related analytics or diagnostic records. We will respond within 30 days. You can also stop all analytics and crash reporting at any time by uninstalling the App.

8. Children's privacy

The App is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under the applicable age of digital consent in your country). If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it. The App contains no chat, messaging, social feed, user-to-user sharing, or user-generated-content feature.

9. International data transfers

Our service providers may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States. Where required, transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

10. Data security

Data in transit is protected with HTTPS/TLS. We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the limited data we handle. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.
