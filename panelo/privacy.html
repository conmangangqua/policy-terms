Privacy Policy

App: Panelo Effective: October 29, 2026 Last Updated: October 29, 2026

Effective date: October 29, 2026 Publisher: Tranquil Mind Technology JSC ("we", "us") Contact: contact@tranquilmind.co

Panelo ("the App") lets you watch vertical short dramas. This policy explains what information the App and the third-party services it integrates handle, and the choices you have.

1. Information we handle

  • No account. The App has no sign-up or login; we do not ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • Device and usage data. Diagnostic and analytics data such as app version, device model, OS version, language, approximate region derived from IP address, screens viewed, and crash reports (Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics).
  • Advertising and attribution identifiers. The Android Advertising ID (GAID) and App Set ID, used for advertising, install attribution and fraud prevention (Google AdMob, Adjust, Meta).
  • Local data. Preferences, watch state and cached posters/video segments are stored on your device (shared preferences, cache) and are removed when you clear app data or uninstall.
  • Purchases. Subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed by Google Play. We receive purchase status (entitlement) but never your payment card details.

2. Permissions the App declares (Android)

Permissions the App declares (Android): android.permission.INTERNET, android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID, android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS.

PermissionPurpose
android.permission.INTERNETLoad drama catalog, posters and video streams; communicate with analytics, ads and purchase services.
android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATEDetect whether the device is online so the App and ad SDKs can load content or show an offline message.
com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_IDRead the Android Advertising ID for ad delivery, measurement and install attribution (Google AdMob, Adjust, Meta). You can reset or delete this ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONSAsk (Android 13+) for permission to send you notifications. The App uses it for local re-engagement reminders, for example when you have not opened the App for a day or have not finished onboarding. The request is shown on the App's Permission screen after Welcome; you may decline or turn notifications off at any time in Android Settings → Apps → Panelo → Notifications, and the App works normally without it.

The App does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files.

4. Sharing and retention

We do not sell your personal information for money. Data is shared only with the service providers above for the purposes described. Analytics and crash data are kept for as long as the providers' standard retention applies; local data stays on your device until you clear it.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Decline or revoke notifications in Android Settings.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Manage ad consent through the in-app consent form where available.
  • Depending on your location (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, LGPD, PIPEDA) you may request access, correction or deletion of data we hold by emailing contact@tranquilmind.co.

6. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Security and international transfers

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections where supported. Service providers may process data in countries other than your own, subject to their safeguards.

8. Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above will change when we do.
