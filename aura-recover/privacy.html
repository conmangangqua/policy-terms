Privacy Policy

App: AuraRecover: File Recovery Pro Effective: October 30, 2026 Last Updated: October 30, 2026

App: AuraRecover: File Recovery Pro (package com.tranquil.aura.recover) Publisher: Tranquil Mind Technology JSC ("we", "us") Effective date: October 30, 2026 Contact: contact@tranquilmind.co

This Privacy Policy explains what information AuraRecover (the "App") accesses, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using the App you agree to this Policy.

1. Information we collect

1.1 Files on your device (processed locally)

The App's core feature is to find and restore photos, videos, audio and documents stored on your device. To do this, the native Android layer of the App queries the device media index (MediaStore) for file path, display name, size, modification date and MIME type, reads file contents to copy a selected file into the restore folder, and generates video thumbnails. The App can also move a file you choose into a private "vault" folder (AuraRecover/.vault) and back to AuraRecover/Restored.

This file access happens on your device. We do not upload your files, file names or file lists to our servers.

1.2 Device and usage data (collected by third-party SDKs)

The App integrates the following SDKs, which may collect data off the device:

  • Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics (Google): app usage events, crash reports, device model, OS version, app version, and app instance identifiers.
  • Firebase Remote Config (Google): fetches app configuration values; it receives device and app instance identifiers.
  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads (Google): shows ads; may collect the Android Advertising ID, IP address, device information and ad interaction data.
  • Ad mediation partners via AdMob: Meta Audience Network, Pangle, Mintegral, AppLovin, Unity Ads and Bigo Ads, which may collect advertising ID, IP address, device information and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads.
  • Meta (Facebook) SDK: configured with automatic initialization, app event logging and advertiser ID collection enabled.
  • Adjust: attribution and measurement of app installs and events, using the advertising ID and device information.
  • IAB TCF consent library (iabtcf_consent_info): reads the consent status you set in the consent dialog, stored on your device.

Locally, the App stores your settings (for example, the restore folder) on your device using shared preferences and local storage.

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email address or phone number inside the App. If you email us, we receive the information you send.

Permissions the App declares (Android): android.permission.INTERNET, android.permission.READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, android.permission.WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, android.permission.MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID

  • INTERNET: required to load ads, send crash and analytics data, and fetch remote configuration.
  • READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE: lets the App read the files on shared storage that you ask it to scan, list and preview (photos, videos, audio, documents).
  • WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE: lets the App write the recovered copy of a file you select into the restore folder (default AuraRecover/Restored) and manage files in the vault folder.
  • MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE ("All files access"): lets the App scan and restore files across shared storage, beyond the media collections of a single app, and copy them to the restore folder. The App asks you to grant this in system settings; you can revoke it at any time in Settings > Apps > AuraRecover > Permissions. Without it, scanning and restore will not work fully. File contents are used only for this purpose and are not sent to our servers.
  • AD_ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID): lets ad and attribution SDKs read the Android Advertising ID for advertising and measurement. You can reset or delete it in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads.

2. How we use information

  • To provide the file scan, preview, restore and vault features.
  • To keep the App stable, diagnose crashes and understand feature usage.
  • To show ads and measure their performance and install attribution.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service.

4. Advertising and consent

The App shows ads. Where required (for example in the EEA, UK), the App presents a consent dialog based on the IAB TCF framework, and ad SDKs follow your choice. You can limit ad personalization or reset your advertising ID in your Android settings.

5. Data sharing and retention

We share data only with the service providers listed above and where required by law. Analytics, crash and advertising data are retained by those providers according to their policies. Files you restore stay on your device; we have no copy. To delete locally stored data, clear the App's storage or uninstall it; files already restored or moved to the vault remain in AuraRecover/ until you delete them.

Depending on where you live (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, LGPD, PIPEDA and similar laws), you may request access, correction, deletion or restriction of your personal data, or object to processing, and opt out of "sale"/"sharing" for advertising. Contact contact@tranquilmind.co. You can also withdraw consent via the in-app consent dialog if available, and reset your advertising ID in Android settings.
