Privacy Policy

App: Pocket Retro Effective: 2026-09-23 Last Updated: 2026-09-23

Pocket Retro ("the App", "we", "us", "our"), operated by Tranquil Mind Technology JSC, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices and rights you have. By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

Pocket Retro is a retro console emulator that lets you play classic game files on your phone, organise them in a local library, and customise controls, skins and video filters. You do not need to create an account to use the App. Signing in with a Google account is optional and is used only if you turn on save-file sync to your own Google Drive.

1. Information we collect

Information collected automatically

  • Device and advertising identifiers — advertising ID (AAID), app instance ID, and similar device identifiers, used by our advertising, attribution and analytics providers.
  • Approximate (IP-derived) location — our advertising partners derive a coarse, country/region-level location from your IP address to select and measure ads. The App does not request or use GPS, and declares no location permission.
  • App activity and diagnostics — screens opened, buttons used, ad impressions and clicks, purchase and paywall events, session and device technical data (device model, OS version, language, region, network type).
  • Crash logs and performance data — stack traces and diagnostic data when the App crashes or misbehaves, collected through Firebase Crashlytics.
  • Push notification token — a Firebase Cloud Messaging registration token, used to deliver notifications you have allowed.
  • Purchase status — whether a subscription or one-time purchase is active, received from Google Play Billing. We never receive or store your card or payment details; payment is handled entirely by Google Play.

Information you provide or authorise

  • Google account identifiers (optional) — if you enable save-file sync, Google Sign-In gives the App your Google account identifier, name and email address, and permission to read and write the App's save files in your own Google Drive. We do not operate a server for this: your save files travel between your device and your Drive. You can revoke this at any time in the App or in your Google account settings.

Information that stays on your device

Your game files, save states, library metadata, control layouts, skins and app settings are stored locally on your device (and, if you enable it, in your own Google Drive). They are not uploaded to our servers — we do not operate a content server for your library.

2. How we use information

  • Provide and operate the App: run games, keep your library, remember your settings and language.
  • Deliver the optional game catalogue and downloads you request.
  • Show advertising, including rewarded ads shown before launching a game, and measure ad performance.
  • Attribute installs and campaigns, and detect invalid or fraudulent traffic.
  • Analyse usage to fix defects and improve the App.
  • Process and restore purchases and subscription status through Google Play.
  • Send notifications you have allowed (for example download progress or sync status).
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information for money. We share data with the providers listed in §3 so they can perform their function, for legal reasons (to comply with law, enforce our Terms, or protect rights and safety), and in the context of a merger, acquisition or asset transfer, in which case this Policy continues to apply.

Under certain U.S. state privacy laws (for example the CCPA/CPRA), showing personalised advertising may be treated as a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information. If you have consented to personalised ads, you can opt out at any time by reopening the privacy options form in the App's settings, by resetting or deleting your advertising ID, or by contacting us at the address in §12.

5. Legal bases (EEA / UK — GDPR)

  • Consent — personalised advertising and, where required, analytics; push notifications; optional Google Drive sync.
  • Legitimate interests — operating and securing the App, non-personalised advertising and frequency capping, crash diagnostics, fraud prevention, product improvement.
  • Performance of a contract — providing the App and processing purchases you make.
  • Legal obligation — retaining records where the law requires it.

6. Data retention

We keep data only as long as necessary for the purposes above or as required by law. Analytics and advertising identifiers are retained by the providers in §3 according to their own retention schedules (typically between 2 and 26 months). Crash and diagnostic data is kept for a limited troubleshooting period. Purchase status is retained while your entitlement is active and for as long as tax and accounting rules require. Your game files and save data live on your device (and in your own Drive if you enable sync) and are removed when you delete them or uninstall the App.

7. Your rights

EEA / UK (GDPR). You have the right to access, correct, delete, port, object to and restrict processing of your personal data, to withdraw consent at any time, and to lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority.

California (CCPA/CPRA). You have the right to know what personal information is collected and disclosed, to request deletion or correction, to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information and of targeted advertising, and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

Other regions. Comparable rights exist under Brazil's LGPD, Canada's PIPEDA and similar laws, and we honour them in the same way.

How to exercise them. Write to contact@tranquilmind.co. Because the App has no account of ours, we cannot identify you from our records alone: please include your advertising ID and, if relevant, the Google account email you used for save sync, plus your device model and approximate date of use, so we can locate and act on the data. We respond within the period the applicable law requires.

Deleting your data yourself. Uninstalling the App deletes local data on your device. Resetting or deleting your advertising ID in Android settings severs the advertising and attribution identifier. Revoking the App's access in your Google account settings ends Drive sync; save files already in your Drive can be deleted from Drive.

8. Children's privacy

The App is intended for users aged 13 and over (16 where a higher age of digital consent applies, such as parts of the EEA). It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children below those ages. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us at contact@tranquilmind.co and we will delete it. The App contains no chat, messaging, comments, user-to-user content sharing or social discovery features.

9. International data transfers

The providers listed in §3 operate globally, so your data may be processed outside your country, including in the United States. Where required, transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or an adequacy decision.
