Privacy Policy

App: Dreamatic Effective: October 29, 2026 Last Updated: October 29, 2026

Effective date: 29 October 2026 Operator: Tranquil Mind Technology JSC ("we", "us") Contact: contact@tranquilmind.co

Dreamatic is a vertical short-drama streaming app. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and the choices you have.

1. Permissions

Permissions the App declares (Android): android.permission.INTERNET, android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID, android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS, android.permission.RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED.

PermissionPurpose
android.permission.INTERNETLoad the drama catalogue and video streams, show ads, process subscriptions, and send analytics and crash reports.
android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATEDetect whether the device is online so the app can show a connection message and avoid failed loads.
com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_IDLet the advertising and attribution SDKs (Google AdMob and mediation partners, AppsFlyer, Meta) read the Android Advertising ID for ad delivery, frequency capping and install attribution. You can reset or delete the Advertising ID in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads.
android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONSSend you notifications: push messages delivered through Firebase Cloud Messaging and local reminders scheduled on your device. On Android 13 and later the app asks for this permission at runtime; you can decline or turn notifications off at any time in system settings, and the app keeps working.
android.permission.RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETEDRe-schedule local notification reminders after the device restarts. It does not collect data.

The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, precise or approximate location, photos or files.

2. Information we handle

  • Device and usage data: app events (for example screens viewed and videos played), device identifier and basic device information, app version, and crash and performance reports. Processed through Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics and a Firebase Realtime Database event log keyed by a device identifier.
  • Advertising data: Advertising ID and ad interaction data, handled by the ad SDKs listed in Section 3.
  • Notification data: a Firebase Cloud Messaging registration token, used only to deliver push notifications to your device.
  • Purchase data: subscription status and purchase receipts handled by RevenueCat and Google Play Billing. We do not receive your payment card details.
  • Local data: preferences and viewing state stored on your device.

The app has no account or sign-up and does not ask for your name, email or phone number.

4. Advertising choices

Reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device settings. Where required (for example EEA/UK), the app shows a consent prompt before personalised advertising.

5. Retention and deletion

Analytics, crash and advertising data are kept by the providers above for their standard retention periods. To request deletion of data associated with your device, write to contact@tranquilmind.co.

6. Children

Dreamatic is not directed to children under 13 (or the age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect children's personal data. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, LGPD, PIPEDA and similar laws) you may request access, correction, deletion or restriction of your data, or object to its use. Contact contact@tranquilmind.co.

8. Security and transfers

We use HTTPS and App Check to protect traffic. Data may be processed in countries other than yours by the providers above, under their safeguards.

9. Changes

We will update this page and its effective date when practices change.
