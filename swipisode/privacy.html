Privacy Policy

App: Swipisode Effective: 2026-09-09 Last Updated: 2026-09-09

Swipisode ("the App", "we", "us", "our"), operated by Tranquil Mind Technology JSC, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices and rights you have. By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

Swipisode is a vertical short-drama streaming app: you swipe through series whose episodes run one to two minutes each. You do not need to create an account to use it — there is no sign-up, login, or user profile, and we never ask for your name, email address, phone number, or contacts.

1. Information we collect

Collected automatically (leaves your device)

The App collects the following automatically, through the third-party services listed in Section 3 and through our own streaming service:

  • Device and advertising identifiers — including the Android Advertising ID, the Firebase installation / app-instance identifier, identifiers generated by our attribution provider, an anonymous purchase identifier generated by our billing provider, and an app-integrity attestation token. Used for advertising, analytics, attribution, purchase validation, and fraud prevention.
  • Approximate location — derived by our advertising, analytics, and attribution providers from your IP address (country or region level, not GPS). The App does not request any device location permission and does not collect precise location.
  • App activity — in-app events and interactions such as screens viewed, series and episodes opened, playback events, ads viewed and rewards earned, and paywall or purchase events. Used for analytics and advertising measurement.
  • Crash logs and diagnostics — crash reports, stack traces, device model, operating-system version, and app version, used to detect and fix stability issues.
  • Purchase status — the fact, type, and status of any premium (VIP) subscription or in-app purchase, processed through Google Play Billing and our billing provider. We do not receive or store your payment card details.
  • App and device integrity signals — our anti-tamper component checks whether the App has been modified, repackaged, or is running under a debugger, hooking framework, emulator, or rooted device, and reports these signals (together with device model, operating-system version, app version, and app signature) to its provider and to our analytics. This protects the licensed video content from unauthorized copying.
  • Streaming service request data — when you browse the catalogue or play an episode, your device requests content from our streaming service at epsdrm.com and from the content delivery networks that host the video files. Those requests include your IP address, the requested series or episode identifier, the language code you selected, and standard technical request data (user agent, timestamps), which are processed to deliver the content and kept in server logs for security and troubleshooting.

Stored only on your device (not collected by us)

Your continue-watching progress and watch history, the series you save or like, your coin balance, your language and subtitle preferences, your ad-consent choices, and any ad-free period earned from a rewarded ad are held in local app storage on your device. They are removed when you clear the App's data or uninstall it. Search runs on your device against the catalogue already downloaded to it — your search terms are not sent to us.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, postal address, contacts, calendar, photos, camera or microphone input, files, SMS or call logs, precise location, health data, or any government or financial identification number. The App requests only the INTERNET permission.

2. How we use information

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the App and deliver the series and episodes you request.
  • Remember your progress, saved series, coins, and preferences on your device.
  • Serve and measure advertising, including personalized advertising where you have consented, and grant the rewards (coins, a temporary ad-free period) earned from rewarded ads.
  • Analyze usage to understand and improve performance, content, and features.
  • Detect, prevent, and investigate fraud, abuse, piracy, tampering, and security issues.
  • Process and validate premium subscriptions and in-app purchases, and restore them for you.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information for money. We share information only as follows:

  • With the service providers and advertising partners listed in Section 3, for the purposes described there.
  • For legal reasons — where we believe disclosure is required by law, regulation, legal process, or an enforceable governmental request, or is necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of our users, the public, or us.
  • In a business transfer — if we are involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to this Policy.

Notice for U.S. state residents. Some U.S. state privacy laws (including the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA) define "sale" and "sharing" broadly enough to cover the transfer of advertising identifiers to advertising networks for cross-context behavioral advertising. Under those definitions, our use of personalized advertising may be treated as a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information. You can opt out at any time using the advertising-ID controls described in Section 3, or by contacting us at contact@tranquilmind.co. We do not knowingly sell or share the personal information of anyone under 16.

5. Legal bases for processing (EEA / UK — GDPR)

  • Consent — personalized advertising, and analytics where consent is required. You may withdraw consent at any time, as described in Section 3.
  • Legitimate interests — operating and securing the App, non-personalized advertising, crash reporting and diagnostics, fraud prevention, anti-piracy and app-integrity protection, and improving our service.
  • Performance of a contract — processing and validating your subscriptions and in-app purchases and restoring your entitlements.
  • Legal obligation — retaining transaction records and responding to lawful requests.

6. Data retention

We keep information only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, or as required by law.

  • Crash and diagnostic reports: typically retained by Firebase Crashlytics for up to 90 days.
  • Analytics event data: retained by Firebase Analytics for the retention period configured in our project (up to 14 months).
  • Advertising and attribution identifiers: retained by each advertising and attribution partner according to its own retention schedule; see the policies in Section 3.
  • Purchase records: retained for as long as needed to validate and restore entitlements and to meet accounting and tax obligations.
  • Streaming server logs: retained for a limited period for security and troubleshooting.
  • Data held on your device is deleted when you clear the App's data or uninstall the App.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have some or all of the following rights.

EEA / UK (GDPR): access your personal data; correct it; have it deleted; restrict or object to processing; data portability; withdraw consent at any time without affecting prior processing; and lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

California and other U.S. states (CCPA/CPRA and similar laws): know what personal information is collected, used, and disclosed; delete it; correct it; opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information and of targeted advertising; limit the use of sensitive personal information; and not be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

Brazil (LGPD), Canada (PIPEDA), and other regions: equivalent rights of access, correction, deletion, portability, and objection under your local law.

How to exercise your rights. Email contact@tranquilmind.co. Because the App has no accounts, we cannot identify you from a name or an email address alone. To let us locate and act on any data held by our advertising, analytics, and attribution partners, please include your Android Advertising ID (Settings → Privacy → Ads → "Your advertising ID"), your device model, and your approximate date of first use. We will respond within the period required by applicable law (30 days under the GDPR, 45 days under the CCPA, in each case extendable where the law allows).

Deleting your data. You can delete all data stored on your device at any time by clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App. To request deletion of data held by us or by our partners, email contact@tranquilmind.co with the details above. Purchase records that we are legally required to keep for accounting or tax purposes are retained for the applicable statutory period.

8. Children's privacy

The App is intended for users aged 16 and over. Some series contain mature dramatic themes. The App is not directed to children, is not part of the Google Play Designed for Families programme, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

In the United States, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 as defined by the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). In the EEA and the UK, we do not knowingly process the personal data of anyone below the applicable age of digital consent (13 to 16 depending on the country). If you believe a child has used the App and provided information to us, contact contact@tranquilmind.co and we will delete the associated data.

9. International data transfers

We are based in Vietnam, and the third-party providers listed in Section 3 operate globally, including in the United States. Your information may therefore be processed in countries whose data-protection laws differ from those in your country. Where we transfer personal data out of the EEA or the UK, we and our providers rely on appropriate safeguards, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and the UK International Data Transfer Addendum, or on an adequacy decision where one applies.

10. Data security

All data transmitted between the App and our servers and our providers' servers is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. Our streaming API requires an app-attestation token (Google Play Integrity via Firebase App Check), and the App includes anti-tamper protection to detect modified or repackaged builds. We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information against loss, misuse, and unauthorized access. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
