Privacy Policy

App: Sentrik Effective: 2026-08-12 Last Updated: 2026-08-12

Sentrik ("the App", "we", "us", "our"), operated by Tranquil Mind Technology JSC, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices and rights you have. By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

Sentrik is a call-screening and spam-blocking utility that identifies and filters unwanted calls and helps you manage nuisance callers on your device. You do not need to create an account to use it.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not upload your contacts or call log to our servers. The following data is processed to make the App work and is limited to what actually leaves the device:

Automatically collected (via our SDK partners):

  • Device & advertising identifiers (including the Android Advertising ID) and general device information (model, OS version, language).
  • Approximate location derived from IP address (country/region level), used by advertising and analytics partners.
  • App activity & diagnostics — feature usage, in-app events, and crash logs used to keep the App stable and improve it.

Processed on your device (used locally to provide call screening):

  • Call information (READ_CALL_LOG, READ_PHONE_STATE, CALL_PHONE) and contacts (READ_CONTACTS) are read on-device to identify incoming numbers, match them against your allow/block preferences, and screen calls. This data is used locally to deliver the screening feature and is not sold or uploaded to us.
  • Notification access (BIND_NOTIFICATION_LISTENER_SERVICE) and call-screening service (BIND_SCREENING_SERVICE) are used only to detect and act on incoming calls/notifications for screening.

2. How we use information

We use the information above to: provide and operate the call-screening features; show and measure advertising; run analytics and improve the App; detect fraud and keep the service secure; and comply with legal obligations. We do not use your contacts or call log for advertising.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information for money. We share data with the third-party partners listed in §3 to operate the App, for legal reasons, or in connection with a business transfer. If ads are personalized, certain U.S. state laws may treat ad-related data sharing as a "sale" or "sharing" — you may opt out as described in §3 and §7.

5. Legal bases (EEA/UK — GDPR)

We rely on: consent (personalized ads and analytics where required); legitimate interests (core functionality, security, improvement); and legal obligation where applicable.

6. Data retention

We keep data only as long as necessary for the purposes above or as required by law. Advertising and analytics identifiers are retained per each partner's retention periods; crash and diagnostic data are kept for a limited period.

7. Your rights

  • GDPR (EEA/UK): access, correct, delete, port, object, restrict, withdraw consent, and complain to a data protection authority.
  • CCPA/CPRA (US): know, delete, correct, opt out of sale/sharing and targeted advertising, and non-discrimination.
  • Other regions (LGPD, PIPEDA, etc.): equivalent rights where they apply.

Because the App has no account, please include your advertising ID and device details when you contact us to exercise a right, so we can locate any related data with our partners.

8. Children's privacy

The App is intended for users aged 18 and over and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it, consistent with COPPA and EEA age-of-consent rules.

9. International data transfers

Your data may be processed in countries other than yours, including the United States. Where required, we and our partners use appropriate safeguards (e.g. Standard Contractual Clauses).

10. Data security

We use encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and other reasonable measures to protect your data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

11. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy. Material changes will be posted with a new "Last updated" date; continued use of the App means you accept the updated Policy.
