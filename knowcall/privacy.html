Privacy Policy

App: Knowcall Effective: October 30, 2026 Last Updated: October 30, 2026

Effective date: October 30, 2026 Operator: Tranquil Mind Technology JSC ("we", "us") Contact: contact@tranquilmind.co

Knowcall helps you see who is calling, block spam calls and read messages that were recalled or not yet seen on your Android device. This policy explains what the app accesses, what stays on your device and what is shared with service providers.

1. Information we collect

Stays on your device (not sent to our servers):

  • Contacts — read from your address book to show caller names and to power the Contact tab and caller lookup.
  • Notification content — when you grant Notification Access, the app reads notifications from messaging apps (WhatsApp, Messenger/Facebook, Instagram, Telegram and others) and stores the sender title, text, source app and time in local app storage (up to 500 items) to show them in the Unseen tab. You can clear them at any time.
  • Block list and settings — your blocked numbers, theme and preferences are kept in local app storage.

Collected automatically by third-party SDKs:

  • Device and app information (device model, OS version, app version, language, approximate location derived from IP address), crash and performance data, and app usage events.
  • Advertising ID (Google Advertising ID) for advertising, attribution and measurement. You can reset or opt out of it in your Android settings.
  • Purchase status when you buy through Google Play (the payment itself is processed by Google; we do not receive your card details).

We do not use call log or SMS permissions, and we do not request READ_CALL_LOG or READ_PHONE_STATE.

Permissions the App declares (Android): android.permission.INTERNET (network access for ads, analytics, remote configuration and purchases); com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID (read the Advertising ID for ads and attribution); android.permission.READ_CONTACTS (show names of callers and contacts from your address book); android.permission.CALL_PHONE (place a call directly when you tap a number in the app); android.permission.REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS (ask to exclude the app from battery optimization so caller screening and the message tracker keep running); android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS (show reminders and alerts); android.permission.RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED (reschedule local notifications after the device restarts); android.permission.BIND_NOTIFICATION_LISTENER_SERVICE (let the Android system bind the app's notification listener so it can read notifications and show unseen or recalled messages, only after you enable Notification Access). The app also registers a call screening service (protected by android.permission.BIND_SCREENING_SERVICE) so that, if you select Knowcall as your caller ID and spam app, Android can pass it the incoming number to identify or block it.

2. How we use information

  • Identify callers and block numbers you choose to block.
  • Show unseen and recalled messages from notifications.
  • Operate, secure and improve the app (analytics, crash reports, remote configuration).
  • Show advertising and measure its performance and attribution.
  • Process purchases and restore entitlements.

4. Sharing

We do not sell your contacts or your notification content. Data above is shared only with the service providers listed, as needed to run the app, or when required by law.

5. Retention and deletion

Local data (contacts cache, unseen messages, block list, settings) remains on your device until you clear it in the app, revoke the permission or uninstall the app. Analytics and advertising data held by providers is retained according to their policies. To request deletion of data we may hold about you, email contact@tranquilmind.co.

6. Your choices

  • Revoke Contacts, Phone or Notification permissions, or Notification Access, in Android Settings at any time.
  • Reset or delete your Advertising ID in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads.
  • Change consent choices in the app where the consent form is offered.

7. Children

Knowcall is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. International transfers and your rights

Our providers may process data in countries other than yours. Depending on your location (e.g. GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, LGPD, PIPEDA) you may have rights to access, correct, delete, port or object to processing of your data and to withdraw consent. Contact contact@tranquilmind.co to exercise them.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical measures; local data is held in app-private storage. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

10. Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above will change and material changes will be shown in the app or on this page.
