Privacy Policy

App: Knowcall Effective: 2026-08-30 Last Updated: 2026-08-30

Knowcall ("the App", "we", "us", "our"), operated by Tranquil Mind Technology JSC, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices and rights you have. By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

Knowcall is a caller ID, call-screening and spam-blocking utility that helps you identify incoming calls, manage your contacts and block unwanted numbers. You do not need to create an account to use it.

1. Information we collect

Data processed on your device (does not leave your device):

  • Contacts — read from your device (with your permission) to display caller names, power the Contacts tab and let you block numbers. Your contact list is used locally on the device to match callers and is not uploaded to our servers.
  • Call information / phone state — used to identify and screen incoming calls, block spam and let you place calls from within the App. Call-log and phone-state data are processed on the device for these features.

Data collected automatically and shared with our service providers:

  • Device & advertising identifiers (including the Android Advertising ID) — for advertising and analytics.
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address — for advertising, measurement and regional compliance.
  • App activity & diagnostics — in-app events, feature usage and interactions used for analytics and to improve the App.
  • Crash logs & diagnostics — to detect and fix stability problems.
  • Purchase status — whether you hold an active premium subscription, to unlock ad-free/premium features.

We do not require you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email or password unless you contact us directly by email.

2. How we use information

  • Provide and operate the App's caller ID, screening and blocking features.
  • Display and manage your contacts on the device.
  • Serve and measure advertising (for non-subscribers).
  • Analytics and product improvement.
  • Fraud prevention and security.
  • Process and validate premium subscriptions.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information for money. We share data with the third-party service providers listed in Section 3 to operate advertising, analytics and purchases; for legal reasons; or in a business transfer. Where personalized advertising is used, some U.S. state laws may treat this as a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information — you can opt out via the in-app consent controls and your device advertising settings.

5. Legal bases (EEA/UK — GDPR)

  • Consent — for personalized ads and analytics where required.
  • Legitimate interests — core functionality, security and improvement.
  • Contract / legal obligation — processing purchases and complying with law.

6. Data retention

We keep data only as long as necessary for the purposes above or as required by law. Advertising and analytics identifiers are retained by our partners for their own defined periods; crash and diagnostic data are kept for a limited period.

7. Your rights

  • GDPR (EEA/UK): access, correct, delete, port, object, restrict, withdraw consent, and complain to a data protection authority.
  • CCPA/CPRA (California): know, delete, correct, opt out of sale/sharing and targeted advertising, and non-discrimination.
  • Other regions (LGPD, PIPEDA, etc.): equivalent rights under local law.

Because the App has no user accounts, when you exercise these rights please contact us by email and include your device advertising ID so we can identify the relevant data.

8. Children's privacy

The App is intended for users aged 18 and over and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it, consistent with COPPA and applicable age-of-consent rules.

9. International data transfers

Your data may be processed in countries other than your own, including the United States. Where required, we and our partners rely on appropriate safeguards (e.g. Standard Contractual Clauses).

10. Data security

We use encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

11. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy. Material changes will be posted here with a new "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated Policy.
