3. The service

Knowcall is a caller-identification and call-screening utility for Android. It matches incoming and searched phone numbers against the contacts saved on your device, lets you maintain your own spam and block lists, keeps a local log of message notifications you have not yet seen, and provides contacts, favourites and dialling shortcuts.

What the App is and is not. Knowcall identifies callers using only the contacts stored on your own device and the lists you create yourself. It does not query any online directory, crowd-sourced database or third-party register of phone numbers, and it therefore cannot identify a number that you have never saved. Any expectation that the App will name an unknown caller is outside what the App does.

Functional requirements and limits. Call screening requires Android 10 or later and requires you to set Knowcall as your device's default caller-ID and spam application in system settings. Reading message notifications requires you to grant notification access in system settings. Both permissions can be withdrawn by you at any time, which will disable the corresponding feature. Android may restrict background execution or terminate the App to save power, and device manufacturers apply their own power-management rules; we cannot guarantee that every call will be screened or every notification captured.

No emergency or safety service. The App is not an emergency service and must not be relied upon for urgent, safety-critical or life-critical purposes. Blocking a number may prevent you from receiving calls you wanted; you are responsible for the numbers you add to your lists. Always use your device's native dialler for emergency calls.
