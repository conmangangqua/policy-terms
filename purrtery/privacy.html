Privacy Policy

App: Purrtery Effective: October 29, 2026 Last Updated: October 29, 2026

Effective date: 2026-10-29 Operator: Tranquil Mind Technology JSC ("we", "us") Contact: contact@tranquilmind.co Policy URL: https://policy.tranquilmind.co/purrtery/privacy

Purrtery ("the App") lets you decorate your phone's status bar and battery indicator with cute cat emoji and stickers, and show a decorative pet on your screen. This policy explains what the App accesses, what leaves your device, and who receives it.

1. Information stored on your device only

Your customisation choices (selected emoji, stickers, pet, language, theme, favourites, blocked-app list and reminder settings) are stored locally on your device. We do not receive them.

2. Information that may leave your device

When you use the App, the following may be sent to third parties through the SDKs listed in Section 3:

  • Advertising ID (Google AD_ID) and device/app identifiers — for ad delivery, measurement and install attribution.
  • IP address, approximate location derived from IP, device model, OS version, language, app version — for ad delivery, fraud prevention and analytics.
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, ad-load results) — for ad measurement and mediation.
  • Usage events, crash and performance diagnostics — for analytics and stability (Firebase).

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data, or precise location. The App has no user accounts.

4. Permissions

Permissions the App declares (Android): android.permission.INTERNET (connect to the network to load ads, settings and diagnostics), android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE and android.permission.ACCESS_WIFI_STATE (read Wi-Fi/mobile signal level so the status-bar icons can be redrawn; no network content is read), android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS (show reminder notifications), android.permission.SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM (deliver reminders at the time you set), android.permission.SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW (draw the pet and notch overlay over other apps), android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE and android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE (keep the pet overlay running in the background at your request), android.permission.RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED (restore reminders and the overlay after the phone restarts), android.permission.VIBRATE (haptic feedback), com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID (access the Advertising ID for ads and attribution), and android.permission.BIND_ACCESSIBILITY_SERVICE (system binding of the App's accessibility service, below).

Accessibility service. The core feature uses Android's AccessibilityService to draw emoji over the real status bar. It is configured with canRetrieveWindowContent=false, so it does not read screen content, and listens only to window-state changes to know which app is open, for the "Not-Allowed App" feature. This information is processed on the device and is not collected or shared. You can turn the service off at any time in Android Settings > Accessibility.

The App lists launchable apps on your device (via a queries declaration, not QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES) to show the "Not-Allowed App" picker. The list stays on your device.

5. Consent and your choices

  • In regions where required (EEA, UK), a Google UMP consent form is shown and you can change your choice later.
  • You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in Android Settings > Google > Ads.
  • You can revoke notification and overlay permissions in system settings.
  • Uninstalling the App removes all locally stored data.

6. Retention and security

Data held by third parties is retained under their own policies. Data in transit is sent over the network using the SDKs' standard protections. No method is completely secure.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. Contact us if you believe a child has provided data and we will help get it removed.

8. Your rights

Depending on your location (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, LGPD, PIPEDA and similar), you may request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of data about you, and object to or withdraw consent for processing. Because we do not hold account data, requests mostly concern advertising and analytics identifiers; email contact@tranquilmind.co and we will respond within the time required by law. We do not sell personal information for money; ad networks may receive identifiers for advertising, which some laws treat as "sharing" — you can opt out as described in Section 5.

9. International transfers

Our partners may process data in countries other than yours, with safeguards required by applicable law.

10. Changes

We will update this page and its effective date when practices change, for example when an ad network is added.
