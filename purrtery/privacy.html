Privacy Policy

App: Purrtery Effective: 29/09/2026 Last Updated: 29/09/2026

Purrtery ("the App", "we", "us", "our"), operated by Tranquil Mind Technology JSC, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices and rights you have. By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

Purrtery is a personalization app for Android. It replaces your status-bar battery icon with an emoji, decorates your status bar with stickers, a notch and custom icon styles, and can show a small pet walking on your screen. You do not need to create an account to use it, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or any other contact details inside the App.

1. Information we collect

1.1 Information that stays on your device

The following is created and stored only on your device and is not sent to us:

  • Your design choices: selected emoji, stickers, notch, colors, sizes, icon styles, pet settings, favourites and recent items, the "battery troll" percentage, and your language setting.
  • Your Excluded Apps selection (the apps in which the decoration hides).
  • Battery level and charging state, which the App reads from the system to draw your battery icon.

Uninstalling the App or clearing its data deletes this information.

1.2 Accessibility Service

Purrtery uses Android's Accessibility Service API for two purposes only:

  1. to draw your chosen decorations on top of the status bar; and
  2. to detect which app is currently in the foreground, so the decoration can hide automatically in the apps you have excluded.

The service is configured so that it cannot read the content of your screen (canRetrieveWindowContent = false). It only receives "window changed" events. It does not read your messages, passwords, notifications or anything you type, and it does not record your app usage history. The foreground-app information is used on your device in real time and is not sent to us or to any third party by our code. The App asks for your consent on a disclosure screen before you enable the service. You can turn it off at any time in your device's Accessibility settings, but the status-bar decoration will then stop working.

1.3 Display over other apps

The pet feature uses the "display over other apps" permission and a foreground service to keep the pet visible on your screen. The App does not collect any data through this permission.

1.4 Information collected automatically by third-party services

The App includes advertising, attribution, analytics and crash-reporting services (listed in Section 3). When you use the App, these services may automatically collect:

  • Device and advertising identifiers, such as the Android Advertising ID, the Firebase installation ID and other device or app-instance identifiers.
  • Approximate location, derived from your IP address (country or city level). The App does not request GPS or precise-location permission.
  • App activity: which screens and features you use, the ads you see and interact with, and session information.
  • Installed apps: the App can see the list of apps on your device that have a launcher icon, so you can choose which apps to exclude. The App itself uses this list only on your device. However, advertising and attribution SDKs included in the App may be able to access app-related information on the device. For that reason, and in line with our Google Play Data safety declaration, we treat installed-apps information as collected and shared with advertising partners.
  • Device and technical information: device model, operating-system version, language, screen size, network type, app version.
  • Crash logs and diagnostics: stack traces, device state at the time of a crash, and performance data.

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, messages, precise location or payment information.

1.5 Information you choose to send us

If you email us at contact@tranquilmind.co, we receive your email address and whatever you include in the message. The in-app "Feedback" screen does not currently send its contents to us.

2. How we use information

We use information to:

  • provide and operate the App, including drawing your decorations and hiding them in excluded apps;
  • show ads, including rewarded ads that unlock items for your current session, and measure ad performance and attribution;
  • understand how the App is used, fix crashes and improve features (analytics and crash reporting);
  • control features remotely (for example, ad frequency) through Firebase Remote Config;
  • detect and prevent fraud, invalid ad traffic and abuse; and
  • comply with legal obligations.

Ad consent

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland (and in other regions where the law requires it), the App shows a consent form through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) when it starts, before personalized ads are shown. You can accept or reject personalized advertising. If you reject it, you may still see non-personalized ads, which are based on context and approximate location rather than your profile. To change your choice, contact us at contact@tranquilmind.co, or clear the App's data or reinstall the App so that the consent form appears again.

Resetting or limiting your advertising ID

You can reset or delete your Android Advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, at any time in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device). After you delete it, apps receive a string of zeros instead of your advertising ID.

4. How we share information

We do not sell personal information for money. We share information:

  • with the third-party service providers and advertising partners listed in Section 3, for the purposes described above;
  • when required by law, legal process or a government request, or to protect the rights, safety and property of our users, us or others; and
  • with a successor entity in connection with a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, subject to this Policy.

U.S. state privacy laws: because the App shows personalized ads, disclosing advertising identifiers and related data to advertising partners may count as a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information, or "targeted advertising", under laws such as the CCPA/CPRA. You can opt out as described in Section 7.

5. Legal bases (EEA/UK — GDPR)

  • Consent: personalized advertising and related tracking, where consent is required. You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legitimate interests: operating and securing the App, crash reporting, basic analytics to improve the App, non-personalized ads that fund a free app, and fraud prevention.
  • Legal obligation: where we must process data to comply with law.

6. Data retention

  • Design choices and Excluded Apps settings stay on your device until you delete them or uninstall the App.
  • Analytics data is kept by Google Firebase for the retention period configured in our Firebase project (at most 14 months).
  • Crash reports are kept by Firebase Crashlytics for up to 90 days.
  • Advertising and attribution partners keep data according to their own retention policies (see Section 3).
  • Emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to handle your request and meet legal requirements.

7. Your rights

EEA/UK (GDPR): you have the right to access, correct, delete or port your personal data, to restrict or object to processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

California and other U.S. states (CCPA/CPRA and similar laws): you have the right to know what personal information is collected, used and shared; to delete or correct it; to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information and of targeted advertising; and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights. To opt out, reset or delete your advertising ID or turn on "opt out of ads personalization" on your device (Section 3), and/or email us.

Other regions (for example Brazil – LGPD, Canada – PIPEDA, Vietnam – Decree 13/2023/ND-CP): you have the rights granted by your local law.

How to make a request: email contact@tranquilmind.co. Because the App has no accounts, we cannot identify your data by name. Please include your device's Advertising ID (or the app-instance ID, if you know it) and your device model, so we and our partners can locate the data. We will respond within the time required by applicable law. You can also delete all locally stored data at any time by clearing the App's data or uninstalling it.

8. Children's privacy

The App is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. If a higher age of digital consent applies where you live (for example, up to 16 in parts of the EEA), you must be at least that age or have a parent's or guardian's consent. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us and we will delete it.

9. International data transfers

Our service providers may process data in countries other than your own, including the United States and Vietnam. Where required, transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses used by our providers.

10. Data security

Data sent by the App and its SDKs is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. We take reasonable measures to protect information, but no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.
