Privacy Policy – Yuki Chat

App: Yuki: AI Chat Companion Effective: October 27, 2026 Last Updated: October 27, 2026

Effective date: 2026-10-27 Operator: Tranquil Mind Technology JSC ("we", "us", "our") Contact: contact@tranquilmind.co

Yuki Chat ("the App") is an AI companion chat app for Android. This Privacy Policy explains what data the App handles, who receives it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we handle

1.1 Chat messages (sent off your device)

When you chat with a companion, the App sends to our chat service (a Cloudflare Worker, chat-api): the language code, the companion's id, name and description, the selected mood, and your most recent messages in the conversation (up to 6 messages as context) plus the new message. The service returns the AI reply. If that service is disabled or fails, the App falls back to an AI model provider (DeepSeek, api.deepseek.com by default, configurable remotely), sending the companion persona, language, mood and up to 10 recent messages. Do not share sensitive personal information in chats.

1.2 Text read aloud (text-to-speech)

When you play an AI reply aloud, the App sends the reply text to the Google Cloud Translation API (language detection only) to choose the voice language. The speech itself is produced by the text-to-speech engine installed on your device.

1.3 Voice input

If you use voice input, the App requests the microphone permission (RECORD_AUDIO) and uses the speech recognition service of your Android device (via the speech_to_text plugin). Audio processing is performed by that system service (typically Google), under its own privacy policy. We do not receive or store your audio.

1.4 Photos

If you choose a profile picture from your gallery, the image is picked through the Android image picker for use inside the App. We do not upload it to our servers.

1.5 Purchase data (Google Play Billing) – NEW

Premium subscriptions (product ids yuki_premium_weekly, yuki_premium_monthly, yuki_premium_yearly) are sold through Google Play Billing. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play; we do not receive or store your card or payment details. The App uses Google Play Billing (in_app_purchase / flutter_iap) to:

  • show plans and localized prices;
  • receive the purchase result (purchased / restored / canceled / error);
  • check whether you still hold an active subscription when the App opens and each time it returns to the foreground (queryPastPurchases), and when you tap "Restore purchases".

The resulting Premium on/off status is stored locally on your device (shared preferences). The App does not currently send purchase tokens to our own server for verification. Purchase events (such as paywall views and purchase results) are also recorded as analytics events (see 1.6). Google processes purchase data under the Google Play Terms and Privacy Policy.

1.6 Analytics, crash and device data (automatic)

We use Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Remote Config (Google LLC), and Adjust (Adjust GmbH) for attribution and event measurement. These may process device identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID and app instance id), device model, OS version, app version, country/language, screen and purchase events, crash logs and IP address. Screen auto-reporting in Firebase Analytics is disabled; the App logs its own screen and event names.

1.7 Advertising

Free use of the App may show ads served through Google AdMob with mediation adapters: Meta Audience Network, Pangle (ByteDance), Liftoff Monetize (Vungle), Mintegral, Unity Ads and AppLovin. These providers may collect advertising ID, IP address, device and ad-interaction data to serve and measure ads, and may use it for personalized advertising where permitted. Users in the EEA/UK are shown a consent form (Google UMP / IAB TCF) and can change their choice where the form allows. Ads can be limited with Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

1.8 Data stored only on your device

Language, onboarding state, chosen companions, chat history, Premium status and counters are stored locally using on-device storage. Deleting the App's data or uninstalling removes them. The App can also delete all messages with a companion from within the chat screen.

1.9 Notifications

The App may show local notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED to reschedule them after restart). You can disable them in system settings.

1.10 Account

The App has no account or sign-in and does not ask for your name, email or phone number.

2. How we use data

To provide AI replies; to provide, verify and restore Premium; to show and measure ads; to measure usage, fix crashes and improve the App; to keep the community safe (see section 6); and to comply with law. We do not sell your personal information for money.

4. Retention

Local data stays until you clear it or uninstall the App. Data held by third parties follows their retention policies. We have not defined a fixed retention period for chat requests processed by our chat service; see the open items noted by the maintainers.

5. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live (for example GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, LGPD), you may request access, correction, deletion or restriction of personal data we hold, and object to certain processing. Because the App has no account, we may need identifiers from you to locate data. Contact contact@tranquilmind.co. You can manage subscriptions in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions, reset your advertising ID in Android settings, and revoke microphone, notification and photo permissions in system settings.

6. Child safety (CSAE)

The App is not intended for children under 13 (or under the age required in your country), and users must be adults to use romantic or mature companion content. We prohibit child sexual abuse and exploitation. Users can report a message in the App ("Report content"); we remove violating content and cooperate with authorities where required. Report child-safety concerns to contact@tranquilmind.co.

7. Security

Data in transit to our chat service and to the providers above uses HTTPS. No method of transmission or storage is fully secure.

8. International transfers

Providers listed above may process data in countries other than yours.

9. Changes

We may update this Policy; the effective date at the top will change. Continued use means you accept the update.
