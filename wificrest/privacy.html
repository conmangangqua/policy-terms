Privacy Policy

App: Wificrest Effective: 2026-10-14 Last Updated: 2026-10-14

Wificrest ("the App", "we", "us", "our"), operated by Tranquil Mind Technology JSC, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices and rights you have. By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

Wificrest is a Wi‑Fi utility that scans the devices on your network, saves hotspots for offline map use, measures signal strength and connection speed, and generates strong Wi‑Fi passwords and QR codes. You do not need to create an account to use it, and the App has no login, no user profiles and no user-to-user communication.

1. Information we collect

Information that stays on your device (we never receive it):

  • Wi‑Fi scan results — network names (SSID), BSSID, signal strength, channel and security type returned by the operating system when you scan.
  • Devices discovered on your local network — IP/MAC addresses and host names of devices on the same Wi‑Fi you are connected to.
  • Saved hotspots and map pins — the locations you choose to save, plus any note or label you add, stored in the App's local database (Hive).
  • Generated passwords and QR codes — created on-device; copied to your clipboard or saved as an image only when you ask.
  • Camera images used for QR scanning — frames are processed in memory to decode a Wi‑Fi QR code and are not stored or uploaded.
  • Precise location — Android requires location permission to return Wi‑Fi scan results and to place a hotspot pin on the map. Your coordinates are used on-device and are not transmitted to us.
  • App settings and preferences — language, theme, reminder timers.

Information collected automatically and sent to our service providers:

  • Device and app diagnostics — device model, OS version, app version, language, country, and crash reports with stack traces (Firebase Crashlytics).
  • App activity and analytics events — screens opened, features used, session start/stop, and a Firebase-generated app instance identifier (Firebase Analytics).
  • IP address (and the approximate, city-level region derived from it) — processed by our service providers when the App contacts them.
  • Network requests needed for features you trigger — map tile requests to OpenStreetMap and download/upload test traffic to Cloudflare's public speed-test endpoints.

The App does not contain any advertising SDK, does not use an advertising ID, does not ask for your name, email, phone number or contacts, and does not upload your Wi‑Fi passwords, SSIDs or network device list to any server.

2. How we use information

  • Provide and operate the App's features (scanning, mapping, speed test, password/QR tools).
  • Remember your saved hotspots, settings and preferences on your device.
  • Diagnose crashes, fix bugs and improve stability and performance.
  • Understand which features are used, in aggregate, to prioritise improvements.
  • Protect against abuse, fraud and security incidents.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling or automated decision-making that produces legal effects.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information for money, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. We share the limited diagnostic and analytics data described above with the service providers listed in Section 3, acting on our instructions. We may also disclose information if required by law, legal process or a valid government request, or in connection with a merger, acquisition or sale of assets (you will be notified of any change in ownership of your data).

5. Legal bases (EEA/UK — GDPR)

  • Legitimate interests — operating the App, keeping it secure and stable, understanding aggregate usage to improve it.
  • Consent — device permissions you grant (location, camera, notifications), which you can withdraw at any time in system settings, and analytics where local law requires consent.
  • Legal obligation — retaining information where required by law.

6. Data retention

Data stored on your device (hotspots, settings, generated passwords) stays until you delete it, clear the App's data, or uninstall the App. Crash and diagnostic data is retained by Firebase Crashlytics for a limited period (up to 90 days for crash data). Analytics data is retained according to our Firebase Analytics retention setting (up to 14 months) and then deleted or aggregated.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, export, restrict or object to the processing of your personal information, and to withdraw consent at any time.

  • EEA/UK (GDPR): access, rectification, erasure, portability, restriction, objection, withdrawal of consent, and the right to complain to your local data protection authority.
  • California (CCPA/CPRA): the right to know, delete and correct, to opt out of sale/sharing (we do neither), and not to be discriminated against for exercising your rights.
  • Other regions (LGPD, PIPEDA and similar laws): equivalent rights under your local law.

Most data never leaves your device — you can erase it yourself by deleting items in the App, clearing app data, or uninstalling. For the diagnostic/analytics data held by our providers, email us at contact@tranquilmind.co. Because the App has no accounts, please include your device model, OS version and the approximate dates of use so we can locate the relevant records.

8. Children's privacy

The App is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under the applicable age of digital consent in your country, which may be up to 16 in the EEA). If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact contact@tranquilmind.co and we will delete it.

9. International data transfers

Our service providers may process data in countries other than your own, including the United States. Where required, such transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

10. Data security

Data in transit is protected with HTTPS/TLS. On-device data is stored in the App's private storage area, which other apps cannot read on a non-rooted device. We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures, but no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

The App displays map data from OpenStreetMap and Google Maps and may open external links (for example, the store page for rating the App). We are not responsible for third-party content, services or sites; please review their own policies.
