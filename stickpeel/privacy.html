Privacy Policy

App: Stickpeel Effective: 2026-10-09 Last Updated: 2026-10-09

Stickpeel ("the App", "we", "us", "our"), operated by Tranquil Mind Technology JSC, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices and rights you have. By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

Stickpeel is a sticker-maker app: you pick a photo from your gallery or take one with your camera, remove its background on your device, decorate it, and export the result as a sticker pack to WhatsApp or Telegram or as a .zip file. You do not need to create an account, sign in, or give us your name, email or phone number to use it.

1. Information we collect

Information collected automatically and sent to our service providers

  • Device and advertising identifiers — Android advertising ID (AAID), Firebase app-instance ID, device model and manufacturer, OS version, app version, language and country, network type.
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address (country / region level only). The App does not request GPS and declares no location permission.
  • App activity and diagnostics — screens opened, features used, ad impressions, clicks and ad revenue events, session length, crash logs, stack traces and performance data.
  • Install attribution data — referrer and campaign information used to measure which marketing channel led to your install.
  • Purchase status — whether you hold an active subscription or lifetime unlock, and the transaction receipt validated by Google Play Billing. We never receive your card number; payment is handled entirely by Google.

Information that stays on your device and is never uploaded to us

  • Your photos and images. Pictures you pick from the gallery or capture with the camera are read, cut out and edited entirely on your device. Background removal runs locally with the open-source image_background_remover library on ONNX Runtime — there is no cloud image service, and your pictures are never sent to us or to any third party by the App.
  • Your sticker packs — the stickers, packs, tray icons, names and favourites you create, stored in the App's private storage and removed when you uninstall the App.
  • Your settings — language, onboarding state, notification-prompt state and similar preferences, stored in local app storage.
  • Installed-app check. To offer the right export button, the App asks Android whether WhatsApp, WhatsApp Business or Telegram is installed (declared in the manifest <queries> block). The answer is used on the device only and is not transmitted.

Information we do not collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, calendar, microphone or audio input, precise location, SMS or call logs. The App requests no such permissions.

2. How we use information

  • To provide and operate the App: import a photo, remove its background, build and export sticker packs.
  • To show advertising, including rewarded ads, frequency capping and ad measurement.
  • For analytics and product improvement (which features are used, where users drop off).
  • For crash reporting, stability and performance monitoring.
  • For fraud prevention, security and abuse detection, including invalid ad traffic.
  • To process and restore in-app purchases and subscriptions and to recognise paying users (so ads are hidden).
  • To show the on-device notifications and in-app review / update prompts you interact with.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not use your information for credit, employment, insurance or any automated decision-making with legal effect.

4. How we share information

  • We do not sell your personal information for money.
  • We share the data described above with the third-party providers listed in §3, strictly for the purposes stated.
  • We may disclose information where required by law, subpoena or valid legal process, or to protect our rights, users' safety or investigate fraud.
  • In a merger, acquisition or asset transfer, information may be transferred to the successor entity.
  • Sticker export. When you export a pack, the sticker image files are handed to the app you choose (WhatsApp, WhatsApp Business, Telegram, or any app you select in the system share sheet), or saved as a .zip you control. From that point the receiving app's own privacy policy applies. We do not upload your stickers to our servers.
  • U.S. state notice: where personalized advertising is enabled, the use of advertising identifiers for targeted advertising may be considered "sharing" or a "sale" under laws such as the CCPA/CPRA. You can opt out via the ad-consent form or the device-level advertising-ID controls described in §3.

5. Legal bases (EEA/UK — GDPR)

  • Consent — personalized advertising, marketing attribution and analytics where consent is required (withdrawable at any time via the consent form).
  • Legitimate interests — providing core functionality, security and fraud prevention, aggregate product improvement, non-personalized advertising.
  • Contract — processing your in-app purchase or subscription so we can deliver the paid features.
  • Legal obligation — retaining purchase and tax records where law requires.

6. Data retention

Analytics and crash data are retained for the retention windows configured in Firebase (typically up to 14 months for event data; shorter for crash and diagnostic data). Advertising and attribution partners retain identifiers according to their own policies (§3). Purchase records are retained as long as needed for entitlement restoration and for the periods required by tax and accounting law. Your photos, stickers, packs and settings stay on your device until you delete them in the App or uninstall it.

7. Your rights

GDPR / UK GDPR — you have the right to access, correct, delete, port, object to or restrict processing of your personal data, to withdraw consent, and to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

CCPA / CPRA — you have the right to know, delete and correct personal information, to opt out of sale/sharing and targeted advertising, and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

Other regions — comparable rights apply under LGPD (Brazil), PIPEDA (Canada) and similar laws.

To exercise any right, email contact@tranquilmind.co. Because the App has no accounts, we cannot identify you by name — please include your advertising ID, device model and Android/iOS version so we can locate and delete the relevant records. Content you created inside the App can be deleted by you at any time by deleting the pack or uninstalling the App. We respond within the time limits set by applicable law.

8. Children's privacy

The App is intended for users aged 13 and over (or the higher age of digital consent in your country) and is not directed to children. The App is not configured as child-directed for advertising purposes and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA where applicable). In line with COPPA, if we learn that we have collected such information we will delete it. If you believe a child has provided information to us, contact contact@tranquilmind.co.

The App contains no chat, messaging, comments, user profiles, social feed or any other way for users to communicate with one another or to publish content to other users, so it hosts no user-to-user interaction that could expose a minor to another user.

9. International data transfers

Your information may be processed in countries outside your own, including the United States, where our service providers operate. Where required, transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

10. Data security

Data in transit is encrypted with HTTPS/TLS. Your images and sticker packs are kept in the App's private storage on your device. We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Third-party content

Advertisements shown in the App come from third-party ad networks and may link to external websites or app stores that we do not control. Exporting a pack hands your files to another app you choose. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of those third parties — please review their policies.
