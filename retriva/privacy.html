Privacy Policy

App: Retriva Effective: 2026-08-28 Last Updated: 2026-08-28

Retriva ("the App", "we", "us", "our"), operated by Tranquil Mind Technology JSC, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices and rights you have. By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

Retriva saves incoming notifications from your messaging apps so you can review messages even after the sender deletes them, and helps you find and restore deleted photos, videos and files on your device. You do not need to create an account to use it, and all saved content stays on your device.

1. Information we collect

Content that stays on your device (NOT collected by us). Retriva reads notifications from the messaging apps you choose and stores the captured messages, photos, videos, documents, audio, voice notes and stickers locally on your device only (in the app's local database). Recovered media found on your device is likewise handled locally. This content is never uploaded to us or to any server, and we cannot access it.

Data collected automatically for stability. When the App crashes, Google Firebase Crashlytics collects crash logs and diagnostic information (e.g. device model, operating-system version, app version, and the technical state at the time of the crash). This is the only category of data that leaves your device.

We do not collect your name, email, contacts, precise location, or the content of your messages, and the App contains no advertising SDKs and no analytics beyond crash reporting.

2. How we use information

We use crash and diagnostic data only to detect, diagnose and fix stability problems and to improve the App. The content Retriva saves is used solely to display it back to you inside the App on your device.

3. Third-party services

The App integrates the following data-transmitting service:

The App contains no advertising SDKs, so there is no ad personalization, no ad-consent (UMP) flow, and no advertising identifier used for ads.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information for money, and we do not share the content Retriva saves with anyone — it never leaves your device. Diagnostic data is shared only with the service listed in Section 3 for the purpose described, or where required by law, or in connection with a business transfer.

5. Legal bases (EEA/UK — GDPR)

We rely on our legitimate interests in maintaining a secure, stable, and functioning App (crash diagnostics), and on legal obligation where applicable. We do not process data for advertising.

6. Data retention

Content saved by Retriva remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App; you can clear saved data at any time from within the App. Crash and diagnostic data is retained by Firebase Crashlytics for a limited period in line with Google's retention practices.

7. Your rights

Because the content Retriva saves never leaves your device, you control it directly — you can view, filter and delete it inside the App at any time.

For the diagnostic data described above, subject to applicable law you have the right to access, correct, delete, port, object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent and lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (GDPR); and to know, delete, correct, and opt out of the sale/sharing of personal information (CCPA/CPRA). Residents of other regions (e.g. LGPD, PIPEDA) have comparable rights. As the App has no accounts, please contact us at the email below and include your device model and app version so we can locate any diagnostic records.

8. Children's privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your region). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us diagnostic data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. International data transfers

Diagnostic data may be processed on servers outside your country, including in the United States, by Google. Where required, appropriate safeguards (e.g. Standard Contractual Clauses) apply.

10. Data security

Diagnostic data is transmitted using encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Content saved by Retriva remains within your device's app storage. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure.

11. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy; material changes will be posted here with a new date. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated Policy.
