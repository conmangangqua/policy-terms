Privacy Policy

App: Ember Drama Effective: October 29, 2026 Last Updated: October 29, 2026

Effective date: October 29, 2026 Operator: Tranquil Mind Technology JSC ("we", "us") Contact: contact@tranquilmind.co

Ember Drama ("the App") lets you watch short serialized dramas. This policy explains what information the App and the third-party services it uses may collect, and the choices you have.

1. Permissions the App declares (Android)

Permissions the App declares (Android): android.permission.INTERNET, android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID, android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS.

PermissionPurpose
android.permission.INTERNETLoad drama catalogue and video streams, fetch remote configuration, show ads, process subscriptions and send analytics/crash data.
android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATEDetect whether the device is online so the App can avoid failed requests and load ads only when connected.
com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_IDLet advertising and attribution services (Google AdMob, AppLovin mediation, Adjust, Meta) read the Android Advertising ID for ad delivery, measurement and install attribution. You can reset or delete the Advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONSAndroid 13+ runtime permission. The App asks on its Permission screen (shown after Welcome) so it can send local re-engagement reminders, for example when you have not opened the App for a day or have not finished onboarding. These reminders are scheduled on your device by the App (local notifications). Denying or later revoking this permission in system settings does not limit access to the App's content.

The App does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files.

2. Information we and our service providers handle

  • Device and usage data: analytics events, app version, device model/OS, crash reports and diagnostics (Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics).
  • Advertising and attribution data: Advertising ID, IP address and ad interaction events (Google AdMob, AppLovin mediation, Adjust, Meta attribution).
  • Integrity signals: Firebase App Check (Play Integrity) tokens and freeRASP (Talsec) device-integrity/threat signals, used to protect the service against tampering and abuse.
  • Purchase data: subscriptions are processed by Google Play Billing; we receive purchase status, not your payment card details.
  • Local data: preferences and viewing state are stored on your device (shared preferences / app storage). Notification reminders are scheduled locally.

We do not ask you to create an account in the App.

4. Advertising choices

Where required, the App shows a consent form (Google UMP) before personalized ads. Subscribers to the paid plan have ads removed. You can limit ad personalization via Android Settings → Google → Ads.

5. Retention and security

Analytics and crash data are retained by the providers according to their own schedules. Data stored locally is removed when you clear app data or uninstall the App. We use HTTPS for network requests.

6. Your rights

Depending on your region (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, LGPD, PIPEDA), you may request access, correction, deletion or restriction of personal data, and may opt out of the sale/sharing of data for advertising. Contact contact@tranquilmind.co.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect their personal data; contact us to have it removed.

8. International transfers

Service providers may process data in countries other than your own, with safeguards required by applicable law.
