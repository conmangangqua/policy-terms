Privacy Policy

App: Bingepop Effective: 2026-11-17 Last Updated: 2026-11-17

Bingepop ("the App", "we", "us", "our"), operated by Tranquil Mind Technology JSC, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices and rights you have. By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

Bingepop is a vertical short-drama streaming app: you swipe through series whose episodes run one to two minutes each. You do not need to create an account to use it — there is no sign-up, login, or user profile, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or contacts.

1. Information we collect

The App collects the following automatically, through the third-party services listed in Section 3 and through our own streaming service:

  • Device and advertising identifiers — including the Android Advertising ID, the App Set ID, the Firebase installation/app-instance identifier, and an identifier generated by our attribution provider. Used for advertising, analytics, attribution, purchase validation, and fraud prevention.
  • Approximate location — derived by our advertising, analytics, and attribution providers from your IP address (country/region level, not GPS). The App does not request device location permission and does not collect precise location.
  • App activity — in-app events and interactions such as screens viewed, series and episodes opened, playback events, ads viewed and rewards earned, and paywall or purchase events. Used for analytics and advertising measurement.
  • Crash logs and diagnostics — crash reports, stack traces, device model, operating-system version, and app version, used to detect and fix stability issues.
  • Purchase history — if you buy a Bingepop VIP plan, we receive from Google Play Billing: the product you bought (weekly, monthly, or lifetime VIP), whether a free trial applies, the purchase and expiry dates, the renewal/cancellation status, the purchase token and order ID, and the price and currency. We use this only to grant, validate, restore, and end your VIP access, to prevent purchase fraud, and to measure the performance of our plans. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play — we do not receive or store your payment card or bank details, your billing address, or your Google account password.
  • App and device integrity signals — our anti-tamper component checks whether the App has been modified, repackaged, or is running under a debugger, hooking framework, emulator, or rooted device, and reports these signals (together with device model, operating-system version, app version, and app signature) to its provider and to our analytics. This protects the licensed video content from unauthorized copying.
  • AI recap content — when you ask for an AI recap of a series (the "Chuyện tới đâu rồi?" feature), the App sends to Groq, an AI inference provider, the series title, the subtitle text of the episodes you have already watched (the English subtitle track, or the first available track, up to about 1,600 characters per episode), and the language you selected. Groq returns a short written recap, which the App shows to you. Before anything is sent, the App checks the subtitle text on your device and does not send text it flags as sexual or graphic. The request goes directly from your device to Groq's API; like any internet request, it includes your IP address and standard technical request data. The App does not attach your advertising ID, device identifiers, name, or email to this request.
  • Streaming service request data — when you browse the catalogue or play an episode, your device requests content from our streaming service at epsdrm.com and from the content delivery networks that host the video files. Those requests include your IP address, the requested series/episode identifier, the language code you selected, and standard technical request data (user agent, timestamps), which are processed to deliver the content and kept in server logs for security and troubleshooting.

Stored only on your device (not collected by us): your continue-watching progress and watch history, the series you save or like, your coin balance, your interface-language and subtitle preferences (including bilingual subtitles), your ad-consent choices, a cached copy of your VIP status, and any ad-free window earned from a rewarded ad. These are held in local app storage and are removed when you clear the App's data or uninstall it. Search is performed on your device against the catalogue already downloaded — your search terms are not sent to us.

Permissions the App declares (Android): android.permission.INTERNET, android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS

  • android.permission.INTERNET — Connect to our servers and to the third-party services described in this policy (content delivery, AI recaps, advertising, analytics, purchase validation and crash reporting). It does not give the App access to your personal files.
  • android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS — Show notifications such as reminders and alerts from the App. Notifications are scheduled locally on your device; the App does not use a push-messaging token. On Android 13 and later the App asks for this permission at runtime; you can decline, or turn notifications off at any time in system settings, and the App keeps working.

Only POST_NOTIFICATIONS shows a system prompt. The App does not request access to your contacts, photos, files, camera, microphone or location.

2. How we use information

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the App and deliver the series and episodes you request.
  • Remember your progress, saved series, coins, and preferences on your device.
  • Serve and measure advertising, including personalized advertising where you have consented, and grant the rewards (coins, temporary ad-free time) earned from rewarded ads.
  • Generate an AI recap of the episodes you have watched when you ask for one, subject to the daily limits in the App.
  • Process, validate, restore, and expire VIP purchases, and keep advertising switched off while your VIP plan is active.
  • Analyze usage to understand and improve performance, content, features, and our plans.
  • Detect, prevent, and investigate fraud, abuse, piracy, tampering, and security issues.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

Google Play Billing and purchase history. All VIP purchases are made through Google Play Billing. Google processes your payment under its own terms and privacy policy and shares with us only the purchase record described in Section 1 (product, dates, status, purchase token, order ID, price and currency). The app checks your active purchases directly with Google Play on your device to validate and restore your VIP access; we do not run our own purchase server and do not link purchases to your name or email. We do not share your purchase history with advertising partners, and we do not sell it.

AI recaps (Groq). The AI recap feature sends the content described in Section 1 to Groq's API so that Groq can write the recap. Groq processes that content under its own privacy policy. The App keeps the recap only in memory for the current session and stores on your device only a daily counter of how many recaps you have requested. Reset or opt out of your advertising ID. You can reset or delete your Android Advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your device settings under Settings → Privacy → Ads (the path may vary by device). Doing so limits personalized advertising.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information for money. We share information with the third-party providers listed in Section 3 to operate the App, deliver content, process purchases, generate AI recaps, and serve advertising; for legal reasons (to comply with law or to protect rights, property, and safety); and in connection with a business transfer (e.g. merger or acquisition).

U.S. state privacy notice. Because the App serves personalized advertising, the sharing of advertising and device identifiers with ad partners may be considered a "sale" or "sharing" (for targeted advertising) under certain U.S. state laws. You can opt out using the ad-consent controls and your device's advertising-ID settings described above. Your purchase history is not shared with ad partners.

5. Legal bases (EEA/UK — GDPR)

  • Consent — personalized advertising and analytics where consent is required.
  • Legitimate interests — providing and securing the service, preventing fraud and content piracy, and improving the App.
  • Contract / legal obligation — processing, validating and restoring VIP purchases, keeping purchase records required by tax and consumer law, and meeting other legal requirements.

6. Data retention

We keep information only as long as necessary for the purposes described or as required by law. Advertising, attribution, and analytics identifiers are retained according to each partner's retention periods; crash and diagnostic data are retained for a limited period to diagnose issues; streaming server logs are kept for a short period for security and troubleshooting. AI recap requests are processed by Groq under its own retention terms; recaps are not stored by us beyond the current session. Purchase history is kept for as long as your VIP plan is active and afterwards for the period required by tax, accounting, and consumer-protection law or to handle refunds and disputes. Data stored locally on your device remains until you clear the App's data or uninstall it.

7. Your rights

  • GDPR (EEA/UK): access, correction, deletion, portability, objection, restriction, withdrawal of consent, and the right to complain to a data protection authority.
  • CCPA/CPRA (California) and other U.S. states: the right to know, delete, and correct, to opt out of sale/sharing and targeted advertising, and to non-discrimination.
  • Other regions (e.g. LGPD, PIPEDA): equivalent rights under applicable law.

To exercise your rights, contact us at contact@tranquilmind.co. Because the App has no accounts, please include your Android Advertising ID and relevant device details (and, for purchase-related requests, your Google Play order ID) so we can identify the associated data. You can also delete the data held on your device at any time by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling it. Deleting app data does not cancel a subscription — see "Managing your VIP plan" below.

Managing your VIP plan. You can view your purchase history and cancel or manage any Bingepop subscription at any time in the Google Play Store app under Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Refund requests are handled by Google Play under its refund policy.

8. Children's privacy

The App is intended for users aged 16 and older and is not directed to children. Some series contain mature dramatic themes. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16 (or the applicable minimum age of digital consent in your region). If you believe a child has provided us with information, contact us and we will delete it.

The App has no chat, messaging, comments, user profiles, or any other way for users to communicate with or send content to each other, and it does not allow users to upload content.

9. International data transfers

Your information may be processed in countries other than your own, including the United States. Where required, we and our providers rely on appropriate safeguards (such as Standard Contractual Clauses) for these transfers.

10. Data security

We use encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS), app-integrity and anti-tampering protections, and other reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.
