Privacy Policy

App: Crosspin Effective: 2026-11-26 Last Updated: 2026-11-26

Crosspin ("the App", "we", "us", "our"), operated by Tranquil Mind Technology JSC, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices and rights you have. By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

Crosspin is a custom crosshair overlay and builder for mobile games: you design a crosshair, save game profiles, preview it on your own screenshots, and show it as a floating overlay on top of other apps. You do not need to create an account to use it.

1. Information we collect

Information that stays on your device. Your crosshair designs, game profiles, settings and the screenshots you pick for preview are stored locally on your device. We do not upload them to our servers. Screenshots you choose for the preview, scope-calibration and colour-suggestion features, and any PNG image you pick as a crosshair, are processed on the device only. Share codes are plain text that you copy and paste yourself; the App does not upload or store them on a server.

Information collected automatically by our analytics and diagnostics services (leaves the device):

  • Device and app identifiers (such as an app-instance ID and a device identifier) used for analytics.
  • App activity and in-app interactions (for example, which screens are opened).
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address.
  • Crash logs and performance diagnostics (device model, OS version, app version, stack traces).

Permissions the App requests. The App declares the following Android permissions; none of them sends personal data to us:

  • INTERNET — to load app configuration and send analytics and crash reports.
  • PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS (Usage access, optional) — only to learn which app is currently in front, so the crosshair can be shown on the games you chose and hidden everywhere else. The App does not read what happens inside other apps, does not store this information and does not send it anywhere; it is processed on your device only and you can switch it off at any time in the system settings.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — to show reminders and the notification that keeps the overlay running.
  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM — to deliver scheduled reminders at the time you set.
  • SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW ("Display over other apps") — to draw the crosshair on top of your game. The overlay only draws your crosshair; it does not read, record, or capture what is on the screen.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE and FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE — to keep the crosshair overlay running while you play.

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, precise location, or audio.

2. How we use information

To operate and keep the App working; to understand which features are used and improve them; to find and fix crashes; to protect the App from abuse; and to comply with legal obligations. We do not use your information for advertising.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information for money. We share the diagnostics and analytics data above with Google as our service provider, when required by law or to protect rights and safety, and in connection with a business transfer.

5. Legal bases (EEA/UK — GDPR)

Legitimate interests (operating, securing and improving the App, crash diagnostics); consent where required for analytics; legal obligation where applicable.

6. Data retention

Local data stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App. Analytics and crash data are kept by our service providers only as long as necessary for the purposes above, under their standard retention periods.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, port, restrict, or object to processing of your personal data, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a data protection authority (GDPR/UK GDPR). Residents of California may know, delete, correct, and opt out of sale or sharing (CCPA/CPRA); we do not sell or share personal information for cross-context advertising. Similar rights exist under LGPD, PIPEDA and other laws. To make a request, email contact@tranquilmind.co and include your device model and, if possible, the app-instance details shown by your device so we can locate the data. You can also clear local data in your device's App settings or by uninstalling the App.

8. Children's privacy

The App is intended for users aged 13 and over (or the higher local age of digital consent) and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact contact@tranquilmind.co and we will delete it.

9. International data transfers

Analytics and crash data may be processed in the United States and other countries where our providers operate. Where required, safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses are used.

10. Data security

Data sent to our service providers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). We take reasonable measures to protect information, but no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.
