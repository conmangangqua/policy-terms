Privacy Policy

App: PulsarBooster Effective: July 30, 2026 Last Updated: July 30, 2026

PulsarBooster ("the App", "we", "us", "our"), operated by Tranquil Mind Technology JSC, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices and rights you have. By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

PulsarBooster is a free, ad-supported Android utility that shows real-time device stats (CPU, memory, temperature, network), organizes your installed games in one hub, and lets you save per-game display preferences such as refresh rate and screen density. You do not need to create an account to use it, and PulsarBooster does not have any login, chat, messaging, or user-to-user features.

1. Information We Collect

We do not ask you to provide personal information such as your name or email address. The App works without an account. The following information is collected automatically by the App and by the third-party services listed in Section 3, and transmitted off your device:

  • Device and advertising identifiers — including your Android advertising ID (AAID), and app-instance/device identifiers used for analytics, attribution, and advertising.
  • Approximate location — coarse location derived from your IP address by our advertising and analytics partners for ad delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention. We do not request GPS/precise-location permission and do not collect precise location.
  • App activity — in-app interactions and events (screens viewed, features used) used for analytics and advertising measurement.
  • Installed apps — to power the Game Hub, the App reads the list of apps installed on your device to detect and display your games. This list is processed on your device to build your library; device and usage signals handled by our advertising/attribution partners may also be associated with advertising and analytics.
  • Crash logs and diagnostics — crash reports and performance/diagnostic data used to keep the App stable and to fix problems.
Data processed only on your device (not collected by us). Live CPU/RAM/temperature readouts, the foreground-app name detected via Usage Access (used only to apply the profile you saved for a game), your saved per-game preferences, and the App's own cache. This information stays on your device and is not transmitted to us.

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide and operate the App — detect your games, apply your saved display preferences, and show device metrics and the optional on-screen HUD.
  • Advertising and measurement — serve ads (including an optional rewarded ad you choose to watch), including personalized ads where you have consented, and measure their performance and attribution.
  • Analytics and improvement — understand how features are used so we can improve the App.
  • Fraud prevention, security, and compliance — detect invalid traffic and abuse, and meet legal obligations.

We do not use your information to process purchases, because the App has no in-app purchases or subscriptions.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information for money. We share the data described above with the third-party services listed in Section 3 so they can provide advertising, analytics, attribution, and crash-reporting functions; for legal reasons (to comply with law or lawful requests); and in connection with a business transfer (e.g. merger or acquisition).

U.S. state privacy note. Because the App serves personalized ads, some U.S. state laws (e.g. California's CCPA/CPRA) may treat the sharing of identifiers and app-activity data with advertising partners for cross-context behavioral advertising as a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information. You can opt out of personalized ads as described in Section 3, which limits this sharing.

5. Legal Bases (EEA/UK — GDPR)

  • Consent — for personalized advertising and, where required, analytics.
  • Legitimate interests — for core functionality, security and fraud prevention, non-personalized advertising, and improving the App.
  • Legal obligation — where we must process data to comply with the law.

You may withdraw consent at any time (see Section 3); withdrawal does not affect processing carried out before withdrawal.

6. Data Retention

We keep information only as long as necessary for the purposes described above or as required by law. Advertising, analytics, and attribution identifiers are retained according to each partner's retention schedule (see their policies in Section 3). Crash and diagnostic data are retained for a limited period needed to diagnose and fix issues.

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the following rights:

  • EEA/UK (GDPR): access, rectification, erasure, portability, restriction, objection, withdrawal of consent, and the right to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.
  • California (CCPA/CPRA): to know, delete, and correct personal information; to opt out of the sale/sharing of personal information and of targeted advertising; and to non-discrimination for exercising these rights.
  • Other regions (e.g. Brazil LGPD, Canada PIPEDA): equivalent access, correction, and deletion rights.

To exercise these rights, contact us at contact@tranquilmind.co. Because the App has no account, please include your Android advertising ID and, if helpful, your device model and app version so we can locate and act on the relevant data. You can also exercise ad-choices directly through the controls in Section 3.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13 (nor under the applicable minimum age of digital consent in your region, such as 16 in parts of the EEA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under that age. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us at contact@tranquilmind.co and we will take appropriate steps to delete it. The App contains no chat, messaging, social, dating, or user-to-user communication features.

9. International Data Transfers

Your information may be processed in countries other than your own, including the United States, where our third-party providers operate. Where required, transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.
