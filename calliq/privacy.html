Privacy Policy

App: Calliq: Caller ID & Block Effective: October 30, 2026 Last Updated: October 30, 2026

Effective date: October 30, 2026 Operator: Tranquil Mind Technology JSC ("we", "us") Contact: contact@tranquilmind.co Package: com.tranquilmind.calliq.caller (Android)

Calliq: Caller ID & Block ("the App") helps you identify callers, block or silence unwanted calls, and see message notifications you have not opened. This policy explains what the App accesses, what stays on your device, and what is sent to third-party services.

1. Information we collect

1.1 Stored on your device only

  • Contacts — read to show your Contact tab and to recognise callers by name. Contact data is not uploaded to our servers.
  • Message notifications — if you turn on Notification Access, the App reads the title, text, source app package and time of notifications (e.g. WhatsApp, Messenger, Instagram, Telegram) and keeps up to 500 of them in local app storage for the "Unseen" tab. You can delete single items or clear all items in the App. This content is not uploaded by the App.
  • Block list and settings — stored locally (SharedPreferences).

1.2 Collected by third-party SDKs

  • Advertising and device identifiers — Google Advertising ID, device and network information, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, ad interaction data, used to serve and measure ads.
  • Analytics and diagnostics — app usage events, crash reports and device model/OS version via Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics.
  • Attribution — install and in-app event data via Adjust and the Meta (Facebook) SDK.
  • Configuration — Firebase Remote Config fetches app settings.

We do not read your call log or SMS. We do not request READ_CALL_LOG or READ_PHONE_STATE.

1.3 Permissions the App declares (Android)

Permissions the App declares (Android): android.permission.REQUEST_DELETE_PACKAGES, android.permission.INTERNET, com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID, android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, android.permission.READ_CONTACTS, android.permission.CALL_PHONE, android.permission.REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS, android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS, android.permission.RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED, android.permission.BIND_NOTIFICATION_LISTENER_SERVICE.

Purpose of each:

  • REQUEST_DELETE_PACKAGES — lets the App open the system dialog to uninstall an app you choose ("Uninstall anyway" button). No package list is sent off device.
  • INTERNET — load ads, remote configuration, analytics and crash reports.
  • AD_ID — lets ad and attribution SDKs read the Google Advertising ID.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — check wifi/cellular connectivity for ads and attribution.
  • READ_CONTACTS — show your contacts and identify callers by name.
  • CALL_PHONE — place a call when you tap a number in the App.
  • REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS — lets you ask Android to exempt the App from battery optimisation so call screening and the notification listener keep running. Granted only by you in a system dialog.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — show daily reminder notifications and status notifications (Android 13+).
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED — reschedule the daily reminders after the device restarts.
  • BIND_NOTIFICATION_LISTENER_SERVICE — lets the App's notification listener service receive notifications you chose to track (Notification Access, which you enable in system settings). Also requires you to grant access manually.

The App also registers a Call Screening service (android.permission.BIND_SCREENING_SERVICE, held by the system) so it can silence or reject calls on your block list on Android 10+.

2. How we use information

  • Identify callers and block/silence calls you choose.
  • Show message notifications you have not opened.
  • Show ads, measure ad performance and attribute installs.
  • Fix crashes, analyse usage and improve the App.
  • Send reminder notifications.

4. Consent and your choices

Where required (e.g. EEA/UK), the App shows a consent form (IAB TCF via Google UMP). You can reset your Android advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in Android settings. You can revoke Contacts, Notification Access, Notifications or battery exemptions at any time in system settings.

5. Retention and deletion

Local data stays until you delete it in the App, clear app storage or uninstall. Data held by third-party SDKs follows each provider's retention. To request deletion of data associated with you, email contact@tranquilmind.co.

6. Sharing

We do not sell your contacts or notification content. Advertising/analytics data goes to the providers in section 3.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect their data; contact us for removal.

8. Your rights

Depending on your location (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, LGPD, PIPEDA) you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, port or object, and to opt out of sale/sharing. Email contact@tranquilmind.co.

9. Security
