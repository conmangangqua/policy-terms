Privacy Policy

App: Chargeflare Effective: 2026-10-15 Last Updated: 2026-10-15

Chargeflare ("the App", "we", "us", "our"), operated by Tranquil Mind Technology JSC, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices and rights you have. By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

Chargeflare plays a full-screen charging animation when you plug in your device, shows battery information, and lets you set wallpapers. You do not need to create an account to use it, and the App has no login, no advertising SDK and no in-app purchases.

1. Information we collect

Information you provide Nothing. The App has no account, no sign-up form, no profile and no user-generated content upload.

Information that stays on your device Your selected charging animation, the wallpapers you choose, notification and display preferences, the cached weather result, and battery readings (level, charging state, temperature) are stored locally on your device (local database and app preferences). Battery data is read on-device to drive the animation and the battery info screen; it is not uploaded to us.

The App does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos library, microphone, camera or precise GPS location.

2. How we use information

  • Provide and operate the App: show the charging animation, battery information and wallpapers.
  • Remember your preferences between sessions.
  • Diagnose crashes and fix bugs (Crashlytics).
  • Measure aggregate feature usage so we can improve the App (Analytics).
  • Show local weather in the charging screen, if you enable it.
  • Security, fraud prevention and compliance with legal obligations.

We do not use your information for advertising or ad targeting: the App contains no advertising SDK.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information for money, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising or targeted advertising. We share data only:

  • with the service providers listed in Section 3, for the purposes stated there;
  • when required by law, legal process, or a valid government request;
  • to protect our rights, users' safety, or investigate fraud or abuse;
  • in connection with a merger, acquisition or asset transfer, where this Policy will continue to apply to the transferred data.

5. Legal bases (EEA/UK — GDPR)

  • Legitimate interests — operating the App, keeping it stable and secure, and improving it through aggregate analytics.
  • Consent — the approximate-location/weather feature (you grant the OS permission), notifications, and analytics where local law requires consent. You can withdraw consent at any time in your device settings.
  • Legal obligation — retaining records where law requires.

6. Data retention

  • Crash reports: retained by Firebase Crashlytics for up to 90 days (Google's standard retention).
  • Analytics events: retained according to our Firebase Analytics retention setting, by default up to 14 months, then deleted or aggregated.
  • Approximate location: used in the moment of the request to Open-Meteo and not stored by us; the resulting temperature is cached only on your device for up to 30 minutes.
  • On-device preferences: kept until you clear the app data or uninstall the App.

7. Your rights

EEA/UK (GDPR): access, rectification, erasure, portability, restriction, objection, withdrawal of consent, and the right to complain to your supervisory authority.

California (CCPA/CPRA): the right to know, delete, and correct personal information, the right to opt out of sale or sharing (we do neither), and the right not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

Other regions (LGPD, PIPEDA, and similar laws): equivalent rights to access, correct and delete your data.

Because the App has no account, we cannot identify you by name. To exercise a right, email us at contact@tranquilmind.co and include your device model, OS version and, if available, your Android advertising ID, so we can locate any related records. You can also:

  • revoke the location permission in Android Settings → Apps → Chargeflare → Permissions;
  • turn off notifications in Android Settings → Apps → Chargeflare → Notifications;
  • reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads;
  • delete all locally stored data by clearing app data or uninstalling the App.

8. Children's privacy

The App is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under the applicable age of digital consent in the EEA/UK). If you believe a child has provided information to us, contact contact@tranquilmind.co and we will delete it.

9. International data transfers

Our service providers (Google, Open-Meteo) may process data outside your country, including in the United States and the European Union. Where required, transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

10. Data security

Data transmitted by the App is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.
