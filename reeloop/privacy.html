Privacy Policy

App: Reeloop Effective: 2026-08-24 Last Updated: 2026-08-24

Reeloop ("the App", "we", "us", "our"), operated by Tranquil Mind Technology JSC, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices and rights you have. By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

Reeloop is a vertical short-drama streaming app: you swipe through bite-sized romance, revenge, billionaire and fantasy series whose episodes run one to two minutes each. You do not need to create an account to use it — there is no sign-up, login, or user profile.

1. Information we collect

The App has no registration, no messaging and no user-generated content, so we collect no name, email address, phone number, photos, contacts, microphone audio or precise GPS location. The App requests only the INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE and advertising-ID (AD_ID) permissions.

The following information is collected automatically and does leave your device:

  • Device and advertising identifiers — your Google Advertising ID (resettable), an app-instance identifier, and device model, manufacturer, operating-system version and language.
  • Approximate location — country and region only, derived from your IP address by our advertising and analytics providers. We do not collect GPS or street-level location.
  • App activity — which series and episodes you open, watch progress, the categories you browse and the terms you type into in-app search, screens viewed, and session length.
  • Crash logs and diagnostics — stack traces, device state at the time of a crash, and performance data.
  • Purchase status — whether a subscription is active, its product identifier, and its renewal state. Payment card details are handled entirely by Google Play; we never see or store them.
  • Device integrity signals — anti-tampering checks (for example, whether the device is rooted or the app package has been modified) used to protect against fraud and piracy.

Watch history, favourites and app settings are stored on your device only and are not uploaded to us.

2. How we use information

  • To provide and operate the App: deliver the video catalogue, resume playback where you left off, and keep the interface in your language.
  • To show advertising and to measure it: serve banner, interstitial, app-open, native and rewarded ads, cap how often they appear, and measure installs and ad performance.
  • To analyse and improve: understand which series and features are used so we can improve the catalogue and the app.
  • To protect the service: detect fraud, abuse, tampered builds and invalid ad traffic.
  • To process purchases: activate and restore ad-free subscriptions.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information for money. We share information only:

  • with the providers listed in section 3, for the purposes stated there;
  • when required by law, legal process or a valid governmental request;
  • to protect the rights, property or safety of our users, the public or us;
  • in connection with a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, subject to this Policy.

Where personalised advertising is enabled, the sharing of advertising identifiers with ad partners may be treated as a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information under certain United States state privacy laws. You may opt out by disabling personalised ads through the consent option in the App or by resetting or deleting your advertising ID as described above.

5. Legal bases (EEA/UK — GDPR)

  • Consent — personalised advertising and, where required, analytics. You may withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legitimate interests — operating the App, non-personalised advertising, security, fraud prevention and product improvement.
  • Performance of a contract — providing and restoring subscriptions.
  • Legal obligation — tax, accounting and lawful requests.

6. Data retention

We keep information only as long as necessary for the purposes described above or as required by law. Analytics and crash data are retained for a limited period under our providers' standard retention settings; advertising identifiers are retained by our ad partners according to their own policies, linked in section 3. Data stored on your device is removed when you clear the App's storage or uninstall it.

7. Your rights

EEA/UK (GDPR). You have the right to access, correct, delete, port, object to or restrict processing of your personal data, to withdraw consent at any time, and to lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority.

California (CCPA/CPRA). You have the right to know what personal information is collected, to delete it, to correct it, to opt out of its sale or sharing and of targeted advertising, and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

Other regions. Equivalent rights apply under Brazil's LGPD, Canada's PIPEDA and comparable laws.

Because the App has no accounts, we cannot identify you from a name or email address. To exercise a right, email contact@tranquilmind.co and include your Google Advertising ID, your device model and the approximate date you used the App, so we can locate the associated records. You can also delete the data held on your device at any time by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling it.

8. Children's privacy

The App is intended for users aged 16 and older and is not directed to children. Some series contain mature dramatic themes. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16, or the applicable minimum age of digital consent in your region. If you believe a child has provided us with information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. International data transfers

Your information may be processed in countries other than your own, including the United States. Where required, we and our providers rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

10. Data security

Traffic between the App and our servers and our providers is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS, and the App performs runtime integrity checks to detect tampering. We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures, but no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

11. Third-party content

The series available in the App are supplied by content licensors, and advertisements are supplied by the ad networks listed in section 3. We are not responsible for the content of third-party advertisements or for external sites they link to. Please review those third parties' own policies before interacting with them.
