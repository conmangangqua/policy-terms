Callverde helps you:

  • check incoming and searched phone numbers against your own contacts and your Block and Spam lists;
  • automatically reject calls from numbers on your Block list and silence calls from numbers on your Spam list;
  • view chat notifications from supported messaging apps in one place;
  • browse your contacts and place calls.

How it works and its limits:

  • Caller identification relies only on your device's contacts and the lists you create. Callverde does not provide a public caller database and cannot identify every unknown number.
