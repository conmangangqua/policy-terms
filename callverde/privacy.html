Privacy Policy

App: Callverde Effective: October 30, 2026 Last Updated: October 30, 2026

Effective date: 30 October 2026 Operator: Tranquil Mind Technology JSC ("we", "us") Contact: contact@tranquilmind.co

Callverde ("the App") is a caller-ID, message-tracker and spam-blocker app for Android. This policy explains what the App accesses, what leaves your device, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

On-device data (not uploaded by us). The App reads your contacts to match incoming/dialed numbers to names, screens incoming calls to silence or block numbers you have marked as spam, and reads notification content from supported messengers (Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, Telegram) to show messages you have not yet opened. Our App code contains no server upload of contacts, call data or notification content; this data is processed locally on your device. Preferences and reminder settings are stored locally.

Data collected through third-party SDKs.

  • Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics and Remote Config (Google): app usage events, crash reports, device model/OS version, app instance identifiers, and configuration values.
  • Advertising: Google AdMob with mediation adapters (Meta Audience Network, Pangle, Mintegral, AppLovin, Unity Ads, Bigo Ads). These collect the Android advertising ID, IP address, device and ad-interaction data to serve and measure ads. The App declares the AD_ID permission for this purpose.
  • Adjust: attribution and ad-revenue measurement (advertising ID, device and install data).
  • Meta (Facebook) SDK: app-event and advertiser-ID data as configured in the App.
  • Consent: where required (e.g. EEA/UK), a consent form based on the IAB TCF is shown before personalised advertising.

Permissions the App declares (Android): android.permission.INTERNET (network access for ads, analytics and remote configuration); com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID (read the advertising ID for ads and attribution); android.permission.READ_CONTACTS (match phone numbers to your saved contact names and show your contact list); android.permission.CALL_PHONE (place a call directly when you tap a number in the App); android.permission.REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS (let you exempt the App from battery optimisation so the notification listener and reminders are not stopped in the background; it is a settings request you can decline); android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS (show alerts and daily reminders); android.permission.RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED (re-schedule local reminders after the device restarts); android.permission.BIND_NOTIFICATION_LISTENER_SERVICE (system-level permission for the App's notification listener service, which reads notifications from supported messenger apps to show unseen messages; it is active only after you enable "Notification access" for Callverde in Android settings and you can revoke it at any time). The App also registers a call-screening service (android.permission.BIND_SCREENING_SERVICE, held by the system) to block or silence calls you have marked as spam. The App does not request call-log, SMS or phone-state permissions.

2. How we use information

To provide caller identification, spam blocking and unseen-message features; to show ads and measure their performance; to monitor stability and fix crashes; to deliver reminders; and to comply with law.

3. Sharing

We share data only with the service providers listed in section 1, acting under their own terms and privacy policies (Google, Meta, Adjust, Pangle, Mintegral, AppLovin, Unity, Bigo). We do not sell your contacts, call information or notification content.

4. Retention and deletion

Local data stays on your device until you clear the App's storage or uninstall it. Notification access, contacts and phone permissions can be revoked in Android Settings. Analytics/advertising data held by providers follows their retention schedules; you may reset your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. To request deletion of data we hold, email contact@tranquilmind.co.

5. Your rights

Depending on your region (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, LGPD, PIPEDA) you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or port your data, and to withdraw consent or opt out of targeted advertising. Contact contact@tranquilmind.co; we respond within the period required by applicable law.

6. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the higher age required locally) and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Security

Data in transit to our providers uses HTTPS. No method of storage is completely secure.

8. International transfers

Providers may process data outside your country, under safeguards such as standard contractual clauses where required.

9. Changes

We will post updates here and revise the effective date.
