Privacy Policy

App: Callverde Effective: 2026-10-14 Last Updated: 2026-10-14

Callverde ("the App", "we", "us", "our"), operated by Tranquil Mind Technology JSC, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices and rights you have. By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

Callverde is a call-recovery, messenger-tracking and spam-blocking tool for Android. It shows your contacts, keeps a list of notification previews from messaging apps you choose to monitor (the "Unseen" tab), and lets you block or silence incoming calls from numbers you add to a block or spam list. You do not need to create an account to use it.

1. Information we collect

Data that stays on your device. The core features of the App work locally. The following data is read or stored only on your device and is not uploaded to our servers or to any third party by the App:

  • Contacts. If you grant the Contacts permission (READ_CONTACTS), the App reads names and phone numbers from your address book to show your contact list and to recognise who is calling.
  • Notification content. If you enable Notification Access (the Android NotificationListenerService), the App reads the sender name, message preview text and time of notifications posted by messaging apps (such as WhatsApp, Messenger, Telegram and Instagram) and stores up to 500 recent entries on your device so you can see messages that were deleted or unseen. You can clear this list at any time inside the App, and you can revoke Notification Access in Android Settings.
  • Block and spam lists, preferences and settings. Phone numbers you add to your block or spam list, theme, language and similar choices are stored locally (Android SharedPreferences). The call-screening service compares incoming numbers with this local list; it does not send the number anywhere.
  • Phone permission. The App may ask for permission to place a call (CALL_PHONE) when you tap a number to call it. We do not read your call log or your SMS messages.
  • Local reminders. The App can post local notifications and re-schedule them after a reboot (POST_NOTIFICATIONS, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED).

Data that leaves your device. Through the analytics, crash-reporting and advertising services described in Section 3, the following may be collected automatically:

  • Device and advertising identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID, Adjust device identifiers).
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it.
  • Device information (model, operating system version, language, app version) and app activity (such as screens opened and interactions).
  • Crash logs and diagnostics.
  • Ad interaction and performance data (ads shown, clicked, revenue attribution).

We do not collect your name, email address or phone number, and we do not upload your contacts or the content of your notifications.

2. How we use information

  • To provide and operate the App and its call-blocking and message features on your device.
  • To show advertising and measure its performance.
  • To analyse usage, fix crashes and improve the App (Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config).
  • To protect against fraud, abuse and invalid ad traffic.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information for money. We share the device, advertising and usage data described in Section 1 with the third-party services listed in Section 3 so they can provide advertising, attribution, analytics and crash reporting. Because advertising and attribution partners may receive advertising identifiers, some U.S. state laws treat this as "sharing" for targeted advertising; you may opt out as described in Section 7. We may also disclose information when required by law or in a business transfer.

5. Legal bases (EEA/UK — GDPR)

  • Consent — personalised advertising and, where required, analytics and device-identifier access.
  • Legitimate interests — operating and securing the App, preventing fraud, fixing crashes and improving the App.
  • Legal obligation — where we must keep or disclose information by law.

6. Data retention

Contacts, notification entries, block lists and settings stay on your device until you delete them, clear the App's data or uninstall the App. Data held by our analytics, crash-reporting, attribution and advertising partners is retained according to their own policies and for no longer than necessary to provide their services or as required by law.

7. Your rights

  • EEA/UK (GDPR): access, correct, delete, port, object to or restrict processing, withdraw consent, and lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.
  • California and other U.S. states (CCPA/CPRA and similar): know, delete and correct personal information, opt out of sale/sharing and targeted advertising, and not be discriminated against for exercising these rights.
  • Other regions (LGPD, PIPEDA and similar): the rights provided by your local law.

Because the App has no accounts, we cannot identify you from our side by name. To exercise a right over data held by our servers or partners, email us at the address in Section 12 and include your Android advertising ID and device details. Data stored only on your device can be deleted by you directly in the App (clear the Unseen list, remove block/spam entries) or by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling it.

8. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the higher age of digital consent in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the App, contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

9. International data transfers

Our service providers may process data in countries other than your own, including the United States. Where required, we rely on safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

10. Data security

We use reasonable measures to protect information, including encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) for data sent by the third-party services. Contacts and notification entries remain on your device under Android's app sandbox. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure.
