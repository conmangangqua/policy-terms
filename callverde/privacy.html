Privacy Policy

App: Callverde Effective: 2026-10-28 Last Updated: 2026-10-28

Callverde ("the App", "we", "us", "our"), operated by Tranquil Mind Technology JSC, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices and rights you have. By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

Callverde is an Android app for caller identification against your own contacts, blocking and silencing unwanted calls, and gathering chat notifications in one place. You do not need to create an account to use it.

1. Information we collect

1.1 Information that stays on your device

The App's core features run on your device. We do not receive, upload or store the following on our servers:

DataWhy the App uses itWhere it stays
Contacts (names and phone numbers)To show whether an incoming or searched number belongs to one of your contacts, and to display your contact list and totals.Read on your device only. Never uploaded.
Block list and Spam list (phone numbers you add)So Android can reject calls from blocked numbers and silence calls from spam numbers.Stored locally on your device.
Incoming call numbersWhen Callverde is your default caller ID & spam app, Android's call screening service gives the App the number of an incoming call. The App checks it against your Block and Spam lists.Checked on your device in real time. Not stored or transmitted by us.
Chat notification content (sender name, message preview, app name and time of notifications from supported messaging apps)To show your chat notifications in the App's Unseen inbox. Only collected while you have granted notification access.Stored locally on your device (up to the most recent 500 items). You can delete it from the App.
Settings and preferences (language, theme, onboarding choices)To remember your choices.Stored locally on your device.

Uninstalling the App or clearing its data removes all of the information above from your device.

1.2 Information collected automatically by third-party services

The App includes advertising, analytics and crash-reporting services. These services automatically collect some information from your device and send it to their servers:

  • Device and advertising identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID, app instance IDs and Firebase installation IDs.
  • App interactions and usage events, such as screens viewed, buttons tapped, sessions, and ad impressions and clicks.
  • Approximate location, derived from your IP address by advertising partners. The App does not request GPS or location permission.
  • Information about installed apps. The App checks whether supported messaging apps are installed. Advertising and attribution partners may also collect app-related signals for ad measurement and fraud prevention.
  • Crash logs and diagnostics, such as stack traces, device model, OS version, memory state and app version.
  • Device and network information, such as device model, OS version, language, time zone, mobile carrier and connection type.

1.3 Information you send us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message.

2. How we use information

We use the information described above to:

  • provide and operate the App's features (caller identification, call blocking and silencing, the message tracker);
  • remember your settings;
  • show advertising and measure its performance, including personalized ads where you have consented or where consent is not required;
  • understand how the App is used and improve it (analytics);
  • detect, investigate and fix crashes and errors;
  • prevent fraud, invalid ad traffic and abuse, and keep the App secure;
  • configure features remotely (Firebase Remote Config), for example to prompt you to update the App;
  • comply with legal obligations.

4. How we share information

We do not sell personal information for money. We do not share your contacts, Block or Spam lists, call numbers or chat notification content with anyone, because that data never leaves your device.

We share or make available the automatically collected information described in Section 1.2 with:

  • the third-party services listed in Section 3, for the purposes described there;
  • authorities or other parties when required by law, or to protect rights, safety and security;
  • a successor entity in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, under the protections of this Policy.

U.S. state privacy laws. Allowing advertising partners to collect identifiers and usage data for personalized ("targeted" or "cross-context behavioral") advertising may count as a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information under some U.S. state laws, such as the California CCPA/CPRA. You can opt out by turning off ad personalization on your device (Section 3) or by contacting us (Section 13).

5. Legal bases (EEA/UK — GDPR)

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on:

  • Consent: for personalized advertising and related device storage and access, collected through the consent form, and for analytics where consent is required. You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legitimate interests: to operate the App, keep it secure, prevent fraud, fix crashes, show non-personalized ads and improve the App.
  • Legal obligation: where processing is needed to comply with law.

6. Data retention

  • On-device data (Section 1.1) stays on your device until you delete it in the App, clear the App's data or uninstall the App.
  • Analytics data is retained according to our Google Analytics for Firebase data-retention setting, which is at most 14 months for user-level event data.
  • Crash reports are retained by Firebase Crashlytics for up to 90 days.
  • Advertising and attribution partners keep data according to their own retention policies (see Section 3).
  • Emails you send us are kept as long as needed to handle your request and for our records.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the following rights.

EEA / UK / Switzerland (GDPR): access, correction, deletion, data portability, restriction of processing, objection to processing (including for direct marketing), and withdrawal of consent at any time. You can also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

California and other U.S. states (CCPA/CPRA and similar laws): to know what personal information is collected and how it is used, to delete it, to correct it, and to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information and of targeted advertising. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

Other regions (for example Brazil's LGPD, Canada's PIPEDA, Vietnam's Personal Data Protection Decree): you have the rights those laws grant, such as access, correction, deletion and withdrawal of consent.

How to exercise your rights or request deletion. Email contact@tranquilmind.co with the subject "Privacy request". Because the App has no accounts, please include your advertising ID (found in Android Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads) and your device model, so we and our partners can locate data linked to your device. We will respond within the time required by applicable law. To delete everything stored on your device, uninstall the App or clear its data in Android Settings.

8. Children's privacy & Child Safety Standards

The App is intended for users aged 18 and over and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under the applicable age of digital consent in your country, such as 16 in parts of the EEA). If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Child Safety Standards (CSAE)

Tranquil Mind Technology JSC, the developer of Callverde, has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM).

  • Prohibited: any content or behavior that sexually exploits, abuses or endangers children. This includes CSAM in any form (images, video, audio, text or AI-generated content), sexualization of minors, grooming, sextortion, solicitation and trafficking of minors, and any attempt to create, request, share or promote such material. Using Callverde in connection with any such activity is strictly prohibited and is a breach of our Terms of Service.
  • How Callverde works: Callverde does not let users post content, send messages or communicate with other users inside the App. Notifications shown in the Unseen inbox come from other messaging apps, stay only on your device and are never received by us. Content sent through those messaging apps is governed by those apps' own safety policies and reporting tools.
  • Reporting: to report a child-safety concern related to Callverde, email our child-safety point of contact at contact@tranquilmind.co with the subject "Child Safety". We review reports promptly.
  • Response: when we learn of apparent CSAM or child endangerment connected to our App, we act on it. This includes reporting to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and/or relevant local law-enforcement authorities, cooperating with investigations, and terminating access where possible.
  • Compliance: we comply with applicable child-safety laws and with the Google Play Child Safety Standards policy.

Data sent by the App's third-party services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). We take reasonable measures to protect information, and we keep your contacts, lists and notification content on your device rather than on our servers. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Please also protect your device with a screen lock.
