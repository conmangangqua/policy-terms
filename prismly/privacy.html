Privacy Policy

App: Prismly Effective: 2026-09-25 Last Updated: 2026-09-25

Prismly ("the App", "we", "us", "our"), operated by Tranquil Mind Technology JSC, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices and rights you have. By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

Prismly is an AI art studio. You choose a photo from your device or type a description, and the App creates a new image or a short video in the style you pick. It also offers AI photo tools (object removal, recolouring, face swap, frame expansion) and an on-device photo editor. You do not need to create an account to use the App.

1. Information we collect

We collect only what the App needs to work, to show ads and to measure and improve the App. The table below lists everything that leaves your device.

What stays on your device and is never uploaded by us:

  • Images and videos you save to your gallery, and the library of artworks inside the App.
  • Crop, filter and brightness / contrast / saturation edits. These are processed entirely on your device.
  • The on-device safety check that screens photos and generated results for adult content.
  • Your language choice, chosen topics, daily free-creation counter and Premium status.
  • Local reminder notifications (they are scheduled on your device and do not use a push service).

We do not collect: your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise (GPS) location, browsing history, or payment card details. The App has no user accounts.

Camera and photo access. If you allow it, the App uses your camera or the system photo picker only so that you can choose a photo. A photo is uploaded only when you start a creation with it, as described above.

2. How we use information

  • To provide the App. We send the photo or prompt you selected to our image service, return the generated image or video to you, and give you a daily allowance of free creations.
  • To show ads. This includes personalised ads where you have agreed or where the law allows it, and the rewarded ads you can watch to get an extra creation.
  • Measurement and attribution. To learn which campaigns bring users to the App and to measure ad performance.
  • Analytics and improvement. To understand how features are used and to fix crashes and defects.
  • Configuration. To deliver the list of art styles and app settings without a new release.
  • Safety, security and fraud prevention. To block prohibited content, review reports you send, and detect misuse of the image service and invalid ad traffic.
  • Legal compliance. Where we are required by law.

We do not use your photos or prompts to train AI models, to identify you, or to build an advertising profile about you.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information for money. We share information only:

  • with the service providers and partners listed in section 3, to run the features you use, to show and measure ads, and to analyse and secure the App;
  • for legal reasons, to comply with law, enforce our terms, or protect rights and safety;
  • in a business transfer, such as a merger, acquisition or asset sale, subject to this Policy.

U.S. state privacy laws. Allowing advertising partners to collect identifiers and app activity to show personalised ads may count as a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information, or "targeted advertising", under some U.S. state laws (for example California's CCPA/CPRA). You can opt out by turning off ad personalisation or deleting your advertising ID in your device settings, or by emailing us at contact@tranquilmind.co.

5. Legal bases (EEA/UK — GDPR)

  • Contract. Sending your photo or prompt to generate the result you requested.
  • Consent. Personalised advertising and related measurement, where consent is required. You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legitimate interests. Non-personalised advertising, analytics, crash reporting, security, fraud prevention and improving the App.
  • Legal obligation. Where retention or disclosure is required by law.

6. Data retention

  • Photos and prompts are kept on our image service only for as long as the generation takes. They are deleted once the result has been returned to you, and they are not archived or added to any dataset.
  • Content reports are kept for as long as we need to review them and to keep a record of safety decisions.
  • Identifiers, app activity and approximate location collected by advertising, attribution and analytics partners are kept according to each partner's retention policy. For Firebase Analytics we use Google's standard retention settings.
  • Crash logs and diagnostics are kept by Firebase Crashlytics for a limited period, in line with Google's retention practices.
  • Data held on your device stays there until you delete it or uninstall the App.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the following rights:

  • EEA/UK (GDPR): access, correct, delete or export your personal data; object to or restrict processing; withdraw consent; and lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.
  • U.S. states (e.g. CCPA/CPRA): know what we collect, delete it, correct it, opt out of sale, sharing or targeted advertising, and not be discriminated against for using these rights.
  • Other regions: equivalent rights under laws such as Brazil's LGPD, Canada's PIPEDA and Vietnam's Personal Data Protection Decree.

How to make a request. Because the App has no accounts, we cannot look you up by name. Email contact@tranquilmind.co and include your advertising ID (found in your device's ad settings), your device model and, if relevant, the approximate date and time of the creation you are asking about. We will use these to locate the records our partners hold and will respond within the time the law requires.

You can also, at any time and without contacting us, delete artworks inside the App, reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation, revoke camera or photo permission in your device settings, and uninstall the App to remove all locally stored data.

8. Children's privacy

The App is intended for users aged 16 and over and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16, or under a higher age of digital consent if your region sets one. If you believe a child has used the App or provided us with personal information, contact us at contact@tranquilmind.co and we will delete it.

The App never creates sexual content involving minors. Such requests are blocked before they are sent. Any apparent child sexual abuse material that reaches us is removed and reported to the competent authorities, including NCMEC where applicable.

9. International data transfers

Your information may be processed in countries other than your own, including the United States, Singapore and the countries where our advertising and analytics partners operate. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

10. Data security

All traffic between the App and our services uses HTTPS/TLS encryption. Credentials for the AI model providers are kept on our servers and are never shipped inside the App. We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures, but no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

11. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. We will post material changes on this page with a new "Last updated" date. If you keep using the App after a change, you accept the updated Policy.
