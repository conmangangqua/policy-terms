Privacy Policy

App: Pixa AI Effective: October 30, 2026 Last Updated: October 30, 2026

Effective date: October 30, 2026

Pixa AI ("the App", "we", "us") is an AI avatar and art studio published by Tranquil Mind Technology JSC. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles, why, and the choices you have. Contact: contact@tranquilmind.co.

1. Information we collect

Photos and prompts you submit for AI processing. When you use an AI feature (image editing, style/art generation, face swap, text-to-image), the App sends the image(s) you selected and/or your text prompt to our image-processing backend (a Cloudflare Worker service) over HTTPS, together with the selected mode and your plan tier (free or premium). The backend returns the generated image. Background removal, depth estimation for parallax video, video encoding and an on-device content-safety (NSFW) check run on your device.

Content reports. If you report AI-generated content, we store the reasons, your optional note, the prompt, a hash-based content identifier (not the image itself), the platform (Android/iOS), a timestamp and a status in our cloud database (Firebase Cloud Firestore). The report contains no account identifier.

Usage, device and diagnostic data. Through Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Remote Config we may collect app interactions, crash logs, device model, operating-system version, app version and app-instance identifiers.

Advertising data. The free version shows ads through Google AdMob mediation. Ad networks included in the app build are Google AdMob, Meta Audience Network, Pangle, Mintegral, AppLovin, Liftoff (Vungle), Unity Ads and Bigo Ads. These partners may collect your advertising ID, IP address, device information and ad interactions to deliver and measure ads. On iOS the App asks for App Tracking Transparency consent. A consent form (IAB TCF) is used where required.

Purchases. Subscriptions and in-app purchases are processed by Google Play Billing or Apple's App Store. We receive purchase status, not your payment card details.

Local data. Your generation history and settings are stored on your device (shared preferences and app storage).

Permissions the App declares (Android): CAMERA, INTERNET, READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE

  • CAMERA — lets you take a new photo inside the App to use as the input for an AI feature. The camera is used only after you grant permission and open the capture screen.
  • INTERNET — required to send your selected image or prompt to the AI backend, receive results, load remote configuration, show ads, process purchases and send analytics and crash reports.
  • READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE — lets the App read an image you choose from your device storage on older Android versions. Photos are chosen through the system photo picker; the App does not browse or scan your library in the background.
  • WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE — lets the App save generated images and videos to your device gallery on older Android versions, and keep temporary files while processing.

2. How we use information

  • To generate, edit and return the images or videos you request.
  • To operate, secure and improve the App, fix crashes, and handle content reports and abuse.
  • To show ads and measure their performance, and to manage subscriptions.
  • To comply with legal obligations and platform policies.

4. Retention

Images and prompts are sent only to produce your result. Content reports and analytics data are kept as long as needed for safety, moderation and product improvement. Data stored on your device is removed when you uninstall the App or clear its storage.

5. Your choices and rights

You can deny or revoke camera and storage access in your device settings, reset your advertising ID, and decline tracking on iOS. Depending on your location (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, LGPD, PIPEDA), you may request access, correction, deletion or restriction of your data by emailing contact@tranquilmind.co. We do not sell personal information.

6. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where applicable) and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Safety of AI content

The App checks images on device for explicit content and provides an in-app way to report AI output. We act on reports and may restrict features that are abused.

8. Security and international transfers

We use HTTPS for transfers. Data may be processed in countries other than yours by our providers, under appropriate safeguards.

9. Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above will change. Continued use means acceptance.
