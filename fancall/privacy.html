Privacy Policy

App: Superstar Call Effective: November 24, 2026 Last Updated: November 24, 2026

Effective date: 24 November 2026 Developer: Tranquil Mind Technology JSC ("we", "us") Contact: contact@tranquilmind.co App: Superstar Call (Android package com.tranquiltmt.fancall; iOS build of the same app)

This Privacy Policy explains what information Superstar Call ("the App") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. The App is an entertainment app that shows simulated video calls and chat conversations with fictional idol characters. It does not let you talk to other real people.

1. Information we collect

We collect the following categories of information. Some are collected by us directly; others are collected automatically by third-party SDKs that run inside the App.

  • Device and app identifiers: Firebase app instance ID, the Google Advertising ID (AD_ID), and attribution identifiers used by our measurement providers (AppsFlyer and Adjust).
  • Device information: device model, operating system version and similar technical details, collected through device_info_plus and by the SDKs listed in Section 3.
  • Usage and analytics events: screens viewed, features used (for example which call scene or chat script you open), and in-app actions, collected through Firebase Analytics.
  • Crash and diagnostic data: stack traces and error reports, collected through Firebase Crashlytics, to fix stability problems.
  • Remote configuration: the App fetches configuration values (such as ad settings and version information) from Firebase Remote Config.
  • Advertising data: ad requests and ad interaction data handled by the advertising SDKs in Section 3. We do not receive your name, email address or contacts from advertising networks.
  • Purchase information: if you buy an in-app item, the store (Google Play or the Apple App Store) processes the payment and sends us a confirmation of the purchase status. We do not receive your payment card details.
  • Camera: see the permission note below. Camera frames are shown on your screen only; they are not recorded, stored or uploaded.

We do not require you to create an account and we do not collect your name, phone number, email address, contacts, location or photos.

Permissions the App declares (Android): ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, AD_ID, CAMERA, INTERNET, VIBRATE

  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: checks whether a network connection is available before loading call content, ads or configuration.
  • AD_ID: lets Google advertising and measurement services use the Google Advertising ID for ads and analytics. You can reset or delete this ID in your device settings.
  • CAMERA: used only to show a live preview from your front camera during a simulated video call. The App does not record, save, take pictures of, or upload camera images, and audio recording is disabled.
  • INTERNET: needed to load call content, advertising, Remote Config, crash reports and analytics.
  • VIBRATE: gives haptic feedback during call effects and notifications.

The App does not request microphone (RECORD_AUDIO) or external-storage write (WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE) permissions; the build removes them.

2. How we use information

  • To run the App, load its content and keep it stable (crash reports, configuration).
  • To understand how the App is used so we can improve it (analytics).
  • To show advertisements and to measure ad performance.
  • To measure installs and in-app revenue for our marketing (attribution).
  • To process and verify in-app purchases through the app stores.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Third-party SDKs and services

The App uses the following third-party services, which may receive the information described above under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config, Core) — analytics, crash reporting, configuration.
  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads and the Google User Messaging Platform — advertising and consent.
  • Advertising mediation partners: Meta Audience Network (Facebook SDK), Pangle (ByteDance), Unity Ads, Liftoff Monetize (Vungle), Mintegral.
  • Measurement and attribution: AppsFlyer, Adjust.
  • Google Play Billing / Apple App Store — in-app purchase processing.
  • Our own content delivery service, which delivers call videos to the App in encrypted form.

Advertising partners may use the Advertising ID and device information to show ads. Where required, we show a consent form (for example in the EU/EEA) before personalised ads are served, and on iOS we ask for tracking permission through App Tracking Transparency.

4. Children

Superstar Call is an entertainment app and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the App, contact us at contact@tranquilmind.co and we will delete it.

5. Data retention and deletion

Analytics, crash and attribution data are retained by the providers listed in Section 3 according to their own retention settings. Video files delivered to the App are stored on your device in encrypted form and are removed when you uninstall the App. To delete data that we hold, email contact@tranquilmind.co from the address you can verify, or uninstall the App to remove on-device content. To limit advertising or analytics tracking, use your device settings (reset or delete the Advertising ID, or turn off ad personalisation).

6. Security

We use encryption for locally stored call media and transport security (HTTPS) for network requests. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EEA/UK under GDPR, California under CCPA/CPRA, Brazil under LGPD, or Canada under PIPEDA), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal information, and to withdraw consent. To exercise these rights, contact contact@tranquilmind.co.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. The effective date at the top shows the latest version. Material changes will be signposted inside the App.
