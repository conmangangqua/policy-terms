Privacy Policy — Mochi Chat

App: Mochi: AI Companion Chat Effective: October 29, 2026 Last Updated: October 29, 2026

Effective date: October 29, 2026 Operator: Tranquil Mind Technology JSC ("we", "us") Contact: contact@tranquilmind.co

Mochi Chat ("the App") is an AI companion chat app for Android. This policy explains what data the App and the third-party SDKs it contains handle.

1. Data we handle

  • Chat content. Messages you type (or dictate) are sent over the internet to our chat backend (chat-api.tranquilmind.workers.dev) and to an AI language-model provider (DeepSeek, api.deepseek.com) to generate the companion's replies. Chat history, settings and the list of messages you reported are stored locally on your device (SharedPreferences).
  • Voice input. If you use the microphone button, speech is processed by the speech recognition service of your device (Android speech-to-text). Spoken replies use the device text-to-speech engine. We do not store audio recordings.
  • Photos. If you choose a profile picture, the image you pick through the system picker is used on your device.
  • Language detection. Short text may be sent to Google Cloud Translation (translation.googleapis.com) to detect its language.
  • Device and usage data. App version, device model, OS version, advertising ID, IP address, crash logs, and in-app events are collected by the analytics, advertising and attribution SDKs listed below.
  • Purchases. Subscriptions are processed by Google Play Billing; we do not receive your payment card details.

2. Permissions the App declares (Android)

Permissions the App declares (Android): android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO (voice-to-text input in chat, only after you tap the microphone button); android.permission.INTERNET (send chat messages, load ads and remote configuration); android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE (check connectivity before network calls and ad loading); android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS (show reminder notifications); android.permission.RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED (re-schedule local reminder notifications after the device restarts); com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID (read the Android advertising ID for ads, measurement and attribution).

4. Advertising and consent

The App shows ads served through AdMob mediation. In regions where required (EEA, UK), we show a consent form (Google UMP) and ads follow your choice. We do not sell your personal data for money.

5. Child safety (CSAE)

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or under the minimum age in your country). We prohibit child sexual abuse and exploitation. You can report any AI message in the App; reported messages are hidden from your view. To report child-safety concerns, write to contact@tranquilmind.co and we will review and act promptly, including cooperating with competent authorities.

6. Retention and deletion

Chat history is stored on your device; clearing app data or uninstalling the App removes it. Data held by SDK providers follows their retention policies. To request deletion of data associated with you, contact contact@tranquilmind.co.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, LGPD, PIPEDA), you may request access, correction, deletion or restriction of your data, or object to processing. Contact contact@tranquilmind.co.

8. Security and international transfers

Data is sent over HTTPS. Data may be processed in countries other than yours by the providers above.

9. Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above will change accordingly.
