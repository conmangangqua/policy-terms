Privacy Policy

App: Genart Effective: October 30, 2026 Last Updated: October 30, 2026

Effective date: October 30, 2026 Operator: Tranquil Mind Technology JSC ("we", "us") Contact: contact@tranquilmind.co

Genart is an AI image app that turns photos and text prompts into art, portraits and stylized edits. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

Content you provide

  • Photos you pick from your library (through the system photo picker) or take with the camera, and text prompts you type. To generate an image, the photo and/or prompt is sent over the internet to our image-generation service (a Cloudflare Workers AI gateway operated by us) and the result is returned to your device.
  • Safety checks: prompts and images may be sent to the same service for content moderation before generation.
  • Content reports: if you report a result, we store the report reason, optional note, content identifier, the prompt, platform (Android/iOS) and a timestamp in Firebase Cloud Firestore.
  • Background removal runs on the device and does not upload your photo for that step.

Data collected automatically

  • Device and app information (device model, OS version, app version, language), crash logs and diagnostics, and usage events through Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics.
  • Advertising identifier and ad-interaction data used by advertising SDKs (Google AdMob, and the Meta/Facebook SDK declared in the app) to serve and measure ads. In regions that require it, we show a consent form (Google UMP / IAB TCF) before personalized ads; on iOS the app can request App Tracking Transparency permission.
  • Remote configuration values fetched from Firebase Remote Config.
  • Connectivity state, used to tell you when you are offline.

Purchases: subscriptions and in-app purchases are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We receive purchase status, not your payment card details.

We do not ask you to create an account.

Permissions the App declares (Android): INTERNET, com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID, CAMERA, WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE

  • INTERNET — send your photo/prompt to the generation service, load ads, analytics, remote config and purchase status.
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID — lets advertising SDKs read the Google Advertising ID to serve and measure ads; you can reset or opt out of it in Android settings.
  • CAMERA — used only when you choose to take a new photo inside the app to use as the source image. Photos are not captured in the background.
  • WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE — declared only for Android 9 and below (API 28 or lower), to save generated images to your gallery. On Android 10+ images are saved through MediaStore and this permission is not requested.

The app does not request access to browse your photo library; photos are chosen with the system photo picker, which grants access only to the images you select.

2. How we use information

  • Generate, edit and save the images you request.
  • Detect and block unsafe prompts or images, and review content reports.
  • Show and measure advertising, and process subscriptions.
  • Diagnose crashes, maintain and improve the app.
  • Comply with law and enforce our Terms of Service.

4. Sharing and retention

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with the providers above, to comply with law, or to protect rights and safety. Images sent for generation are used to produce your result; generated images saved to your gallery stay on your device until you delete them. Content reports and analytics data are kept as long as needed for safety review and service operation.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Deny or revoke the camera permission in system settings; you can still pick photos with the photo picker.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in device settings; change your consent choice where the consent form is offered.
  • Depending on where you live (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, LGPD, PIPEDA and similar laws) you may request access, correction or deletion of your data, or object to processing. Email contact@tranquilmind.co.
  • You can uninstall the app at any time to stop all collection.

6. Children

Genart is not directed to children under 13 (or the age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Security and international transfers

We use HTTPS to transmit data. No method is perfectly secure. Your data may be processed in countries other than your own, with safeguards required by applicable law.

8. Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above will change when we do.
