Privacy Policy

App: Palmtale Effective: 2026-11-26 Last Updated: 2026-11-26

Palmtale ("the App", "we", "us", "our"), operated by Tranquil Mind Technology JSC, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App uses, where it is stored, and the choices and rights you have. By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

Palmtale is a palm reading, horoscope and tarot app for entertainment and self-reflection. You do not need to create an account to use it. Your palm photos, readings, profile details and chat messages are processed on your device and are not sent to our servers. The App does, however, use third-party advertising, attribution and crash-reporting SDKs, described in section 3, which may send limited technical and advertising data to their providers.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect your personal content on our servers. Everything the App uses for its features is created and kept on your device:

  • Palm photos. When you scan your palm with the camera or pick a photo from your gallery, the image is analyzed on your device by an on-device hand-landmark model (Google MediaPipe Tasks Vision, bundled in the App). The photo is not uploaded anywhere.
  • Profile details you enter (for example name, birth date, birth time and birthplace used for horoscope and birth-chart readings, and profiles of other people you add). These are stored only in the App's local storage on your device.
  • Readings and history (palm readings, tarot draws, horoscope results) — stored locally on your device.
  • Chat with Mira, the in-app palm guide. Mira is an on-device assistant that answers from your own readings and content bundled in the App. Your questions and Mira's answers are stored only on your device and are never sent to a server or to an AI provider.
  • Settings such as language and notification preferences — stored locally.

Advertising and attribution data. The App shows ads through Google AdMob with mediation partners (AppLovin MAX, Bigo Ads, Meta Audience Network). These SDKs can read the Android Advertising ID and device and network information (such as IP address, device model, operating system version, app version, and ad interaction data) to show and measure ads. The App also sends install and attribution events to Adjust, which may use device identifiers for attribution. Before showing personalized ads in regions that require it (such as the EEA and UK), the App asks for your consent through the Google-certified consent form, and you can change your choice again in the App's settings. You can also reset or delete the Advertising ID in your Android settings.

Crash and diagnostic data. In release builds, the App sends crash reports and fatal error information (stack trace, device model, operating system version and app version) to Firebase Crashlytics to help us fix bugs. The App also fetches configuration values from Firebase Remote Config (such as which ad settings to use).

Photos picked from your gallery are selected through the Android system photo picker; the App does not request access to your whole photo library.

Text-to-speech. If you use the "read aloud" feature, the text is spoken by the text-to-speech engine already installed on your device (for example Google Text-to-Speech). The App does not send this text to us.

Sharing. If you choose to share a reading or the App link, the content is passed to the app you pick in the Android share sheet; what happens next is governed by that app's privacy policy.

Permissions the App declares (Android): ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, AD_ID, CAMERA, INTERNET, POST_NOTIFICATIONS, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED

  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — to check whether the device is online before loading ads or remote configuration. No personal data is collected by this permission itself.
  • AD_ID — to read the Android Advertising ID for ad serving, ad measurement and attribution (AdMob, mediation partners and Adjust). You can reset or delete it in Android settings.
  • CAMERA — to take a photo of your palm for the on-device palm reading. Images stay on your device. Optional: you can pick an existing photo instead, or deny the permission.
  • INTERNET — to load ads, fetch remote configuration, send crash reports and attribution events, and run the App's online features. Palm photos, readings and chat content are not sent over this connection.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — to show the daily horoscope / reading reminders you turn on. Optional: you can turn reminders off in the App or in system settings.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED — to restore your scheduled daily reminders after the device restarts. No data is collected; it is used only if reminders are on.

2. How we use information

  • On your device: to provide the palm, horoscope, tarot and chat features; to remember your profiles, readings, language and settings between sessions; and to show the reminders you enable.
  • Advertising, measurement and app stability: to show ads (including, where you consent, personalized ads), measure ad performance and attribution, and find and fix crashes.

We do not sell your personal content, and we do not use palm photos, readings, profile details or chat messages for advertising or profiling.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal content, and we do not receive your palm photos, readings, profile details or chat messages. Advertising and measurement data is shared with the providers listed in section 3 so that they can show and measure ads and attribution. We do not share data with data brokers. Content leaves your device only when you choose to share it through the Android share sheet.

5. Legal bases (EEA/UK — GDPR)

Processing of your data on your device rests on the performance of the service you asked for. Advertising and attribution processing rests on your consent where the law requires it (for example, through the consent form shown in the EEA and UK, and the notification or camera permissions you grant). You can withdraw consent at any time by changing your ad choice in the App, resetting the Advertising ID, or revoking a permission in system settings.

6. Data retention

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete everything at any time in Me → Delete all my data, which clears your readings, profiles and chat history. Uninstalling the App or clearing its storage in Android settings also removes all of it. We hold no copy of your readings or profiles. Advertising, attribution and crash data is retained by the providers listed in section 3 under their own policies.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under the GDPR/UK GDPR (access, rectification, erasure, portability, restriction, objection, withdrawal of consent, and the right to complain to a data protection authority), the CCPA/CPRA (to know, delete, correct, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information, and non-discrimination), and other laws such as Brazil's LGPD and Canada's PIPEDA.

Because we do not store your personal content on our servers, you can exercise access and deletion of that content directly in the App (see section 6). For advertising and attribution data, you can change your ad choices in the App, reset the Advertising ID in Android settings, or contact the providers listed in section 3. For any other question or request, contact us at contact@tranquilmind.co. We do not sell personal information.

8. Children's privacy & Child Safety Standards

The App is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13 (or the higher minimum age of digital consent in your country, such as 16 in parts of the EEA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the App does not transmit your personal content to us, we hold no children's content; if you believe a child has used the App, you can delete all data in the App or uninstall it, and you may contact us at the address below.

Child Safety Standards (CSAE)

Palmtale includes a chat with "Mira", an on-device palm guide. Tranquil Mind Technology JSC, the developer of Palmtale, has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM).

  • Prohibited content. Using the App to create, request, share or promote any content that sexualizes minors — including images, video, audio, text or AI-generated content — and any grooming, sextortion, solicitation or trafficking of minors is strictly prohibited. This applies both to what users type and to any response the App produces.
  • How the App prevents it. Mira does not use a generative AI model and does not connect users with other people: it only answers from the user's own readings and fixed content written and reviewed by us and bundled in the App. There is no user-to-user messaging, no public posting and no uploading of content to other users.
  • Reporting. Every Mira message has an in-app Report action that flags the message on your device. To make sure a concern reaches us, please also email our child-safety contact below with a description or screenshot; we review every report we receive and act on it, including removing or correcting bundled content in an update.
  • Cooperation with authorities. We comply with applicable child-safety laws and the Google Play Child Safety Standards policy, and we report apparent CSAM we become aware of to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and/or the relevant local authorities.
  • Child-safety point of contact: contact@tranquilmind.co (Palmtale — Tranquil Mind Technology JSC).

9. International data transfers

We do not receive your palm photos, readings, profile details or chat messages, so we do not transfer them to other countries. Advertising, attribution and crash data is processed by the providers listed in section 3, which may process it in countries outside your own with appropriate safeguards where required. If you contact us by email, your message is processed by our email provider, which may be located outside your country, with appropriate safeguards where required.

10. Data security

Your data is stored in the App's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read. Please protect your device with a screen lock. No method of storage is 100% secure.
