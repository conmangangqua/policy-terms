Privacy Policy

App: Palmtale Effective: 2026-11-19 Last Updated: 2026-11-19

Palmtale ("the App", "we", "us", "our"), operated by Tranquil Mind Technology JSC, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App uses, where it is stored, and the choices and rights you have. By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

Palmtale is a palm reading, horoscope and tarot app for entertainment and self-reflection. You do not need to create an account to use it, and the App works fully on your device: it contains no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting SDK and no in-app purchases, and it does not send your photos, readings, profile or chat messages to our servers or to any third party.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information on our servers. Everything the App uses is created and kept on your device:

  • Palm photos. When you scan your palm with the camera or pick a photo from your gallery, the image is analyzed on your device by an on-device hand-landmark model (Google MediaPipe Tasks Vision, bundled in the App). The photo is not uploaded anywhere.
  • Profile details you enter (for example name, birth date, birth time and birthplace used for horoscope and birth-chart readings, and profiles of other people you add). These are stored only in the App's local storage on your device.
  • Readings and history (palm readings, tarot draws, horoscope results) — stored locally on your device.
  • Chat with Mira, the in-app palm guide. Mira is an on-device assistant that answers from your own readings and content bundled in the App. Your questions and Mira's answers are stored only on your device and are never sent to a server or to an AI provider.
  • Settings such as language and notification preferences — stored locally.

Photos picked from your gallery are selected through the Android system photo picker; the App does not request access to your whole photo library.

Text-to-speech. If you use the "read aloud" feature, the text is spoken by the text-to-speech engine already installed on your device (for example Google Text-to-Speech). The App does not send this text to us.

Sharing. If you choose to share a reading or the App link, the content is passed to the app you pick in the Android share sheet; what happens next is governed by that app's privacy policy.

Permissions the App declares (Android): CAMERA, POST_NOTIFICATIONS, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED

  • CAMERA — to take a photo of your palm for the on-device palm reading. Images stay on your device. Optional: you can pick an existing photo instead, or deny the permission.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — to show the daily horoscope / reading reminders you turn on. Optional: you can turn reminders off in the App or in system settings.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED — to restore your scheduled daily reminders after the device restarts. No data is collected; it is used only if reminders are on.

2. How we use information

All processing happens on your device, only to:

  • provide the palm, horoscope, tarot and chat features;
  • remember your profiles, readings, language and settings between sessions;
  • show the reminders you enable.

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or analytics.

4. How we share information

We do not sell, rent or share your personal information, because we do not receive it. We do not share data with advertisers or data brokers. Content leaves your device only when you choose to share it through the Android share sheet.

5. Legal bases (EEA/UK — GDPR)

Because your data is processed only on your own device to provide the features you request, the processing rests on the performance of the service you asked for and on your consent (for example, when you grant the camera or notification permission). You can withdraw consent at any time by revoking the permission in system settings.

6. Data retention

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete everything at any time in Me → Delete all my data, which clears your readings, profiles and chat history. Uninstalling the App or clearing its storage in Android settings also removes all of it. We hold no copy.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under the GDPR/UK GDPR (access, rectification, erasure, portability, restriction, objection, withdrawal of consent, and the right to complain to a data protection authority), the CCPA/CPRA (to know, delete, correct, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information, and non-discrimination), and other laws such as Brazil's LGPD and Canada's PIPEDA.

Because we do not store your data on our servers, you can exercise access and deletion directly in the App (see section 6). For any question or request, contact us at contact@tranquilmind.co. We do not sell or share personal information.

8. Children's privacy & Child Safety Standards

The App is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13 (or the higher minimum age of digital consent in your country, such as 16 in parts of the EEA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the App does not transmit personal data to us, we hold no children's data; if you believe a child has used the App, you can delete all data in the App or uninstall it, and you may contact us at the address below.

Child Safety Standards (CSAE)

Palmtale includes a chat with "Mira", an on-device palm guide. Tranquil Mind Technology JSC, the developer of Palmtale, has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM).

  • Prohibited content. Using the App to create, request, share or promote any content that sexualizes minors — including images, video, audio, text or AI-generated content — and any grooming, sextortion, solicitation or trafficking of minors is strictly prohibited. This applies both to what users type and to any response the App produces.
  • How the App prevents it. Mira does not use a generative AI model and does not connect users with other people: it only answers from the user's own readings and fixed content written and reviewed by us and bundled in the App. There is no user-to-user messaging, no public posting and no uploading of content to other users.
  • Reporting. Every Mira message has an in-app Report action that flags the message on your device. To make sure a concern reaches us, please also email our child-safety contact below with a description or screenshot; we review every report we receive and act on it, including removing or correcting bundled content in an update.
  • Cooperation with authorities. We comply with applicable child-safety laws and the Google Play Child Safety Standards policy, and we report apparent CSAM we become aware of to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and/or the relevant local authorities.
  • Child-safety point of contact: contact@tranquilmind.co (Palmtale — Tranquil Mind Technology JSC).

9. International data transfers

The App does not transfer your personal data to us or to other countries. If you contact us by email, your message is processed by our email provider, which may be located outside your country, with appropriate safeguards where required.

10. Data security

Your data is stored in the App's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read. Please protect your device with a screen lock. No method of storage is 100% secure.
