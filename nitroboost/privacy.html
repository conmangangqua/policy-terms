Privacy Policy

App: NitroBoost Effective: July 27, 2026 Last Updated: July 27, 2026

NitroBoost ("the App", "we", "us", "our"), operated by Tranquil Mind Technology JSC, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices and rights you have. By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

NitroBoost is a game utility that gathers your installed games into a Game Hub, lets you save per-game display profiles (refresh rate and screen density), tune GFX and display density, clear the App's own cache, and monitor device metrics such as CPU, memory, and temperature. You do not need to create an account to use it.

1. Information We Collect

We do not ask you for personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number, and the App has no login. We collect only the limited data below, automatically, through the third-party services listed in Section 3:

  • Device and advertising identifiers — including the Google Advertising ID (AAID) and app-instance identifiers, used for advertising, attribution, and analytics.
  • Approximate location — derived by our advertising partners from your IP address for ad delivery and measurement. We do not collect precise/GPS location (the App requests no location permission).
  • App activity — in-app interactions and events (which features you use), for analytics and advertising measurement.
  • Crash logs and diagnostics — crash reports and basic performance/diagnostic data to keep the App stable.
  • Device and technical information — device model, operating-system version, language, and similar technical attributes.
On-device only (not collected by us): to apply the display profile you saved for a game, the App uses Android's Usage Access to detect which app is in the foreground; it may also read system metrics (CPU, memory, temperature) and manage notifications during Focus Mode. This information is processed on your device to operate those features and is not transmitted to us or to third parties. Clearing cache affects only the App's own cache directories.

2. How We Use Information

  • Operate and provide the App's features (Game Hub, display profiles, cleanup, monitor, Focus Mode).
  • Display and measure advertising, including attribution of installs and campaigns.
  • Analytics and product improvement (understanding feature usage and stability).
  • Fraud prevention, security, and diagnosing and fixing crashes.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information for money. We share the limited data above with the third-party services in Section 3 so they can provide advertising, attribution, and analytics; for legal reasons (to comply with law or protect rights and safety); and in connection with a business transfer (merger, acquisition, or asset sale).

U.S. state privacy notice. Because the App serves personalized advertising, the sharing of device/advertising identifiers with advertising partners may be considered "sale" or "sharing"/"targeted advertising" under certain U.S. state laws (e.g. California CPRA). You may opt out via the device-level ad-personalization controls described in Section 3 and the consent choice where offered.

5. Legal Bases (EEA/UK — GDPR)

  • Consent — for personalized advertising and, where required, analytics.
  • Legitimate interests — core functionality, security, fraud prevention, and product improvement.
  • Legal obligation — where processing is required to comply with law.

6. Data Retention

We retain data only as long as necessary for the purposes above or as required by law. Advertising and attribution identifiers are retained according to our partners' retention periods; crash and diagnostic data are kept for a limited period to investigate and fix issues.

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, port, object to, or restrict processing of your data, and to withdraw consent or lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (GDPR — EEA/UK); to know, delete, correct, and opt out of the sale/sharing and targeted advertising of your data, and to non-discrimination (CCPA/CPRA — California); and similar rights in other regions (e.g. LGPD in Brazil, PIPEDA in Canada).

To exercise these rights, contact us at contact@tranquilmind.co. Because the App has no account, please include your Advertising ID and device details in your request so we can identify the relevant data.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your region). If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

9. International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed in countries other than your own, including the United States, where our service providers operate. Where required, appropriate safeguards (such as Standard Contractual Clauses) are used.

10. Data Security

We use encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and other reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.
