Privacy Policy

App: Edgelume Effective: October 8, 2026 Last Updated: October 8, 2026

Edgelume ("the App", "we", "us", "our"), operated by Tranquil Mind Technology JSC, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices and rights you have. By downloading, installing, or using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

Edgelume is an Android edge lighting and wallpaper personalization application that provides glowing animated borders around your device screen, edge lighting triggers for incoming calls, charging, and notifications, dynamic live wallpapers, and a curated library of pre-packaged static wallpapers. You do not need to create an account, log in, or provide any personal contact information to use the App.

This Privacy Policy applies solely to the Android version of Edgelume available under the application identifier com.tranquiltmt.edgelume.

1. Information we collect

We believe in strict data minimization. We only collect data that is strictly necessary to provide and maintain the App's core functionality, diagnostics, and analytics.

A. Information automatically collected by third-party SDKs (data transmitted off-device)

When you use the App on your Android device, certain diagnostic and analytical information is automatically collected and transmitted to our trusted third-party service provider (Google Firebase):

  • Device and software identifiers: Mobile device hardware model, operating system version (Android OS), unique installation identifiers (such as the Firebase Installation ID / App Instance ID), and system performance characteristics.
  • Approximate location: Coarse geographical location at the country, territory, or city level derived automatically from the device's IP address. We do not access, collect, or transmit your precise GPS coordinates.
  • Usage and interaction data: App opens, screen views, feature interactions (e.g., selecting border styles or setting wallpapers), session durations, and general engagement statistics collected in aggregated form via Firebase Analytics.
  • Crash reports and diagnostics: Technical stack traces, device state at the time of an application crash or freeze, operating system state, and performance diagnostics collected via Firebase Crashlytics to detect and fix technical bugs.

B. Sensitive Android permissions and on-device processing (data that NEVER leaves your device)

To provide its dynamic edge lighting effects, Edgelume requests specific Android permissions. All data processed through these sensitive permissions is handled strictly on your local device in volatile memory and is never recorded, stored on disk, or transmitted over the internet:

  1. Phone state access (android.permission.READ_PHONE_STATE): - Purpose: Edgelume requests this permission solely to detect incoming and active telephone call states (specifically CALL_STATE_RINGING, CALL_STATE_OFFHOOK, and CALL_STATE_IDLE) to trigger the glowing screen border when your phone rings or during a call, and to stop the animation when the call ends. - What we DO NOT do: The App does not read, log, access, or transmit your telephone number, caller identification, contacts, call history, address book, or call audio. The detection is performed transiently in device memory through official Android Telephony APIs and immediately discarded.
  1. Notification listener service (android.permission.BIND_NOTIFICATION_LISTENER_SERVICE): - Purpose: If you choose to enable the notification glow feature, Android requires granting Notification Access so our EdgeNotificationListener can detect when a new notification arrives on your device, triggering a temporary 8-second animated edge border pulse. - What we DO NOT do: The App does not inspect, read, copy, log, or transmit any notification text, messages, chat content, sender names, notification titles, or notification extras. It only checks the presence of a notification event from third-party apps to trigger the visual lighting pulse.
  1. Display over other apps (android.permission.SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW): - Purpose: Allows Edgelume to render an overlay window displaying the animated lighting border around the perimeter of your screen while using other applications. - What we DO NOT do: The overlay window is configured with FLAG_NOT_FOCUSABLE and FLAG_NOT_TOUCHABLE. It does not intercept touch events, does not interfere with underlying user interactions, and cannot view, read, or record screen content, keystrokes, or text displayed by other applications.
  1. Foreground service permissions (android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE, android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE): - Purpose: Required by modern Android versions (Android 14+) to keep the overlay lighting service (edge_lighting_overlay) active and stable while edge lighting is triggered. It displays a mandatory ongoing system notification ("Edgelume is running") with a quick "Turn off" action.
  1. Post notifications permission (android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS): - Purpose: Required on Android 13+ to display the ongoing foreground service notification while the edge overlay is active, and to deliver occasional local engagement reminders scheduled on your device.
  1. Start at boot (android.permission.RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): - Purpose: Allows our local BootReceiver to automatically restore your active edge lighting settings after your device restarts, so you do not need to manually re-enable the lighting overlay.
  1. Set wallpaper permission (android.permission.SET_WALLPAPER and Live Wallpaper service): - Purpose: Allows you to apply pre-packaged wallpapers to your device's Home screen, Lock screen, or both via the standard Android WallpaperManager, and enables the optional LED live wallpaper engine (LedWallpaperService).

C. Information stored locally on your device

Edgelume stores your personal customization preferences locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences and local database storage (Hive). This information never leaves your device and includes:

  • Customized edge border parameters (colors, animation speeds, border widths, corner radius, notch dimensions, display hole styles, run styles).
  • Custom border presets and saved favorite combinations.
  • Onboarding status, selected application language, and user interface preferences.
  • Category interest preferences selected during setup to prioritize the ordering of wallpaper categories.

D. Information we DO NOT collect

  • No user accounts: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, usernames, or passwords.
  • No financial or payment data: The App is free; we do not collect credit card, billing, or banking details.
  • No precise location: We do not access GPS hardware or precise location data.
  • No camera, microphone, or biometric data: We do not request or access camera, microphone, or biometric sensors.
  • No user files or media: All wallpapers and border shapes provided by Edgelume are bundled locally within the App assets. The App does not read, scan, or access your personal photo library, storage files, or media.
  • No advertising identifiers for ad tracking: Edgelume contains no third-party advertising SDKs or ad mediation networks.

2. How we use information

We use the limited information collected through the App for the following legitimate purposes:

  • Operating and maintaining the App: Ensuring edge lighting animations, live wallpapers, and wallpaper customization functions operate properly on your device.
  • Diagnostics and technical stability: Monitoring application crashes, software exceptions, and performance bottlenecks via Firebase Crashlytics to resolve bugs and enhance stability across various Android hardware configurations.
  • Analytics and product improvement: Evaluating aggregate engagement trends, screen navigation paths, and feature popularity via Firebase Analytics to guide product design and improve user experience.
  • Security and fraud prevention: Protecting the integrity of the App, preventing abusive automated interactions, and ensuring compliance with Google Play Developer policies.
  • Legal obligations: Satisfying applicable laws, regulations, and legal processes where mandatory.

We do not use your information for targeted behavioral advertising, cross-app tracking, profiling, or automated decision-making.

Our legitimate interests do not override your fundamental privacy rights and freedoms.
  • Consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR): Where required by applicable local law (such as for certain analytics identifiers or specific device permissions), we process data based on your explicit consent granted through your Android device permission dialogs or application settings. You may withdraw your consent at any time by revoking permissions in your device settings.
  • Compliance with legal obligations (Article 6(1)(c) GDPR): Processing required to comply with statutory and regulatory obligations.

6. Data retention

We adhere to data minimization and retain data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy:

  • Firebase Crashlytics data: Technical crash logs, stack traces, and associated device metrics are retained by Firebase for up to 90 days, after which they are automatically purged.
  • Firebase Analytics data: Aggregated analytics events and user property metrics are retained by Google Firebase for standard retention periods (ranging between 2 and 14 months depending on event configurations).
  • Local device preferences: Your border styles, custom presets, language selection, and category preferences remain stored locally on your device until you clear the App's storage via Android Settings or uninstall the App.

As a result, technical diagnostics and aggregated analytics data may be transferred to, stored, and processed in jurisdictions other than your home country.

When transferring personal data from the EEA, UK, or Switzerland to countries that have not received an adequacy decision from the relevant regulatory authorities, Google utilizes recognized legal transfer mechanisms, such as European Commission Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) and the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, to ensure your information receives an adequate level of data protection.

10. Data security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect the information processed by the App:

  • Encryption in transit: All network communication between the App and Google Firebase endpoints is encrypted using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (HTTPS/TLS).
  • On-device isolation: Local preferences, presets, and configuration data are stored within the App's secure private sandbox storage using Android standard file permissions, inaccessible to other standard applications.
  • No storage of sensitive content: Sensitive operational states (such as incoming call detection and notification triggers) are processed strictly in volatile memory and never committed to disk.

While we take rigorous precautions, no electronic transmission over the internet or mobile system architecture can be guaranteed to be 100% secure. We encourage you to maintain appropriate security protections on your personal device.
