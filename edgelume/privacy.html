Privacy Policy

App: Edgelume Effective: October 14, 2026 Last Updated: October 14, 2026

Edgelume ("the App", "we", "us", "our"), operated by Tranquil Mind Technology JSC, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices and rights you have. By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

Edgelume adds animated glowing borders around the edges of your screen, can light them up for incoming calls, charging and notifications, and offers wallpapers and live wallpapers. You do not need to create an account to use it. The App does not show ads and does not offer in-app purchases.

1. Information we collect

We collect only a limited set of technical information, automatically, through Google Firebase. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location, and the App has no account or sign-in.

Information collected automatically (sent off your device):

  • Device and app identifiers: the Firebase app-instance ID and, where available, the device's advertising ID, used for analytics only (not for advertising).
  • App activity: how you use the App, such as screens viewed, features used (for example, which edge-lighting modes you turn on), session length, and first-open events.
  • Crash logs and diagnostics: crash reports, stack traces and performance data, along with device model, operating-system version, app version, language and similar technical details.
  • Network information: your IP address is received by Firebase when the App connects. Google may use it to infer a coarse, country- or city-level region for analytics reporting. We do not collect GPS or precise location.

Information that stays on your device (not collected by us):

  • Notification access (optional). If you enable notification lighting, the App's notification listener only detects that a notification has arrived so it can light up the edges. It does not read, store or transmit the content of your notifications.
  • Phone state (optional). If you enable call lighting, the App detects only that a call is ringing so it can light up the edges. It does not read, store or transmit phone numbers, contacts or call logs.
  • Display over other apps. This permission lets the App draw the glowing border on top of other apps. It does not capture or record what is shown on your screen.
  • Your settings and content. Your edge-lighting designs, saved presets, favorites, interests chosen during onboarding, language and other preferences are stored locally on your device.
  • Wallpapers. Wallpapers and live wallpapers are bundled inside the App. Setting a wallpaper, saving one to your gallery, or sharing one through the Android share sheet happens on your device. We do not receive the image or know who you share it with.

2. How we use information

We use the information described above to:

  • provide, operate and maintain the App;
  • understand how the App is used so we can improve features, design and performance;
  • detect, diagnose and fix crashes, bugs and compatibility problems;
  • keep the App secure and prevent misuse;
  • comply with legal obligations.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not build advertising profiles.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We share information only:

  • with the service providers listed in Section 3, who process it on our behalf;
  • when required by law, regulation, legal process or a government request, or to protect the rights, property or safety of our users, us or others;
  • in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing or sale of all or part of our business, in which case this Policy will continue to apply to the transferred information.

5. Legal bases (EEA/UK — GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, we process your information on these legal bases:

  • Legitimate interests: to operate, secure and improve the App, and to diagnose crashes and errors.
  • Consent: where local law requires consent for analytics, we rely on it, and you may withdraw it at any time.
  • Legal obligation: where processing is needed to comply with the law.

6. Data retention

  • Firebase Analytics data is kept according to the retention setting of our Firebase project (no longer than 14 months for event-level data) and is then deleted or aggregated.
  • Crashlytics crash reports are kept for up to 90 days.
  • Data stored only on your device stays there until you clear the App's data or uninstall the App.

We may keep information longer if the law requires it.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the following rights.

EEA/UK (GDPR): to access, correct or delete your personal data; to receive a portable copy; to object to or restrict processing; to withdraw consent at any time; and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

California and other U.S. states (CCPA/CPRA and similar laws): to know what personal information we collect, use and disclose; to request deletion or correction; to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information and of targeted advertising (we do not sell or share it, and we do not use it for targeted advertising); and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

Other regions: residents of Brazil (LGPD), Canada (PIPEDA), Vietnam (Decree 13/2023/ND-CP) and other jurisdictions may have similar rights, which we honor as required by law.

How to make a request: email contact@tranquilmind.co. The App has no account, so we cannot link your data to you by name. Please include your device model and, if possible, your advertising ID (found in your Android ads settings) so we can locate the related records. We will respond within the time required by applicable law. You can also stop all future collection at any time by uninstalling the App.

8. Children's privacy

The App is intended for a general audience aged 13 and older and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under the minimum age of digital consent in your country, such as 16 in some EEA countries). If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us at contact@tranquilmind.co and we will delete it.

9. International data transfers

Our service providers may process information on servers in the United States and other countries whose data protection laws may differ from those in your country. Where required, these transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

10. Data security

Information sent from the App to our service providers is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information, but no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.
