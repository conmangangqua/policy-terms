Klarvo is a media player. It lets you add IPTV playlists you already have — by M3U or M3U8 link, by Xtream Codes login, or from a playlist file on your device — and then browse, search, organise, and play what those playlists contain, with a program guide, favorites, collections, watch history, resume, casting to Chromecast, and handoff to an external player.

Klarvo does not provide, host, store, transmit, curate, sell, or resell any television channel, film, or series. We supply no content library and no subscription to any content service. Every stream you watch through a playlist comes from a source you chose and added yourself.
