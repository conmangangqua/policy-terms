Privacy Policy

App: Klarvo Effective: 2026-08-21 Last Updated: 2026-08-21

Klarvo ("the App", "we", "us", "our"), operated by Tranquil Mind Technology JSC, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices and rights you have. By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

Klarvo is an IPTV player for the M3U, M3U8, and Xtream Codes playlists you supply. You do not need to create a Klarvo account to use it. Any username, password, or server address you enter (for example, Xtream Codes credentials) is used only to connect to the playlist provider you choose; it is stored on your device to load your channels and is not sent to us.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask you to register or provide personal details to use Klarvo. The App works with data collected automatically by third-party services for ads, analytics, and stability:

  • Device & advertising identifiers — including the Android Advertising ID (via the AD_ID permission), used for advertising and measurement.
  • Approximate location — derived by ad partners from your IP address (we do not use GPS or precise-location permissions).
  • App activity & diagnostics — in-app events, feature usage, in-app search terms, and crash logs used to keep the App stable and to improve it.

Content you add — playlist links, uploaded .m3u files, favorites, watch history, and your parental-control PIN — is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to us.

2. How we use information

We use information to: provide and operate the App; remember your playlists, favorites, and playback progress; serve and measure advertising; run analytics to fix bugs and improve features; and detect fraud and protect security.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information for money. Information is shared with the third-party services listed above so they can perform their functions, and may be disclosed for legal reasons or as part of a business transfer. If ads are personalized, some U.S. state laws may treat this data flow as "sharing" for targeted advertising; you can opt out as described in Sections 3 and 7.

5. Legal bases (EEA/UK — GDPR)

We rely on: your consent for personalized ads and analytics where required; our legitimate interests in providing core functionality, security, and improvement; and legal obligations where applicable.

6. Data retention

We keep data only as long as necessary for the purposes above or as required by law. Ad and analytics partners retain identifiers per their own retention periods. Crash and diagnostic data is kept for a limited period. Data stored on your device remains until you delete it or uninstall the App.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, port, object to, or restrict processing of your data, and to withdraw consent or complain to a data protection authority (GDPR); to know, delete, correct, and opt out of the sale/sharing or targeted advertising of your data, without discrimination (CCPA/CPRA); and similar rights under LGPD, PIPEDA, and other laws. Because Klarvo has no account system, please contact us and include your advertising ID and device details so we can act on your request.

8. Children's privacy

Klarvo is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided such information, contact us and we will delete it. We handle age-restricted regions in line with COPPA and applicable EEA age-of-consent rules.

9. International data transfers

Your data may be processed in countries other than your own, including the United States. Where required, we and our partners use appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

10. Data security

We use encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and reasonable measures to protect data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

11. Third-party content

Klarvo is a player only. It does not host, provide, or control any channels, playlists, or media — all content comes from the playlists you supply, and some streams (including embedded live feeds) originate from third parties. We are not responsible for third-party content, streams, or links, or for your right to access them; please review the relevant providers' policies.

12. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy. Material changes will be posted here with a new "Last updated" date; continued use of the App means you accept the updated Policy.
