Privacy Policy

App: Bunnattery: Emoji Battery Icon Effective: 2026-10-04 Last Updated: 2026-10-04

Bunnattery: Emoji Battery Icon ("the App", "we", "us", "our"), operated by Tranquil Mind Technology JSC, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices and rights you have. By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

Bunnattery is a personalization app that lets you replace your battery icon with cute bunny and emoji designs, decorate your status bar, cover the notch, add on-screen pet characters and apply playful battery effects. You do not need to create an account, sign in, or give us your name or email to use it.

1. Information we collect

Information collected automatically

  • Device and advertising identifiers — Android advertising ID (AAID), app-instance ID, device model, OS version, language and country, network type.
  • Approximate location derived from IP address (country/region level). The App does not request GPS or any location permission.
  • App activity and diagnostics — screens opened, features used (which battery skin, emoji, status bar style, notch cover or pet you apply), ad impressions and clicks, session length, crash logs, stack traces and performance data.
  • Install attribution data — referrer/campaign information used to measure which marketing channel led to your install.

Information stored only on your device (never uploaded to us)

  • Your chosen battery icons, emoji, stickers, pets, status bar and notch styles, favourites, prank/troll settings, the "not-allowed apps" list, notification and reminder preferences and other app settings. These are saved in local storage on your phone and are deleted when you uninstall the App.
  • Accessibility service data. The App uses an Android Accessibility Service solely to draw its decorative overlay on top of the real status bar and to detect which app is currently in the foreground so it can hide the overlay in apps you excluded. The service is configured with canRetrieveWindowContent="false" and subscribes only to window-state-change events — it cannot and does not read the content of your screen, your messages, passwords, form fields or any text you type, and nothing from this service is transmitted off your device.
  • Network state. The App reads the Wi-Fi/mobile signal level (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, ACCESS_WIFI_STATE) only to redraw the signal icons inside its own status bar overlay. It does not inspect, log or transmit your network traffic, SSID list or browsing activity.

Information we do not collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, camera or microphone input, precise location, SMS or call logs. The App requests no such permissions.

2. How we use information

  • To provide and operate the App and remember your customizations.
  • To show advertising, including measurement and frequency capping.
  • For analytics and product improvement (which features are used, where users drop off).
  • For crash reporting, stability and performance monitoring.
  • For fraud prevention, security and abuse detection (including invalid ad traffic).
  • To send local reminder notifications you enabled (these are generated on your device).
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not use your information for credit, employment, insurance or any automated decision-making with legal effect.
